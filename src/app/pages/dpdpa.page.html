The compliance reality

You already know what's wrong.
You just don't know where to start.

Section 6(10)Consent lives in a spreadsheet

A checkbox at signup, a tick on a form. But can you produce a timestamped, purpose-specific record of exactly what was shown and agreed to? The burden of proof sits with you.

Rule 14(3)Rights requests arrive on WhatsApp

A user asks for their data to be deleted. It sits in an inbox. The statutory clock is already running — with no SLA tracking and no audit trail to prove you resolved it in time.

Section 8(6) + Rule 7No breach plan exists

A breach at 11 PM on Saturday: who classifies severity, who drafts the Board notification, who signs off? The 72-hour clock does not pause for weekends.

Section 8(2)Your vendor list is a liability

Payment gateways, cloud hosts, email platforms, analytics tools — each processes personal data on your behalf, and each requires a signed Data Processing Agreement. How many do you actually have?

Frequently asked questions
What is the penalty for non-compliance?
The highest tier — failure to take reasonable security safeguards — reaches up to ₹250 crore per instance. Other breaches of obligation carry their own penalty bands.
We're already RBI-regulated. Isn't that enough?
No. DPDPA adds consent, data-principal rights, Board notification and DPO obligations that RBI frameworks do not cover. The two run in parallel.
How long does compliance take?
A typical mid-size enterprise: 2 weeks assessment, 4–6 weeks remediation and deployment — audit-ready in roughly 8–12 weeks.
Do we need a Data Protection Officer?
Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint one. Our vDPO-as-a-Service provides a qualified, India-based officer at a fraction of a full-time hire.
Can we buy just the platform, or just the consulting?
Either. Most clients take both because software without process fails audits, and process without software cannot produce evidence at speed.
Where is our data stored?
In Indian Tier-4 datacenters, encrypted at rest and in transit, with immutable evidence storage for audit trails.
What happens if we have a breach during the engagement?
Our DFIR team engages immediately, the dual clock starts in the platform, and notification packs are generated to meet both CERT-In and DPDPA windows.
How does the free assessment work?
A short discovery of your data flows and current controls, returned as a scored gap report with prioritised actions — yours to keep, with no obligation.
