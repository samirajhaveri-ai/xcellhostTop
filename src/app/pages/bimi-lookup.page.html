How BIMI connects email to your brand

A logo location is one part of the display decision.

BIMI, or Brand Indicators for Message Identification, lets domain owners publish logo preferences for supporting mail services. The assertion normally lives at default._bimi.example.com. It references a brand indicator and, where supplied, evidence about the mark.

Logo display also depends on mail authentication, enforcement coverage, provider policy and accepted evidence. This page gives you DNS and asset observations. It does not act as a mailbox receiver or certify a sender's identity.

l= LOGO

The HTTPS indicator location, normally an SVG Tiny P/S file.

a= EVIDENCE

An optional evidence location, commonly a VMC or CMC certificate.

SELECTOR

A label choosing the assertion; default is common, but custom selectors exist.

Further guidance: BIMI Group implementation guide · BIMI selectors.
