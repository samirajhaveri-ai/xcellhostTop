Overview — What is Microsoft 365?

Microsoft 365 (formerly Office 365) is Microsoft's cloud-based productivity platform, trusted by over 345 million users worldwide. It bundles your team's email, calendar, video meetings, document collaboration and cloud storage into a single subscription — hosted on Microsoft's globally distributed Azure infrastructure.

For Indian businesses, Microsoft 365 means your team gets @yourcompany.com professional email, the full Office desktop apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Microsoft Teams for video meetings and chat, and 1 TB OneDrive storage per user — all from one subscription billed in INR with GST.
