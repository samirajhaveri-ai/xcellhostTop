How DMARC uses policy and alignment

A published request, supported by actual message authentication.

DMARC connects the visible From domain to SPF or DKIM authentication. A message passes if at least one mechanism passes and its authenticated identifier aligns with the From domain. A policy published at _dmarc.example.com describes the domain owner's handling preferences for failures and requests reports where configured.

Publishing a policy does not verify that your senders authenticate correctly or that every receiver will apply the requested handling. Review real sending traffic, reporting evidence, forwarding and mailing-list behavior before changing enforcement.

p / sp / np

Declare domain and inherited subdomain preferences; actual policy discovery can affect which record applies.

adkim / aspf

Choose relaxed organizational-domain alignment or strict exact-domain matching.

rua / ruf

Request aggregate or message-specific failure reports; publication does not guarantee receipt.

Standards: RFC 9989: current DMARC · RFC 7489: legacy DMARC.
