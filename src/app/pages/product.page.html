Why You Need Acronis Advanced EDR

Traditional antivirus is no longer adequate for today’s connected, fast-moving threats.

Limitations of traditional antivirus

  1. Signature-based detectionDetects only known threats and fails against new or unknown attacks.
  2. Slow response to zero-day attacksNo protection against zero-day exploits and fileless malware.
  3. No visibility across systemsLimited visibility into attack origin, lateral movement, and user behavior.
  4. Cannot detect advanced threatsFails to stop ransomware variants, APTs, and insider threats.
  5. No context or correlationCannot connect multiple events to understand the full attack story.
  6. Reactive, not proactiveActs after infection, not before.
  7. Endpoint-only protectionNo integration with network, cloud, or identity systems.
  8. High false positives / missed threatsLeads to alert fatigue and missed real attacks.
  9. No automated responseRequires manual intervention, slowing response time.
  10. Ineffective against modern ransomwareModern attacks encrypt data fast and spread across the network.

Why you need Acronis Advanced EDR

  1. Behavioral & AI-based detectionDetects unknown threats using behavioral analysis and machine learning.
  2. Full attack visibilitySee the entire attack timeline, entry point, and spread across endpoints.
  3. Real-time threat detection & responseIdentify and stop threats in real time before damage occurs.
  4. Correlation of eventsConnect multiple signals and alerts into a complete attack story.
  5. Advanced ransomware protectionDetect encryption behavior, stop the attack, and roll back to a clean state.
  6. Automated incident responseIsolate infected devices, kill malicious processes, and remediate instantly.
  7. Cross-platform protectionProtect endpoints, servers, and cloud workloads across your environment.
  8. Deep forensic insightsRoot-cause analysis, detailed reporting, and compliance support.
  9. Continuous monitoring (24/7)Always-on monitoring, threat hunting, and protection.
  10. Integrated cyber protection platformAcronis combines EDR, anti-malware, backup, and disaster recovery in one platform.
