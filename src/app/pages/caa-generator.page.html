Turn certificate choices into DNS records

CAA expresses issuance rules; it does not issue a certificate.

A CAA record has a flags field, a property tag and a value. The builder creates one record per selected issuer and property, plus any reporting destinations. The resulting set is a proposal until you publish it.

issue

Names an ordinary-certificate issuer.

issuewild

Supplies separate wildcard grants.

iodef

Supplies a reporting destination.

Compare the generated set with existing CAA records and the requirements of your certificate workflows. CA-specific extensions and alias processing require separate review.

References: CAA record syntax · CAA deployment guidance.
