What affects email health?

DNS configuration is important evidence, but only part of delivery.

SPF describes authorized envelope senders, DKIM publishes signing keys and DMARC connects authenticated identifiers to the visible From domain. MX records route inbound mail, while transport-policy records publish security and reporting preferences.

A domain can publish all of those records and still have a sending issue. Message authentication, content, sending-IP reputation, actual TLS support and mailbox-provider policy must be evaluated separately. This checker helps you locate DNS configuration concerns and choose the next dedicated test.

AUTHENTICATION DNS

Policy and key publication does not prove that a real message passes.

TRANSPORT DNS

MTA-STS and TLS-RPT markers do not prove a live encrypted SMTP session.

REPUTATION

Audit your actual outbound sender IPs and provider-specific evidence separately.

References: SPF · DMARC · MTA-STS · TLS reporting.
