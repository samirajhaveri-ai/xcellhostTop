What Scrutiny DLP does

Most data does not leave through a dramatic breach. It walks out on a USB stick, in a personal webmail attachment, in a screenshot, in a photo taken of the screen, or pasted into a public AI chatbot. Scrutiny DLP covers every one of those channels from a single policy engine, and runs fully on-premise if your compliance position requires it. Discover. Classify. Monitor. Protect.
