What Scrutiny DLP does

Discover. Classify. Monitor. Protect.

Most data does not leave through a dramatic breach. It walks out on a USB stick, in personal webmail, in a screenshot, in a photo of a screen, or pasted into a public AI chatbot. Scrutiny DLP covers those channels from one policy engine and can run fully on-premise.

Every exit route, one policy engine

Control USB, print, clipboard, web and application uploads, RDP, screenshots, email and removable media.

Built for how data leaks today

Govern GenAI uploads, detect screen photography, add hidden watermarks and inspect images with OCR.

Evidence that stands up later

Capture screens, window changes and video, sync evidence offline, quarantine risky files and retain immutable logs.

A genuine on-premise option

Choose a local console, your private cloud or hybrid deployment, with support for employee-owned devices.
