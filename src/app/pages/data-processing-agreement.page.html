01
FOUNDATION

Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) supplements the applicable services agreement, order form or statement of work between XcellHost Cloud Services Pvt. Ltd. (“XcellHost”) and the customer identified in that agreement (“Customer”).

It governs XcellHost’s processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer in connection with the contracted services. The parties intend this DPA to support compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules, and with other data-protection requirements expressly agreed for the services.

02
SHARED LANGUAGE

Definitions

Personal Data
Digital data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to that data.
Processing
Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, retrieval, disclosure, transfer or deletion.
Customer
The party that determines why and how Customer Personal Data is processed, acting as Controller or Data Fiduciary as applicable.
XcellHost
The party processing Customer Personal Data on documented instructions, acting as Processor or Data Processor as applicable.
Sub-processor
A third party appointed by XcellHost to process Customer Personal Data for part of the services.
Personal Data Breach
A confirmed breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Customer Personal Data.
03
PROCESSING DETAILS

Scope and nature of processing

XcellHost processes Customer Personal Data only as needed to deliver, secure, monitor, maintain and support the contracted services; manage the Customer account; prevent abuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Purpose

Provisioning and operating the selected cloud, hosting, cybersecurity, backup, communications or managed services.

Duration

For the service term and any limited period required for return, deletion, backup expiry or lawful retention.

Data subjects

Customer personnel, authorised users, end customers and other individuals whose data the Customer places in the services.

Data categories

Account and contact data, service content, credentials, logs, support records and other data determined by the Customer.

XcellHost does not use Customer Personal Data for independent advertising or sell it. Additional processing details may be set out in the applicable service order.

04
CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

Customer obligations

The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness, quality and accuracy of Customer Personal Data and for its use of the services. The Customer will:

  • provide lawful, complete and documented processing instructions;
  • give required notices and obtain valid consent or another lawful basis where applicable;
  • configure its accounts, access permissions, endpoints and applications securely;
  • avoid uploading data prohibited by the service agreement or not needed for the stated purpose; and
  • respond to Data Principal requests and regulatory enquiries for which it is responsible.
05
XCELLHOST RESPONSIBILITY

XcellHost obligations

XcellHost will:

  • process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions, unless processing is required by applicable law;
  • ensure personnel authorised to process the data are subject to confidentiality obligations;
  • maintain appropriate technical and organisational safeguards proportionate to the service and risk;
  • provide reasonable assistance with security, impact assessments, lawful regulatory consultations and Data Principal requests;
  • notify the Customer without undue delay after confirming a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data and provide available information needed for the Customer’s response; and
  • maintain processing records where required by applicable law.
06
SAFEGUARDS

Technical and organisational security measures

Controls are selected according to the contracted service, deployment model and risk. They may include the following:

domain

Physical

  • Controlled facility access
  • Visitor management
  • Environmental monitoring
  • Power, cooling and fire controls
shield_lock

Logical

  • Role-based access controls
  • Multi-factor authentication where supported
  • Tenant and network segmentation
  • Encryption in transit and at rest where applicable
monitor_heart

Operational

  • Monitoring and logging
  • Vulnerability and patch management
  • Incident-response procedures
  • Security awareness and access reviews

Specific controls, certifications, recovery objectives and service levels are governed by the relevant service description, architecture and SLA.

07
SERVICE DELIVERY

Sub-processors

The Customer gives XcellHost general authorisation to appoint Sub-processors needed to deliver the services, including infrastructure, software, connectivity, support and specialist service providers.

  • XcellHost will require each Sub-processor to protect Customer Personal Data through written obligations appropriate to its processing.
  • XcellHost remains responsible for its Sub-processors’ performance to the extent required by the applicable agreement and law.
  • Where contractually required, XcellHost will provide advance notice of a material new Sub-processor and a reasonable opportunity to raise a documented data-protection objection.
08
DATA LOCATION

International transfers

Service locations and data-residency commitments are those selected in the service order. XcellHost will not transfer Customer Personal Data contrary to an agreed location restriction or applicable law.

If an authorised transfer requires additional safeguards, XcellHost will use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism and require the recipient to provide protections consistent with this DPA. Remote support access does not change the primary hosting location unless the parties agree otherwise.

09
INDIVIDUAL REQUESTS

Data Principal rights

The Customer is responsible for responding to requests concerning Customer Personal Data. If XcellHost receives a request directly, it will not independently respond on the Customer’s behalf unless legally required. XcellHost will promptly forward the request where the Customer can be identified.

Taking account of the nature of the processing, XcellHost will provide reasonable assistance through available service functionality or support processes for valid requests involving access, correction, completion, updating, erasure, grievance handling or nomination.

10
ASSURANCE

Audits and compliance information

On reasonable written request, XcellHost will provide information needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. Where available and appropriate, this may include certifications, control summaries, independent assessment reports or responses to security questionnaires.

If that information is insufficient, the Customer may request an audit no more than once in a 12-month period, unless a confirmed breach or regulator requires otherwise. Audits require reasonable advance notice, must protect other customers’ information, avoid service disruption and be conducted during normal business hours at the requesting Customer’s cost.

11
SERVICE END

Return, deletion and liability

At the end of the services, XcellHost will return or delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with the Customer’s documented choice, available service functionality and the applicable agreement. Data may remain temporarily in protected backups until ordinary expiry and may be retained where required by law.

Each party’s liability arising from this DPA is subject to the exclusions and limitations in the agreement governing the services, except where those limitations are prohibited by applicable law. Nothing in this DPA reduces a party’s independent obligations to individuals or regulators.
