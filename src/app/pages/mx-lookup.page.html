How a domain directs incoming mail

Read the destination and its preference together.

An MX record names a receiving mail server and gives it a numeric preference. Sending systems use that information to choose where to attempt delivery. A smaller preference number is considered before a larger one; equal values form a shared preference group.

Website hosting and email hosting can use entirely different providers. An MX target may therefore look unrelated to your website’s address. Compare it with your email provider’s published setup instructions before making a change.

EXCHANGER

The hostname of a mail destination. Its A or AAAA answers supply addresses.

PREFERENCE

A routing order, with lower numbers preferred. It is not an availability score.

TTL

The remaining cache lifetime in seconds. Updates can take time to appear in cached answers.
