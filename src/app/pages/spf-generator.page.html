How SPF connects your sending sources

One policy at the envelope-sender domain.

SPF lets a receiving server compare a sending IP with the policy published for an envelope-sender domain. The policy starts with v=spf1, lists mechanisms such as provider includes or IP ranges, and normally ends with an all mechanism.

This generator builds that configuration. It does not determine your actual sending infrastructure, resolve nested includes or run a sender-specific SPF evaluation. Keep the provider account instructions and your complete sender inventory alongside the draft.

INCLUDE

Reference another domain's SPF policy. Nested policies can add lookup cost.

IP4 / IP6

Authorize an address or CIDR range without a DNS lookup for that term.

ALL POLICY

Describe the result when no earlier mechanism matches; the receiver still decides handling.
