school

One instant. Many clock readings.

Separate timestamp units from calendar formatting.

A Unix timestamp is a count from the UTC epoch. Time-zone selection changes how that instant appears on a calendar, while seconds and milliseconds describe the same instant at different scales.

Seconds

1735689600 is 2025-01-01 at midnight UTC. A decimal such as .123 preserves 123 milliseconds.

Milliseconds

1735689600123 is the same instant as 1735689600.123 seconds. JavaScript Date values use milliseconds.

Time zones

Asia/Kolkata displays that midnight as 05:30. Named zones account for seasonal and historical rules supplied by your browser.
