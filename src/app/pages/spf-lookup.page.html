How SPF describes authorized senders

A DNS policy used during SMTP authentication.

SPF publishes a TXT policy beginning with v=spf1 at the envelope identity's domain. A receiving mail system evaluates its mechanisms against a sending IP and identity. Mechanisms can reference literal networks, DNS addresses or other SPF policies.

This page reads those TXT policies and explains their structure. It can reveal configuration issues, but it does not receive an email, test an IP or decide a message's SPF result. The ten-term limit applies to terms actually evaluated during a check, not simply the number of DNS requests sent by this page.

INCLUDE

Evaluates another policy. Its pass result is what matches the include mechanism.

ALL

Always matches when reached. Later mechanisms and any redirect are ignored.

TEN TERMS

A sender-aware evaluation may process at most ten DNS-querying terms across recursion.

Reference: RFC 7208: Sender Policy Framework.

Inspect a policy in four steps

Start with the exact sending domain.

  1. Enter the envelope domain

    Use the MAIL FROM or HELO identity you want to inspect, including a subdomain when applicable.

  2. Choose a resolver and scope

    Select Google or Cloudflare, and choose whether to expand static dependencies.

  3. Review policy text and diagnostics

    Check duplicates, malformed terms, ignored mechanisms and unresolved targets.

  4. Validate changes with real senders

    Use sender-aware SPF checks before deployment, then keep a report for review.
