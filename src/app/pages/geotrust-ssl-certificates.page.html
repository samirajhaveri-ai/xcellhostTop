Overview — What is a GeoTrust SSL certificate?

A GeoTrust SSL/TLS certificate encrypts the connection between your website and your visitors, so passwords, payments and personal data can't be intercepted. GeoTrust — part of the DigiCert family of certificate authorities — is trusted by 99.9% of browsers and mobile devices, and shows the padlock (and, for EV, your verified business name) that customers look for before they buy.
