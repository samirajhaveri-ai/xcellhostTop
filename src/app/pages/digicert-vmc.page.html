Before you order

VMC readiness checklist

Completing these items first keeps validation predictable.

1

Enforce DMARC

Publish a DMARC policy of p=quarantine or p=reject, with SPF and DKIM aligned for your approved senders.

2

Confirm trademark eligibility

Your logo must be registered with an accepted intellectual-property office, such as IP India, or qualify as an official government mark.

3

Prepare the logo

Convert the approved square mark to the BIMI-compatible SVG profile without changing the registered design.

4

Complete DigiCert validation

Provide organization and identity evidence. Validation may include notarised identification and a verification call.

5

Publish BIMI DNS

Add the BIMI record pointing to the hosted logo and VMC files, then verify DNS and mailbox-provider checks.
