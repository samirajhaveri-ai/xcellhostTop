What Vortex SEG does

Email is still where most intrusions begin. Vortex SEG sits in front of your mail platform—as an MX gateway, API integration or SMTP relay—and inspects inbound, outbound and internal messages for phishing, impersonation, malware and sensitive data. Advanced email security. Complete protection.

01

Stops the threat before the mailbox

Inbound, outbound and internal mail
  • Phishing, credential harvesting and malicious URLs
  • Impersonation and display-name fraud
  • Multi-engine anti-malware with dynamic sandboxing
  • Ransomware and zero-day payloads detonated before delivery
02

Stops the data leaving

DLP is built in, not a separate product
  • Content inspection and classification on outbound mail
  • OCR scanning of images and scanned attachments
  • Quarantine policy and reviewer workflows
  • Encrypted delivery through a branded secure portal
