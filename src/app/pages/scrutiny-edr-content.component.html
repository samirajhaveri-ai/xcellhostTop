What Scrutiny EDR does

A lightweight agent on every Windows, macOS and Linux endpoint continuously records process, file, registry and network activity. Self-learning behavioural analytics flag what looks wrong—known malware, unknown variants and zero-day techniques alike—and the same agent carries out the response. Detect. Investigate. Respond. Recover.
