What Vortex SOC does

Vortex SOC is the security operations suite at the centre of the Cyberstanc platform. It collects logs, events and telemetry from endpoints, cloud, network, identity and applications into a single data lake, then applies detection rules, behavioural baselining and threat intelligence so raw alerts become scored, evidenced cases an analyst can work. Detect advanced threats. Correlate every signal. Automate the response. Reduce the risk.
