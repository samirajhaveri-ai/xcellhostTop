How a DMARC policy works

Authentication, alignment and requested handling are different checks.

DMARC connects the domain people see in an email's From header to authenticated SPF or DKIM identifiers. A policy at _dmarc.example.com tells receivers how the domain owner prefers failing messages to be handled and where reports may be sent.

A message passes when at least one SPF or DKIM result passes and aligns with the From domain. A published policy does not prove that your senders authenticate correctly. Review real aggregate reports and message headers before changing enforcement.

POLICY

p sets the domain preference; sp and np describe inherited subdomain branches.

ALIGNMENT

Strict means exact domain. Relaxed uses a shared organizational domain.

REPORTING

rua requests aggregate reports; ruf requests message-specific failure information.

Standards: RFC 9989: DMARC · Aggregate reporting. Current interpretation uses t testing; removed pct, ri and rf values are shown as legacy data.
