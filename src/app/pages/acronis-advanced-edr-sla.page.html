Purpose

This Service Level Agreement defines the service commitments, support standards, incident response, security monitoring and customer responsibilities for the Acronis Advanced EDR Security service provided by XcellHost Cloud Services Pvt. Ltd.

Acronis Advanced EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) delivers AI-powered threat detection, endpoint protection, behavioral analysis, incident investigation, automated response and ransomware protection to help organizations defend against advanced cyber threats.

Important Notes

This SLA covers the management, monitoring and support of the Acronis Advanced EDR platform. It does not guarantee prevention of every cyberattack or malware incident.

If XcellHost provides this as a Managed Detection and Response (MDR) service, additional SLAs can be defined for continuous SOC monitoring, proactive threat hunting and incident remediation activities.

Customer cooperation is required for endpoint deployment, incident investigation and remediation to achieve the stated response and recovery objectives.
