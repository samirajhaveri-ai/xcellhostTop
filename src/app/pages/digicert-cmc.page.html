Before you order

CMC readiness checklist

Good evidence and clean email authentication keep validation moving.

1

Enforce DMARC

Use p=quarantine or p=reject and confirm SPF/DKIM alignment for all legitimate senders.

2

Prove logo history

Collect at least 12 months of verifiable public use on a domain you control—such as dated site captures and brand materials.

3

Prepare the logo SVG

Convert the established square logo into the BIMI-compatible SVG profile without altering the design being validated.

4

Complete DigiCert checks

Provide organization, domain and logo evidence and respond to the validation specialist’s confirmation requests.

5

Publish the BIMI record

Point DNS to the approved logo and certificate files, then test provider visibility and authentication results.
