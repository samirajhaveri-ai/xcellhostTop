What is identity resilience, and why isn't native IdP backup enough?

Identity resilience is the ability to protect, investigate and recover your identity providers — Entra ID, Active Directory and Okta — so people and services keep working during an incident. Native tools keep limited history and restore coarsely. XcellHost delivers Druva's immutable identity backups with granular, identity-aware recovery, managed 24×7 from India.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Around 90% of breaches begin with compromised credentials
  • Native IdP recycle bins retain little and restore coarsely
  • Recovery must remove the attacker's changes, not users' access
  • One policy should cover cloud, on-prem and hybrid identity
