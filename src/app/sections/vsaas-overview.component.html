What is Video Surveillance-as-a-Service?

VSaaS is cloud-hosted video surveillance: recording, live viewing, storage, AI analytics and alerts delivered as a subscription instead of on-site DVR/NVR servers. XcellHost VSaaS, powered by XcellCamCloud, adds 40+ AI analytics, device health checks and command-centre workflows to the cameras you already own — billed in INR with a GST invoice.

WHY BUSINESSES MOVE TO VSaaS
  • Proactive threat detection — Real-time analytics flag intrusions, fights, theft or fire before they escalate.
  • Cloud-first, no heavy infrastructure — No on-site DVR/NVR sprawl or high local storage costs.
  • AI that adds business value — Footfall, heatmaps, workforce compliance and patient-safety insights.
  • Real-time alerts & automated response — Notifications on mobile, email or dashboard — no constant watching.
  • Centralised multi-site monitoring — Retail chains, warehouses and campuses from one dashboard.
