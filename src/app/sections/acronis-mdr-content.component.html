What is managed detection and response (MDR)?

Managed detection and response (MDR) is a 24×7×365 service in which a security operations centre (SOC) team monitors your environment, investigates suspicious activity and responds to threats on your behalf — containing, remediating and reporting, not just forwarding alerts. XcellHost MDR runs on Acronis EDR/XDR, so response can also include rolling back ransomware changes and restoring data from backup. It is offered in Standard and Advanced tiers and billed per protected workload in INR.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • MDR is an outsourced SOC: people who act, not just software that alerts
  • Round-the-clock cover in-house usually needs five or more analysts
  • Analysts investigate and contain — traditional MSSPs mostly forward alerts
  • Integrated backup lets response include rollback and recovery
  • Standard for containment and guidance; Advanced for full remediation and hunting

Why outsource your security operations?

The talent gap

Security analysts are scarce and expensive, and 24×7 shifts need several of them. MDR gives you the whole team for a per-device fee.

Attacks happen out of hours

Ransomware is often launched at night and on weekends, when nobody's watching the console. MDR is watching.

Alert fatigue

Tools produce hundreds of alerts. Analysts separate the real incident from the noise and act on it.

Predictable cost

A monthly per-workload fee replaces salaries, tools, training and attrition — with no hiring risk.

Compliance & insurance

Documented 24×7 monitoring and incident reports support DPDP Act, RBI/SEBI expectations and cyber-insurance questionnaires.
