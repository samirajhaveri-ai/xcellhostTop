Overview

What is Acronis MDR?

Acronis Managed Detection and Response (MDR) combines security technology with a team of specialists who monitor, investigate and respond to threats on your behalf. The Acronis Threat Research Unit provides round-the-clock security operations, helping your organisation identify suspicious activity and contain attacks.

Delivered by XcellHost, the service brings together continuous monitoring, proactive threat hunting, incident response and integrated recovery. Your team gets expert support to manage security incidents without having to build and operate its own security operations centre.

Why MDR

You have the tools. Do you have the operations?

MDR is a managed service, not a product — a SOC that operates your detection and response for you, so protection is always on.

You can’t staff a 24/7 SOCClients expect always-on protection, but building and running a round-the-clock SOC in-house is neither cost-effective nor scalable for most businesses.
Tools aren’t enoughEDR and XDR give visibility and investigative insight — but they still rely on human experts to analyse alerts, respond to threats and run root-cause investigations.
Alerts outpace peopleModern threats move fast and generate constant alerts. Without a dedicated team, real incidents get lost in the noise — and dwell time grows.
Response time is the riskAn internal team’s typical MTTR is ~11 hours. With Acronis MDR, defined SLAs cut that to under 1 hour — and critical incidents to ~15 minutes.
