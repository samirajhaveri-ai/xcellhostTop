Overview — What is XcellHost Remote Support?

XcellHost Remote Support, powered by TSplus, lets your support team securely view and control any computer without travelling to the desk or walking someone through every step on the phone. Use on-demand sessions with a short code or unattended access for always-on machines.

Agents connect from a web browser and can chat, transfer files, work across multiple monitors and record sessions for audit. It is an affordable TeamViewer alternative that XcellHost installs, secures and manages—with a GST invoice and 24×7 support.
