Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on validation, installation, pricing and renewals.

What is the difference between Basic, Pro and Enterprise?+

Basic signs and encrypts email. Pro adds document signing and client authentication. Enterprise includes everything in Pro and also validates your organisation, which appears in the certificate.

Is Comodo the same as Sectigo?+

Yes. Comodo CA became Sectigo in 2018; Personal Authentication certificates are issued by Sectigo under the Comodo product name.

What is client authentication?+

The certificate proves who you are when you log in to a VPN, Wi-Fi network or web application, acting as a strong second factor alongside a password.

How long does issuance take?+

Basic and Pro are issued quickly after you verify your email address. Enterprise also needs organisation validation, typically 1–3 working days.

Which email clients are supported?+

Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird, iPhone and iPad, and Gmail with Google Workspace S/MIME.

Should I choose this or DigiCert S/MIME?+

Comodo PA is the lower-cost option with document signing on Pro. DigiCert S/MIME carries the DigiCert brand and OV includes unlimited devices. We can advise based on your team size and systems.

Are prices inclusive of GST?+

No. Prices are in INR per user and exclusive of 18% GST.
