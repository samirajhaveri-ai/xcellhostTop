Overview

What is Microsoft 365 Enterprise?

Microsoft’s flagship productivity and security suite for organisations that need more than the 300-user-capped Business plans — higher seat counts, advanced identity governance, enterprise device management, threat protection, compliance tooling and Windows 11 Enterprise, all under one identity and admin centre.

Capabilities

Microsoft 365 Enterprise at a glance

Five planes — identity, device, threat, compliance and productivity — wired together under one user identity, one admin centre and unified telemetry.

Microsoft Entra ID (P1/P2)

Cloud identity for 3,500+ apps — Conditional Access, MFA, SSPR, Privileged Identity Management and risk-based Identity Protection.

Microsoft Intune

MDM for company devices, MAM for BYOD, Windows Autopilot zero-touch provisioning, and compliance across Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and Linux.

Defender for Office 365

Safe Attachments sandboxing, Safe Links click-time scanning, anti-phishing with impersonation detection, and (P2) automated investigation & response.

Defender for Endpoint

EDR, threat & vulnerability management, threat intelligence and advanced hunting across every endpoint OS.

Microsoft Purview

Sensitivity labels with auto-classification, DLP for PAN/Aadhaar/account numbers, eDiscovery, audit, communication compliance and insider risk.

Teams Phone

Cloud PBX with auto-attendant, call queues, voicemail transcription and PSTN via Direct Routing or Operator Connect — replace legacy PBX.

Power BI Pro (E5)

A per-user Power BI Pro licence for every E5 user — the foundation for self-service analytics across the organisation.

Windows 11 Enterprise

Bundled upgrade rights with E3 and E5 — Application Guard, Credential Guard, AppLocker and BitLocker managed via Intune.
