Overview — What is a VMC and BIMI?

BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification) is the email standard that lets your brand logo appear next to your messages in the inbox. A Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) from DigiCert authenticates that logo—confirming you own the trademark—so mailbox providers like Gmail, Apple Mail and Yahoo can display it, along with the blue verified checkmark in Gmail.

Prerequisites for DigiCert Verified Mark Certificate (VMC)

Make sure these conditions are met before purchasing a DigiCert VMC Certificate. Follow these steps for an easier setup.

1

Secure a Legal Trademark for your Logo

Your logo must be legally trademarked with the appropriate authority in your region. Government entities may be exempt.

2

Save your Logo in .SVG Format

Prepare the trademarked logo in SVG Tiny PS format with a transparent background and all BIMI formatting requirements met.

3

Set up DMARC

Use a p=quarantine or p=reject policy to block unauthorised senders, with SPF and DKIM configured.

How DigiCert VMC Certificate Works?

  • DMARC Compliance: The domain must enforce DMARC with a policy of p=quarantine or p=reject.
  • Trademarked Logo in SVG Tiny PS Format: Your registered brand logo must meet the BIMI SVG Tiny Portable/Secure specification.
  • VMC Issuance: DigiCert verifies domain ownership, trademark rights and organisational identity before issuing the certificate.
  • DNS Record Setup: A BIMI DNS record connects the verified logo and VMC to your sending domain.
  • Sender Authentication: The receiving mailbox checks VMC, DMARC, DKIM and SPF before trusting the message.
  • Brand Logo Display: When every check passes, supported email clients can display the verified logo and blue checkmark.
