What is an Azure Managed Service Provider?

An Azure MSP runs your Azure cloud for you. XcellHost plans and delivers your migration, then handles 24×7 monitoring, security, patching, backups, cost optimisation and DevOps.

Is the Azure bill included in the price?

No. The plan price is the XcellHost management fee. Your Azure infrastructure usage is billed separately at cost.

Can you migrate us to Azure from on-premise or another cloud?

Yes. We migrate servers, databases and applications with minimal downtime, then validate performance, security and backups before go-live.

Do we keep control of our Azure subscription?

Absolutely. It is your Azure account and your data. You keep full ownership and visibility at all times.

How do you reduce our cloud costs?

We right-size resources, remove idle services, apply savings plans and reserved capacity, and review your Azure spend every month.

What about security and compliance?

We harden your environment, manage patching, monitor threats and help you meet standards such as ISO 27001, SOC 2 and PCI-DSS.

What support and SLA do we get?

You receive 24×7 monitoring and support from our NOC and SOC, a named account manager and response-time SLAs based on your plan.

How is it priced, and do I get a GST invoice?

Managed Azure uses a predictable monthly management fee, right-sized to your workloads. Every invoice is GST-compliant.
