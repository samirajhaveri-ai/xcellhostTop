What is an AI voicebot?
An AI-powered virtual agent that talks with callers. It uses speech recognition and natural language processing to understand what people say and respond — or complete tasks — by voice.
How does an AI voicebot work?
Speech recognition turns the caller's words into text, NLU works out the intent, dialog management decides the reply using your data and rules, and text-to-speech speaks it back — in real time.
What are the benefits?
24×7 availability, shorter waits, automated repetitive calls, higher satisfaction, lower costs and personalised conversations.
Can it handle multiple languages?
Yes — Hindi, English and 40+ other languages, including switching language mid-call.
How is data kept secure?
Calls and transcripts are encrypted, access is controlled, and data is hosted in XcellHost's Indian data centres.
Can it integrate with our business tools?
Yes — CRMs such as Zoho and Salesforce, helpdesks, e-commerce platforms, telephony/SIP and your own APIs.
How do we implement it?
We map your call flows, connect your systems and phone numbers, train the agent on your content and go live — usually starting with one use case and expanding.
Can it make outbound calls?
Yes — reminders, lead qualification, surveys and collections, with compliant calling windows and do-not-call handling.
