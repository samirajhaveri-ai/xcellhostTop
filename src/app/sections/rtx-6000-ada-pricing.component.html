What is the RTX 6000 Ada cloud GPU used for?

The NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada Generation is a 48 GB professional GPU for 3D design, CAD, real-time ray tracing, video and generative AI. XcellHost rents it as a cloud workstation or server from Indian data centres from ₹59,900 a month (excl. GST), so teams can open large models and render from any laptop.

  • About 2.4× the FP32 throughput of RTX A6000
  • Runs Revit, SolidWorks, 3ds Max, Unreal and Omniverse as a remote desktop
  • Three AV1 encoders for video teams
