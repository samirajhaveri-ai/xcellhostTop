What is managed Oracle Database?
Managed Oracle Database is Oracle run for you by a provider — hosting, patching, RMAN backups, Data Guard standby and monitoring are handled, with optional DBA services for tuning and upgrades. XcellHost designs placement around your Oracle licence agreement, pins cores and documents your licence position, in Indian data centres.
Can we bring our own Oracle licences?
Yes — that is the usual model. Placement and core allocation are designed around your agreement and tracked.
Do you support Data Guard?
Yes, with a physical standby at a second site and switchover rehearsed on an agreed cadence.
How are backups run?
RMAN to object storage with tested restores and retention mapped to your compliance calendar.
Can you take on an existing estate?
Yes. Assessment, licence mapping, landing zone and cutover are run by our team with your DBAs.
Will resizing affect our licence position?
Not without your approval — core allocation is pinned, and any change that touches licensing is raised with you before it is made.
How is a fixed monthly quote different from hyperscaler pricing?
Everything in the plan you choose is included, so the figure you agree is the figure you pay — rather than a bill that moves with every metered component.
Can capacity grow with us?
Yes. Add vCPU, memory, storage or nodes in place; the estate is re-baselined at thresholds you approve.
Can security services be added to the same bill?
Yes — virtual firewalls, WAF and endpoint security can be added to the same XcellHost invoice.
Where is my data stored?
In XcellHost's Indian data centres, so your data stays in India — useful for DPDP Act, RBI and SEBI obligations.
