Overview — What is email-signature management?

A great email signature is a small thing on every email that quietly does a lot — it makes your team look professional, keeps your branding consistent, and turns dead space at the bottom of an email into a marketing channel. But managing signatures by hand across a whole company is a nightmare: everyone formats them differently, mobile drops them, and campaigns never get updated.

XcellHost Email Signature, powered by Crossware, fixes that. Design signatures centrally, roll them out to every employee automatically, and keep them perfect on desktop, web and mobile — with marketing banners, campaign scheduling, and department-level control. XcellHost designs your templates, sets it up and manages it for you, with a GST invoice and 24×7 support.
