Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on validation, installation, pricing and renewals.

What does a Class 1 S/MIME certificate validate?+

It confirms that you control the email address. It does not verify your name or organisation — for that, choose DigiCert S/MIME (OV).

Which email clients support it?+

Outlook on Windows and Mac, Apple Mail on Mac, iPhone and iPad, Thunderbird, and Gmail with Google Workspace S/MIME enabled. Some mobile apps need extra setup, which we help with.

Can recipients read signed emails without a certificate?+

Yes. Anyone can read a signed email. To send you an encrypted email, the recipient needs your public certificate, which they receive when you send them a signed message.

Can I use it for document signing?+

No. Class 1 covers email signing, encryption and client authentication. For signing Office documents, choose DigiCert S/MIME (OV) or a document-signing certificate.

How long does issuance take?+

Usually minutes, once you click the verification link sent to the mailbox.

Can I use one certificate on several devices?+

Yes. We export it securely so you can install it on your computer and phone.

Are prices inclusive of GST?+

No. Prices are in INR per mailbox and exclusive of 18% GST.
