What is AI Review MagicQR?

AI Review MagicQR is a review-request QR code and link: customers rate their visit, are invited to post a Google review in one tap, and can also send private feedback to the owner. XcellHost sets it up for your business — or white-label for consumer — as part of the XcellHost Growth Suite.
