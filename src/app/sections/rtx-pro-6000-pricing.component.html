What is the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell cloud GPU?

The NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell is a 96 GB GDDR7 professional GPU that handles both AI and graphics. On XcellHost it is rented by the hour or month from Indian data centres, starting at ₹1,15,000 a month (excl. GST), for LLM fine-tuning and inference, generative video, digital twins and heavy 3D rendering.
