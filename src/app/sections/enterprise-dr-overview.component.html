What is cloud disaster recovery?

Cloud disaster recovery keeps an up-to-date copy of your servers and data in a second, geographically separate cloud site, so you can bring your systems up there within minutes when the primary site is hit by an outage, ransomware or a disaster. XcellHost Disaster Recovery replicates continuously to a geo-separated Indian data centre, powered by OpenText™ Availability — a recovery point moments behind production, failover in seconds to minutes and a 24×7 DR desk.

The DR lifecycle, handled end to end

Protect

Agents or hypervisor-level replication copy every change to the DR site.

Detect

24×7 monitoring spots an outage, corruption or ransomware activity and raises the alarm.

Failover

Runbooks boot servers in the right order, re-map IPs and DNS, and bring apps online.

Operate

Your business runs from the DR site while we fix the primary — support stays on the line.

Failback

Changes sync back and we return to the primary in a planned window, with no data loss.
