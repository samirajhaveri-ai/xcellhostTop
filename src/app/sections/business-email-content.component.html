Overview — What is XcellBizMail?

XcellBizMail is business-class email and collaboration, hosted in India. Give every employee a professional mailbox at your own domain, with the calendars, contacts, chat, video calling, file sync and an online office suite they need to work together — all from one unified hub, on the web, desktop and mobile.

It’s the ultimate alternative to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace for teams that want a simpler, more affordable suite with data hosted locally. Email is protected with DKIM, SPF, DMARC, anti-spam, anti-phishing and anti-ransomware, and runs from a Tier IV data centre with a 99.99% uptime SLA and 99.95% email-delivery SLA. XcellHost sets it up, migrates your existing mail, and bills you locally with a GST invoice.
