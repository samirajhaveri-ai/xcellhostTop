Overview — What is SiteLock?

SiteLock is a cloud-based website security service that scans your site every day for malware, vulnerabilities and blacklisting, removes threats automatically, and blocks attacks with a web application firewall (WAF). Its built-in CDN also makes your site faster while it protects it—and the SiteLock Trust Seal shows visitors your site is verified safe.

XcellHost is an authorised SiteLock partner in India. We provision, configure and monitor SiteLock for you—tuning the firewall, handling malware clean-up and keeping the seal live—with a GST invoice on every order and 24×7 support from our Mumbai SOC.
