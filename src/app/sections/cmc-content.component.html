Overview — What is a CMC and BIMI?

BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification) is the email standard that lets your brand logo appear next to your messages in the inbox. A Common Mark Certificate (CMC) from DigiCert is the way to qualify that logo without a registered trademark—using a mark you have had in prior use, typically for 12 months or more, or a government-registered mark—so supporting mailbox providers can display it.

A CMC is an accessible route to BIMI for brands that are not trademarked yet.
