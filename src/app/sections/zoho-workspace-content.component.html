Overview — What is Zoho Workspace?

Zoho Workspace gives your team professional email on @yourcompany.com, without advertising, tracking or scanning of email content. It brings business email and collaboration together in one platform.

XcellHost licenses, deploys, migrates and supports Zoho in INR with a GST-compliant invoice. Domain verification, MX records, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are handled for you.
