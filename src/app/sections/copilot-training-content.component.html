Copilot in the tools you use every day

Training covers common prompt flows across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook and Copilot Chat — including the difference between work- and web-grounded data.

Word

Draft, rewrite and summarise documents — from a blank page to a polished draft in seconds.

Excel

Analyse data, spot trends, build formulas and generate charts with a plain-English prompt.

PowerPoint

Turn a document or a prompt into a designed deck — and restyle or condense it instantly.

Teams

Recap meetings, capture action items and catch up on chats you missed — automatically.

Outlook

Summarise long threads, draft replies in your tone and triage your inbox faster.

Copilot Chat

Ask across your work data or the web, and use agents for complex, multi-step tasks safely.
