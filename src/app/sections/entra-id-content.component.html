Overview — What is Microsoft Entra ID?

Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure Active Directory) is Microsoft’s cloud identity and access management service. It gives every employee, partner and customer a single, secure identity they use to sign in to your apps — Microsoft 365, thousands of SaaS tools and your own applications — with single sign-on, so there’s one login instead of dozens of passwords.

On top of sign-in, Entra ID adds the security controls modern organisations need: multi-factor authentication, conditional access based on user, device, location and risk, self-service password reset, and identity protection that spots and blocks suspicious sign-ins.
