What are the NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada Generation specifications?

  1. 1

    Display outputs

    Four DisplayPort outputs; in the cloud these drive your remote virtual workstation via NICE DCV, Parsec or HP Anyware.

  2. 2

    48 GB ECC memory

    48 GB GDDR6 with ECC keeps city-scale BIM models, 8K timelines and diffusion models on the GPU.

  3. 3

    18,176 CUDA · 142 RT cores

    Third-generation RT cores and DLSS 3 make ray-traced viewports interactive.

  4. 4

    Blower cooling

    Exhausts heat out of the chassis, so cards pack densely in 2- and 4-GPU servers without thermal throttling.

  5. 5

    PCIe Gen 4 x16

    Full host bandwidth for streaming large textures and datasets to the GPU.
