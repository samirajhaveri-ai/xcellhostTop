Overview — What is DMARC?

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance) is the standard that stops criminals from sending email that pretends to be from your domain. It builds on SPF and DKIM to tell receiving mail servers what to do with messages that fail authentication — and sends you reports on exactly who is using your domain to send mail.

Without DMARC, anyone can spoof your domain to run phishing and fraud in your name — and your legitimate email is more likely to land in spam. XcellHost Enterprise DMARC, powered by XcellDMARC, gets you safely to an enforcement policy (p=reject), adds SPF, DKIM and BIMI, and gives you clear dashboards and reporting — so your domain is protected and your email actually gets delivered. We set it up and manage it for you, with a GST invoice.
