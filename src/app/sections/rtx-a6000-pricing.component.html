What is the RTX A6000 cloud GPU and who should use it?

The NVIDIA RTX A6000 is a 48 GB Ampere professional GPU for 3D rendering, CAD, simulation and mid-size AI. It supports NVLink, so two cards share 96 GB. XcellHost rents it from Indian data centres from ₹48,900 a month (excl. GST) — a balanced choice between the budget RTX 8000 and the faster RTX 6000 Ada.

Key takeaways
  • 48 GB ECC memory, 96 GB with NVLink
  • 2.4× the FP32 throughput of Quadro RTX 6000
  • Supports BF16/TF32 for modern deep-learning frameworks
  • Mature driver support for CAD and DCC software
