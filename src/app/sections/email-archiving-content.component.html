Quick answer · Updated September 2026

What is email archiving and why do you need it?

Email archiving captures every sent and received email — with attachments and metadata — in tamper-proof, searchable storage for a defined retention period. Unlike backup, which restores data after loss, an archive proves what was communicated: it keeps messages even when users delete them, prevents alteration, and supports eDiscovery and legal hold. This helps businesses meet retention rules, answer audits and legal requests, and investigate incidents without relying on users’ mailboxes.
