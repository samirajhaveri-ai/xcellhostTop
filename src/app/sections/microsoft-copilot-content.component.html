Overview — What is Copilot for Microsoft 365?

Copilot for Microsoft 365 is an AI assistant built right into the apps your team already uses every day — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams — plus a secure Copilot Chat for work. Ask it in plain language and it drafts documents, analyses spreadsheets, builds slide decks, summarises long email threads and recaps meetings you missed, in seconds. Because it works on your own documents, emails and chats — grounded in Microsoft Graph and protected by enterprise-grade security — the answers are relevant to your business, and your data stays yours. XcellHost handles licensing, readiness and rollout, and helps your team actually adopt it — with a GST invoice and local support.
