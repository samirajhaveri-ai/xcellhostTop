Overview — What is XcellHost Remote Access?

XcellHost Remote Access, powered by TSplus, lets you publish Windows applications and full desktops to users on any device through a simple browser or lightweight client.

Users sign in to a branded portal and launch the exact apps they need, running securely on your server while only the screen is streamed. XcellHost installs, secures and manages it with load balancing, 2FA, GST billing and 24×7 support.
