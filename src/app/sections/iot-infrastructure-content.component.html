How it works

How data flows, device to cloud

The same pipeline runs everywhere — connect, process at the edge, analyse, then sync — so latency stays low and bandwidth stays cheap.

01

Connect

Devices connect over MQTT, CoAP, LoRaWAN and more through the multi-protocol edge gateway, each with a verified X.509 identity.

02

Process at the edge

The edge node filters, aggregates and enriches telemetry locally — cutting bandwidth up to 80% and delivering sub-70ms responses.

03

Analyse & act

Kafka + Flink stream analytics drive real-time dashboards, alerts and automated actions on live device data.

04

Sync & manage

Data syncs to the Mumbai cloud for long-term analytics; OTA updates and device management flow back down securely.

Industry use cases

From factory floors to farm fields.

XcellHost IoT powers diverse industries — each with a real challenge, the infrastructure that solved it, and the outcome delivered.

Smart Manufacturing

Challenge

Factory-floor PLCs, CNC machines and robotic arms produced siloed telemetry with no way to predict failures before costly downtime.

Infrastructure

Multi-protocol edge gateways ingest Modbus/OPC-UA data, streamed into XcellHost’s real-time analytics pipeline (Kafka + Flink).

Outcome

Predictive-maintenance alerts fire before failures occur, cutting unplanned downtime significantly.

Agriculture & Rural IoT

Challenge

Soil sensors, weather stations and irrigation controllers spread across hundreds of acres needed reliable connectivity with no cellular coverage.

Infrastructure

Private LoRaWAN network deployed on XcellHost edge infrastructure, covering a multi-kilometre radius per gateway.

Outcome

Hundreds of acres brought online on a single deployment, enabling automated irrigation scheduling.

Smart Cities

Challenge

Street lighting, waste management, water quality and traffic sensors ran on disconnected legacy systems with no unified visibility for city operators.

Infrastructure

Unified multi-protocol gateway (MQTT/CoAP) feeding a single secure dashboard on XcellHost’s sovereign infrastructure.

Outcome

City-wide sensor fleet consolidated onto one platform, cutting operational reporting time.

Healthcare & Wearables

Challenge

Patient monitors, glucose meters and ECG patches generated sensitive health data requiring HIPAA-ready ingestion with zero tolerance for delay.

Infrastructure

End-to-end encrypted ingestion pipeline (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) with per-device X.509 identity.

Outcome

Real-time alerting on patient vitals with full compliance and a complete audit trail.
