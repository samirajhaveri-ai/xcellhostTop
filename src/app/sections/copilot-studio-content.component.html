Overview — What is Copilot Studio?

Microsoft Copilot Studio is a low-code platform for building your own AI agents and chatbots that answer questions, take actions and automate work. Describe what you want in plain language, add knowledge sources and build without a data science team.

Ground agents in websites, documents and SharePoint, connect them to 1,000+ systems, give them actions that trigger real work, and publish to Teams, your website, apps and other channels. XcellHost designs, builds, governs and manages your agents with local support and GST billing.
