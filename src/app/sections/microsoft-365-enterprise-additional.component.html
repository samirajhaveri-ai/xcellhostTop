Bundling

Smart add-on bundling strategies

Some add-ons compound in value when bought together.

Copilot + Teams Premium

Intelligent recap and action items get richer with Copilot for follow-up content. ₹3,210/user/mo — the natural pair for client-facing teams.

Defender for Endpoint P2 + Sentinel

Sentinel ingests Defender XDR at zero cost and correlates it with Entra, AD, firewall and IdP logs. This IS the modern SOC stack.

Entra ID Governance + Purview Premium

JML automation and access reviews plus PAM and 1-year tamper-evident audit — the audit-ready posture for BFSI, healthcare, listed companies. ₹1,920 on top of E3.

Power BI Premium P1 + Fabric F4

Production dashboards to unlimited consumers plus a small Fabric capacity for lakehouse and pipeline experimentation.

Teams Phone + Teams Premium

PSTN calling, IVR and queues plus intelligent recap of every customer call and branded webinars — ₹1,500/user/mo for 5–15% of headcount.

Viva Suite + Entra ID Governance

Intranet, learning, OKRs and engagement on the people side; JML automation and access reviews on identity — the modern workplace + modern identity foundation.
