Key features

Everything the AnyData Engine gives
you

AnyData Engine

Patented disk-imaging captures a single-pass, image-based backup in a universal format — recover files, apps or whole systems from one backup.

Universal Restore

Restore Windows or Linux systems to dissimilar hardware or bare metal in minutes — and migrate any-to-any: P2V, V2V, V2P or cloud.

Instant restore

Boot a machine and start applications long before the restore completes — blocks are restored as the OS needs them.

Dedup & compression

Built-in block-level deduplication and compression cut backup volumes by up to 90%, speeding backups and easing storage and network load.

Incremental & differential

Back up only what changed — saving time, storage and bandwidth, with GFS and event-triggered scheduling.

Replication & staging

Automatically replicate backups between locations, including Acronis Cloud and XcellHost Cloud; stage older backups as retention expires.

AES-256 encryption

Military-grade encryption in transit and at rest, plus a policy engine and role-based access for secure, compliant operations.

Centralised management

Assign plans, review status, generate reports and receive alerts for every physical, virtual and application backup from one console.

Throttling & scheduling

Limit backup impact during production hours and run backups on schedules or events — without slowing the business.

Disk, tape & cloud

Store to your choice of disk, tape or cloud, with granular access to every version of files, applications and systems.

Granular recovery

Recover individual Exchange emails and mailboxes, SQL tables, SharePoint items or files — without restoring the whole system.

Modular architecture

Start with what you need today and add protection for new servers, VMs and workstations as you grow — no re-platforming.

How it works

Capture once. Recover anywhere.

Backup, optimisation, storage and recovery — managed by XcellHost.

01

Capture

A single-pass, image-based backup captures the whole system, application data and files at block level.

02

Optimise

Deduplication and compression shrink the backup by up to 90%; incrementals capture only changes.

03

Store & replicate

Keep copies on disk, tape and cloud — replicated to XcellHost Cloud in India with retention and staging.

04

Recover anywhere

Restore files, items, apps or whole systems — to any hardware, VM or cloud — in minutes with Universal Restore.
