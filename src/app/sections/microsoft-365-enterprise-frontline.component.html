Overview

What is Microsoft 365 Frontline?

Microsoft’s purpose-built licensing track for the deskless workforce — communication, basic productivity, shift management and security at a fraction of E-plan cost, in exchange for 2 GB Kiosk mailboxes, no desktop Office install rights and a mandatory E-plan anchor. A 4,000-operator plant on flat E3 costs ~₹14.4 cr/yr; the same on F1 + 200 F3 supervisors + 50 E3 managers lands near ₹1.4 cr — a 10× reduction without giving up Walkie-Talkie, Shifts, Tasks, Intune or threat protection.

Feature spotlight

The surfaces that turn a ₹230 seat into a transformational tool

Teams Walkie-Talkie

Push-to-talk over Wi-Fi or cellular — replaces two-way radio fleets across plants, stores, wards and hotels. Channel-based, no spectrum licence, Bluetooth PTT buttons supported.

Teams Shifts

Digital roster in the Teams app — managers schedule; workers view, swap, request time off, clock in/out with geofencing. Exports to Darwinbox, Keka, Zoho People, SAP SuccessFactors.

Teams Tasks

HQ-published required-task lists on every store/plant/ward tablet; location-specific lists; real-time completion dashboards; photos attach as compliance evidence.

Teams Approvals

Leave, expense, shift-swap and operational sign-offs inside Teams — Approve/Reject with a full audit trail, replacing email chains.

Teams Praise

Built-in recognition badges workers and managers post in chat — a powerful retention tool for hourly-wage workforces.

Viva Connections

A single corporate feed inside Teams — news, role-based dashboards and leadership messages for workers who never open intranet portals.

Power BI Frontline (F3)

Consumption-only dashboards for managers — store KPIs, plant OEE, ward census — paired with Power BI Pro on E5 for authors.

Lists & Forms

Daily quality checks, NPS at till, end-of-shift handover notes — offline on mobile, syncing on reconnect, driving Power Automate flows.

By industry

Frontline by industry — six reference designs

Manufacturing

Shop-floor operators on F1 with shared Android tablets per line; Walkie-Talkie replacing radios; Shifts with swap approvals; Tasks for quality checklists. Supervisors on F3, plant managers on E3.

Retail

Store associates and cashiers on F1 — Walkie-Talkie for stock checks, Shifts for swaps, HQ-published Tasks for planogram audits. Store managers on F3 with Power BI Frontline; HQ on E5.

Healthcare

Nursing aides, ward boys and lab technicians on F1 on shared workstations with FastSwitch; Walkie-Talkie replacing overhead paging. Nurses on F3; doctors and admins on E3/E5 (1:8).

Hospitality

Housekeeping, F&B and kitchen on F1 with shared iPads; Walkie-Talkie between rooms and kitchen; Tasks for HACCP logs. Supervisors on F3; GMs on E3/E5.

Field service

Utility, telco and delivery technicians on F3 — offline mobile Office for work orders and drawings, Power Apps forms with offline cache, Defender P1 on rugged mobiles, Shifts for on-call rota.

Public sector

Police, traffic and field inspectors on F1/F3 with rugged Intune-compliant devices; command-channel Walkie-Talkie, Tasks for duty briefings, Forms for incident capture. Senior officers on E3 with PIM.
