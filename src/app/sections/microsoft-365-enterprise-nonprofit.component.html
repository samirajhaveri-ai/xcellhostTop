Eligibility

Eligibility & Tech Soup India verification

Administered by Tech Soup India, Microsoft Philanthropies’ verification partner. XcellHost reviews 80G, 12A, FCRA and registration documents pre-submission and handles the whole process at no charge.

Legal nonprofit status

Trust, Society or Section 8 Company. Government bodies and academic institutions use separate programmes.

80G certificate (current)

Income Tax Act certificate for donor tax-deductibility — post-2021 certificates have explicit validity; must be current.

12A registration

Income-tax exemption registration — required alongside 80G for full recognition under Microsoft Philanthropies.

Registration document

Trust Deed, Society Registration Certificate or Certificate of Incorporation showing mission, governance and signatories.

Non-discriminatory mission

Programmes open to everyone regardless of religion, caste, gender or ethnicity.

FCRA (if applicable)

Required if you receive foreign donations — declare upfront with the certificate to avoid Tech Soup queries.

PAN, board resolution, audited financials

Organisation PAN, a resolution authorising the named signatory, and audited statements for the last two years.

NGO-domain email

A contact email on your own domain — Gmail/Yahoo are not accepted. We set up the domain first if needed.

FCRA compliance

FCRA compliance with Microsoft 365 E5 Nonprofit

Pragmatic pattern: E5 only for finance, FCRA officer, leadership and donor managers (5–15% of staff); E3 for programme managers; E1 free for general staff.

Purview DLP for foreign donor data

Auto-detects donor PII (foreign passports, US SSN/TIN, EU IBAN, UK NI) in email, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint and blocks accidental external sharing.

Sensitivity labels for FCRA-funded documents

Auto-classify foreign-funded programme documents as “FCRA-Funded — Internal” with encryption that follows the file.

Advanced eDiscovery for FC-4 returns

Assemble all foreign-contribution correspondence, financials and beneficiary records into one case for the MHA quarterly return.

Defender for Office 365 P2 vs spear-phishing

Catches impersonation of donor agencies, MHA officials and board members targeting finance teams — with AIR remediation.

Purview Audit (1-year retention)

Every admin and user action logged for a year, so the FCRA officer can show who accessed which donor record and when.

Entra ID P2 PIM

Just-in-time admin activation with approval and time limits for finance head, CIO and FCRA officer — satisfying foreign-donor security clauses.

Communication Compliance

Policy-based scanning for safeguarding and regulatory infractions — increasingly required by EU donors.

Insider Risk Management

ML detection of donor-list exfiltration to personal OneDrive or unauthorised partner sharing — before the data is gone.
