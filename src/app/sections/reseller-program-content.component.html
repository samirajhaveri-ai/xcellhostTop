Overview — What is the XcellHost Reseller Program?

The XcellHost Reseller Program lets agencies, MSPs, IT consultants and entrepreneurs sell a full catalogue of cloud, security and AI services — without building any of it. You get wholesale pricing, optional white-label branding, and a single partner portal to provision services, manage clients and track your earnings.

Add new revenue streams overnight: resell what your customers already need, set your own margins, and earn recurring commissions on every renewal. XcellHost handles infrastructure, uptime and 24×7 support behind the scenes — you own the customer relationship, with GST-compliant billing throughout.
