Case studies — what teams achieve

LF

Moving a document-AI model from US GPUs to India cut latency and settled data-residency questions

Lending fintech, 120 staff · Fintech · BengaluruIllustrative case study

Challenge

The team served a 34B-parameter document-understanding model on US-hosted GPUs. Round-trip latency from India was high, egress bills were unpredictable, and the compliance team wanted borrower data kept in India under the DPDP Act.

What we set up

Two XG.PRO6K.1-32 instances in our Mumbai zone running vLLM with FP8 weights, a private network to their app servers, and MIG slices for staging. We migrated weights and set up Grafana GPU dashboards.

Configuration: 2 × XG.PRO6K.1-32 on a 12-month term

62%lower p95 inference latency
38%lower monthly GPU bill in INR
100%borrower data kept in India

Generative video previz for ad agencies without building a render farm

Ad-film post studio · Media · MumbaiIllustrative case study

Challenge

Agencies wanted AI-generated animatics within a day. The studio's 24 GB workstations ran out of memory on text-to-video models, so jobs failed or had to be cut into short clips.

A plant digital twin that engineers and the AI team share on one GPU pool

Auto-components maker · Manufacturing · PuneIllustrative case study

Challenge

Engineering needed a real-time Omniverse twin of a new line, while the data team trained vision models for defect detection. Buying separate workstations and a training server would take a full quarter's budget.
