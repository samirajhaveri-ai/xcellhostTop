Security and operations

How does Cloud Drive protect business files?

AES-256 encryption protects stored data while TLS 1.2/1.3 protects transfers. Administrators can apply role-based access, multi-factor authentication, audit logging, file versioning and password- or expiry-protected sharing. The published SLA commits to 99.9% platform uptime and 24×7 emergency support.
