Everything a great receptionist does

Answers every call

Picks up instantly, day or night, weekends and holidays — no voicemail, no missed leads.

Books appointments

Checks your calendar and books, moves or confirms appointments during the call.

Takes detailed messages

Captures name, number and reason for calling, and flags anything urgent.

Routes by department

Sends sales, support or accounts calls to the right person or number.

Learns your website

Reads your website to answer questions about services, prices, hours and location.

Summary after every call

An email with the caller's details, what they asked and what was agreed.

Choice of voices

Pick a voice and tone that suits your brand — friendly, formal or somewhere between.

API & CRM integration
