What are the NVIDIA RTX A6000 specifications?

  1. 1

    Display outputs

    Four DisplayPort outputs; in the cloud these drive your remote virtual workstation via NICE DCV, Parsec or HP Anyware.

  2. 2

    48 GB ECC memory

    48 GB GDDR6 with ECC on a 384-bit bus for big scenes, datasets and batch sizes.

  3. 3

    NVLink bridge

    Joins two cards at high bandwidth so renderers and training jobs see one large 96 GB memory pool.

  4. 4

    Blower cooling

    Exhausts heat out of the chassis, so cards pack densely in 2- and 4-GPU servers without thermal throttling.

  5. 5

    PCIe Gen 4 x16

    Twice the host bandwidth of Gen 3 for data-heavy training pipelines.
