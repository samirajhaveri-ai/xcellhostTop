What is a GPU cluster, and how is it different from renting a single GPU?

A single rental is one card on one host. A cluster is many servers — each with 4 or 8 GPUs — linked by NVLink inside the node and InfiniBand or RoCE between nodes. Single GPUs suit inference and small fine-tunes; clusters are for distributed training, 70B+ pre-training, RLHF and HPC.

EVERY CLUSTER INCLUDES
  • NVLink inside nodes, InfiniBand between them
  • CUDA, PyTorch, vLLM and NCCL pre-installed
  • Slurm, Ray, SkyPilot or managed Kubernetes
  • Parallel storage for datasets and checkpoints
  • DCGM, Prometheus and Grafana monitoring
