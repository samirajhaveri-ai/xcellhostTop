What is a QR digital menu?

A QR digital menu lets guests scan a code to view categories, items, prices and photos on their phone, while owners update prices, specials and sold-out items instantly. XcellHost sets it up for your business — or white-label for agencies — as part of the XcellHost Growth Suite.
