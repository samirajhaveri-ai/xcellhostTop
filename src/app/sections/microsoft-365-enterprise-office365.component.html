Overview

What is Office 365 Enterprise?

The productivity-only variant of Microsoft’s Enterprise family — cloud email, real-time collaboration, document storage and meetings — explicitly excluding the Windows 11 Enterprise upgrade, Intune endpoint management and Entra ID P1/P2. Deliberate: if you already license Windows via OEM/MPSA/EA, run Jamf, Workspace ONE or Ivanti, or federate identity to Okta or Ping, paying the Microsoft 365 premium is unnecessary spend.

Capabilities

Office 365 Enterprise at a glance

Exchange Online (Plan 1 / 2)

50 GB in E1; 100 GB in E3/E5 with auto-expanding archive to 1.5 TB. Litigation hold, in-place eDiscovery, mail-flow rules, hybrid coexistence.

Microsoft Teams

Chat, channels, meetings, webinars (1,000) and live events (10,000). Teams Phone Standard cloud PBX included in E5.

Office desktop apps (E3, E5)

Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Access, OneNote, Publisher — 5 PCs/Macs + 5 tablets + 5 phones per user; macros, VBA, Power Query.

SharePoint & OneDrive

Team and communication sites, versioning and co-authoring. OneDrive 1 TB (E1) or 5 TB+ pooled (E3/E5).

Defender for Office 365 P2 (E5)

Safe Attachments sandboxing, Safe Links, anti-phishing with impersonation detection, AIR, Attack Simulation Training.

Purview Information Protection

Sensitivity labels and basic DLP for PAN/Aadhaar (P1 in E3); Advanced eDiscovery, Communication Compliance, Insider Risk (P2 in E5).

Power BI Pro (E5)

A per-user Power BI Pro licence for every E5 user — the foundation for self-service business intelligence.

Entra ID Free → your IdP

Basic directory, Security Defaults, MFA. Federate to Okta, Ping, Workspace or on-prem AD via SAML/OIDC.

When it’s right

When Office 365 Enterprise is the right choice

Mac-heavy organisation on Jamf

60–100% Apple fleet managed by Jamf Pro — Intune in Microsoft 365 E3 gives you nothing. Office 365 E3 + Jamf is the right stack.

Existing Volume Licensing for Windows 11

A 3- or 5-year EA/MPSA with per-device Windows 11 Enterprise SA — Microsoft 365 E3 double-pays. Saves ₹500–800/user/mo.

Non-Microsoft identity (Okta, Ping, Workspace)

Conditional Access, MFA and SSPR come from your IdP; Entra ID P1 is redundant. Office 365 E3 with Entra ID Free federated to your IdP.

Regulated industry with existing EDR

CrowdStrike, SentinelOne or Sophos already on endpoints — Office 365 E5 gives Defender for Office 365 P2 + Purview P2 + Teams Phone + Power BI for ₹1,770/user/mo less than M365 E5.
