Overview — What is a dedicated server?

A dedicated server is an entire physical machine reserved exclusively for you—no shared CPU, memory or disk. You get the full power of enterprise hardware, complete root or administrator access, and the freedom to run the operating system, control panel and applications your workload needs.

XcellHost Dedicated Servers combine Intel Xeon, AMD Ryzen or EPYC processors, ECC memory and fast NVMe storage with Indian Tier IV data centres, free DDoS protection, local INR billing, and your choice of managed or unmanaged operation.
