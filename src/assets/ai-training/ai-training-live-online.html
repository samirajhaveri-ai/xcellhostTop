    Responsible AI & India compliance

    Safe habits in the tools, and in the virtual classroom too.

    Session 10 covers the rules in depth, and every lab uses sample data. The course explains the rules; it is not legal advice, and your organisation's policy comes first.

    In AI tools, always
    • Use your organisation's approved tools and work account
    • Check facts, figures and citations before you share output
    • Keep a person in charge of decisions about people
    • Say when AI helped, if your policy asks
    In AI tools, never
    • Paste customer or employee personal data into public chatbots
    • Share passwords, API keys or client documents under NDA
    • Act on an urgent "CEO" voice note or email without checking
    • Let an agent send or pay without an approval step
    In our live classes
    • Sessions are recorded for the cohort; we tell you before recording starts
    • Camera is optional; breakout rooms are not recorded*
    • Recordings stay in the course space, access ends after 90 days*
    • Ask us to remove your contributions at any time
    DPDP Act 2023 · Rules 2025

    Rules notified November 2025, phased; most obligations from May 2027. Breach intimation to the Board without delay, detailed report within 72 hours.

    CERT-In · 28 Apr 2022

    Specified cyber incidents reported within 6 hours; ICT logs kept for 180 days. Learners practise escalating AI-enabled phishing fast.

    MeitY · 5 Nov 2025

    India AI Governance Guidelines: principles-based, relying on existing laws; accountability and human oversight run through the course.

    EU AI Act · Art. 4

    AI-literacy measures for staff of providers and deployers apply from 2 February 2025; completion records can support that evidence.
