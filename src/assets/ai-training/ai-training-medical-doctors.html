    Why this course

    Doctors are already using AI. Few were ever taught to use it safely.

    Chatbots are open on clinic computers and phones. This course replaces guesswork with a method: which tasks AI helps with, how to check its output, and where it must not be used at all.

    Copy-paste risk

    Patient details typed into a free chatbot may be stored or used by the provider. We teach de-identification and which tools your hospital has approved.

    Confident, wrong answers

    AI can invent references and doses. You learn verification habits that take seconds, not hours.

    Paperwork that grows every year

    Discharge summaries, referral letters and insurance forms. AI can draft them; you stay the author.

    Rules are catching up

    NMC, ICMR, CDSCO and the DPDP Act all now touch AI use. You leave knowing what applies to your practice.

    Responsible AI & India compliance

    Clinical AI in India sits under several rulebooks. We teach what each one asks of you.

    A neutral summary to help you ask the right questions. It is not legal advice; your hospital's policies and counsel take precedence. Checked October 2026.

    Four rules we never bend
    1. AI assists. It never replaces clinical judgement.The treating doctor makes and owns every clinical decision.
    2. No identifiable patient data in public AI tools.Use de-identified text, or tools your hospital has approved under contract.
    3. Every output is reviewed before use.Notes, letters, leaflets and summaries are checked and signed by a clinician.
    4. We make no diagnostic claims.This course teaches safe use of general tools. It does not certify any tool for diagnosis.
    NMC · 6 Oct 2026Ethical advertising guidelines

    NMC's Ethics and Medical Registration Board issued guidelines on advertising and public communication. They bar AI-made patient testimonials and misleading AI content, and say patient data used in AI must follow privacy and confidentiality rules.

    ICMR · March 2023Ethical guidelines for AI in biomedical research & healthcare

    Ten ethical principles, including autonomy, safety, data privacy, accountability, non-discrimination and validation before use. ICMR calls it a living document.

    DPDP Act 2023 · Rules 2025Digital Personal Data Protection

    Health data is personal data. The Rules were notified in November 2025, with most obligations applying from May 2027. Consent, purpose limits, security and breach reporting all apply.

    ABDMConsent-based health records

    Under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, records linked to a patient's ABHA are shared only with the patient's consent through a consent manager. We cover what that means for AI tools.

    CDSCO · 2026Medical device software guidance

    CDSCO published guidance in July 2026 on software under the Medical Device Rules 2017. Software with a medical purpose, including AI, can be a regulated device in risk classes A to D. Ask vendors for its status.
