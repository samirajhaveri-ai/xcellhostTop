    What it means in your classroom

    Classroom red lines we teach
    • No student names or personal data in public AI tools. Anonymise work, or use tools your institution has approved.
    • The teacher is the assessor. AI drafts feedback and marks; you decide.
    • Detectors are never sole proof. AI-writing detectors are unreliable; talk with the student and look at the process.
    • Children's data needs care. Under the DPDP Act, learners under 18 are children: verifiable parental consent, and no tracking or targeted ads.
    • Follow tool age limits. Check each provider's terms and your institution's policy before students use a tool.
