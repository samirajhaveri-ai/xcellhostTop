What is XcellHost Performance Cloud?

XcellHost Performance Cloud is a range of NVMe-backed cloud servers hosted in India, offered in three instance families — General Purpose (1 vCPU : 4 GB), CPU-Optimised (1 : 2) and Memory-Optimised (1 : 8), from 1 to 64 vCPU — so each workload gets the right balance of cores, memory and disk. Servers launch in minutes, run Windows or Linux, bill hourly or monthly in INR with a GST invoice, and are supported 24×7 by XcellHost engineers. Linux plans start from ₹930 per month and Windows from ₹1,330, with 12% off on an annual term.

Performance Cloud FAQs

What is the difference between General Purpose, CPU-Optimised and Memory-Optimised?
They differ in memory per vCPU. General Purpose gives 4 GB per vCPU for balanced workloads; CPU-Optimised gives 2 GB per vCPU for compute-heavy work at the lowest price per core; Memory-Optimised gives 8 GB per vCPU for databases, caches and analytics. Every vCPU is dedicated to you.
Which instance family should I choose?
Start from the bottleneck. General production app: General Purpose. CPU pinned at 100% for long periods: CPU-Optimised. Database or cache that wants more RAM: Memory-Optimised. If you're unsure, tell us what you run and we'll size it free.
Which operating systems are supported?
Windows Server 2019, 2022 and 2025, plus Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux, CentOS Stream and FreeBSD. Red Hat Enterprise Linux is available on a bring-your-own-licence basis, and you can upload a custom ISO.
Is Windows licensing included?
Yes — Windows plans include the Windows Server licence in the monthly price (about ₹400 more per vCPU than Linux), so you never buy a perpetual licence up front. Linux distributions carry no licence charge.
What storage do the servers use?
Every plan includes a 30 GB enterprise NVMe boot disk, and you can attach block storage volumes of any size at ₹6.20 per GB per month (up to 25,000 IOPS). Snapshots are ₹6.20/GB and backups ₹2/GB per month.
Can I resize a server later?
Yes. You can add vCPU, memory or storage from the console without rebuilding the server; a CPU or RAM change needs a brief reboot. You can also move between instance families as the workload changes.
How does billing work — hourly or monthly?
Both. Pay hourly to test or for short-lived environments, or take a monthly plan. Committing to 3, 6 or 12 months saves 5%, 7.5% or 12%. Every invoice is in INR with GST, so you can claim input tax credit.
Are there charges for bandwidth or data transfer?
Each plan includes a generous monthly bandwidth allowance, and we don't bill per GB of outbound traffic within it. If you consistently exceed it, we'll talk to you about a larger allocation before any overage appears.
Where are the servers hosted, and does my data stay in India?
Servers run in Indian data centres and your data stays in India unless you choose otherwise — useful for DPDP Act obligations and for low latency to Indian users.
How secure is XcellHost Performance Cloud?
Tenants are isolated at the hypervisor, every server gets a stateful virtual firewall and upstream anti-DDoS, and our operations are ISO 27001 certified with 24×7 NOC and SOC oversight. Acronis Cyber Protect adds backup and anti-malware if you want it.
What uptime do you guarantee?
A 99.95% uptime SLA on the platform, backed by redundant power, cooling, network and host hardware, with service credits if we miss it.
Do you help with migration?
Yes, and it's free when you move to XcellHost. We assess the current setup, write a cut-over plan, rehearse it on a copy, and switch over out of hours so users barely notice.
Can I run Docker and Kubernetes?
Yes. Deploy Docker hosts or a K3s cluster from the one-click catalogue, connect nodes over private networking, and put a load balancer in front.
Can I try it before committing?
Yes — start on hourly billing with no lock-in, prove the workload, and move to a monthly or term plan once you're happy. Ask us about a guided proof-of-concept for larger migrations.
What support do I get?
24×7 support by phone, WhatsApp and ticket from XcellHost engineers in Mumbai, in English and Hindi. It's included with every plan — there's no paid tier to unlock a human.
