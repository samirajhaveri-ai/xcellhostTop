What is managed MariaDB?

Managed MariaDB is a hosted MariaDB database where XcellHost handles provisioning, backups, monitoring, updates and failover, so your team focuses on the application. Choose a standalone instance or a Galera Cluster with synchronous multi-master replication, with hourly backups, point-in-time recovery and one-click restore in Indian data centres.
