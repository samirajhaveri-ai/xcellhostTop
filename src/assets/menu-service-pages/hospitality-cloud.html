Overview

What is XcellHost Hospitality Cloud?

It is managed IT built around how a hotel runs: a front desk that checks guests in at all hours, reservations arriving from many channels, outlets billing to the room, guests expecting fast Wi-Fi, and owners who want occupancy and revenue across properties in one view. We host and operate the systems behind each of these.

PMS without the server room

Your property management system runs in Indian data centres with backup and recovery, not on a single machine on site.

Group-wide visibility

Central reservations and reporting give head office one view of occupancy, rates and revenue across properties.

Guest records protected

ID scans, Form C data and guest profiles are stored with access controls and handled in line with the DPDP Act.

What runs on it

The systems behind every stay
