Overview

What is Insurance Cloud?

Insurance Cloud is a managed private cloud for the systems an insurance business depends on — policy administration, claims, distribution portals and document stores. It runs in Indian Tier IV data centres with a DR site and SOC monitoring, operated by an ISO 27001 and ISO 20000-1 certified team with documented controls for audits.

Built for insurance workloads

Policy, claims, portal and document systems run on dedicated, segregated infrastructure sized for seasonal peaks.

Policyholder data protected

Encryption, role-based access and activity logs protect KYC, health and financial details of policyholders.

Ready for audits

Logs, change records, backup reports and DR drill results are kept ready for IRDAI-mandated audits.
