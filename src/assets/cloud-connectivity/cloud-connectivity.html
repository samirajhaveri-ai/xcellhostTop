Overview

Cloud is only as good as the network that reaches it.

Most slow "cloud" complaints are network complaints: a branch on one broadband line, guest Wi-Fi sharing the same network as payments, ERP traffic crossing the public internet, or two clouds that can't route to each other. Each problem has a different fix — and a different vendor, contract and support number if you solve them one at a time.

XcellHost Cloud Connectivity puts the whole path under one team: Wi-Fi and SD-WAN at the edge of your business, virtual security appliances, internet transit and private lines into the clouds, and a cloud router that ties them together in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre. Each service stands on its own; together they share one design, one NOC and one INR invoice.

    India compliance

    The rules that touch your network, in one place.

    Services help you meet these obligations; compliance also depends on your own policies and systems.

    0days

    CERT-In log retention

    CERT-In's April 2022 directions ask organisations to keep ICT logs for 180 days within India and report specified incidents within 6 hours. Network, Wi-Fi and routing logs are kept in India for that period.

    DoT · public Wi-Fi

    Public and guest Wi-Fi users must be authenticated (OTP) with records kept — see Managed Wi-Fi for how we handle it.

    DoT / TRAI licensing

    Internet and carrier circuits come from licensed providers through partner exchanges*; XcellHost designs and manages them.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Segmented networks, private cloud paths and encrypted links support reasonable security safeguards for personal data.

    RBI & SEBI

    Redundant links, network segmentation and change records support IT, outsourcing and cyber-resilience reviews in BFSI.
