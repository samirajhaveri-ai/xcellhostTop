    Overview · what is IP transit?

    Your network, connected to the whole internet over a port you control.

    IP transit is a dedicated connection that carries your traffic to and from every network on the internet. Unlike office broadband, the capacity is yours alone (1:1, not shared), it is delivered on an Ethernet port in a data centre, and routing is under your control. If you have your own Autonomous System Number (ASN) and IP space, you run a BGP session with us, announce your prefixes and receive routes back. If you don't, we route a static block of XcellHost addresses to you.

    XcellHost hands off transit inside its Tier IV data centre in Mumbai — straight into your co-location rack by cross-connect, or into your XcellHost cloud network. Traffic leaves over blended upstreams*, IPv4 and IPv6 run side by side, and a DDoS protection add-on sits on the same circuit. Pay a flat commit, or commit lower and pay bursts at the 95th percentile. The same 24×7 NOC that runs our cloud watches your sessions, and billing is in INR with GST.

    India compliance

    Internet resources, logs and incident clocks, handled the Indian way.

    CERT-In directions (2022)

    Report specified cyber incidents within 6 hours, keep ICT logs for 180 days in India and sync clocks to NIC or NPL time servers. Our session and DDoS logs, timestamps and fast alerts help you meet all three.

    IRINN & APNIC resources

    In India, ASNs and IP space come from IRINN (run by NIXI, India's National Internet Registry under APNIC) or from APNIC directly. We help prepare route objects, ROAs and the LOA you need.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Personal data your services carry stays in your systems; our access controls, logs and ISO 27001 practices help you show reasonable security safeguards and support breach intimation.

    RBI · SEBI for BFSI

    Regulated entities are expected to run resilient, monitored network links. Dual sessions, DDoS protection and NOC evidence help you meet RBI and SEBI IT and cyber-resilience expectations.

    DoT-authorised delivery*

    Internet services in India are authorised by the Department of Telecommunications. XcellHost delivers transit over DoT-authorised upstream networks*, details confirmed in your proposal.
