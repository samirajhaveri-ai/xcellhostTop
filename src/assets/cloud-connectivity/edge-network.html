    Overview · what is edge networking?

    Move the network closer to the work, keep the core in Mumbai.

    Edge networking shortens the path between where data is created — a store till, a factory camera, a sensor, a phone — and where it is processed. Instead of every packet travelling to a distant data centre, traffic is handed off at a nearby point of presence (PoP), processed on an edge-cloud node where speed matters, and only the useful part travels on to the core. Light in fibre covers roughly 200 km per millisecond, so distance and the number of hops set a floor on response time.

    XcellHost designs that path end to end. Sites and devices connect over encrypted backhaul (IPsec or SD-WAN on fibre, 4G or 5G) to edge PoPs at partner data centres and exchanges*, where edge-cloud nodes and CDN caches sit. The core — cloud, IoT platform and origin — runs in our Tier IV data centre in Mumbai. For campuses we integrate 5G and private 5G. The NOC watches every link 24×7 and billing is in INR with GST.

    India compliance

    Spectrum, logs and personal data — kept on the right side of the rules.

    Private 5G · DoT CNPN framework

    Under DoT's captive non-public network (CNPN) guidelines of June 2022, an enterprise can get a private 4G/5G network from a licensed telecom operator (through network slicing or an isolated on-site network using the operator's spectrum), lease spectrum from an operator, or apply for spectrum directly from DoT. Direct assignment has eligibility conditions, including a minimum net worth of ₹100 crore, and depends on DoT's process; TRAI recommended a separate CNPN-provider authorisation in February 2025. A CNPN cannot interconnect with public networks. XcellHost helps you assess the options and integrates the network with your edge and cloud; spectrum and licences stay with licensed operators or DoT.

    CERT-In directions (2022)

    Incident reporting within 6 hours, ICT logs kept for 180 days in India and clocks synced to NIC or NPL time servers. Tunnel, gateway and node logs are retained to help you meet all three.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Processing at the edge lets you send only what's needed to the core — less personal data moving, which helps with data minimisation and reasonable security safeguards.

    RBI · SEBI for BFSI

    Encrypted branch links, monitoring and logs help regulated entities meet RBI and SEBI expectations for network security and resilience.

    M2M & IoT SIMs

    Cellular IoT SIMs are issued by licensed operators under DoT rules for M2M connections; we work with the operator you choose*.
