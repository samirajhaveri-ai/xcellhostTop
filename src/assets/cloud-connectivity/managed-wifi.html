    Overview

    Wi-Fi is now the network people actually use — so run it like one.

    Managed Wi-Fi means one team owns your wireless network end to end: where each access point goes and why, which channels and power they use, who may join which network, and what happens at 2 a.m. when one stops answering. Most Wi-Fi complaints — the dead corner, the conference room that drops calls, the guest network that slows billing — come from guesswork at install time and nobody watching afterwards.

    XcellHost starts with a survey and heatmap, designs for the devices you really have (including Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 on the 6 GHz band India de-licensed in January 2026), installs and configures it, then monitors it from the 24×7 NOC in Mumbai. Guest access uses mobile-number OTP with login records kept for a year, staff and IoT devices sit on separate segments, and you get one per-access-point bill in rupees with GST.

      India compliance

      Guest Wi-Fi in India comes with rules — we design them in from day one.

      XcellHost helps you meet these requirements; your legal and compliance team decides what applies to your setup.

      The numbers that matter
      6 hCERT-In incident reporting window
      180 daysCERT-In rolling ICT log retention
      1 yearWi-Fi login records (DoT / PM-WANI)
      500 MHzlower 6 GHz band, licence-free since 20 Jan 2026

      Sources: DoT Wi-Fi directions (23 Feb 2009), PM-WANI guidelines, CERT-In Directions (28 Apr 2022), DoT G.S.R. 47(E) (20 Jan 2026).

      DoT · public Wi-Fi authentication

      DoT's 2009 directions say hotspot users are authenticated centrally — for example a login sent by SMS to their mobile — and the mobile-to-login record is kept for one year. Leased-line customers who run their own Wi-Fi are expected to run central authentication or use their provider's.

      PM-WANI hotspots

      Under DoT's PM-WANI framework, users verify their mobile number by OTP and the aggregator stores user data for one year, with user data and usage logs kept in India. We can design for PM-WANI or for private guest Wi-Fi.

      CERT-In Directions, April 2022

      Report specified cyber incidents within 6 hours, keep ICT system logs for a rolling 180 days, and sync clocks to NIC or NPL time servers. Controller, AP and portal logs are kept and time-synced to support this.

      DPDP Act 2023

      A guest's mobile number is personal data. The portal shows a notice, takes consent for the stated purpose, and records are deleted when the retention period ends.

      Lower 6 GHz band rules

      DoT exempted 5925–6425 MHz from licensing for low-power indoor (up to 30 dBm EIRP) and very-low-power (up to 14 dBm) Wi-Fi. Use in cars, trains, boats, drones and most aircraft is excluded. We deploy only India-compliant 6 GHz APs.

      BFSI · RBI & SEBI

      Banks, NBFCs and regulated entities are expected to segment networks and control wireless access. Guest isolation, 802.1X for staff and logged changes give auditors clear evidence.
