    Overview

    Your links stay with your ISPs. The decisions move to software.

    SD-WAN puts a small edge device at each branch that bonds every link it has — MPLS, business broadband, 4G or 5G — into encrypted tunnels and decides, packet by packet, which link each application should use. It measures latency, jitter and loss on every path many times a second, so a call moves off a failing link before anyone hears it, and bulk traffic stops crowding out billing.

    XcellHost runs it as a service. We design the policies, ship pre-configured boxes that come up on their own, connect branches to your data centre — including XcellHost's Tier IV facility in Mumbai — and to cloud security, and the 24×7 NOC watches every link and chases the ISP when one degrades. Vendor-agnostic: we work with leading SD-WAN platforms* and bill per site in rupees with GST.

    Architecture

    Branches, hubs and cloud security — one overlay, one orchestrator.

    Branches build encrypted tunnels to a hub at your data centre or XcellHost Mumbai, and send internet traffic to an SSE point of presence. The orchestrator and the NOC sit above it all.

    Branch edge

    Physical box or virtual edge at each site with 2–4 links, LAN segments and local breakout rules.

    Hub

    Head-end at your DC or in XcellHost's Mumbai Tier IV facility, as an appliance or a virtual edge.

    Cloud security

    SSE / SASE point of presence for web, SaaS and remote-user access with one policy.

    Orchestrator & NOC

    Policies, firmware and SLA monitoring from one console, operated by the 24×7 NOC.

      CERT-In Directions, April 2022

      Report specified incidents within 6 hours, keep ICT logs for a rolling 180 days, and sync clocks to NIC or NPL. Edge, orchestrator and SSE logs are retained and time-synced to support this.

      DPDP Act 2023

      Personal data crossing the WAN — billing, patient, customer records — travels in encrypted tunnels, with access and change logs as evidence of reasonable security safeguards.

      RBI · banks & NBFCs

      RBI's IT governance and cyber-security directions expect segmented, monitored networks with tested failover. Branch segmentation, dual links and SLA reports support those reviews.

      SEBI CSCRF

      SEBI's 2024 Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework asks regulated entities for network security, logging and resilience. Central policy and log retention make evidence easy to produce.
