Overview

The highest validation, without the fixed bundle.

True BusinessID EV (FLEX) is GeoTrust's Extended Validation certificate with a flexible name list. Every entry is a fully qualified domain name — your main site, www, payment and login hosts, or a second brand — and you can start with one or two names instead of the usual three, then add more up to 250 whenever you need them.

EV is the most thorough check a public certificate authority runs: legal existence, physical address, operational history, a verification call and domain control. The result is your verified legal company name, jurisdiction and registration number inside the certificate, backed by a US$1.5M warranty. GeoTrust is a DigiCert brand; XcellHost prepares the EV paperwork from your MCA or GST records, installs on every server and handles the reissues.

Four checks, done once. Every name rides on them.

GeoTrust's EV authentication follows the industry's EV guidelines: an enrolment form, organisation authentication, a telephone verification and a control check for each domain. Most Indian companies clear it in 1–5 business days.

    EV in India

    Regulators expect encryption. Customers deserve to know who's behind it.

    No Indian rule names a certificate brand. But every major framework expects data in transit to be protected, and EV ties your registered company to every site on the list.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards against personal-data breaches. Encrypting every login, payment and form is the baseline.

    CERT-In directions

    Cyber incidents must be reported within 6 hours, and CERT-In advisories often cover phishing of Indian brands. A verified identity makes look-alike sites easier to call out.

    RBI & SEBI entities

    Banks, NBFCs, payment aggregators and brokers work under cyber-security frameworks that expect strong encryption and a certificate inventory. One EV FLEX keeps that inventory short.

    MeitY & government sites

    MeitY's guidelines for government websites (GIGW) expect HTTPS. EV lets citizens confirm the department or PSU behind a portal.
