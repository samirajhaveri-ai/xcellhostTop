Overview

A certificate authority only your organisation trusts — deployed fast, run by experts.

A private CA issues certificates for the things the public internet never sees: intranet portals, VPN and Wi-Fi logins, laptops, servers, containers and devices — with the names, key types, lifetimes and usages you choose. A managed private CA puts that root and issuing CA on audited, compliant PKI operated by the vendor. The root key sits in a hardware security module, revocation services stay online, and you never buy an HSM, rack a server or staff a key ceremony. It costs less than building the same thing yourself, and it is ready far sooner.

XcellHost is vendor-neutral across the main private CA platforms. We deliver hosted private CAs on DigiCert and Sectigo, and help you compare them with Keyfactor, Venafi, AppViewX, KeyTalk and ManageEngine before you commit — without weeks of sales calls. One Mumbai team handles demos, technical questions, the rollout and 24×7 support, with a GST invoice in rupees. The goal: internal certificates issued by policy in minutes, not by ticket, and no more self-signed warnings that staff learn to click past.

      Why it matters in India
      Indian rules expect strong encryption and controlled keys. A private CA proves both inside your network.
      None of these rules says "buy a private CA". All of them expect encrypted internal traffic, strong authentication and records you can show on request.
      
    
      
      
      vs 398 dPublic TLS, today200 days
      Maximum lifetime of a publicly trusted TLS certificate since 15 March 2026 — falling to 100 days in 2027 and 47 days in 2029 under CA/Browser Forum rules.
      
        
      398 duntil Mar 2026200 dnow100 dMar 202747 dMar 2029
      
        
      Internal certificates on your private CA follow your policy — and public TLS hierarchies are also moving to server-authentication only, so client and device certificates belong on a private CA.
      
      
      
        
      DPDP Act 2023
      Reasonable security safeguards
      Data fiduciaries must protect personal data with reasonable safeguards, including encryption; penalties go up to ₹250 crore. Certificate-based mTLS and device identity keep personal data reachable only from managed systems.
      
        
      CERT-In 2022
      Report in 6 hours, keep logs 180 days
      Issuance and revocation logs give the evidence trail incident handlers need — and revoking a compromised laptop's certificate cuts its access in minutes.
      
        
      RBI · SEBI
      Strong authentication & key control
      RBI's IT governance directions and SEBI's CSCRF expect strong authentication, encryption and key management. A documented hierarchy and HSM-held root give auditors a clear answer.
      
        
      IT Act · CCA
      Not for legal e-signatures
      Legally recognised digital signatures in India come from CAs licensed by the Controller of Certifying Authorities. A private CA is for internal trust; for DSC or eSign, use a CCA-licensed CA.
      
      
      
    
      
    
      Summaries for planning, not legal advice. Confirm how each rule applies to you with your legal and compliance team.
