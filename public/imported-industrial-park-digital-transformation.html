Overview

A park is a small city. Run it like one.

An industrial park operator looks after roads, power, water, sewage and effluent treatment, gates, fire safety and security for dozens or hundreds of factories. Most parks still run these on separate vendor systems, spreadsheets and site visits — and each tenant buys its own internet, servers, backup and security from scratch. That is slow to manage, hard to audit and expensive for small units.

XcellCloud Digital Transformation for Industrial Parks puts the park on one managed stack. We lay out the digital backbone — fibre and private 5G readiness, Wi-Fi, SD-WAN and edge nodes — connect meters, sensors and cameras to a digital-twin command centre, protect OT with a 24×7 SOC, and give tenants a catalogue of cloud, backup and security services that the park bills per tenant.

    OT security · IEC 62443 zones & conduits

    Plant networks split into zones. Traffic between them only through conduits you approve.

    IEC 62443 (the ISA/IEC series for industrial automation and control systems) asks you to group assets into zones with a common security need, connect zones only through defined conduits, and set a target security level for each. We use that model for park utilities and, on request, inside tenant plants — after a passive OT assessment that does not touch running processes.

    India compliance

    The rules your park and tenants answer to — and how we help you meet them.

    Checked October 2026 against the issuing body's own publications. We help you meet these requirements; the park and each tenant remain responsible for their own compliance and filings.

    CERT-InDirections under s.70B, IT Act · 28 Apr 2022

    Report cyber incidents within 6 hours; keep logs 180 days in India

    Applies to service providers, data centres, body corporates and government bodies — so both the park operator and its tenants.

    How we help SOC triage inside the window, log retention in India, NTP-synced clocks, incident report drafts.

    DPDPDPDP Act 2023 · Rules notified Nov 2025, phased in

    CCTV, gate logs and access data are personal data

    Applies to anyone processing visitor, driver and worker data — cameras, ANPR, biometrics.

    How we help privacy notices at gates, retention limits, face blurring for analytics, access logs, breach runbooks and a vDPO.

    CEACyber Security in Power Sector Regulations, 2026 · in force 1 Apr 2027

    If the park or a tenant runs captive power or storage at scale

    Applies to generating companies, captive plants and energy storage at the thresholds CEA sets (reported as 50 MW and above), building on CEA's 2021 guidelines. Check your applicability.

    How we help OT kept apart from IT and the internet, one-way gateways where data must flow, 6-hour incident reporting support, CISO support via vCISO.

    CPCB OCEMSCPCB directions under s.18(1)(b), Water & Air Acts · 5 Feb 2014

    Online effluent and emission monitoring, data to SPCB and CPCB

    Applies to 17 categories of highly polluting industries, CETPs, STPs and common waste incinerators, as the boards direct.

    How we help resilient links for data loggers, uptime and gap alerts, exceedance alerts, dashboards. Analysers and board-specified software stay with your approved vendors.

    SEBI BRSRTop 1,000 listed companies · BRSR Core assurance phased to FY 2026-27

    Listed tenants need energy, water and waste data they can defend

    Applies to listed tenants (and their value chain on a voluntary basis since March 2025).

    How we help metered data per tenant with meter IDs and audit trail, exported as a data pack for their BRSR team.

    Fire NOCState fire services · renewal cycles vary by state

    Keep fire-safety records digital and current

    Applies to the park's common facilities and each tenant building, under state rules.

    How we help fire-panel status on the twin, test and drill records with time stamps, renewal reminders. Inspections stay with the fire authority.

    SEZ / EOUSEZ Act 2005 and rules · zone and customs procedures

    Controlled gates and records that hold up to checks

    Applies to SEZ developers, co-developers and units, under their zone's procedures.

    How we help gate automation with ANPR, movement logs, CCTV retention and highly available IT for the systems units use to transact. We do not file on your behalf.

    IFSCACyber security & resilience guidelines for IFSC regulated entities · Mar 2025

    Tenants in GIFT IFSC with their own cyber rulebook

    Applies to entities regulated by IFSCA, in force from 1 April 2025.

    How we help SOC, VAPT and log retention mapped to the guideline; see also BFSI Cloud.
