Responsible AI & regulation

FREE-AI's seven sutras, turned into desk rules.

A neutral summary of the instruments that touch AI use in Indian banks. It is not legal or regulatory advice; your bank's policies and RBI's own text come first. Checked November 2026.

1of 7 sutras
RBI FREE-AI Committee · 13 Aug 2025

At the desk

The committee, chaired by Dr Pushpak Bhattacharyya, made 26 recommendations under six pillars: Infrastructure, Policy and Capacity to enable innovation; Governance, Protection and Assurance to reduce risk. It is a committee framework; RBI decides how it is applied.

Regulatory register
Red lines we teach
  1. No customer data in public AI.Names, account numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, statements and KYC copies stay in bank-approved systems.
  2. AI is a maker, never the checker.A named person reviews and signs every output used for a decision.
  3. Recompute and trace.Every number goes back to its source document before it is used.
  4. Tell the customer.FREE-AI asks that customers know when they deal with AI and can challenge its decisions.
  5. Report fast.Suspected AI misuse or a data leak goes to your security team at once; CERT-In's clock is six hours.

Independence. XcellHost is independent of the Reserve Bank of India, the Reserve Bank Innovation Hub, IBA, IDRBT, IIBF and any government body. This course is not an RBI-mandated or IBA-approved programme and carries no CPD, CPE or IIBF credit.
