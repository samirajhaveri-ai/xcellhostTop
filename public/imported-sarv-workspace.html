Overview

Your team's whole working day, in one suite.

Most Indian businesses pay for mail with one vendor, file sharing with another, video calls with a third and a chat app on top — each with its own login, admin screen and foreign-currency invoice. Sarv Workspace puts business email with a built-in calendar, Docs, Sheets and Slides with real-time co-editing, a secure Drive, Meet, Webinar and Chat behind a single sign-in.

The interface is simple enough that most people are working in it on day one, and it's protected with encryption and multi-factor authentication.
