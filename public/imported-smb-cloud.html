What SMB Cloud is

Everything a growing office runs on — from one provider.

A 40-person firm in India typically pays for email, antivirus, backup drives, a server AMC, a firewall renewal and a part-time IT person — six contracts, six renewal dates and nobody accountable when something breaks. Meanwhile it still has to issue GST e-invoices once turnover crosses ₹5 crore, back up its books daily on servers in India if it is a company, and prepare for the DPDP Act.

XcellCloud SMB Cloud replaces that patchwork with one managed stack: Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace run for you, email security, backup, endpoint protection, cloud servers for Tally and ERP, cloud desktops, and a managed firewall and Wi-Fi for the office. One team answers the phone, one invoice arrives each month.

Too many vendors

Email, antivirus, backup, firewall and server AMC each from someone different — and each blames the other.

One accountable team

One contract, one helpdesk number and one monthly INR invoice with GST for the whole stack.

An ageing office server

Tally or ERP on a box under a desk, with no spare, no UPS plan and no copy off-site.

Cloud server in Mumbai

Tally, Busy or ERP on a managed cloud server in a Tier IV data centre, reachable from office and home.

Ransomware exposure

One clicked attachment can encrypt shared drives; USB backups are often days old or also encrypted.

Blocked, isolated, restored

Email filtering, endpoint protection that can isolate a PC, and immutable backups to restore from.

Joiners and leavers

Accounts created by hand, shared passwords, ex-employees still in the mailbox months later.

Same-day on and off

We create, licence and secure accounts on request and close every access on the last day.

Compliance creeping in

GST e-invoicing, daily backup of books in India, audit trail in accounting software, DPDP — on a small team.

Evidence ready

India-hosted servers, backup logs, access records and a DPDP basics pack your auditor can read.

Patchy office network

Consumer routers, one shared Wi-Fi password for staff and guests, no failover when the line drops.

Managed firewall & Wi-Fi

A firewall we patch and watch, separate staff and guest Wi-Fi with OTP login, 4G backup on request.
