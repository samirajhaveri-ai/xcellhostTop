Overview

Encryption plus a verified business name — the standard for company websites.

Sectigo OV SSL is an Organisation Validated certificate for one domain. Besides proving domain control, Sectigo checks that your organisation genuinely exists — typically against government or trusted business records and a contact check — before issuing. The certificate then shows your business name and location.

It is the right choice for company sites, login pages and customer portals where visitors should be able to see who runs the site. It carries a $1,000,000 warranty, unlimited server licences and reissues, a dynamic Sectigo seal and is typically issued in 1–3 days. XcellHost bills it in rupees with a GST invoice.
