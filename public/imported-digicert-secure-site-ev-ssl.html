Overview

Premium EV, with the extras that matter after the padlock closes.

DigiCert Secure Site EV is DigiCert's high-assurance certificate for a single website. Before it is issued, DigiCert checks that your organisation legally exists, operates from a real address, is actively trading and has authorised the request. The verified legal name, jurisdiction and registration details are then written into the certificate, where anyone can inspect them.

Secure Site adds what a standard EV certificate doesn't: priority validation that moves your order ahead of the regular queue, the DigiCert Secured seal, vulnerability assessment, the CertCentral console and a US$1.75M warranty. DigiCert has run public trust infrastructure for decades and its roots are recognised by more than 99% of browsers and devices. XcellHost prepares your MCA or GST documents, installs the certificate and handles every reissue.

    EV in India

    Regulators expect encryption. Customers deserve to know it's really you.

    No Indian rule names a certificate brand. But every major framework expects data in transit to be protected — and EV adds verified identity on top, which matters most where money and personal data change hands.

    RBI-regulated entities

    Banks, NBFCs and payment players work under RBI's IT and cyber-security directions, which expect strong encryption on customer channels. EV on login and payment pages makes identity checks visible.

    SEBI's cyber framework

    Brokers, depositories and other SEBI-regulated entities must protect data in transit and manage certificates as security assets — inventory and expiry tracking included.

    CERT-In directions

    CERT-In's 2022 directions require cyber incidents to be reported within six hours. Expired or misconfigured certificates are a common, avoidable incident — we track and reissue for you.

    DPDP Act & MeitY

    The DPDP Act requires reasonable security safeguards for personal data. MeitY's website guidelines (GIGW) expect government sites to run on HTTPS — EV suits citizen portals.
