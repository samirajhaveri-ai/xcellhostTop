    What is Microsoft Defender for Business?

    Microsoft's endpoint security for organisations with up to 300 users. It combines next-generation antivirus, endpoint detection and response (EDR), attack surface reduction, vulnerability management and automated investigation and remediation across Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. Microsoft India lists it at ₹250 per user per month, paid yearly, GST extra. Each user licence covers up to five devices, and it is also included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium.

    What Defender does that antivirus cannot.

    Traditional antivirus recognises known threats by signature. Defender adds behavioural analysis to catch new attacks, fileless malware and misuse of legitimate tools.

    How XDR works

    Four products see four pieces. Defender XDR joins them into one incident.

    A phishing email, a risky sign-in, a script on a laptop and a mass download look minor alone. Correlated on the same user and device, they are one attack — and Defender can act on all of it at once.

    1. 01
      Collect

      Sensors on devices, mailboxes, domain controllers and cloud apps stream signals into one portal.

    2. 02
      Correlate

      Alerts sharing a user, device, mailbox or IP are grouped into one incident with one timeline.

    3. 03
      Investigate

      Automated investigation reviews each piece of evidence and marks it malicious, suspicious or clean.

    4. 04
      Disrupt & remediate

      High-confidence attacks trigger automatic containment; everything can be reviewed and undone.
