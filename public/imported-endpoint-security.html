    06 · Ransomware readiness · CERT-In

    The first six hours decide the next six weeks.

    CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require specified incidents, ransomware included, to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them. Here is what our SOC does in that window — and your own deadline.

    1. 0:00
      Alert lands

      EDR flags the chain; the SOC analyst picks it up and opens a case.

    2. 0:15
      Isolate the device

      Network-isolate the host from the console; it still talks to the SOC.

    3. 1:00
      Scope

      Hunt the same indicators across every endpoint and server.

    4. 2:30
      Preserve evidence

      Export timeline, hashes and logs; keep the 180-day log trail intact.

    5. 4:00
      Draft the report

      Fill CERT-In's incident format with what is known; send updates later.

    6. ≤ 6:00
      Report to CERT-In

    07 · Rules & references

    Four dates that shape endpoint security in India.

    General information as of 19 Nov 2026, not legal advice. Check the source before you rely on it.

    28 Apr 2022

    CERT-In directions

    Report specified incidents within 6 hours of noticing them; keep ICT logs for a rolling 180 days within India; sync clocks to NIC or NPL NTP, or servers traceable to them.

    Source: CERT-In Directions under s.70B(6) IT Act, 28 Apr 2022, and CERT-In FAQs (May 2022).
    13 Nov 2025

    DPDP Rules, 2025

    Reasonable security safeguards include encryption, access control and keeping logs for breach detection for at least one year. A detailed breach report to the Board is due within 72 hours. Most obligations apply from 13 May 2027.

    Source: MeitY notification, 13 Nov 2025; as summarised by Khaitan & Co, 15 Nov 2025.
    CIS v8.1

    Control 10 · Malware defenses

    Seven safeguards: deploy anti-malware, auto-update signatures, disable autorun, scan removable media, enable anti-exploitation, manage centrally, use behaviour-based detection. The gap tool above maps to them.

    Source: CIS Controls v8.1 (June 2024), Control 10 safeguards 10.1–10.7.
    14 Oct 2025

    Windows 10 end of support

    No more free security updates for Windows 10 after this date; Extended Security Updates are paid. Unpatched laptops are the easiest way in.

    Source: Microsoft Lifecycle. Not re-verified on 19 Nov 2026.
