What is a WHOIS lookup?

A WHOIS lookup shows the public registration record of a domain name: the registrar it was bought through, when it was registered and when it expires, its status codes (such as clientTransferProhibited), its nameservers and, where not privacy-protected, the registrant's organisation and country. Today most registries publish this through RDAP, the structured successor to WHOIS. XcellHost's free tool queries RDAP and live DNS so you get registration details and current nameservers in one search.

What you can find with a WHOIS lookup

Registrant information

The organisation and country behind a domain — where the owner hasn't enabled privacy protection.

Important dates

Registration, expiry and last-updated dates, so you know exactly when a domain is due for renewal.

Nameserver details

The DNS servers a domain points to — often a clue to its hosting or DNS provider.

Domain status

EPP status codes showing whether a domain is active, locked against transfer, on hold or pending deletion.

Registrar info

Which accredited registrar manages the domain, with its abuse-contact details.

Privacy status

Whether contact details are redacted or proxied — common under GDPR and registrar privacy services.

Domain status codes, explained

clientTransferProhibitedLocked against unauthorised transfer — normal and recommended.
clientUpdateProhibitedChanges to contacts or nameservers are locked at the registrar.
clientDeleteProhibitedThe domain can't be deleted without first removing the lock.
serverHoldSuspended by the registry — the domain won't resolve.
redemptionPeriodExpired and deleted, but the owner can still restore it for a fee.
pendingDeleteAbout to be released — it may become available to register soon.

WHOIS, RDAP and your privacy

RDAP is the modern, structured replacement for WHOIS, and it's what this tool queries first. Under GDPR, India's DPDP Act and registrar privacy services, personal contact details are often redacted — you'll still see the registrar, dates, status and nameservers.
