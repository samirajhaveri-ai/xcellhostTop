    Overview

    E3, with five more layers for the accounts attackers want.

    Microsoft 365 E5 is Microsoft's top enterprise suite below E7. Per Microsoft, it includes everything in Microsoft 365 E3 and adds Microsoft Defender XDR — Defender for Office 365 Plan 2, Defender for Endpoint Plan 2, Defender for Identity and Defender for Cloud Apps — Microsoft Entra ID P2, Purview's advanced compliance tools, Power BI Pro and Teams Phone with Audio Conferencing.

    NGOs rarely need E5 for everyone. Give it to the people with privileged roles and money flows — finance, the FCRA officer, the CEO and board — and keep programme staff on E3 Nonprofit and volunteers on the free E1 Nonprofit tier. On XcellHost's card E5 Nonprofit is ₹2,420 per user a month against ₹4,920 commercial*.

        Eligibility & TechSoup India verification

        Four steps to nonprofit pricing, done with your team.

        01 Qualify

        Per Microsoft: recognised legal status in India, not-for-profit, community-benefit mission. Government bodies, schools and universities, financial institutions and political groups are excluded.

        02 Prepare

        Registration certificate, PAN, 12A / 80G, NGO Darpan ID and FCRA registration if you have one.

        03 Validate

        Your employee or strategic volunteer registers on Microsoft for Nonprofits. Per TechSoup, Microsoft has run validation itself since August 2025; TechSoup India still helps with documents.

        04 Activate

        We assign E5 seats, enable PIM for admins and baseline Defender — then re-check eligibility when Microsoft asks for re-attestation.

        *Microsoft decides eligibility and changes nonprofit offers from time to time — eligibility and pricing are confirmed at quote.
