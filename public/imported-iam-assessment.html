Overview

Every breach report has an identity chapter. Find yours before someone else writes it.

Over the years, access piles up. Staff change roles and keep their old rights, vendors get an account for one project and never lose it, service accounts run with passwords set to never expire, and MFA covers most users but not the admins who matter most. Each gap looks small. Together they are how attackers move.

XcellHost IAM Assessment is a fixed-scope, read-only review of how identities are created, authenticated, authorised, governed and monitored in your organisation. We combine configuration exports from Microsoft Entra ID, Active Directory and other identity providers with short process interviews, score eight pillars on a four-stage scale, and give you a ranked, costed roadmap.

Why it matters

Attackers log in more often than they break in. Regulators have noticed.

Stolen or guessed credentials, MFA fatigue, over-privileged accounts and forgotten vendor access are recurring themes in public incident reports worldwide. In India, those gaps now carry regulatory weight as well as business risk.

01

Accounts nobody owns

Leavers still enabled, contractors past their end date, shared mailboxes with passwords in circulation.

02

MFA with holes

Admins on SMS codes, legacy protocols that skip MFA entirely, and exclusions nobody remembers adding.

03

Standing privilege

More global or domain admins than the work needs, assigned permanently instead of just in time.

04

Little evidence

Sign-in logs kept for 30 days, no access-review records, no proof of who approved what.
