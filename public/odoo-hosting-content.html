What Is Odoo Hosting?

Odoo hosting gives you a dedicated cloud server running Odoo — the open-source business suite for CRM, sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting, projects, websites, eCommerce and HR. With XcellHost you deploy Odoo 16, 17 or 18 in one click on an isolated VPS with PostgreSQL and full root access, then scale CPU, RAM and storage as your users and data grow — billed in INR.
