How to secure your website with TLS/SSL

Two layers of protection. Then keep them valid.

A certificate does two jobs at once: it encrypts what travels between browser and server, and it proves who runs the server. How strongly it proves that depends on the validation level you buy.

Scramble everything in transit

Card details, personal data and passwords are encrypted before they leave the browser, so nobody on the network can read or alter them.

    Website security in India

    Regulators expect encryption. Customers expect to know it's really you.

    No Indian rule names a certificate brand, but the major frameworks all expect data in transit to be protected and certificates to be managed as security assets.

    RBI-regulated entities

    Banks, NBFCs and payment players work under RBI's IT governance and cyber-security directions, which expect strong encryption on customer channels. EV or OV on login and payment pages makes the identity check visible.

    SEBI's CSCRF

    SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework asks regulated entities to protect data in transit and keep an inventory of critical assets — certificates and their expiry dates included.

    CERT-In directions

    CERT-In's 2022 directions require reporting of cyber incidents, including website defacement and compromise, within six hours. Malware and blocklist checks help you spot trouble before customers do.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Anyone processing personal data must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent breaches. Encrypting every form, login and API call is the first of them.

    Guidance only — regulations change and apply differently by sector. Your compliance team has the final word.
