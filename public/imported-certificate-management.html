Overview

One platform for every certificate you own, whoever issued it.

Sectigo Certificate Manager (SCM) is a certificate lifecycle management (CLM) platform. It finds the certificates already running across your servers, load balancers, cloud accounts and Microsoft CA, puts them in one inventory, and then issues, renews, replaces and revokes them on policy. It is CA-agnostic: certificates from other public CAs and your own private CAs are tracked alongside Sectigo's, so one console replaces spreadsheets and calendar reminders.

Why now: public TLS certificate lifetimes are shrinking in steps — to 200 days in March 2026, 100 days in 2027 and 47 days in 2029. Renewing by hand at that pace is where outages come from. XcellHost deploys SCM, connects it to your stack through ACME, SCEP and EST, and runs it as a managed service from our 24×7 NOC in Mumbai — with GST invoicing in rupees.

    Automate against shrinking lifespans

    From 398 days to 47. Manual renewal stops scaling.

    The CA/Browser Forum has approved a step-down in maximum public TLS certificate lifetime. Each step multiplies the renewals your team must get right.

    Max validityRenewals / cert / yr

    Google's Moving Forward, Together roadmap argued that shorter lifetimes limit the damage from mis-issued or compromised certificates and push the ecosystem toward automation. Apple then sponsored the CA/B Forum ballot that set the 47-day schedule, approved in 2025.

    For Indian enterprises

    Regulators expect control over keys and certificates. CLM is how you show it.

    None of these rules says "buy a CLM". All of them expect encryption that doesn't lapse, controlled keys and logs you can produce on request.

    CERT-In

    Directions of April 2022

    Report notifiable incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for 180 days. SCM's audit trail records who issued, renewed or revoked what, and when.

    RBI

    IT governance & cyber controls

    Banks, NBFCs and payment players are expected to manage cryptographic keys and certificates under documented controls. An expired gateway certificate is also an availability incident.

    SEBI

    CSCRF for regulated entities

    SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework asks for asset inventories and strong cryptographic controls — certificates included.

    DPDP Act

    Reasonable security safeguards

    Personal data in transit needs working encryption. Lapsed or weak certificates are an avoidable gap when you must show safeguards.
