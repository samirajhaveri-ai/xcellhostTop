Pharmaceutical Cloud

Validated systems need qualified infrastructure underneath.

A LIMS or QMS is only as trustworthy as the servers, storage, clocks and backups beneath it. US FDA 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11 and India's revised Schedule M all expect computerised systems to be validated, access-controlled and audit-trailed, and inspectors from export markets will ask how your hosting provider is qualified and controlled.

XcellCloud Pharmaceutical Cloud gives you that foundation. We are GxP-ready, with validation support* — not "GxP certified", because no such certificate exists for a host. We qualify the infrastructure, document it, keep it under change control, and support your team as you validate the applications that run on it.
