    Overview

    The full Office desktop. The compliance toolkit. None of the device bundle.

    Office 365 E3 is the plan Microsoft built for knowledge workers. Per Microsoft, each user gets Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote and Access (PC) — on up to five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five phones, plus Exchange Online Plan 2 with a 100 GB mailbox and an archive that can grow to 1.5 TB.

    It also adds data loss prevention, retention and eDiscovery (Standard). What it leaves out is the device-and-identity bundle — Intune, Entra ID P1 and Windows Enterprise — which is why it suits companies that already manage PCs and phones with another tool. That is the ₹1,090 per user per month difference from Microsoft 365 E3.
