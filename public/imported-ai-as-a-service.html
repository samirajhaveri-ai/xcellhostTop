Overview

Stop assembling AI from ten vendors. Consume it as one service.

Most AI projects in India stall between the demo and production. The model works in a notebook, but then someone has to find GPUs, host the model, connect it to company documents, keep personal data out of prompts, satisfy the risk team and pay five foreign invoices in dollars. XcellAI AI as a Service is XcellHost's answer: every layer of a production AI stack — compute, platform, models, applications and governance — offered as a service you switch on, from one Indian provider.

Start small with pay-per-token serverless inference on open models, add a vector database and RAG over your documents, give staff managed Gemini, OpenAI or Microsoft 365 Copilot, and move steady workloads to reserved L4, RTX PRO 6000 or H200 GPUs. Underneath, the XcellHost 24×7 NOC and SOC run the platform in Mumbai, and an ISO/IEC 42001-aligned governance layer keeps an inventory, risk register and guardrails in place. One contract, one INR invoice with GST.

      India compliance

      Built for Indian rules from the first prompt.

      Keeping prompts, documents and logs in India does not make you compliant on its own. It removes a hard question and gives your risk team evidence. We help you meet these; you stay responsible for them.

      IndiaData residency

      Serverless and dedicated endpoints, vector databases and retained logs run in XcellHost's Mumbai data centre and partner DCs*. Managed Gemini, OpenAI and Copilot follow each provider's own data-location terms, which we map for you.

      DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

      The Rules were notified in November 2025 with phased commencement: Consent Manager provisions from November 2026 and most obligations from May 2027. A breach goes to the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours. Gateway PII masking and logs help you meet this.

      MeitYIndia AI Governance Guidelines

      Unveiled on 5 November 2025 under the IndiaAI Mission: seven guiding sutras and recommendations across six pillars, relying on existing laws rather than a new AI act. Our governance layer maps your inventory and risk register to them.

      IndiaAIIndiaAI Mission

      Approved by the Union Cabinet in March 2024 with ₹10,371.92 crore over five years; its seven pillars include IndiaAI Compute, AIKosh datasets and Safe & Trusted AI. We help you evaluate these options next to private capacity.

      6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions

      The April 2022 directions require reportable cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for a rolling 180 days in India. AI gateway and platform logs can be retained in Indian data centres.

      RBIRBI FREE-AI framework

      The committee report of 13 August 2025 sets out 7 sutras and 26 recommendations for regulated entities. ISO 42001-aligned inventories, AI policies and monitoring give banks and NBFCs evidence for them.

      Regulatory summaries reused from our AI Adoption Framework page (sources checked October 2026) for orientation only — not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with MeitY, CERT-In, the Data Protection Board or your sector regulator.
