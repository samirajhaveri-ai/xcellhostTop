What Construction Cloud is

The office, the studio and the site — on one cloud.

A construction business runs on files that are too big to email and sites that move every two or three years. Revit and CAD models live on a few powerful workstations, the drawing on site is often one revision behind, the site office depends on one broadband line, and drone surveys and photos pile up on hard disks that nobody backs up.

XcellCloud Construction Cloud moves that work into our Mumbai Tier IV data centre. Designers use GPU cloud desktops, estimators and accounts use cloud desktops, sites connect over managed SD-WAN with 4G/5G backup, and drone and photo archives sit on S3-compatible object storage in India — with backup, EDR and a 24×7 SOC on top.

Industry challenges

Six problems every project team already knows.

Pinned like drawing sheets, because that is how they show up — on the site board, usually late.

CH-01

Models outgrow laptops

Federated Revit and CAD models run to gigabytes. Copies go to consultants by drive or link, and nobody knows which one is current.

GPU desktops; the model never leaves the data centre
CH-02

Wrong revision on site

A superseded drawing printed at the site office means rework, wasted material and a dispute about who issued what.

One drawing register with issue logs
CH-03

Sites with weak links

Container offices on temporary broadband or one SIM card. When it drops, billing, attendance and ERP stop.

SD-WAN bonding broadband, 4G and 5G
CH-04

Footage nobody can find

Drone surveys, progress photos and CCTV add terabytes per project — on hard disks in a cupboard, if anywhere.

Object storage in India with lifecycle rules
CH-05

Personal data everywhere

Buyer KYC, allotment letters and worker records sit in inboxes and site laptops — a DPDP problem waiting to happen.

Central, access-controlled, logged
CH-06

Ransomware at bid time

A stolen site laptop or a fake payment email in tender week can cost more than the project margin.

EDR, email security and 24×7 SOC
