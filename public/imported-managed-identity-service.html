Overview

Identity is the new perimeter. Someone has to run it.

Buying an identity platform is the easy part. Keeping it right is daily work: accounts that follow HR changes, access requests approved by the right person, reviews closed before the auditor asks, MFA that covers every user, admin roles that expire, and someone watching risky sign-ins at 2 a.m. Skilled IAM engineers are hard to hire and harder to keep. XcellSecure Managed Identity Service puts that work with a team that does it every day.

We run identity governance (IGA), privileged access (PIM and PAM) and access management on Microsoft Entra ID first — XcellHost is a Microsoft Gold Partner — and support leading IAM, IGA and PAM platforms* where you already have them. Delivery is remote from Mumbai, with an IST business-hours desk, 24×7 cover for critical incidents, a named delivery lead and backup engineers so knowledge never sits with one person.

A planned handover, not a cold takeover.
