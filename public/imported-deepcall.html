Overview

One cloud number system for every customer call.

DeepCall AI is Sarv's cloud contact centre. It takes the place of the office EPABX, the separate IVR vendor, the dialer and the spreadsheet of call logs: callers dial a toll-free or 10-digit business number, a smart IVR asks what they need, and the call reaches a free agent who answers on a laptop, IP phone or mobile — in the office or at home.

Above the telephony sits an AI layer. Speech analytics reviews calls for sentiment, agent performance and call patterns, summarises reports and forecasts call traffic so you roster the right number of people. XcellHost resells DeepCall in India: we design your IVR, set up or move your numbers, train your team and put it all on one GST invoice in rupees.

Telephony in India

The rules are specific. We handle the paperwork.

Business calling and messaging in India come with registrations and documentation. We take you through each one before go-live.

Number KYC

Toll-free and virtual numbers need business documents. We collect them, file them and tell you the expected activation time on your quote.

TRAI 160-series

Service and transactional calls from regulated businesses are moving to 160-series numbers so customers can recognise them. DeepCall can provision them.

DLT for SMS

Commercial SMS in India must use DLT-registered sender IDs and templates. We help register yours before your first OTP or campaign goes out.

DPDP Act & recordings

Call recordings and transcripts are personal data. Tell callers they are recorded, set retention periods and restrict who can listen — we configure these with you.

Guidance only — telecom and data-protection rules change. Your legal and compliance teams have the final word.
