Logistics runs on systems that must agree with each other minute by minute: the TMS that plans trips, the WMS that knows where every pallet is, the trackers on every truck, the ERP that raises invoices and e-way bills, and the customer portal that answers "where is my shipment?". When they sit on different servers, links and vendors, nobody sees the whole picture — and an expired e-way bill or a dead warehouse link costs real money.

XcellCloud Logistics Cloud brings them together in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre: IoT Cloud for fleet devices, edge nodes at hubs, warehouse Wi-Fi and SD-WAN with 4G/5G, hosted TMS, WMS and ERP with logged GST integrations, and a 24×7 SOC across all of it.

    Quick check · e-way bill validity

    How many days does the e-way bill run?

    Rule 138: one day for up to 200 km and one more for every 200 km or part of it; over-dimensional cargo counts 20 km a day. Illustrative — confirm on the e-way bill portal.
