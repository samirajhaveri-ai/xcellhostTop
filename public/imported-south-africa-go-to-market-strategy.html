Executive summary

Enter South Africa through partners, with data kept in-country

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$3.8B

Cloud, cyber security and online education in South Africa, 2025, summed from the lower estimate in each segment315. That is about . The higher estimates sum to more than twice as much.

  • Cloud: US$3.0B–6.44B in 2025. BMIT puts it at R49.6B, rising to R101.5B by 202934.
  • Cyber security: US$0.29B–1.46B in 2025, by two sources with very different scopes12.
  • Data centres: about 400 MW today, with plans that could take the country to at least 1,200 MW6.
  • Online education: US$494M in 2025, growing 22.5% a year to 20345.
Why now
8,000+ breaches

The Information Regulator had received more than 8,000 security-compromise notifications by September 2026, over 1,220 of them since 1 April 202616. See the trigger calendar.

  • Joint Standard 2 of 2024 on cyber security and cyber resilience has applied to banks, insurers, pension funds and market infrastructures since 1 Jun 202522.
  • POPIA breach reports have gone through the Regulator's eServices portal, a mandatory tool, since 1 Apr 202513.
  • National Policy on Data and Cloud (31 May 2024): government data on national security and sovereignty must be stored only in South Africa2728.
  • AI policy: the April 2026 draft was withdrawn. A redraft is due to go to Cabinet by March 202731.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

A South African DC partner plus local SI/MSP resellers first. Open a (Pty) Ltd between month 6 and 12.

  • Host regulated workloads in a South African partner DC in Johannesburg or Cape Town, so the hosting itself is not a cross-border transfer.
  • Company incorporation is streamlined, with minimal capital requirements10. A local company lets us bill in rand and register for VAT at 15%33.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC, under written POPIA operator terms with each customer.

    Risks & mitigations

    HighRising breach volumes

    More than 8,000 security-compromise notifications in total, and the Regulator expects over 3,000 this financial year.

    Mitigation: harden our own South African footprint first, and sell detection with tested incident runbooks.
    HighDeep-pocketed competition

    Hyperscalers and global colocation groups are investing heavily, and Teraco is already large.

    Mitigation: lead with managed security, digital trust and AI skills, not raw compute.
    MediumPolicy uncertainty

    The AI policy was withdrawn, the cloud Joint Standard is pending and section 54 is unproclaimed.

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with South African counsel, and refreshes to this page.
    MediumData transfers to India

    Mumbai support is a cross-border transfer under POPIA, and banks review offshoring under D3/2018.

    Mitigation: in-country hosting by default, operator agreements for support access, and a Joint Standard evidence pack.
    MediumB-BBEE & tax

    Empowerment rules and withholding tax were not re-verified and may shape the entity choice.

    Mitigation: take advice in month 0–3 and keep the partner-led model until it is resolved.
    MediumPower supply

    Eskom has flagged possible supply constraints by 2029 as data-centre demand grows.

    Mitigation: choose partner DCs with on-site generation and renewable supply, and keep DR in a second site.
    LowerFX & collections

    Changes in ZAR, INR and USD rates, plus partner credit risk.

    Mitigation: a US$ price book, prepaid or quarterly billing, and partner credit limits.
    LowerTalent

    Skilled security and cloud staff are in demand.

    Mitigation: a local country lead, and partner engineers trained by XcellHost.
