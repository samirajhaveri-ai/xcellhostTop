    Overview

    A computer lab is a timetable problem. HyLabs treats it like one.

    Accops HyLabs is Accops' virtual-lab product for universities, colleges, schools and corporate training teams. Instead of booking rooms full of PCs, you publish labs — Python, Tally, Java, databases, CAD — as virtual desktops that appear for each class, reservation or workshop, and students open them from campus or home on the device they already own, per Accops.

    XcellHost builds and runs it for you. We import your timetable, prepare a gold-master image for each course, wire HyLabs to your directory and LMS, and host the lab desktops in our Mumbai cloud — or install everything on your campus servers. Labs are prepared before each period, cleared after it, and watched by our 24×7 team in Mumbai.

    Key capabilities

    Eight things HyLabs does, per Accops.

    Each one verified against Accops' HyLabs product page and documentation. Tap a module to see what it means for your department.

    1. Create, manage and monitor every virtual lab from one central portal — courses, gold masters, reservations and reserved VMs in one place.

    2. Labs are provisioned from academic schedules, reservations, workshops or training sessions. A university timetable can be imported and labs created automatically.

    3. HoDs and faculty can deploy and change labs with limited IT dependency. Faculty have no change rights by default; you enable them granularly.

    4. Students reach labs securely from their own devices, through a browser with no heavy software to install, from campus or home.

    5. Links to learning platforms such as Moodle and Blackboard so lab access follows the curriculum and academic workflow.

    6. Lab-utilisation insights that help you plan capacity and timetables — and show which labs are actually used.

    7. Deploy on-premises or in the cloud. Accops lists VMware vSphere and Azure as supported platforms, with an Azure Virtual Desktop delivery option.

    8. Departments get controlled access to manage their own lab requirements — Computer Science, Commerce and Mechanical each run their own.

      Security & India compliance

      SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

      Student data stays in India. Lab access stays controlled.

      180days of access logs, kept in India — helps you meet CERT-In directions
      6 hCERT-In incident-reporting window — logs ready when you need them
      DPDPpersonal data of students and staff processed in India, with role-based access

      General information, not legal advice. You remain the data fiduciary for student data.

      • Sign-in through HyID and HySecure

        HyLabs uses the Accops workspace for access, so you can add MFA for faculty and admins and keep the lab behind a secure gateway.

      • Roles, not shared passwords

        Reservation admin, in-charge, assistant and participant roles decide who sees and changes what, per Accops docs. Users come from AD or an internal database.

      • Nothing stays on student devices

        Labs open in a browser; software and files live on the lab desktop, not on a personal phone or laptop.

      • Activity logs & session recording

        The Corporate edition lists activity logging, session recording and policy enforcement for audits and certification exams, per Accops.

      • Care with children's data

        For school students under 18, the DPDP Act expects verifiable parental consent; we help you keep consent records and minimise what the lab collects.

      • Backups by XcellHost

        Gold masters, portal configuration and student home folders backed up daily in our Mumbai cloud on the hosted option.
