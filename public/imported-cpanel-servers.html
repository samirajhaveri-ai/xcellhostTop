    Overview

    Your own cPanel server. Our engineers behind it.

    A cPanel server is a VPS or dedicated machine that runs cPanel & WHM — WHM for the server owner (create accounts, set packages, run security and updates) and cPanel for each website owner (email, files, databases, domains, SSL and backups).     How licensing works

    cPanel is priced by accounts. We pick the tier for you.

    Per cPanel's current pricing and licensing guide, the licence tier depends on how many cPanel accounts the server hosts and whether it runs on a VPS (Cloud) or dedicated hardware (Metal). XcellHost buys, binds and adjusts it as part of your plan.

    Solo1 account · VPS
    Adminup to 5 · VPS
    Proup to 30 · VPS
    Premierup to 100 · VPS or Metal
    Premier +each account above 100
    Bound to the server's IP

    A licence is issued for the main public IPv4 of one server. Extra IPs on the same server don't need their own licence.

    VPS vs dedicated

    Solo, Admin and Pro are for virtual servers. Dedicated hardware uses Premier (Metal), per cPanel's guide.

    What counts as an account

    Each cPanel login that manages a website is one account — whether you or a reseller created it. Addon domains inside an account don't count separately.

    Premier scales monthly

    Above 100 accounts, cPanel bills each extra account. We pass it through per account on your next invoice*.

    Upgrade or downgrade

    Fixed tiers change when your account count crosses a limit; cPanel prorates changes and we reflect them in your next bill*.

    Not portable between servers

    Licences aren't transferable between servers. Moving to a bigger XcellHost server? We issue the licence on the new IP.

    Licence rules summarised from cPanel's pricing page and Customer Licensing Guide 2025; cPanel may change them. Prices and tiers on your quotation prevail.
