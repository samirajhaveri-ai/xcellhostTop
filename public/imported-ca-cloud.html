        Security & client confidentiality

        SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

        CA Cloud gives you controls that support your obligations. It does not take them over: the firm stays responsible for how client information is used.

        ICAI
        Confidentiality under the CA Act and Code of Ethics

        Confidentiality is a fundamental principle of ICAI's Code of Ethics. Under Clause 1 of Part I of the Second Schedule to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, disclosing information gained in an engagement without the client's consent is professional misconduct.

        DPDP
        DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

        Client and employee personal data you process must be protected with reasonable security safeguards, and breaches reported to the Data Protection Board and affected people. XcellHost acts as your processor and supports your breach response.

        CERT-In
        CERT-In directions, April 2022

        Service providers must keep ICT logs for 180 days in India and report notified cyber incidents within 6 hours. Sign-in and session logs for your desktops are retained accordingly.

        General information, not legal advice. Check your obligations with your advisers.
