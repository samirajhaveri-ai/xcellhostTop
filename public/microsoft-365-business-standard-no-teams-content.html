Microsoft 365 Business Standard (no Teams) FAQs

What is the price of Microsoft 365 Business Standard (no Teams) in India?
₹565 per user per month on annual paid upfront (₹6,783 per user per year) or ₹782 per user per month on monthly no-commit. Prices exclude 18% GST.
What is the list price?
The Microsoft list price (MSRP) is ₹665 per user per month. Annual paid upfront saves 15% and monthly no-commit 2%.
Does this include Teams?
No. This product is Microsoft 365 Business Standard (no Teams), so Microsoft Teams is not included. If you need Teams, choose Business Standard (with Teams), SKU CFQ7TTC0LDPB:0001.
What is the licensing model?
A per-user subscription. You buy a number of user licences and assign them to individual users in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
Is it perpetual or a subscription?
This SKU (CFQ7TTC0LDPB:0002) is a subscription, not a perpetual licence.
Will I get a GST invoice?
Yes. XcellHost issues a proper GST invoice for B2B procurement in India.
How is it delivered?
As ESD or via CSP subscription provisioning — not physical media.
Can we add or remove users during the term?
User changes are managed in the Microsoft 365 admin center by assigning or unassigning licences; extra seats can be added anytime.
Is online activation required?
Yes. Microsoft 365 services are cloud-based and need internet connectivity for sign-in and service access.
What is the SKU code?
CFQ7TTC0LDPB:0002.
