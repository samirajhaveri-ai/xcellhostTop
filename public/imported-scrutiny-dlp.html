    Overview

    Scrutiny DLP is a unified data loss prevention platform. It finds the sensitive information your business holds — customer lists with Aadhaar and PAN, salary registers, tenders, design files, source code — labels it, watches how it moves and stops it leaving through the wrong door. Endpoints, USB drives, email, web uploads, the clipboard, printers and remote desktop sessions all report into one dashboard, with real-time alerts and reports your auditors can read. It goes beyond classic DLP with OCR inspection of images and scans, screenshot protection, hidden watermarking and governance of what staff paste into GenAI tools. XcellHost deploys Scrutiny DLP, designs the policies with you, tunes out false positives and watches the alerts from its 24×7 NOC/SOC in Mumbai — billed in rupees with GST.

    Seven channels · one policy

    Every way data leaves — covered.

    Most leaks are ordinary: a copy to a pen drive, a file mailed home, a sheet pasted into a chatbot. Scrutiny DLP watches each of these doors with the same classification and the same policy.

    Endpoint protection

    Real-time protection on laptops, desktops and servers — files watched as they are opened, copied, renamed and moved.

    Device control

    Allow, read-only or block USB drives and external storage — with exceptions for approved, encrypted devices.

    Email DLP

    Inbound and outbound mail checked for confidential data — block, encrypt or hold for a manager's approval.

    Web upload protection

    Stop files reaching unsanctioned cloud drives, file-sharing sites and web forms, while approved services keep working.

    Clipboard protection

    Copy-paste of sensitive text between apps is inspected, masked or blocked — the quietest leak of all.

    Print protection

    Control what gets printed and by whom. Sensitive prints are blocked or stamped with a traceable watermark and logged.

    Remote desktop control

    Secure data during remote sessions — clipboard, file transfer and drive mapping governed by policy.

    One dashboard

    Centralised visibility, real-time monitoring, smart alerts and complete reporting for all seven channels.

    Beyond traditional DLP

    Screenshot protection

    Screen captures of sensitive windows are blocked or logged — closing a gap most DLP leaves open.

    Hidden watermarking

    Invisible marks tie a document or print back to the user and device it came from.

    OCR content inspection

    Text inside images, scanned PDFs and photographed documents is read and classified like any other file.

    GenAI usage governance

    Prompts pasted into AI tools are checked — personal data masked or blocked before it leaves.

    It reads documents and behaviour — then tells you what to fix.
