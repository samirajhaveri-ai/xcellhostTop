    Overview

    Your people already use AI. Now you can see it — and steer it.

    Staff paste client lists into free chatbots, install AI note-takers that join every meeting, and connect AI agents to the CRM with a personal API key. Banning AI rarely works; it moves the use to phones and personal accounts. XcellAI AI Security Platform sits between your users and the AI apps they reach, so you can see which tools are in use, keep personal and confidential data out of the wrong ones, and record what happened.

    XcellHost deploys the platform, writes the first policies with you, and watches its alerts from our 24×7 SOC in Mumbai. It is licensed per AI user per month, billed in INR with GST. The platform runs on partner technology that XcellHost names in your proposal*; you deal with one Indian provider for licences, setup and support.

    Frameworks mapping

    Controls mapped to the frameworks your auditors and regulators use.

    The platform supplies controls and evidence. It does not by itself make you compliant — your organisation remains responsible.

    India regulatory snapshot · verified October 2026
    DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

    Rules notified 13 Nov 2025; most duties, including breach notice, apply from May 2027.

    CERT-In

    6-hour incident reporting and 180-day log retention (2022 directions); advisory on AI language-based apps.

    MeitY India AI Governance Guidelines

    Released 5 Nov 2025 under IndiaAI — principle-based guidance, not a statute.

    RBI FREE-AI

    Committee report, 13 Aug 2025: 7 sutras, 26 recommendations for regulated entities.

    SEBI AI/ML

    Consultation paper (20 Jun 2025) on responsible AI/ML use by market participants — proposed.

    EU AI Act (exporters)

    GPAI duties since 2 Aug 2025; Annex III high-risk moved to 2 Dec 2027 by the 2026 omnibus.
