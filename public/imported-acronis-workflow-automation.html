    Overview

    Workflow management for service teams, already inside your Acronis console.

    Most IT teams and MSPs repeat the same jobs dozens of times a week: protect the laptop that just joined, clear an alert that never matters, isolate a machine when EDR raises an incident, set up the next customer. Each job is a few clicks — until it is a few hundred clicks spread across technicians, shifts and tenants, done slightly differently each time.

    Acronis Workflow Automation is a no-code, drag-and-drop automation engine built into the Acronis Cyber Platform (formerly Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud). Per Acronis, it covers backup, disaster recovery, RMM, EDR/XDR and PSA events, needs no separate software, and comes with the platform for every Acronis partner. XcellHost turns it into running workflows: we onboard your tenant, build and test your first five workflows, propagate them to your customers and watch the results 24×7.

    Rule-based automation

    AI suggests. A workflow decides — the same way, every single run.

    Acronis positions Workflow Automation as deterministic: a trigger fires, conditions are checked, and the same actions run in the same order. That is what makes it safe to hand routine jobs to software — and what keeps AI-driven work predictable.

    Same trigger · same steps · every tenantanimated illustration · sample
    Complements your AI work

    Rules give AI-assisted operations firm rails: AI can summarise or suggest, while the workflow defines exactly what runs.

    Less busywork

    Routine events — a new device, a low-priority alert, a new customer — get handled the moment they happen, not when someone is free.

    Consistent for every client

    The same steps run for every customer, so service quality no longer depends on which technician picked up the job.

    Built to scale

    Design a workflow once in the cloud console and apply it across all your customers instead of rebuilding it per tenant.
