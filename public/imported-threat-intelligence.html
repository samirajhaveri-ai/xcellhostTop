Overview

More threat data was never the problem. Knowing which 1% matters is.

Every day brings new advisories, vulnerabilities exploited in the wild, ransomware campaigns and chatter about Indian brands. Most of it has nothing to do with you. The part that does tends to arrive buried in a feed nobody has time to read.

XcellHost Threat Intelligence turns that stream into insight about current and emerging threats that apply to your business. Our analysts agree your intelligence requirements with you, collect from open, community and closed sources, check each item against your sector and technology, and deliver it two ways: machine-readable into your security tools, and human-readable as briefs your SOC, IT head and board can act on.

Why it matters in India

Indian businesses are targeted by sector, and the clock starts the moment you are.

Attackers reuse what works against one bank, hospital or factory on the next one. Intelligence that knows your sector gives you the warning your peers didn't get.

Six hours to report

CERT-In expects specified incidents reported within 6 hours. Early warning means you spot activity before it becomes an incident — and have context ready if it does.

Sector-led campaigns

BFSI, healthcare, manufacturing and government suppliers see repeated, sector-specific campaigns. Knowing what hit a peer last week is the cheapest defence there is.

Patch windows are shrinking

Vulnerabilities in internet-facing appliances are often exploited within days of disclosure. Intel tells you which of yours to patch first.

Brand & credential exposure

Lookalike domains, fake profiles and leaked staff credentials are early signs of fraud. We flag them at outcome level and start a takedown workflow.

Methodology

The intelligence cycle, run every day and reviewed every quarter.

Six phases, starting with your questions rather than our sources. Requirements are reviewed each quarter and after any major incident.

  1. 01Direction

    Priority intelligence requirements (PIRs) agreed with you: which assets, sectors and threats matter.

  2. 02Collection

    Open, community, advisory and closed sources mapped to each requirement.

  3. 03Processing

    De-duplicated, normalised, scored and given an expiry date.

  4. 04Analysis

    Analysts add context: ATT&CK tactic, confidence, your exposure, recommended action.

  5. 05Dissemination

    TLP-marked, to the right reader: tools, SOC, IT owners, leadership.

  6. 06Feedback

    What was useful, what was noise — the next cycle is tuned on your answers.

Agreed in writing

Requirements, sources, sharing rules and contacts recorded before onboarding.

TLP 2.0 handling

CLEAR, GREEN, AMBER, AMBER+STRICT and RED respected end to end.

Data handling under NDA

Your asset list and findings stay in India, need-to-know only. Leaked credentials are shown masked.

Passive and defensive

We observe and report. No interaction with threat actors and no access to systems we are not authorised for.
