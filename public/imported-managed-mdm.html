    Overview

    An MDM licence is a toolbox. Managed MDM is the team that uses it.

    Most businesses buy an MDM platform, enrol a few devices and stop there. Policies drift, new joiners wait days for a configured phone, kiosk tablets get unlocked by curious customers, and nobody can say how many laptops are actually encrypted. The platform was never the problem — the hours were.

    XcellHost Managed MDM puts our Mumbai NOC in charge of the day-to-day. We design your enrolment and policy baseline, run app and OS update rollouts, answer your staff's device tickets, handle lost and stolen devices the same hour, and send you a monthly compliance report. You keep ownership of the tenant and the devices; we keep them in shape.

      Moving from another MDM?

      Devices can't be "transferred" between MDM platforms — they have to be re-enrolled. We plan it so nobody loses a working day.

      1. Inventory every device, owner and profile in the old platform.
      2. Rebuild policies and apps in the new platform and test on pilot devices.
      3. Re-enrol in waves by site or team, with a call-back slot for each user.
      4. Retire the old platform once the last device checks in.
