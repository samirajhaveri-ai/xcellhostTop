    Overview

    One tool for the PC in Pune, the server in Mumbai and the phone in the field.

    TeamViewer is remote connectivity software from TeamViewer SE of Germany. Its TeamViewer Remote plans let your IT team or support desk see and control computers, servers and — with an add-on — mobile devices, either with the user present or unattended. TeamViewer Tensor is the enterprise version, adding single sign-on, conditional access and audit tooling for larger estates.

    XcellHost sells the licence that fits your team — counted by licensed users, concurrent channels and managed devices — and does the work around it: rolling out the client to every PC by MSI, switching on two-factor authentication and allowlists, setting up your company profile and teaching your technicians. You pay in rupees on a GST invoice and call one number in Mumbai when something needs fixing.

    Encrypted end to end

    Per TeamViewer, current clients use mutually authenticated TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy and AES-256-GCM; the routing servers pass traffic they cannot read. Older versions use RSA-4096 key exchange with AES-256.

    Unattended access

    Reach servers, PCs, kiosks and POS terminals at any hour — wake them with Wake-on-LAN, reboot and reconnect without a person at the desk.

    Sessions for teams

    Invite a second technician, hand a session over or escalate to L2 without the user reconnecting.

    File transfer & printing

    Drag files both ways during a session, queue large transfers and print remote documents on a local printer.

    TeamViewer AI

    Session summaries and in-session troubleshooting suggestions, per TeamViewer — included or added depending on plan.

    Mass deployment

    Push the client to hundreds of PCs with an MSI package through Group Policy or Intune, already assigned to your company profile.

    Every platform

    Connect from and to Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS and Raspberry Pi; Android and iOS with the mobile add-on.

    Helpdesk integrations

    Start sessions from tickets in tools such as ServiceNow, Jira, Salesforce or Microsoft Teams — availability varies by plan.

    How a session works

    Four steps from "my screen is frozen" to a closed ticket.

    The highlighted step follows the session as it moves along the line.

    1. 01Start

      The user calls or raises a ticket. Your technician opens the device from the contact list, or the user reads out an ID from QuickSupport.

      attended or unattended
    2. 02Consent & verify

      The user accepts the prompt. Unattended devices need the account's 2FA and an allowlist entry; Tensor adds SSO and access rules.

      prompt · 2FA · rules
    3. 03Fix

      Control the screen, chat, move files, switch monitors, reboot and reconnect — with the user watching what you do.

      control · chat · files
    4. 04Close & record

      The session ends, the connection report is logged and, if your policy says so, the recording is saved for review.

      report · recording

        Safe remote access

        Good practice we apply on every XcellHost rollout — and that we recommend to anyone using remote-access tools.

        Time-based codes or push approval on technician accounts, enforced for the whole company profile.

        Least privilege

        Never let a stranger connect

        No genuine bank, telecom, courier, tax or police office will ask you to install remote-access software or read out an ID and password. If a caller does, hang up. In India, report cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

        FAQ

        TeamViewer in India, answered.

        TeamViewer is remote access and remote support software. IT teams and support desks use it to see and control computers, servers and mobile devices over the internet — to fix problems, manage servers, help customers and run a helpdesk without travelling. XcellHost licenses, deploys and supports it in India.

        According to TeamViewer, sessions are end-to-end encrypted (TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM on current versions), accounts support two-factor authentication, repeated wrong passwords are slowed down exponentially and devices can be limited to an allowlist. Security still depends on setup — we switch on 2FA, allowlists and hardened unattended access for every rollout.

        Attended access is for helping a person: they start the session or accept a prompt and watch while you work. Unattended access is set up in advance on servers and PCs you manage so an authorised technician can connect when no one is there — protected by account 2FA, device assignment and an allowlist.

        Remote Access suits one person reaching up to three of their own devices. Business suits a single technician supporting up to 200 managed devices. Premium covers up to 15 users with 300 devices, and Corporate up to 30 users with 3 concurrent channels and 500 devices. Larger estates, or anyone needing SSO and access rules, should look at Tensor. Use the plan picker above, or ask us.

        Licensed users are the people who can sign in and use the licence. A channel is one live connection at a time — Remote Access, Business and Premium have one, Corporate has three, and Premium and Corporate can add more. Managed devices are the computers you keep in your list for quick or unattended access.

        Yes, with the Mobile Device Support add-on, which TeamViewer offers with Business, Premium, Corporate and Tensor — not with Remote Access. What you can do depends on the device: many Android devices allow remote control, while iOS allows screen sharing for guided help.

        Tensor is TeamViewer's enterprise remote connectivity platform. On top of remote support it adds single sign-on with providers such as Microsoft Entra ID, Okta and Google Workspace, conditional access rules, multitenancy, event-log auditing, integrations such as Intune and Jira, and agentless access through a gateway. It's priced on quote.

        No. TeamViewer's free licence is for personal, non-commercial use. Supporting colleagues, customers or company devices needs a paid plan, and TeamViewer may restrict free sessions it detects as commercial.

        Yes. XcellHost bills in rupees on a GST invoice with our GSTIN and itemised lines, so GST-registered businesses can claim input tax credit. A proforma is available before payment. Prices on this page are indicative and exclude 18% GST.

        Tell staff never to install remote-access software or share an ID and password because a caller asked them to. Restrict who can connect to company devices with allowlists, keep consent prompts on, and review connection reports. Report cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

        Session traffic is end-to-end encrypted between the two devices. TeamViewer states that servers holding sensitive data are in Germany or Austria. Account and connection data may therefore leave India — we'll walk you through TeamViewer's data-processing terms so you can meet your duties as a data fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023.
