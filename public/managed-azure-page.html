    Overview

    You own the Azure tenant. We keep it healthy, secure and lean.

    Managed Azure is XcellHost's operations service for Microsoft Azure. Your subscriptions stay in your tenant and your name; our engineers set the foundations, watch every resource around the clock and keep improving cost and security every month.

    Most Azure estates in India start with a few VMs and grow into dozens of subscriptions, networks and databases that nobody has time to govern. XcellHost Managed Azure puts a structure underneath: a landing zone with management groups, hub-spoke networking and Azure Policy guardrails, then day-to-day operations — Azure Monitor alerting, ticketed incident response, Update Manager patch rings, Azure Backup and Site Recovery checks.

    Every month you get a report on incidents, patch compliance, backup success, Defender for Cloud findings and spend. Our FinOps reviews look for idle resources, oversized VMs and where a savings plan or reservation pays off. Azure usage is billed at actuals through XcellHost in rupees with GST, together with our management fee — one invoice, one team in Mumbai.
