    Overview

    One platform between your devices and the decisions they should drive.

    Most IoT projects stall in the same place: every device type needs its own bridge, credentials get copied from one unit to the next, the site loses internet and data goes missing, and nobody owns the broker at 3 a.m. XcellHost IoT Cloud puts that plumbing in one managed service — device registry, per-device identity, a multi-protocol broker, edge gateways, a rules engine, time-series storage, dashboards and over-the-air updates.

    You keep control of the devices and the data model. We run the platform from Mumbai, patch it, watch it 24×7 and help with the paperwork that comes with connected devices in India — DPDP Act duties when telemetry identifies people, CERT-In's six-hour reporting and 180-day log retention, and sector rules where they apply. Pricing is per connected device per month, in rupees, with a GST invoice.

    Security & India compliance

    Every device proves who it is. Every action leaves a log.

    Connected devices bring obligations. The platform is set up to help you meet them — the duties stay yours, the tooling and evidence come from us.

    Identity, not passwords

    Unique X.509 certificate per device, mutual TLS, no shared or default credentials. Certificates can come from IoT Device Certificates or your own CA via IoT PKI.

    Encrypted end to end

    TLS 1.2+ or DTLS in transit, encryption at rest, per-topic access policies and least-privilege console roles with MFA.

    Signed firmware only

    Devices accept updates whose signature checks out; campaigns roll out in waves and roll back on failure.

    TEC IoT code of practice

    The platform supports the Telecommunication Engineering Centre's guidance for securing consumer IoT — unique credentials, update paths, secure storage and secure communication.
