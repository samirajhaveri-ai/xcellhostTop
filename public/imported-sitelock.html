    Overview · what is SiteLock?

    Find it. Fix it. Stop it coming back.

    SiteLock is a cloud website-security service. Every day it scans your site for malware, known vulnerabilities and blacklisting, and its SMART tool removes malicious code from your files automatically. The Pro and Business plans add a web application firewall (WAF) and a content delivery network (CDN), so attacks are filtered before they reach your server and pages load faster for real visitors. The SiteLock Trust Seal shows visitors the site has been scanned.

    Website security in India

    Regulators now expect you to notice an attack fast. SiteLock helps you see it.

    No Indian rule names a security product. These are the obligations a website-security service helps you meet — it does not make you compliant on its own.

    0hours

    CERT-In incident reporting

    CERT-In's April 2022 directions require reportable cyber incidents — the list includes website defacement and malicious-code attacks — to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them. Daily scans and SOC alerts mean you notice sooner and have the scan log to attach.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Expects reasonable security safeguards for personal data and breach intimation to the Data Protection Board. A malware-free site and a firewall in front of customer forms are basic safeguards.

    Payment pages · RBI & PCI DSS

    PCI DSS v4.0 expects public-facing web apps to be protected by an automated solution that detects and blocks attacks, such as a WAF. Business includes a firewall PCI report for your acquirer or payment aggregator.

    Search & browser blacklists

    A Google Safe Browsing warning can cut traffic overnight. Blacklist monitoring and clean-up help get it lifted faster.

    Backups you can restore

    Daily file and database backup (2–10 GB by plan) gives you a clean copy to roll back to — keep it alongside your host's backups.
