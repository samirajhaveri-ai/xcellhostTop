    How it works

    Server-side, client-side, or both.

    There are two ways to put a managed signature on an email. Most companies use both: server-side for consistency on every device, client-side so people see the signature while they write.

    Signature added after send, in the mail flow
    Server-side
    • Works from any device or app that sends through your tenant — phones included.
    • Users can't edit or delete it; the brand stays locked.
    • A mail flow rule routes outbound mail through the service and back for delivery.
    • Senders don't see it while composing; Sent Items needs a sync setting to show it.
    Client-side
    • The signature appears in the message as people type, and in Sent Items.
    • People can pick between templates you allow, e.g. a short reply signature.
    • Mail does not pass through the signature service.
    • Useful for S/MIME-signed or encrypted mail, which can't be changed after sending.

    Mechanisms differ by platform and edition. On Microsoft 365, server-side mode uses Exchange Online mail flow rules and connectors; client-side mode uses an Outlook add-in. On Google Workspace, signatures are set through the Gmail settings that admins control, with routing options for server-side stamping*. We confirm the method for your tenant during setup.

    India compliance

    What Indian law asks of your business correspondence.

    General information, checked October 2026 against the Acts as published. It is not legal advice — confirm the wording with your company secretary or counsel. XcellOffice Email Signature helps you apply the particulars consistently; you stay responsible for what they say.

    Companies Act, 2013 · Section 12(3)(c)

    Every company must show these on its business letters

    • Company nameas registered
    • Registered office addressfull address
    • Corporate Identity Number (CIN)21 characters
    • Telephone numberand fax, if any
    • E-mail addressif any
    • Websiteif any

    The section covers business letters, billheads, letter papers, notices and other official publications. Companies widely treat business email as correspondence and put these particulars in the email footer. Under Section 12(8) the default attracts a penalty of ₹1,000 a day while it continues, up to ₹1 lakh, for the company and each officer in default.

    LLP Act, 2008 · Section 21

    Limited liability partnerships

    Invoices, official correspondence and publications must show the LLP's name, registered office address and registration number (LLPIN), and a statement that it is registered with limited liability. The Act sets penalties for non-compliance.

    Disclaimers & DPDP Act, 2023

    Confidentiality and privacy notices

    A confidentiality notice does not replace security controls, but many firms add one to external mail. If your footer links to a privacy notice, point to the one you publish under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Rules can append it to external mail only.

    How we apply it
    • Legal block stored once, locked against edits
    • Different text for each legal entity in a group
    • External-only rules, so internal mail stays short
    • Change of address or name updated for everyone at once

    Sources: Companies Act, 2013, s.12(3)(c) and s.12(8) as amended; Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, s.21; Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Wording summarised, not quoted.
