Overview

A flight simulator for your security team.

Most teams meet their first serious incident on the day it happens. Analysts have read about ransomware but never worked one end to end; the CISO has a plan on paper that has never met a clock; leadership has not decided who calls the regulator. A cyber range closes that gap: a realistic, isolated network where people practise detection, response and decisions under pressure — with nothing real at risk.

XcellSecure Cyber Range gives you virtual environments for security simulation, run as a service. We build the network (IT, OT/ICS and cloud segments with a working SOC stack), write the scenario, play the adversary and the white cell on the day, score every team decision and hand you a debrief with gaps mapped to MITRE ATT&CK and the NICE Framework.

Defensive by design. Simulated adversary activity runs only inside the sealed range, built by our exercise team. We do not teach attack techniques, hand out tooling or touch your production systems. The goal is to train defenders.

6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions

CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require specified cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours of being noticed and ICT logs to be kept for 180 days in India. Our drills run that clock and test log retrieval.

CERT-InNational cyber exercises

CERT-In conducts cyber security exercises and mock drills with ministries, states and critical sectors, and has hosted international ones such as the ransomware-themed "Synergy". Range practice helps your team arrive prepared. XcellHost is not affiliated with these exercises.

NCIIPC · CCMPCritical infrastructure

NCIIPC is the national nodal agency for protecting critical information infrastructure under section 70A of the IT Act. CERT-In's Cyber Crisis Management Plan asks organisations to keep crisis plans and test them. Tabletops give you that test record.

RBIBanks & NBFCs

RBI's cyber security framework for banks and its 2023 IT governance Master Direction expect a cyber crisis management plan, incident response and periodic drills. We supply the scenario, the scored drill and the after-action report.

SEBI CSCRFSecurities market entities

SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (August 2024) includes periodic cyber-security drills and, for larger categories, red-teaming exercises. Check your category; we run the drill and document it.

DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

A personal-data breach must be intimated to the Data Protection Board and affected people. Our crisis exercises include that decision and the notice draft. See our vDPO service for the privacy programme.

ATT&CK v19MITRE ATT&CK

Version 19 (April 2026) has 15 Enterprise tactics; Defense Evasion was split into Stealth and Defense Impairment. Our scenario tags follow v19, and OT scenarios use ATT&CK for ICS.

NICE v2.2.0NICE Framework

NIST's NICE Framework Components v2.2.0 (April 2026) define work roles, competencies and tasks. Skills assessments map each task to a work role so results feed role-based development plans.

SP 800-84NIST test, training & exercise

NIST SP 800-84 describes how to plan tabletop and functional exercises for IT plans. Our design, conduct and after-action stages follow the same structure.

ISO 27001Incident management controls

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A covers incident management planning and response. Exercise records support evidence for these controls. See our ISO 27001 readiness service.

ISO 22301Business continuity exercising

ISO 22301 asks organisations to exercise their continuity plans. A combined cyber and continuity tabletop covers both in one session.

NeutralCertifications

Labs support practice for common blue-team and incident-handling certifications. We are not affiliated with any certification body; exam fees and vouchers are not included.
