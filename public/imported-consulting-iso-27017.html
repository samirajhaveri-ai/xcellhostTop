Overview

ISO 27001 tells you to secure information. ISO 27017 tells you how, in the cloud.

ISO/IEC 27017:2015 is the code of practice for information security controls in cloud services. It takes the controls of ISO/IEC 27002, adds cloud-specific guidance to 37 of them, and introduces seven cloud-only controls — from who owns which responsibility, to tenant segregation, VM hardening and getting your data back when a contract ends. Unusually, it speaks to both sides: the cloud service provider and the cloud service customer building on it.

27017 is not certified on its own. It sits on an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS: an accredited certification body audits your ISMS with the 27017 controls in scope and issues a 27001 certificate that references 27017. XcellHost prepares you for that audit — scope, responsibility matrix, controls and evidence — and coordinates with the certification body. We run ISO 27001, 27017 and 27018 in our own cloud operations from Mumbai, so the advice comes from practice, not slides.

The seven cloud-only controls

What 27017 adds that ISO 27001 never had to say.

Each CLD control has guidance for the provider and for the customer. Pick one to see what each side does and the evidence we prepare for the audit.

Provider or customer

One standard, both sides of the contract.

27017 is unusual: every control comes with guidance for whoever runs the cloud and whoever uses it. Most of our clients are on one side; cloud and managed-service businesses are often on both.

Cloud service providerYou run the platform
  • Publish the split. Tell customers in writing which controls you operate and which are theirs.
  • Isolate tenants. Segregate compute, storage and networks between customers — and prove it.
  • Harden what you supply. Secure base images and defaults for the VMs and services you hand over.
  • Let customers see. Give them logs and monitoring for the parts they are responsible for.
  • Plan the exit. Return data in a usable format, then delete it — backups included.

How 27017 certification works

A code of practice, certified through your ISO 27001.

There is no separate "27017 certificate" process. The cloud controls go into your ISMS, the certification body audits them with everything else, and the certificate scope says so.

ISO/IEC 27018PII in public cloud · optional
ISO/IEC 2701737 guided controls + 7 CLD controls
ISO/IEC 27001 ISMSclauses 4–10 · Annex A · SoA
Certificate scopeISO/IEC 27001incl. ISO/IEC 27017 controlsAccredited CB
  1. 1Have — or build — a 27001 ISMS

    27017 needs the management system underneath it: scope, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, internal audit and management review.

  2. 2Bring the cloud controls into scope

    Cloud risks join the risk register, the seven CLD controls and the cloud guidance go into the SoA, and the responsibility matrix becomes evidence.

  3. 3The certification body audits it

    Stage 1 reviews documentation; Stage 2 tests implementation. If you already hold 27001, the 27017 extension is often added at a surveillance or special audit.

  4. 4Certificate, then a three-year cycle

    The certificate references 27017 in its scope. Annual surveillance audits follow, with recertification in year three.

XcellHost is not a certification body and does not issue certificates. Certification is granted by an accredited certification body (accredited by NABCB or another IAF member). We prepare you, coordinate the audits and can recommend certification body partners.

      Data fiduciaries & processors

      A data fiduciary stays responsible for safeguards even when a cloud provider processes the data. The responsibility matrix shows who does what.
