For most new cloud deployments: Virtual Networks first (VPCs and subnets cost ₹0 in Mumbai), then a Virtual Firewall, then DNS. Add a Load Balancer once you run two or more servers for one app, and a WAF once the app is public. The finder above does this ranking for your goals.

Usually, yes. They look at different things. A firewall decides which addresses and ports may talk to which servers. A WAF reads the web request itself and blocks attacks such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting and abusive bots, which arrive on the same port 443 the firewall must keep open.

CloudConnect links your offices and branches to XcellHost cloud, usually over encrypted IPsec tunnels across the internet, or over SD-WAN or MPLS. DirectConnect is a private physical path: a cross-connect inside our Mumbai Tier IV data centre or a carrier leased line, with onward private links to hyperscalers through partners*. Use CloudConnect for people, DirectConnect for steady, high-volume or latency-sensitive traffic.
