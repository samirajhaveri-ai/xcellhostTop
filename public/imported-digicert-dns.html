    Overview

    DNS is the first thing every visitor touches. It should never be the thing that fails.

    DigiCert DNS is DigiCert's managed authoritative DNS platform, built on UltraDNS. It holds the official records for your domains and answers every lookup — for your website, apps, APIs and email — from a globally distributed anycast network. DigiCert describes it as a way to cut latency, add resilience and protect DNS with DDoS mitigation and DNSSEC, at enterprise scale.

    XcellHost brings it to Indian businesses as a managed service. We audit your current zones, migrate records from your registrar or existing provider without downtime, switch on DNSSEC, set up health-checked failover between origins such as Mumbai and Singapore, and watch the platform 24×7 from our Mumbai NOC. Licences, onboarding and support arrive on one INR invoice with GST.

    How it works

    Where DigiCert DNS sits in every lookup.

    A visitor types your name. Their resolver walks the DNS tree and lands on DigiCert's authoritative servers, which answer with a signed, steered record. Sample path.

    01Ask

    The browser asks its resolver for shop.example.in.

    02Walk the tree

    Root and the .in registry point the resolver to your name servers — and the DS record vouches for DNSSEC.

    03Answer

    The nearest UltraDNS node returns a signed record for the healthiest origin.

    04Connect

    The browser connects to Mumbai, or to Singapore if Mumbai failed its health checks.

    Why it matters in India

    Availability and integrity are compliance topics now, not just uptime.

    Resilient, signed DNS with an audit trail helps you meet several Indian obligations. General information, not legal advice.

    CERT-In Directions, April 2022

    Report cyber incidents — including DNS hijacking and DDoS on DNS — within 6 hours, and keep ICT logs in India for 180 days. We alert you fast and keep change records and audit-log exports in India for 180 days*.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Requires reasonable security safeguards against personal data breaches. Hijacked DNS can send customers to a fake login page; DNSSEC and locked-down DNS access reduce that risk.

    RBI & SEBI

    RBI's IT governance directions and SEBI's CSCRF expect availability, resilience testing and audit trails for customer-facing systems — failover drills and reports give you evidence.

    IT Act 2000

    Section 43A ties liability to reasonable security practices; role-based DNS access and MFA are easy wins to document.
