Overview · on-premises data-centre build

Your room, your hall, your site. Built and handed over as one project.

Some workloads have to stay on your premises: plant systems, latency-sensitive branches, regulated records, or GPU clusters you want next to your data. Building the room for them pulls in civil works, electrical, mechanical, fire, security, cabling and monitoring, usually from different contractors. XcellHost Data Center Build Services runs all of it as a single programme, from the first site survey to the day our NOC starts watching your alarms.

We size the facility from your racks and kW, set a design intent against Tier topology concepts and TIA-942 ratings, specify equipment OEM-agnostically, manage the build, and commission every system from factory tests through integrated systems testing. You get as-built drawings, test records and an operating manual. Then we can run it for you under AMC with 24×7 monitoring from Mumbai. Estimate costs first, or compare with colocation in our Tier IV data centre.

Built for India

Regulation, climate and grid. Designed in from day one.

A data hall in India has to cope with monsoon, high ambient heat, variable grid supply and local approvals, as well as data rules. The design helps you meet them. It does not replace your own compliance work.

DPDP Act 2023

Access control, CCTV, visitor logs and media handling support the reasonable security safeguards the Act expects for personal data.

CERT-In directions (2022)

Time sync, log capture and 24×7 monitoring designed in, helping you meet 6-hour incident reporting and 180-day log retention.

MeitY & government workloads

Facility controls and documentation prepared for the audits that government and public-sector hosting asks for.

RBI / SEBI data localisation

Keeping payment and market data on infrastructure you control in India, with the physical controls your regulator's audit will check.
