    Overview

    The workplace your staff log into every morning. Run like infrastructure.

    Workspace Operations is XcellHost's managed service for the end-user side of IT — identities, productivity apps, devices, desktops and the people who call when something breaks.

    Most Indian offices run their workplace on a mix of tools: Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for mail and files, a few laptops enrolled in Intune and many that aren't, phones on WhatsApp, a Tally server on a cloud desktop, and one IT person who also orders the toner. XcellHost Managed Workspace Operations puts all of it under one 24×7 team — accounts and licences, device policies and patches, cloud desktops, awareness drills and a helpdesk with ticket numbers and SLAs.

    We take over through a structured transition: inventory, runbooks for joiners, movers and leavers, a security baseline and monitoring. Then our Mumbai NOC works every incident and request to ITIL 4 practices, escalates to Microsoft or Google as your partner, and sends a monthly service report you can take to management. You keep ownership of tenants, devices and data; licences and service can come on one INR invoice with GST.

    Operating model

    ITIL 4 practices, written runbooks. Work shifts left every month.

    Every request and incident follows a documented practice. Repeat tickets become runbooks; repeat runbooks become automation or self-service.

    Incident management

    Something is broken for a user — Outlook won't open, a laptop won't sign in, Teams calls drop. We log, prioritise P1–P4, restore service and keep the user updated until closure.

    • SLA clock starts at ticket creation
    • P1 gets a phone call, not just an email
    • Escalation to Microsoft or Google as partner

    Service request management

    Standard asks with a known path: new user, shared mailbox, distribution list, app install, licence change, new laptop. Each has a catalogue item, an approver and a target time.

    • Request catalogue agreed at onboarding
    • Approvals from your named managers
    • Many requests automated end to end

    Problem management

    When the same incident keeps coming back, we look for the root cause — a bad policy, an expired certificate, a driver — and fix it once, with a known-error record in the meantime.

    • Trend review every month
    • Known-error database you can read
    • Fix the cause, not the ticket

    Change enablement

    Policy changes, Conditional Access rules, mail-flow rules and update rings go through a change record: risk, test group, approval, rollback plan and a quiet window.

    • Pilot rings before company-wide
    • Agreed maintenance windows
    • Rollback written before go

    Monitoring & event management

    Service health, sign-in risk, device compliance, backup jobs and licence usage are watched by the NOC. Alerts become tickets automatically, so nothing waits for a user to complain.

    • Alert → ticket, no copy-paste
    • Service health from Microsoft and Google
    • Security events handed to our SOC
