Why Sectigo

A long-established certificate authority — with a certificate for every job.

Sectigo is the certificate authority previously known as Comodo CA. It was carved out of the Comodo Group by Francisco Partners in 2017 and renamed Sectigo in 2018; by its own figures it had issued more than 100 million SSL certificates in over 200 countries at the time. Its roots are trusted by current browsers and operating systems — Sectigo publishes a 99.9% browser-compatibility figure.

The range covers DV, OV and EV SSL, wildcard and multi-domain (UCC) certificates, ACME certificate-as-a-service for automated renewal, OV and EV code signing, plus website security services such as HackerGuardian scanning and CodeGuard backup. XcellHost sells each one in rupees, runs the validation with you and installs it.
