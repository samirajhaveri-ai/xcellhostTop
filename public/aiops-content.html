Overview

Monitoring tells you something broke. AIOps tells you what, why and what to do.

Modern estates produce more signals than people can read: thousands of alerts a day from servers, cloud services, networks, databases and applications, most of them echoes of the same few problems. Gartner describes AIOps as combining big data and machine learning to automate IT operations processes such as event correlation, anomaly detection and determining causality. XcellAI AIOps brings that to your estate as a service — the platform, the models and the people who act on them.

We ingest your metrics, logs, traces and events — natively or through OpenTelemetry — learn what normal looks like for each service, group related alerts into incidents, rank the likely root cause on your service topology and, where you allow it, run pre-approved fixes. Every incident is worked by the XcellHost 24×7 NOC in Mumbai, ticketed in your ITSM tool and reported monthly in rupees and minutes.

    India compliance

    Faster detection and complete logs help you meet Indian rules.

    AIOps does not make you compliant on its own. It shortens the time to know, keeps the evidence and gives your incident team a timeline they can file.

    6 hCERT-In incident reporting

    CERT-In's April 2022 directions require reportable cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them. Correlated incidents with timelines help your team notice sooner and report with facts.

    180 dCERT-In log retention

    The same directions ask for ICT system logs to be kept for a rolling 180 days within India. We can route and retain the logs AIOps ingests in Indian data centres.

    DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

    As the DPDP Rules phase in, a personal-data breach must be intimated to the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours. Faster detection and a clean timeline help you meet that.

    RBI · SEBIBFSI IT resilience

    RBI's IT governance master direction and SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) expect monitoring, incident management and capacity planning. AIOps reports give you evidence for each.

    Regulatory summaries checked October 2026 for orientation only — not legal advice. Your organisation remains responsible for its own reporting and compliance; confirm current requirements with CERT-In, the Data Protection Board, RBI or SEBI.
