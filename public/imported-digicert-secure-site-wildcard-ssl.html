    How issuance works

    Prove the domain by DNS, the company once. Then deploy everywhere.

    Wildcards follow slightly different rules from single-domain certificates. The biggest one: control of the domain can't be proved with an HTTP file — only DNS or email.

      Wildcard TLS in India

      Every subdomain handles someone's data. Encrypt them all the same way.

      Indian frameworks don't name a certificate type, but they expect data in transit to be protected everywhere — including the subdomain a vendor set up last quarter.

      DPDP Act, 2023

      Anyone processing personal data must take reasonable security safeguards. A wildcard means a new sign-up or careers subdomain is encrypted from the first day.

      CERT-In directions

      Incidents such as website compromise must be reported within six hours, and logs kept for 180 days. Knowing every server that holds the wildcard key is part of being ready.

      RBI & SEBI

      RBI's IT governance directions and SEBI's cyber-resilience framework expect encrypted customer channels and managed keys. OV shows your verified entity on every login subdomain.

      MeitY & government sites

      Ministries, PSUs and their vendors follow MeitY guidance such as GIGW, which expects portals to be served securely over HTTPS — often across many subdomains of one domain.
