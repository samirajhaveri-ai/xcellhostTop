Overview

Rubber-banding, sudden kicks and a server that falls over the night of a tournament usually trace back to the same thing: shared hardware a long way from your players. XcellHost Gaming Dedicated Servers put a whole bare-metal machine in our Mumbai data centre behind you — high-clock Intel Xeon, AMD Ryzen and AMD EPYC processors, enterprise NVMe, 1 or 10 Gbps ports and an always-on DDoS shield that filters attack traffic at the network edge. You get full root plus KVM/IPMI access, so you can run Minecraft with Forge, a Rust server with Oxide plugins, ARK clusters, CS2 match servers, Valheim, Palworld or your own mobile game backend — one game or several. Players in Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai and Kolkata connect over short domestic routes, and Amsterdam, New York and US West are available on request for overseas communities. Billing is in rupees with a GST invoice, and our 24×7 NOC watches the hardware.
