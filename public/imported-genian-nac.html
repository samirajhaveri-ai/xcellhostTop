Access decisions, enforced first

Decide before a device can do damage — not after the alert.

Network access control is the gate every device passes on its way in. Genian NAC checks who and what is connecting in real time and acts before the device touches anything that matters.

Genian NAC watches every switch segment and Wi-Fi network through a lightweight network sensor. The moment something connects, it works out what the device is, who is using it, what state it is in and where it is connecting from — then applies your policy. Devices that pass get the network they need; devices that don't are moved to a quarantine network, helped to fix themselves or cut off.

It needs no rip-and-replace: the sensor sits on an existing trunk port, works with the switches you already own and starts in a monitor-only mode. XcellHost sizes it, installs it, writes the policies with you and — if you want — runs it 24×7 from our Mumbai NOC & SOC, billed in rupees on one GST invoice.

How it works

Thirty devices, six steps — scroll from unknown to compliant.

The same thirty devices on a typical office floor move from an unknown crowd to sorted, risk-scored, decided, enforced and reported.

  1. 01Seconds after connectionDiscover

    The network sensor listens to ARP and broadcast traffic on each segment and lists every device that speaks — managed, unmanaged, legacy, wired or wireless — with no agent and no network scan storm.

    L2 sensor · passive + active
  2. 02Device Platform IntelligenceIdentify

    Each device is fingerprinted against Genians' device database: maker, model, platform, connection type and even a product photo, so “unknown 00:1A:…” becomes “IP camera on the gate VLAN”.

    Maker · model · OS
  3. 03ContinuousAssess

    Every platform is matched to end-of-life and end-of-support dates, vendor status and known CVEs, giving each device a risk you can sort and act on.

    EOL · EOS · CVE
  4. 04At every connectionDecide

    Policy combines identity, posture and context — user group, device type, OS, location, time, risk — to choose allow, guest network, quarantine or block.

    500+ conditions · per Genians
  5. 05The right layer for each caseEnforce

    802.1X and RADIUS for switches and Wi-Fi that support it, ARP enforcement where they don't, switch-port shutdown over SNMP, and agent controls on managed PCs.

    RADIUS · ARP · SNMP · agent
  6. 06Daily · monthlyRemediate & report

    Quarantined users get a page that tells them what to fix; once fixed, they are re-admitted on their own. You get a compliance trend, an asset list and evidence for audits.

    Self-remediation · reports
