Ports & bundles

Three port speeds. Bundle them when one isn't enough.

Every DirectConnect port lands on an XcellHost edge router in Mumbai. Several ports of the same speed can be bundled with LACP into one logical link. VLANs keep your environments apart on the same port.

  • LACP bundles — 2 to 8 same-speed ports as one link (IEEE 802.1AX). Lose a member and the rest keep carrying traffic.
  • 802.1Q VLANs — production, DR and management on separate VLANs and BGP sessions over one port.
  • BGP-4 with your public or private ASN. Prefix filters, communities and MED or AS-path prepending to choose primary and backup paths.
  • BFD — sub-second failure detection, where your router supports it. We measure the real failover time at go-live.
  • MACsec (IEEE 802.1AE) on supported optics and routers, on request*. Or run IPsec over the circuit.
  • Jumbo frames up to 9,000 bytes on request*, end to end, tested at go-live.

    Tool · latency & RTT estimator

    Distance sets the floor. No circuit beats physics.

    Light in fibre covers about 1 km every 5 µs, one way. That's about 1.5 times slower than in a vacuum. Real routes follow roads and railways, so the estimator multiplies the straight-line distance by a route factor and adds a small allowance for each device on the path.

    Fibre seldom runs in a straight line. 1.3–1.7× is typical between Indian metros. Your carrier's route survey gives the real figure*.
    Compare with a budget
    These are example RTT budgets. Use your application vendor's figure. For voice, ITU-T G.114 recommends no more than 150 ms one way, which is 300 ms round trip.

    Method: great-circle distance from city coordinates × route factor × 5 µs per km (CommScope, "Latency in optical fiber systems", 2017: refractive index ≈ 1.5, so ≈ 5 µs/km), plus your per-device allowance, doubled for round trip. Queuing under congestion and carrier detours are not modelled. Measure the real figure on the delivered circuit.

    India compliance

    A private path helps. Records prove it.

    A dedicated circuit doesn't make you compliant on its own. It supports these obligations, alongside your own policies and systems.

    CERT-In directions, 28 April 2022

    Report specified cyber incidents within 6 hours of noticing them, and keep ICT logs for a rolling 180 days in India. Port, BGP and change logs for your circuits are kept in Mumbai for that period.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards. Keeping personal data off the public internet in transit supports that.

    RBI & SEBI expectations

    Regulated entities are expected to have resilient, documented network paths. Diverse circuits and failover test records help in IT and outsourcing reviews.

    Leased lines, MPLS and wavelengths come from licensed telecom providers through partners*. XcellHost arranges and manages them and does not claim a telecom licence.
