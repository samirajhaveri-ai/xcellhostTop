Overview

What is a OV md wildcard certificate? In plain words.

An SSL/TLS certificate encrypts everything between your visitors and your server and proves the site really belongs to its domain. Sectigo OV Multi-Domain Wildcard is a Organisation Validation certificate from Sectigo, one of the world's largest certificate authorities: domain control + your company is verified as a real, registered business, and it is typically issued in 1–3 working days.

Coverage: Several domains, each with all its first-level subdomains. XcellHost sells it in rupees with a GST invoice, creates the CSR, helps with validation, installs it on your servers and reissues it free during the subscription — which matters now that certificates must be replaced at least every 200 days. Best for: company websites, customer portals, APIs and login pages where visitors should be able to see who runs the site.

    Regulators, customers and browsers all expect it.

    Personal data must be protected with reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit — penalties for failures run up to ₹250 crore.

    A spoofed or expired certificate on a customer portal can become a reportable incident. Keep certificates valid and monitored.

    Cyber security frameworks for regulated entities expect strong encryption for every customer-facing channel.
