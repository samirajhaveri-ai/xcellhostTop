          How it works

          Four checkpoints between a device and your data — and the data never leaves.

          A typical hosted layout. Blue packets are allowed sessions; the orange one fails a posture check and is stopped at the gateway.

          01Prove who you are

          HyID checks the password against your directory, then asks for a second factor — push, OTP, biometrics or a FIDO key.

          02Prove the device is safe

          HySecure reads device posture — OS updates, antivirus, location, binding — and only then opens a tunnel to the apps you're allowed.

          03Land on a workspace

          The HyWorks controller places you on a shared or personal desktop, or opens just the published app you need.

          04Data stays put

          Files and databases never leave the data centre. Clipboard, USB and downloads follow your policy.

          Security & compliance · India

          Nothing to steal on the device. Everything logged in India.

          Accops controls who gets in and what leaves the session. XcellHost adds backups, monitoring and India-resident logs.

          MFA on every session

          HyID factors include SMS, email and app OTP, push, fingerprint and face, FIDO keys, QR passwordless and PKI certificates (per Accops; options vary by edition).

          Device entry control

          HySecure checks posture — OS updates, antivirus, geolocation, device binding — and blocks devices that fail.

          No data on the endpoint

          Clipboard control, peripheral control and in-session watermarks in HyWorks; copy, internet and browser controls for BYOD in HySecure*. Print and USB redirection set per policy.

          Backups by XcellHost

          Daily backups of profiles, file shares and session-host images in our Mumbai cloud, with restore tests.

          CERT-In ready logs

          Access, MFA and gateway logs kept 180 days in India, with incident details ready for the 6-hour reporting window.

          DPDP Act 2023

          Personal data stays in your data centre or ours in India; role-based access and audit trails support your obligations as data fiduciary.

          * Feature availability depends on the Accops edition (Silver, Gold, Platinum) and add-ons, per Accops; session recording is an add-on below Platinum. General information, not legal advice.
