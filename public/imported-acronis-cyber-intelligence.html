      Overview · the reporting problem

      Reports built by hand eat hours and still miss the gaps.

      Your Acronis data already knows which backups failed, which patches are pending and which tickets are open. Turning it into something a client or a board can read is where the time goes.

      Most service teams still export numbers from each console, paste them into spreadsheets or a separate BI tool, build charts, write the commentary and email a different file to every client. It is slow, it is easy to send one client another client's data, and it rarely shows where a customer is under-protected. Indian MSPs and group IT teams that report to many companies or plants feel this every month and every quarter.

      Acronis Cyber Intelligence is the AI-enabled analytics and reporting layer inside Acronis Cyber Console, part of the Acronis Cyber Platform. Per Acronis, it turns service data into dashboards, widgets and client-ready reports, summarises results in plain language and highlights growth opportunities. It is in early access for service providers. XcellHost brings it to Indian teams with the Acronis licences, onboarding, report design and 24×7 support from Mumbai.

      Why it matters in India

      Reports that help you answer regulators, auditors and boards.

      Reporting does not make anyone compliant on its own. Clear, per-client evidence of backup, patching and security status helps you meet what Indian rules expect — the obligations stay with each organisation.

      DPDP Act, 2023

      Per-client reports and role-based access support purpose limitation and "reasonable security safeguards" for personal data you process for clients. Backup and protection status shows those safeguards are in place.

      CERT-In directions

      Dashboards that surface security incidents and failed protection help teams spot reportable incidents early for the 6-hour reporting window. Cyber Intelligence keeps up to 3 months of history in early access, so XcellHost keeps 180-day logs in India through your logging setup.

      RBI & SEBI frameworks

      Banks, NBFCs and regulated entities report backup, patch and incident status to IT committees and boards. Scheduled reports help you meet periodic reporting under the RBI IT governance directions and SEBI CSCRF.
