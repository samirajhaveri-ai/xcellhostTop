    Overview

    Office 365 for people who live in the browser.

    Office 365 E1 is Microsoft's entry enterprise plan. Per Microsoft, it gives every user the web and mobile versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote and Outlook, an Exchange Online Plan 1 mailbox of 50 GB with a 50 GB archive, 1 TB of OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams — but no desktop Office apps.

    That makes it the right licence for reception, accounts assistants, branch staff and anyone whose day is email, a few spreadsheets and Teams. It costs less than half of Office 365 E3, and you can mix both in one tenant. XcellHost bills it in rupees, migrates your mailboxes and supports users 24×7 from Mumbai.

    India compliance

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    Email is evidence. Keep it governed.

    Mailboxes hold invoices, contracts and customer data. E1 gives you audit, retention and eDiscovery (Standard); XcellHost helps you map them to Indian rules.

    • DPDP Act 2023 — retention policies and eDiscovery help you find, export or delete personal data on request.
    • CERT-In — 6-hour incident reporting and 180-day log retention; Purview Audit (Standard) keeps 180 days by default, per Microsoft.
    • RBI · SEBI · IRDAI — regulated entities usually need Conditional Access and DLP too: plan E1 + Entra P1, or E3.
    • India regions — Microsoft runs cloud regions in Pune, Chennai, Mumbai and Hyderabad (since Aug 2026) and offers India data residency for core Microsoft 365 data at rest — confirmed per service.
