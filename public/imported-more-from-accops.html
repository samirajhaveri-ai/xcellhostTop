    Overview

    Beyond desktops and gateways — four tools for the last mile.

    HyWorks, HySecure and HyID cover virtual desktops, zero-trust access and MFA. Accops also makes four products for the harder edges: proving the real person is at the keyboard, managing the phones and tablets your field teams carry, giving vendors a clean workspace on a USB drive instead of a laptop, and keeping risky websites off the endpoint entirely.

    XcellHost scopes, deploys and supports each one alongside your Accops workspace — in our Mumbai cloud or on your own servers. Most customers add one or two to a HySecure or HyID rollout. Each is quoted to your users, devices and edition, with one INR invoice and one 24×7 support number.

    Plug, reboot, work

    Zero-touch, self-service: users plug the drive into their own device and restart. No IT set-up on the PC.

    Hardened Linux

    A customised, read-only Ubuntu Live OS that runs entirely from the USB drive.

    Native Accops client

    Connects to HySecure for application access or HyWorks for virtual apps and desktops — VPN client or VDI terminal.

    Blocks local disk, external USB drives, printing, copy-paste, peripherals and internet from the session.

    Wiped on reboot

    All Live OS data is erased when the device restarts, so the next boot is clean.

    Nano blocks local storage and wipes on reboot; RBI keeps web code off the PC; HyMobile enforces DLP on phones.
