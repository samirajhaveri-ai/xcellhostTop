The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$478B

  • Cyber security: US$69.5B–92.7B in 2025, by two sources34.
  • E-learning: US$133.7B in 2024, growing 8.81% a year to 20335.
  • AI headroom: 19.8% of US businesses used AI in early May 2026, against 37% of firms with 250+ staff8.
Why now
13 dates

Thirteen dated compliance, technology and rule-making milestones fall between January 2027 and April 2030. Each one starts a project. See the trigger calendar.

  • CIRCIA: CISA sent the final incident-reporting rule to the White House on 1 Oct 2026. It sets 72-hour incident and 24-hour ransom-payment reports2324.
  • CCPA regulations took effect on 1 Jan 2026. Automated decision-making duties apply from 1 Jan 2027, cyber-audit certifications from 1 Apr 202817.

    HighScale of competition

    The three largest hyperscalers are American, and thousands of MSPs already serve small firms.

    Mitigation: sell through MSPs, not against them, and lead with overnight SOC, digital trust and AI skills.
    HighFragmented, moving rules

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with US counsel, and refreshes to this page.

    MediumLiability & litigation
