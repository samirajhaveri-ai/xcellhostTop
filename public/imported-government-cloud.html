Government Cloud

Public services, public money, public scrutiny — a cloud built for all three.

Government bodies are moving portals, file systems and scheme databases to cloud, but the rules travel with them. MeitY's GI Cloud (MeghRaj) programme points departments to MeitY-empanelled cloud service offerings, MeitY's contractual guidance expects government data to stay on servers in India, CERT-In sets reporting and logging duties, and GIGW 3.0 asks that every citizen-facing site be accessible and certified safe to host.

XcellCloud Government Cloud puts those expectations into one service. Workloads run in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, DR runs at a partner data centre in India*, and each environment comes with a 24×7 SOC, WAF and DDoS protection, privileged access control, immutable backups and the documents your audit, finance and IT committees will ask for.
