    Overview

    A plant is not an office network with more cables.

    In IT, a security test that slows a laptop is an inconvenience. In a plant, an aggressive scan can freeze a controller, trip a line or confuse a safety system. That is why XcellHost OT Assessment starts by listening: we copy traffic from a mirror port and build your asset inventory without sending anything to a live device.

    From that inventory we draw your zones and conduits the way IEC 62443-3-2 describes them, set a target security level for each zone, compare it with what is actually in place, and rank every gap by its consequence on people, production and the environment — not by a generic score.

    Why it matters now

    Indian plants are connecting faster than they are being segmented.

    Remote monitoring, ERP integration and OEM support links have joined plant networks to the office — and to the internet. Most of the equipment on the other side was designed for a closed world.

    01

    IT/OT convergence

    Dashboards, MES and cloud analytics need plant data. Each new link is a path in, unless it passes through a controlled boundary.

    02

    Systems you cannot patch

    Controllers and HMIs run for 15–25 years. Many can only be updated during a shutdown — some never. Compensating controls matter more than patch lists.

    03

    Vendor and integrator access

    OEMs, system integrators and AMC partners often keep their own remote tools into the plant. Few are logged, time-boxed or protected with MFA.

    04

    Consequences are physical

    A cyber incident in OT can mean a stopped line, a spoiled batch, a safety trip or an environmental release — not just lost files.

    05

    Regulators are asking

    CERT-In directions apply to plants too, power-sector rules call for periodic OT audits, and critical infrastructure falls under NCIIPC guidance.

    Methodology

    Seven phases, passive first, plant-safe throughout.

    Aligned with IEC 62443-3-2 risk assessment and NIST SP 800-82 guidance for OT. Scroll to follow the flow.

    1. 01 · Authorise & plan

      Scope, written authorisation, rules of engagement, site safety induction and shutdown calendar agreed.

    2. 02 · Document review

      Network drawings, asset lists, P&IDs where relevant, remote-access contracts and existing policies.

    3. 03 · Site walk-down

      Escorted visit to control rooms and panels; interviews with operations, maintenance and IT.

    4. 04 · Passive capture

      A collector on a mirror port or tap records traffic for an agreed window. Nothing is sent to live devices.

    5. 05 · Zones & risk

      Zones and conduits drawn, SL-T set per zone, SL-A measured, risks rated by consequence.

    6. 06 · Report & roadmap

      Findings workshop with plant and IT leads; phased roadmap matched to shutdown windows.

    7. 07 · Retest

      After fixes, we re-verify the critical and high findings and update the risk register.

    Plant-safe by designGuard-rails written into every engagement
