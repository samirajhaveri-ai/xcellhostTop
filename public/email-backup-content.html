What Is Email Backup?

Email backup keeps a separate, encrypted copy of every mailbox — emails, attachments, calendars and contacts — outside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, so you can restore a single message, a folder or a whole mailbox after accidental deletion, ransomware, a misconfiguration or an employee leaving. XcellHost Email Backup runs automatically in the background, restores in a few clicks and is managed from one dashboard, billed in INR with a GST invoice.
