Team Folders with roles at every level

Each department or project gets a Team Folder. Members get one of five roles, and sub-folders can be shared more narrowly than the parent, so a payroll folder inside Finance stays with HR and the CFO.

  • Roles: Admin, Organizer, Editor, Commenter, Viewer
  • Sub-folder access narrower than the Team Folder
  • Unlimited version history; check-in and check-out locks
  • Ownership transfer when people move or leave

Share outside the company, on your terms

Admins can switch off external sharing, limit who may create links, force passwords and expiry dates, and keep an allowlist of partner domains. Rules apply to the whole organisation or a single folder.

  • Password and expiry on external links
  • Allowlist of domains and users
  • Guest users for clients and contractors, revocable anytime

Find sensitive files before they leave

WorkDrive's data loss prevention scans files for sensitive content such as card numbers and personal data, using built-in detection, keyword and regex identifiers, and health-record detection. Matches can alert admins, restrict access or quarantine the file. Classification labels can limit sharing, copying, printing and downloads.

  • Automatic and manual classification
  • Regex identifiers we write for PAN, GSTIN and account formats*
  • Admin alerts and quarantine
  • Labels that restrict share, copy, print and download

  • eDiscovery search by user, Team Folder, status and date

Security, compliance & data location

Who protects what. Stated plainly.

WorkDrive runs on Zoho's cloud, so Zoho's controls and certifications cover the platform. XcellHost's certifications cover our own services: setup, migration and support.
