Executive summary

Enter Cambodia through partners, ahead of new data laws

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$2.34B

Cambodia's ICT market in 2025, by Mordor Intelligence, rising to US$3.61B by 20312. That is about . It is a small market, so we size the plan to match.

  • IT services were the largest segment, at 35.48% of 2025 revenue, about US$0.83B by our arithmetic2.
  • Cloud and virtualisation: US$197.3M in 2023, forecast at US$946.3M by 2032 (Astute)3; another estimate gives US$371M by 20274.
  • Cyber security is the fastest-growing ICT product, at a projected 8.24% a year to 20312. We found no reliable Cambodia-only cyber market size.
  • Mobile-first: 18.9 million e-wallet accounts and 1.325 billion Bakong transactions in 20258.
Why now
3 draft laws

Personal data protection, cybersecurity and digital government were all in consultation or review in 2026. Buyers who prepare early need advisers now. See the trigger calendar.

  • Draft PDP law, as released in Jul 2025: a certified DPO for every controller and processor, breach notice, and fines up to 10% of turnover13. Validated on 5 Aug 202614.
  • Banks: the National Bank of Cambodia's new Technology and Cyber Risk Management Guidelines replace the 2019 framework1011.
  • Fraud: the Law on Combating Online Scams has been in force since 6 Apr 2026, with fines for legal entities of KHR 1–30 billion18.
  • 5G went live on 1 Jan 2026 across Phnom Penh and 14 provinces38.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

A Phnom Penh DC partner plus Cambodian SI/MSP resellers first. Register a local company and seek the MPTC cloud-service licence between month 6 and 12.

  • Host bank data in a Phnom Penh partner DC: NBC guidelines restrict international transfers for banks and financial institutions12.
  • For other sectors, no general localisation rule or transfer restriction applies today12. Re-check when the PDP law is adopted.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC, 1.5 hours behind Phnom Penh.

    Pricing & tax approach

    • USD price book for partners and customers, since Cambodia is highly dollarised. The riel averaged KHR 4,011 per US$ in 202543.
    • VAT: 10%27. Business customers reverse-charge VAT on digital services bought from non-residents; B2C sales need VAT registration28.
    • Withholding tax: a final 14% on payments to non-residents27. We found no India–Cambodia tax treaty to reduce it29, so gross up or invoice locally.
    • CIT: 20% for a local company27, with possible QIP exemptions of 3–9 years30. Local invoicing removes the withholding drag.

    Marketing

    • Khmer and English microsite, offer sheets and webinar decks, reviewed by native speakers. No machine-only translation.
    • Partner-hosted events in Phnom Penh, timed to the trigger calendar: draft PDP law steps, NBC guidance and the AI strategy.
    • Content: a draft-PDP readiness checklist (DPO, breach notice), a TCRMG evidence pack and an anti-fraud playbook.
    • LinkedIn and partner co-marketing, plus outreach through India–Cambodia business networks and the Association of Banks in Cambodia's events.

    Sales motions

    • Compliance-led: a fixed-fee NBC TCRMG or draft-PDP readiness assessment that leads into managed services.
    • Follow the customer: Indian firms and regional groups with Cambodian offices and factories.
    • Training-led: AI literacy cohorts aligned to the draft AI strategy's skills priority25, which open AI platform and RAG projects.
    • Co-sell: with partners, plus Microsoft, Google and AWS, where XcellHost runs the managed layer.

    HighLaws still in draft

    The PDP, cybersecurity, cybercrime, ICT and digital-government laws have no adoption dates, and their final texts may differ from the drafts.

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with Cambodian counsel, and refreshes to this page.
    HighSmall market

    The whole ICT market was US$2.34B in 2025, so a few large deals decide the year.

    Mitigation: a lean, partner-led cost base and a regional view with the Vietnam, Malaysia and Indonesia plans.
    MediumBank data must stay local

    NBC rules restrict international transfers for banks and financial institutions.

    Mitigation: Phnom Penh partner DCs for regulated data, with Mumbai limited to tooling and telemetry.
    MediumSlower economy in 2026

    The World Bank cut its 2026 growth forecast to 3.5%, citing a tourism slump and fuel costs.

    Mitigation: lead with cost-saving offers (cloud desktops, backup, managed M365) and with export sectors that are still growing.
    MediumWithholding tax

    14% final withholding on cross-border fees, with no India treaty relief found.

    Mitigation: open the local company by month 12 and take tax advice per contract.
    MediumFraud environment

    Online fraud and identity misuse drove a new criminal law in 2026, and both buyers and providers face more scrutiny.

    Mitigation: strict KYC on partners and customers, and a hardened XcellHost footprint before selling.
    LowerLanguage & talent

    Many buyers prefer Khmer, and skilled security staff are scarce.

    Mitigation: a native country lead, localised content and partner engineers trained by XcellHost.
    LowerLicensing

    Cloud services in our own name likely need an MPTC licence; e-commerce needs Ministry of Commerce authorisation.

    Mitigation: sell through licensed partners until our own licence is granted.
