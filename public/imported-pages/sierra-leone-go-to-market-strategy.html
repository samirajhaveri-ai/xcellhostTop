Executive summary

Enter Sierra Leone through Freetown partners and donor-funded programmes

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunityUS$262.5M

Telecom operator market in 2025, forecast to reach US$360.1M by 2030 (6.53% a year)23. That is about ₹2,498 crore.

  • World Bank digital financing: a US$50M national project14 and a share of a US$137M regional programme16.
  • A US$45M second submarine cable, announced November 202618.
  • 8.92M mobile connections1; about 25% of people use the internet3.
  • Cloud, cyber security and AI: no reliable country-level market estimate found.
Why now5 changes

Five tax, cyber and infrastructure changes landed between January and November 2026. Two are still drafts. The data protection law has not been enacted.

  • Critical-infrastructure protection plans drafted for four sectors, 26 Aug 202612.
  • Review of the 2021 cyber Act tendered 14 Aug 202613.
  • Finance Act 2026: GST representative for foreign digital suppliers from 1 Jan 202625.
  • Second submarine cable consortium announced Nov 202618.
  • World Bank regional programme approved 10 Mar 202616.
Recommended entry modelPartner-led

Freetown SI/MSP resellers and one operator partner first. Decide on a local entity or JV at month 12.

  • Bid for donor-funded work with local prime contractors15.
  • Host in-country where a suitable facility exists; we did not identify a carrier-neutral data centre.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC.

Key bodies: the National Cybersecurity Coordination Centre (NC3), the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation, the National Communications Authority (NatCA), the Bank of Sierra Leone and the National Revenue Authority.

Risks & mitigations

HIGH

Small market

Only about a quarter of people use the internet3, and the telecom market is US$262.5M in total23.

Mitigation: keep costs variable: partners first, no local hires before month 12.

HIGH

Public finances

Debt is at high risk of distress and reserves cover about two months of imports4 6.

Mitigation: donor-funded and private work first; prepaid or milestone terms.

HIGH

Single cable

One submarine cable serves the country, and route diversity is rated poor19 24.

Mitigation: offline-tolerant backup, satellite fallback and local caching in designs.

MEDIUM

Hosting capacity

We did not identify a carrier-neutral data centre; national data centres are planned22.

Mitigation: verify operator facilities on site; use regional hosting where the customer allows.

MEDIUM

Regulatory flux

The data protection bill is not enacted and the cyber Act is under review11 13.

Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with local counsel, and refreshes to this page.

MEDIUM

Currency and inflation

The IMF expects inflation of 11.6% by year-end and wants the exchange rate to adjust to shocks5 4.

Mitigation: USD price book, prepaid or quarterly billing, partner-held leone exposure.

MEDIUM

Unverified rules

Banking, e-signature and withholding-tax rules were not verified for this briefing.

Mitigation: a local legal memo before the first regulated contract.

LOWER

Tax leakage

15% GST with a local representative25 27 can stack with withholding on direct invoices.

Mitigation: partner-billed sales; tax adviser review before the first invoice.
