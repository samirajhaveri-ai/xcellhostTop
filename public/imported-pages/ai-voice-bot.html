Why it matters in India

Security & Compliance — AI Voice Bot

Voice is still how most customers here prefer to enquire. A bot that talks to them has to respect both their language and the law.

Speaks The Way Customers Do

English, Hindi and major regional languages, including the everyday mix of Hindi and English in one sentence.

DPDP Act Ready Practices

Clear notice that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant, consent for recording, purpose-limited data and deletion on request.

Outbound Within TRAI Rules

Campaigns are planned around TRAI's commercial-communication framework: consent records, DND preferences and permitted calling hours.

Data Kept In India

Hosting options in Indian data centres for recordings, transcripts and lead data, for sectors that need local storage.
