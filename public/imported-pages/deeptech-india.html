The sector in 2026

Science-led companies, patient money. And now a legal definition.

Deeptech startups build on new science or engineering rather than a new business model. In India they are growing faster than tech startups overall, AI dominates the count, and since February 2026 DPIIT recognises them as a separate category with a longer runway.

What "deep tech startup" now means in law

Startup (general)10 yrsfrom incorporation₹200 crturnover ceiling
Deep tech startup20 yrsfrom incorporation₹300 crturnover ceiling
  • Builds on new scientific or engineering knowledge
  • Spends a high share on R&D
  • Owns or is creating significant novel IP, with a plan to commercialise it
  • Extra documents are filed to prove the criteria

Policies & funds

The money is mostly patient. The rules are mostly new.

Status as checked on official sources in November 2026. Amounts are scheme outlays, not grants to any one company.

Research, Development & Innovation (RDI) Fund

₹1 lakh crore

Approved by Cabinet on 1 July 2025, launched on 3 November 2025. Long-term, low- or nil-interest money flows through a Special Purpose Fund under ANRF to second-level fund managers, then to companies at TRL 4 and above. On 8 October 2026 the first 19 fund managers (14 AIFs, 5 Focused Research Organisations) were approved, with ₹7,131 cr committed. Budget 2026-27 provided ₹20,000 cr.

₹10,000 crore

Approved 14 February 2026. Invests through SEBI-registered AIFs, with priority for deep tech and tech-driven manufacturing, early-growth founders and smaller domestic funds. FFS 1.0 committed its full ₹10,000 cr to 145 AIFs, which invested ₹25,500 cr+ in 1,370+ startups.

DPIIT recognition

2 lakh+ startups

The February 2026 notification raised the general turnover ceiling to ₹200 cr and created a deep tech startup category: up to 20 years from incorporation and ₹300 cr turnover. Co-operative societies are now eligible too.

Startup tax holiday · 80-IAC → section 140

3 of 10 years

Eligible recognised startups can deduct 100% of profits for three consecutive years out of their first ten, after Inter-Ministerial Board certification. Budget 2025 extended eligibility to companies incorporated before 1 April 2030. From 1 April 2026 the benefit sits in section 140 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 — confirm with your CA.

    FAQ

    Deeptech in India, answered.

    More than 4,200, including over 550 founded in 2025, according to the NASSCOM–Zinnov tech start-up report released on 25 February 2026. They raised $2.3 billion in 2025, up 37% on 2024; AI-led companies were 84% of the count and 91% of the funding.

    Since the February 2026 DPIIT notification, a deep tech startup builds on new scientific or engineering knowledge, spends heavily on R&D and owns or is creating significant novel IP. It can stay recognised for up to 20 years from incorporation with turnover up to ₹300 crore, against 10 years and ₹200 crore for other startups.

    The Research, Development and Innovation Fund was approved in July 2025 and launched in November 2025. Money flows from a Special Purpose Fund under ANRF to second-level fund managers — 19 were approved on 8 October 2026 with ₹7,131 crore committed — who then invest in companies working at TRL 4 and above. Startups approach those fund managers rather than the government.

    Not as far as we could find. A draft was released for consultation by the Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser on 31 July 2023, and we found no final notified policy as of November 2026. The DPIIT deep tech startup category created in February 2026 is in force.

    The IndiaAI Mission offers subsidised GPU access — 38,000+ GPUs at about ₹65 per GPU-hour on average, per PIB in February 2026 — through empanelled providers and an application process. XcellHost GPU servers are a separate commercial option, hosted in India and priced on quote, for teams that need dedicated capacity, specific configurations or a long-term reservation.

    GPU pricing depends on the model, count, interconnect, storage, term and hosting, and supply changes month to month. We quote each configuration in INR with GST. Use the GPU-hours planner to arrive with a sized request and the quote is faster.

    Encrypt them with keys held in a hardware security module, restrict who can decrypt, sign every release so tampering is detectable, and log access. Our Cloud HSM partitions, code signing service and DSPM cover those steps; a PQC roadmap workshop prepares long-lived IP for quantum-era cryptography.

    They can. India controls dual-use goods, software and technology through the SCOMET list administered by DGFT, and imported chips, EDA tools or controlled technical data can also carry the exporting country's rules. Get a classification from qualified counsel; we help with the technical side — region locks, access controls and audit logs.

    If you process digital personal data of people in India — patients, trial participants, drivers, customers or employees — yes. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and the core duties apply from 13 May 2027. CERT-In's 6-hour incident reporting and 180-day log retention already apply.

    Eligible DPIIT-recognised startups certified by the Inter-Ministerial Board can deduct 100% of profits for any three consecutive years out of their first ten. Budget 2025 extended eligibility to companies incorporated before 1 April 2030. From 1 April 2026 the deduction sits in section 140 of the Income-tax Act, 2025; confirm the details with your chartered accountant.
