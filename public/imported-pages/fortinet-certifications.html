Overview & levels

The NSE ladder after 15 July 2026.

NSE 1 to NSE 4 are shared by everyone. NSE 5, 6 and 7 branch into four tracks — Secure Networking, SASE, Cloud Security and Security Operations. NSE 8 is a practical expert exam. MSSP Security and OT Security sit outside the ladder as Industry certifications. Click any block to filter the catalogue.

FORTINETTraining Institute

Plain-text name, not the Fortinet logo. Vendor names and marks belong to their owners; official digital badges are issued by Fortinet via Credly.

  • Two years. Every Fortinet certification expires two years after you earn it.
  • Pearson VUE. NSE 4–7 and Industry exams run at Pearson test centres or online with OnVUE. NSE 8 electives can also be proctored remotely through ProctorU.
  • Free to learn. Self-paced course content is free; on-demand labs and instructor-led classes are paid.
  • Credly badges. Fortinet now issues its badges through Credly. Training also earns ISC2 CPE credit (one per hour).

What happened to FCF, FCA, FCP, FCSS and FCX?

On 15 July 2026 Fortinet mapped active certifications to the new levels automatically. The old names stay in your certification history; no new FCF–FCX certificates are issued.

How renewal works

Only active certifications renew. The recertification assessment is a later version of the proctored exam, offered when the exam is under two years old.

NSE 8 points: 50 per NSE 4–6 exam, 75 per NSE 7 or Industry exam, 200 per NSE 8 practical exam, plus capped points for training, beta tests and exam content work. A passed NSE 8 practical renews all active NSE 1–7 certifications.

Partner programme

Fortinet Engage tiers and the certified staff they need.

The Fortinet Engage Partner Program has four tiers — Advocate, Select, Advanced and Expert — across three business models: Integrator, MSSP and Marketplace. Each tier asks for a number of certified individuals; specializations add sales training plus a specialist exam.

    Specialization benefits at Select, Advanced and Expert: specialization badge, Partner Locator listing and a discounted NFR kit. The brochure names exams from the FCSS era; Fortinet has not yet published the NSE-era version on a public page.

    Engage Preferred Services Partner

    An accreditation for partners at Expert level with elite certification, service delivery and support. Fortinet lists the benefits as exclusive accreditation, advanced training and close collaboration; the extra requirements are in the Partner Portal, not on a public page.

    FAQ

    Fortinet certification, answered.

    Since 15 July 2026 the NSE Certification Program has eight levels. NSE 1 to NSE 4 are shared; NSE 5 to NSE 7 split into four tracks — Secure Networking, SASE, Cloud Security and Security Operations; NSE 8 is the expert level. MSSP Security and OT Security are separate Industry certifications.

    Fortinet mapped active certifications automatically on 15 July 2026. FCF became NSE 1 and 2, FCA became NSE 3, FCP became NSE 4 plus NSE 5 or 6, FCSS became NSE 6 or 7, and FCX became NSE 8, with the same issue and expiry dates. The old names stay in your certification history.

    Two years from the date you earn it. NSE 4 to 7 and Industry certifications renew with a recertification assessment or by passing the proctored exam again. NSE 8 renews with 200 activity points, and a passed NSE 8 practical exam renews all your active NSE 1 to 7 certifications.

    From 2 November 2026 Fortinet lists, in US dollars excluding tax: NSE 4 to 6 exams and recertification assessments US$200; NSE 7 exams and assessments US$400; Industry exams US$400; each NSE 8 practical exam US$800; the NSE 8 recertification exam US$400. NSE 1 and 2 are free online courses. Ask the channel team about partner vouchers before paying.

    Fortinet says its self-paced course content is free, including the lectures. On-demand lab access and instructor-led classes cost extra. Fortinet training also earns ISC2 CPE credit, one credit per hour.

    NSE 4 to 7 and Industry exams are proctored by Pearson, at a test centre or online with OnVUE. The NSE 4 FortiOS 7.6 exam has 50–55 multiple-choice and drag-and-drop questions in 100 minutes, with no partial credit. After a fail you wait 15 days. The NSE 8 Core practical is on-site only; the three NSE 8 electives can be on-site or remote through ProctorU.

    NSE 7 needs NSE 4 plus NSE 5 or NSE 6 in the same track. NSE 8 needs NSE 4, NSE 5 or 6, and NSE 7 in the same track; then you pass the NSE 8 Core practical and one elective — Secure Networking, Application Security or Security Operations — within one year.

    Yes. Each Engage tier — Advocate, Select, Advanced, Expert — needs a number of certified individuals, and specializations need sales training plus a specialist exam. XcellHost's own tier is being confirmed and is not claimed here.
