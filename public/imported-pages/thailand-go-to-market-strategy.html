Why now
3 moving files

PDPA enforcement, the draft Cybersecurity Act amendments and the draft AI Act all moved in 2025–2026. Each one starts compliance projects. See the trigger calendar.

  • The PDPC announced eight fines of about THB 14.5 million on 1 Aug 2025, for weak security, unreported breaches and missing DPOs21. It logged 2,672 complaints by Jan 202624.
  • Draft Cybersecurity Act amendments (Jul 2025) would bring cloud and data-centre providers serving critical infrastructure into scope, with a 24-hour first report25.
  • ETDA published a draft AI Act on 9 Jul 2026, with a risk-based model and possible data-localisation terms for government and CII vendors31.
  • A general election was held on 8 Feb 202642, and Parliament re-elected the prime minister in March43. We take no political position.
