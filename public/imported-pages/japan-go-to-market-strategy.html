Executive summary

Enter Japan through Japanese partners, data in Tokyo

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$40.8B

Public cloud plus cyber security spend in Japan, 2025: ¥6.44 trillion in total, from IDC Japan and Yano Research14. That is about . The two overlap, for example security sold as SaaS, so treat it as an upper bound.

  • Public cloud: ¥4.49 trillion in 2025, up 20.3%. IDC Japan expects ¥10.1 trillion by 2030, a 17.6% CAGR1.
  • Cyber security: ¥1.95 trillion in FY2025, up 9.2%4. Security products alone were ¥743 billion, and about 60% of that went to foreign vendors3.
  • AI: 86.4% of surveyed companies use generative AI in at least one task10, yet close to 30% have no organised approach to it11.
  • Ransomware: 226 cases reported to police in 2025. About 60% hit SMEs, and over half of victims spent ¥10 million or more to recover9.
Why now
17 Jan 2027

Tougher APPI penalties apply, 65 days from now. The new supplier security star rating is due to launch by March 2027. See the trigger calendar.

  • Active cyber defence: since 1 Oct 2026, designated critical-infrastructure operators notify key assets and report incidents, with a detailed report generally within 30 days22.
  • APPI amendment: passed on 10 Jul 2026. It adds administrative surcharges, under-16 consent rules and a consent exception for statistics and AI training. Main reforms apply by 17 Jul 202818.
  • Supplier star rating (SCS): METI plans to launch ★3 and ★4 levels by the end of FY2026. Buyers will set the level they require from each supplier26.
  • Government cloud: about 1,800 local governments had to move 20 core systems by March 2026. 29.1% of systems got more time17.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

Japanese SIer and MSP partners first, with hosting in a Tokyo partner DC. Open a Japanese company (GK or KK) between month 9 and 15.

  • Host customer data in Japan, in XcellHost's Tokyo partner DC (availability is confirmed at order) or a partner's racks.
  • Lead with the India corridor: 1,434 Japanese companies are registered in India12, and Japan aims for ¥10 trillion of private investment in India over a decade13.
  • Run tooling and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC. India is not on the APPI's list of equivalent countries, so transfers and remote access need consent or contractual measures21.
