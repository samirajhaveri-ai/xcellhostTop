Overview & levels

Two academies, one ladder for partners.

The Partner Academy runs from Intro to Druva, through foundational sales and technical certifications, to sales specialist and technical specialist certifications by solution area — with a parallel MSP track. The Druva Academy teaches admins the product, with Level 1 to 3 courses and foundation badges. Click any block to filter the catalogue.

DRUVAPartner Academy · Academy

Plain-text name, not the Druva logo. Vendor names and marks belong to their owners; official certificates and digital badges are issued by Druva (Druva Cloud Platform Foundations via Credly).

  • 2 or 3 years. Druva asks for sales certifications to be renewed every two years and technical ones every three, at minimum.
  • Free. The Partner Academy is free through the Druva Partner Portal. Druva also rewards completions with gift cards and apparel, by region.
  • Short. Specialist courses run 15 to 72 minutes, self-paced, each ending in a short exam from a question pool.
  • Certificates. Download from My Profile or the completion email and add them to LinkedIn.

Partner programme

Druva Partner+ tiers and the accredited staff they need.

The Druva Partner+ Program for resellers has three tiers — Authorized, Certified and Elite — in Druva's Partner+ guide (Q4 2023 edition). Each tier asks for accredited sales and technical staff, alongside revenue and business-plan requirements. MSPs have a separate MSP programme.

    FAQ

    Druva certification, answered.

    Twenty Partner Academy certifications: foundational (Sales Professional, Sales Plays, Technical Associate), sales specialist by solution area (data, cyber, AI and identity resilience), technical specialist, and an MSP set including the Managed Services Center certifications. Admins also earn Druva Academy badges such as Druva Cloud Platform Foundations.

    Not that we found. Each Partner Academy certification ends in a short online exam drawn from a question pool — the Technical Associate exam has 30 questions. Druva does not publish a passing score.

    Druva asks for sales certifications to be renewed every two years and technical certifications every three years, as a minimum. It updates the training regularly and recommends revisiting it in between.

    The Partner Academy is free through the Druva Partner Portal. The Druva Cloud Platform Foundations badge is free on Credly. Druva Academy course pages show no prices.

    With Intro to Druva, a 23-minute course that the FAQ calls the prerequisite for the rest of the curriculum. MSP staff take Intro to Druva – MSP. Then follow the suggested path for your role on this page.
