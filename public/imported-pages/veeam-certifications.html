Overview & levels

The Veeam track after the 2026 reset.

Four layers: free learning for everyone, partner accreditations (VMSP and VMTSP) for sales and pre-sales, University Pro courses that lead to the VMCE+ exam, and the expert step — VMCSE, with an invite-only architect review reported above it. Click any block to filter the catalogue.

VEEAMUniversity · Certification

Plain-text name, not the Veeam logo. Vendor names and marks belong to their owners; official certificates and digital badges are issued by Veeam (VMCA and VMCE badges via Credly).

  • No expiry. Veeam says its certifications do not expire — but each is tied to a version. When an exam retires, you retrain for the latest one.
  • Pearson. Book through your veeam.com account, then Pearson — at a test centre or with online proctoring from home or the office.
  • Training first. The official courses are a hard requirement for the VMCE+ and VMCSE exams. They come with a Veeam University Pro subscription.
  • Partners. ProPartners may need current certifications to stay compliant; free University Free certificates do not count for that.

Partner programme

Veeam ProPartner levels and the certified staff they need.

Resellers in the Veeam ProPartner programme (Value-Added Reseller, VVAR) sit at four metal levels — Registered, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Veeam says Gold and Platinum need certifications from the Pro programme. Service providers join the Veeam Cloud & Service Provider (VCSP) programme; both earn competencies with certified staff.

Competencies and what they ask for

    Rules from the Veeam competency blog (published 12 April 2024, updated 11 September 2024), written before VMCE+. How VMCE+ and VMCSE count towards competencies: confirm in the ProPartner portal.

    Level benefits (veeam.com)

    Registered: portal access, standard discounts, deal registration. Silver: higher deal-registration discount, NFR and internal-use keys, Marketing Center. Gold: assigned Veeam contact, MDF, partner-finder listing, performance rebate. Platinum: invitation only, dedicated account team, business planning, higher rebates. Exact certification counts per level are in the ProPartner programme guide, not on a public page.
