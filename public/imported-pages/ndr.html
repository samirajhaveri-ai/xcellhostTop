What it is

Logs can be wiped. Agents can be killed. Packets still have to cross the wire.

Network detection and response watches a copy of your traffic, learns what normal looks like for each segment, and flags the movement attackers can't avoid: scanning, hopping between servers, calling home and moving data out.

SIEM

Sees what systems log. Blind where logging is off, tampered or never existed.

EDR

Encrypted traffic analytics

Most traffic is encrypted. Its shape still gives malware away.

We don't break TLS by default. Decryption adds risk, cost and privacy duties; behaviour and fingerprints catch most of what matters.

TLS 1.3 session · samplepayload unreadable
Client fingerprint (JA4)t13d1715h2_sample_a1rare in your estate
Server certificateself-signed · 3 days oldsuspicious
Server name (SNI)cdn.example.netvisible today
Packet sizes212 B out · 96 B in, every timeuniform
Timingevery 60 s ± 4 s · 24 hbeacon
Verdict: likely command-and-control beacon — escalated to SOC
JA3 → JA4+

JA3 (2017) hashed the TLS ClientHello. JA4+, published by FoxIO in 2023, is a family of readable fingerprints for TLS, QUIC, HTTP, SSH and certificates that is harder to evade by shuffling extensions.

Encrypted Client Hello (RFC 9849)

Published by the IETF in March 2026, ECH hides the server name from on-path observers. As adoption grows, SNI-based rules weaken — timing, size, fingerprint and DNS signals carry more of the load.

Decryption, only where it's justified

Where policy allows, inspection runs on your firewall or a dedicated decryption point — never on our sensors — with exclusions for banking, health and personal categories under your DPDP notice.

Tool · beacon spotter

Malware phones home on a timer. Find the timer.

One hour of synthetic outbound connections from six pseudonymised hosts. For each host-destination pair we measure how regular the gaps are (coefficient of variation). Very regular plus repeated plus not allow-listed = likely beacon.
