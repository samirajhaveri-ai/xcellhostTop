Executive summary

Enter Brazil through São Paulo partners and the LGPD wave

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$3.68B

Brazil's cyber security market in 2025 (Mordor)1, about . We found no reliable Brazilian cloud or AI services estimate that we could open, so TAM uses cyber security only. Brazil also holds about half of Latin America's data-centre capacity3.

  • Cyber security: US$3.68B in 2025, US$6.57B by 2031 (10.13% CAGR). BFSI buys 26.25%; cloud deployment grows fastest, at 17.25%1.
  • Reach: 185M internet users, 86.9% of the population, and 150M social media identities2.
  • Payments: nearly 80 billion Pix transactions in 2025, up 25.7%, moving over R$35 trillion6.
  • Data centres: 706 MW operating and 660 MW under construction, 56% pre-committed3.
Why now
4 shifts

A stronger regulator, binding transfer clauses, a new data-centre tax regime and a seven-year tax transition. See the trigger calendar.

  • The ANPD became an agency with financial autonomy under Law 15,352 of 25 Feb 202610.
  • ANPD standard clauses became mandatory for transfers relying on them after 23 Aug 202511.
  • Redata (Law 15,504, 15 Sep 2026) suspends PIS/COFINS, IPI and import duty on data-centre equipment17.
  • CBS replaces PIS/COFINS from 1 Jan 2027; IBS replaces ICMS and ISS by 203318.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

Start with a São Paulo DC partner* and Brazilian SI and MSP resellers*. Open a Brazilian Ltda between month 6 and 12.

  • Host customer data in a Brazilian partner DC* in São Paulo or Campinas, so hosting itself is not an international transfer under the LGPD.
  • Invoicing services from India stacks IRRF 15%, CIDE 10%, PIS/COFINS 9.25% and ISS 2–5%. The treaty caps technical fees at 10%201922. A local Ltda changes that maths.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC, under ANPD standard clauses for any access to personal data11.
