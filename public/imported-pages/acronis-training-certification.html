Partner programme tie-in

How certified staff count toward an Acronis partner tier.

The Acronis Partner Program for service providers has four tiers — Registered, Authorized, Gold and Platinum — reviewed every year. Acronis says Academy certifications are essential for staying compliant with the programme. Its partner data sheet (November 2024) sets a certificate count per tier.

FAQ

Acronis training, answered.

Credly lists every Acronis Academy badge on this page as free. Partners take the courses in the Acronis Partner Portal (training.acronis.com) with their partner login. The acronis.com/academy page still mentions free training “until 31-Dec-2023”, so confirm with the channel team if a course ever asks for payment.

Acronis Academy has a Sales track and a Technical track, each with three levels: Fundamentals, Associate and Professional. Credly labels them Foundational, Intermediate and Advanced. There is no Expert level. The Essentials programme for the Acronis Cyber Platform and MSP Academy badges are Foundational.

Acronis says its Sales and Technical certifications are valid for 12 months, so you recertify every year. Credly also lists short recertification courses, such as Cloud Sales Associate Security + XDR. Acronis does not state a validity period for Essentials or MSP Academy badges, so the tracker leaves their expiry blank unless you enter one.

Most badges need you to complete the course in the Acronis Partner Portal and score 70% or more on the assessment; some are a 70% exam on its own. Acronis offers self-guided online courses, live instructor-led classes, hands-on labs, e-labs and boot camps, in eight languages. Essentials takes 4–5 hours in total; MSP Academy modules take 3–7 minutes each.

Start with Essentials – Intro to Acronis Cyber Platform, then the Fundamentals course for your track: Cloud Sales Fundamentals for sales roles, Cloud Tech Fundamentals for technical roles. After that, follow your role path. The paths on this page are XcellHost suggestions, not Acronis requirements.

Yes. Acronis says Academy certifications are essential for staying compliant with its Partner Program. Its November 2024 data sheet asks for 2 certificates at Registered, 4 at Authorized, 8 (including 2 certified individuals) at Gold and 16 (including 4 certified individuals) at Platinum. XcellHost's own tier is being confirmed and is not claimed here.
