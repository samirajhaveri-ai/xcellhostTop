    Overview

    Your origin stays in Mumbai — your content travels to the visitor.

    A content delivery network keeps copies of what you publish at edge locations close to your audience, so each request travels a short hop instead of the full distance to your server.

    XcellCloud CDN sits in front of your website, app, API or media library. The first visitor in a region pulls a file from your origin; every visitor after that gets it from the nearest edge in milliseconds. Your server handles a fraction of the requests it used to, pages render faster on mobile networks, and a sale-day or results-day spike lands on the edge instead of your database.

    You keep control of what is cached and for how long, purge a new release everywhere in seconds, and see hits, misses, bandwidth and errors live. XcellHost plans the cache rules with you, moves your DNS with a planned cut-over, and supports it 24×7 from our Mumbai NOC & SOC — on one INR invoice with GST.

    How it works

    One DNS change, five hops that get shorter every day.

    Your hostname points to the CDN. From then on the edge decides: serve from cache, or fetch once through the shield and keep a copy.

    1. 01

      Point a CNAME

      cdn.yourbrand.in (or www) points to your XcellCloud CDN hostname. Nothing changes on your server.

    2. 02

      Nearest edge answers

      Anycast and DNS steering send each visitor to a nearby edge at a partner data centre*.

    3. 03

      HIT — served at once

      If the edge has a fresh copy, it answers immediately, over TLS and HTTP/3.

    4. 04

      MISS — one shield fetch

      The edge asks the origin shield; only the shield ever goes back to your origin.

    5. 05

      Cached on the way back

      The response is stored per your TTL rules until it expires or you purge it.

    Security & India compliance

    Fast at the edge, careful with data — built for Indian rules.

    A CDN touches every request, so it is part of your compliance story. Here is how XcellCloud CDN helps you meet the rules that apply in India.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Cache rules keep personal data out of the cache: logged-in pages, carts, forms and account APIs bypass the edge by default. Logs can mask IP addresses, and access is role-based.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Incidents must be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for 180 days. We retain CDN access logs for 180 days* and our 24×7 SOC helps you prepare the report.

    RBI · SEBI · IRDAI data localisation

    Where regulated data must stay in India, keep the origin in Mumbai, bypass the cache for that data and pin cacheable content to India edges*.

    MeitY-empanelled provider

    XcellHost is MeitY empanelled, ISO 27001 / 27017 / 27018 certified and SOC 2 Type I & II attested — evidence your auditors can ask for.

    Encryption & access

    TLS 1.2/1.3 to the visitor and to your origin, signed URLs for paid content, origin locked to CDN addresses only.
