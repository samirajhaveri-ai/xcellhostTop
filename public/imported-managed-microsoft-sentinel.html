Overview

Microsoft's cloud SIEM and SOAR — run for you by people who watch it all night.

Microsoft Sentinel is Microsoft's cloud-native SIEM and SOAR on Azure. It collects security data at cloud scale through data connectors, detects threats with KQL analytics rules, Microsoft threat intelligence, UEBA and Fusion multistage detections, lets analysts investigate incidents on an entity graph, and responds through Logic Apps playbooks and automation rules. Microsoft now runs it inside the Defender portal alongside Defender XDR.

The tool is only half the job. Someone has to connect the right sources, write and tune the rules, read the incidents at 3 a.m., approve the playbook and file the report. XcellHost Managed Sentinel does all of it from our 24×7 SOC in Mumbai — in your own Azure tenant, in Azure India regions, with Azure consumption billed at actuals in rupees and our management fee on one GST invoice.

How managed Sentinel works

From raw logs to a closed incident — scroll through one day in our SOC.

Six stages. The same 30 pieces of security data move from scattered logs to rules, an entity graph, a playbook, a hunt and a monthly report.

  1. 01Always onCollect

    Data connectors stream Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Defender XDR, Azure, AWS, firewalls and on-prem servers into one Sentinel workspace in your tenant. We decide which tables go to the analytics tier and which to the low-cost data lake.

    Connectors · table tiering
  2. 02Every few minutesDetect

    Analytics rules written in KQL, Microsoft threat intelligence, UEBA and Fusion multistage detections run against your data. Our use-case library is mapped to MITRE ATT&CK and tuned to what is normal for you.

    MITRE-mapped use cases
  3. 03Minutes after an alertInvestigate

    Related alerts are grouped into one incident. An analyst walks the entity graph — user, device, IP, mailbox, file — to decide what is real, how far it went and what to do next.

    Incident triage · entity graph
  4. 04Pre-approved actionsRespond

    Playbooks on Logic Apps and automation rules disable users, revoke sessions, isolate devices, block IPs and open tickets — automatically for agreed cases, after an analyst approves for the rest.

    SOAR playbooks · automation rules
  5. 05Weekly or monthlyHunt

    Analysts search for what rules have not caught yet: rare processes, unusual sign-in patterns, dormant accounts waking up. Good hunts become new analytics rules.

    Threat hunting · notebooks
  6. 06Monthly · quarterlyReport & improve

    A monthly report and quarterly review show incidents, response times, MITRE coverage, false-positive trend and Sentinel cost — with the tuning and new use cases planned for next month.

    Monthly report · QBR

    Built for Indian rules

    The clocks that start when something goes wrong — already wired into your SOC.

    Indian regulators set short deadlines after an incident. Sentinel holds the evidence; our SOC makes sure the clock is met.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Report specified cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them, and keep ICT logs for a rolling 180 days within India. Our runbook starts the clock at detection.

    DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025

    A personal-data breach must be intimated to the Data Protection Board and affected people without delay, with a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours. We supply the timeline and evidence.

    RBI cyber-security framework

    Banks, NBFCs and payment players are expected to run continuous security monitoring with a SOC. Managed Sentinel gives you that capability with documented coverage.

    SEBI CSCRF & IRDAI guidelines

    SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework expects regulated entities to have SOC monitoring; IRDAI's guidelines set similar expectations for insurers. We map evidence to them.
