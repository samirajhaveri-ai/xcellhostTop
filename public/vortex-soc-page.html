    What XcellSOC does

    Five consoles tell five stories. XcellSOC tells one.

    XcellSOC is the security operations suite at the centre of the Cyberstanc platform. It collects logs, events and telemetry from your endpoints, cloud, network, identity and applications into a single data lake with one schema.

    Detection rules, behavioural baselining and threat intelligence then run across all of it, so hundreds of raw alerts become a handful of scored, evidenced cases — each with an owner, a timeline and a response already under way.
