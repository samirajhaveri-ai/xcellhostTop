    Overview

    Backup gets your data back. DR gets your business back.

    Most small businesses have a backup — a USB drive, a NAS, maybe a cloud copy. When the office server fails, that backup still has to be restored onto new hardware, reinstalled and tested. That takes days, and in that time billing, GST filings, dispatch and payroll stop.

    Disaster recovery keeps a ready-to-run copy of your servers in a second location. XcellCloud DR replicates your servers, VMs, Tally and ERP systems and file servers to our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, or a partner DR site in India*. When something goes wrong, your systems start there in minutes, your team connects over VPN or remote desktop, and our engineers bring everything back to the office afterwards.

      RPO / RTO tiers

      How much data can you lose, and for how long can you stop?

      RPO (recovery point objective) is the most data you can afford to lose, measured in time. RTO (recovery time objective) is how long you can be down. Tighter targets need more ready capacity — and cost more.

      Ransomware recovery

      Replication copies the damage too. Immutable copies do not.

      If ransomware encrypts your server, replication faithfully sends encrypted files to the DR site. That is why every plan keeps point-in-time, immutable copies with separate credentials — so there is always a clean point to fail over to.

      1. T + 0Encryption startsA user opens a malicious attachment; files on the file server begin to change.
      2. T + minutesDetected & isolatedEndpoint protection or our SOC flags it; the office network and replication are paused.
      3. T + 15 min*Clean point chosenWe scan recovery points and pick the last copy before the attack — locked, so it was never touched.
      4. T + 30–60 min*FailoverServers start at the Mumbai DR site from that clean point; staff reconnect over VPN.
      5. Days 1–3*Clean-up & root causeOffice machines rebuilt, entry point closed, evidence kept for CERT-In reporting.
      6. When safeFailbackChanges made at DR are synced back; the office becomes primary again.
      Why the copy survives
      • Object Lock / immutabilityCopies cannot be changed or deleted until their retention date — not even by an admin.
      • Separate credentialsThe DR vault uses different accounts and MFA from your office domain.
      • Many recovery pointsHourly and daily points kept for weeks, so a slow, hidden attack can still be rolled back.
      • Scanned before restoreRecovery points are checked for malware before they go live.

      Daily · IndiaCompanies Act · books of account

      Rule 3(5) of the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014 has required a daily backup of electronic books on servers physically in India since the August 2022 amendment (G.S.R. 624(E)). Rule 46(8) of the Income-tax Rules 2026 adds a similar daily India backup. See Rule 46(8) daily backup.

      6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions

      CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs to be kept for 180 days within India. Replication, failover and restore logs stay in Mumbai, timestamped for your report.

      DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

      Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules lists reasonable security safeguards, including measures for continued processing if data is compromised or lost, "such as by way of data-backups", and one-year retention of logs. Replicas stay in India. See our vDPO service.

      RBISmall NBFCs

      RBI's IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Directions (7 Nov 2023, in force 1 Apr 2024) ask covered entities for a DR plan, defined RTO / RPO and DR drills at least half-yearly for critical systems. They cover NBFCs in the middle layer and above; base-layer NBFCs often adopt them as good practice.

      SEBIStock brokers & DPs

      SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF, 20 Aug 2024; timelines extended 28 Mar 2025; FAQs 11 Jun 2025) expects tested backups and DR. Its FAQs describe RTO of 2 hours and RPO of 15 minutes for critical systems — our warm and hot tiers are designed to those figures*.
