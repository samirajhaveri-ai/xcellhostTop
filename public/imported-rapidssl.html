Overview

The padlock, without the paperwork. Issued in minutes.

RapidSSL is DigiCert's budget-friendly certificate brand for domain validation (DV). There are no company documents to submit: you prove you control the domain — by approving an email, adding one DNS record or uploading one small file — and the certificate is issued automatically, usually within minutes. It chains to DigiCert's long-established roots, so browsers show the padlock instead of a "Not secure" warning.

Choose Standard for one website (it covers both www and the bare domain) or Wildcard to secure every first-level subdomain with one certificate. XcellHost generates the CSR, installs the certificate on your server, reminds you before every renewal and bills it in rupees with GST — so a ₹1,299 certificate never turns into a weekend outage.
