Overview

Shared RPC providers are a fine place to start — until your dApp, indexer or trading bot starts hitting rate limits, 429 errors, latency spikes and compute-unit bills that grow with every call. XcellNode gives you a private RPC endpoint on bare-metal hardware reserved only for you in XcellHost's Mumbai data centre. You choose the chain, the node type — full, archive or validator — and the region; we provision the server, sync it from a recent snapshot and hand over HTTPS and WSS endpoints locked to your IP allow-list. Requests are not metered: you pay one flat monthly price per node in INR, with a GST invoice. Run it yourself with full root access, or add managed operations and let our 24×7 NOC handle client upgrades, monitoring and failover.
