What is a General Compute instance?

A General Compute instance is a general-purpose cloud server that balances processor and memory — 4 GB of RAM for every vCPU — so most business software runs well without tuning. XcellHost runs General Compute on KVM in Indian data centres with NVMe storage, a dedicated public IPv4, hourly, monthly or term billing in rupees and 24×7 support.
