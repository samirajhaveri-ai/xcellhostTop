How it works

Agents join your channels. People stay in charge.

An AI worker is a named member of your workspace with a job, a set of tools and a rulebook. You talk to it in the channel where the work already happens, and everything it does is written into the thread.

  1. 01Connect your apps

    Sign in to Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Zoho, your CRM, helpdesk and payment gateway. Each connection is scoped to read or write.

    OAuth · least privilege
  2. 02Pick or build agents

    Start from the catalogue — Ticket Triage, Quote Drafter, Invoice Matcher — or describe a new one in plain language in the builder.

    no code needed
  3. 03Mention it in a channel

    Type @ and the agent's name. It replies in a thread, shows each tool call and check, and asks when it needs more information.

    visible to the team
  4. 04Approve & audit

    Refunds, payments, deletions and outside emails stop at an approval card for the named person. Every action lands in the audit log.

    human-in-the-loop

Approvals & guardrails

Agents can do a lot. You decide what they may do alone.

Each workspace has an approval policy. It says which actions an agent may take on its own, which wait for a person, and who that person is.

approval-policy.yamlsample
  1. workspace: "finance-ops"
  2. auto_allowed:
  3. - read records, draft replies, summarise
  4. - update ticket status, add internal notes
  5. needs_approval:
  6. refund: "> ₹5,000* → finance-lead"human
  7. payment_release: "any → two approvers"4-eyes
  8. email_external: "first send → owner"human
  9. delete_record: "always → data-owner"irreversible
  10. pii: redact [aadhaar, pan, card, bank_ac]
  11. rate_limit: "30 actions / min / agent*"
  12. kill_switch: admins, it-security
  13. retention: "audit 365 d · threads 90 d*"
  14. hosting: "india · xcellhost-mumbai"
Who can approveNamed people or roles per action — "Finance lead" for refunds, two approvers for payment release. Approvers can be away-delegated, never the requesting agent.
Irreversible actions listRefunds, payments, deletions, external emails, contract sends, access grants and production deploys are approval-gated by default.
PII redactionAadhaar, PAN, card and bank numbers are masked before text reaches a model, and unmasked only for the approved tool call.
Rate limits & budgetsPer-agent action and spend caps*. A runaway loop stops at the limit and pings the owner.
Kill switch · try it One switch pauses every agent in the workspace. Running steps finish their current call and stop; queued actions are held for review. Admins and IT security hold the switch.
