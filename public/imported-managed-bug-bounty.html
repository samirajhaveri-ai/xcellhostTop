Overview

Researchers are already looking at your apps. Give them a legal door.

Independent security researchers test public websites, APIs and mobile apps every day. Without a published channel, the ones who find something either stay quiet, email a support inbox that nobody triages, or post it online. A managed bug bounty turns that into a controlled programme: written scope, written rules, a safe-harbour promise for good-faith testing, and rewards paid only for valid, unique findings.

XcellHost runs it for you. We write the policy, choose and vet the researchers, run 24×7 intake, reproduce and rate every report, remove duplicates and noise, and send your developers a clean ticket with a fix suggestion. When the fix is in, we retest it, pay the reward from your pool and handle disclosure on the terms you agreed.

Scope · what researchers may test

A clear line between in scope and off limits — written before anyone starts.

Scope is the most important page of the programme. It protects your production systems and tells researchers exactly where safe harbour applies.

In scope · typical
  • Web applicationscustomer portals, storefronts, admin consoles you name
  • APIspublic and partner APIs, with documentation or a test key
  • Mobile appsAndroid and iOS builds, plus the APIs behind them
  • Sign-in & account flowslogin, password reset, OTP and SSO on test accounts
  • Cloud-hosted assetsnamed domains and storage you own, not the cloud provider
  • Devices & firmwareby arrangement, with test units supplied
Always out of scope
  • Denial of service & load testingnothing that degrades availability
  • Social engineeringno phishing, calls or messages to your staff or customers
  • Physical accessoffices, branches, data centres
  • Third-party servicesplatforms you use but don't own or control
  • Real customer datatesting stops at first sight of personal data
  • Unverified scanner outputautomated reports without a working proof
Researcher rules
  • Test accounts onlysupplied by you, never other users' accounts
  • Agreed testing windowsfor anything sensitive, with a named contact
  • Minimum proofshow the issue, access nothing beyond it
  • Delete what you sawno data retained after reporting
  • No public disclosurewithout written consent, per policy
  • Identity verified for payoutsPAN/KYC handled by XcellHost

Safe harbour & governance

Written authorisation for researchers. Written protection for you.

A bounty only works when both sides know the rules. Every programme starts with documents, not testing.

  • Safe-harbour statementYou authorise good-faith testing that stays inside the written scope and rules. It is a contractual promise not to pursue compliant researchers — it cannot override Indian law, so out-of-scope activity is not covered.
  • Programme terms & NDAResearchers accept terms, confidentiality and data-handling rules before they see private scope.
  • Rules of engagementTesting windows, rate limits, test accounts, a named contact and a stop procedure either side can trigger.
  • No production impactNo availability testing, no data beyond minimum proof, high-risk checks only in staging or agreed windows.
  • Data handlingReports stored encrypted with access limited to the programme team; personal data in proofs is redacted.
  • Disclosure policyCoordinated, discretionary or non-disclosure — your choice. For flaws in a third-party product, we can coordinate with the vendor or through CERT-In's disclosure process.

Use cases by industry

Where a standing researcher programme pays for itself first.

Lending, payments and wallet apps

Mobile apps and APIs change weekly. Researchers focus on account takeover, payment logic and authorisation flaws that scanners miss.

  • Android / iOS apps and their APIs
  • OTP, login and KYC journeys on test accounts
  • Evidence for RBI testing expectations

Customer portals and partner integrations

Start with a private programme on non-core portals and partner APIs, then expand as your change process keeps pace.

  • Net-banking adjacent portals, policy servicing
  • Partner and aggregator APIs
  • Board-level monthly summary

Storefronts, checkout and loyalty

Sale seasons bring new code and new attackers. Researchers look at coupon abuse, account flaws and exposed order data.

  • Checkout and order flows
  • Coupons, wallets and loyalty points
  • Customer data under the DPDP Act

Multi-tenant products selling abroad

Enterprise buyers ask whether you run a vulnerability programme. Tenant isolation and API authorisation are the usual focus.

  • Tenant isolation checks on test tenants
  • Public API and webhook surface
  • Security questionnaire evidence

Patient apps, labs and teleconsult

Health records are sensitive personal data. Scope excludes real records; researchers work on seeded test patients only.

  • Appointment and report-download flows
  • Strict stop-on-personal-data rule
  • Retest records for audits

Vendors building citizen-facing apps

Private, tightly scoped programmes on staging builds before go-live, with non-disclosure as the default.

  • Staging and pre-release builds
  • Non-disclosure model, NDA-bound cohort
  • CERT-In coordination where needed
