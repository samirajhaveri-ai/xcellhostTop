Executive summary

Enter Europe through the India corridor and partners

The regional case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months. Germany, Poland and the UK have their own deep-dive pages; this page covers the region and the EU-wide rules.

The opportunity
US$290.4B

European cloud plus cyber security in 2025, summed from two published estimates14. That is about . Scopes differ, so treat it as an upper bound.

  • Cloud: US$220.9B in 2025, rising to US$535.4B by 2031 (Mordor)1. European providers hold only about 15% of their home cloud-infrastructure market2.
  • Cyber security: US$69.5B in 2025 (MarketsandMarkets)4; IDC expects European security spending to grow 11.8% in 2025 and reach nearly US$97B by 20285.
  • Sovereign cloud IaaS: US$6.9B → US$12.6B from 2025 to 2026, up 83% (Gartner)3.
  • AI use: 20.0% of EU firms in 2025, up from 13.5%; Denmark leads at 42.0%13.
Why now

Corridor + partners

Start with Indian firms in Europe and European firms with Indian GCCs, then add MSP and distributor partners. Open an EU entity between month 6 and 12.

    Risks & mitigations

    HighTransfer rules

    India has no EU adequacy decision, so every Mumbai access to personal data needs SCCs and a TIA.

    Mitigation: EU hosting by default, EU-held keys, documented TIAs and minimal, logged support access.
    HighSovereignty preference

    Sovereign cloud spend is set to rise 83% in 2026, and public buyers ask for national schemes we do not hold.

    Mitigation: certified partner infrastructure, transparency on where staff sit, and no public deals without a prime.
    HighPrice competition

    Three hyperscalers hold ~70% of the market and local MSPs compete on price and language.

    Mitigation: lead with managed security, compliance and AI skills rather than raw compute; use Mumbai delivery cost.
    MediumPatchy NIS2 timing

    France, Spain and Ireland lacked national laws in Oct 2026, so scope and dates differ by country.

    Mitigation: country-by-country offers, refreshed when each law passes.
    MediumDORA audits

    Banks and insurers audit every ICT provider and weigh concentration on designated CTPPs.

    Mitigation: DORA contract annex, audit and exit rights, and tested exit plans.
    MediumLegal change

    The data Omnibus, EUCS and CSA2 are still proposals, and the India–EU FTA is not yet signed.

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with EU counsel, and refreshes to this page.
    MediumTax presence

    Selling staff in Europe before an entity exists can create a permanent establishment.

    Mitigation: partner-led and representative-only first; entity before hiring sellers.
    LowerFX & collections

    Changes in EUR, GBP, INR and USD rates, plus partner credit risk.

    Mitigation: EUR and GBP price books, prepaid or quarterly billing, and partner credit limits.
