Frequently asked questions

What is the NVIDIA L4 GPU and when should I use it?
The NVIDIA L4 is a 72 W Ada Lovelace GPU with 24 GB of GDDR6, FP8 Tensor Cores and AV1 media engines, built for efficient AI inference, video processing and edge workloads. Choose it for always-on inference and video services; choose L40S or A100/H100 for heavier generative AI or training. XcellHost rents it in India from ₹36,700 a month (excl. GST).
When should I choose L4 instead of L40S or A100?
Choose L4 for efficient inference, video and edge workloads. Choose L40S for heavier generative AI and graphics, and A100, H100 or H200 for large-scale training. You can mix GPU types at XcellHost as workloads change.
Is the L4 good for LLMs?
Yes, for inference. With FP8 or 4-bit quantisation it serves 7B–13B models for chatbots and RAG efficiently. It isn't designed for large training runs.
Does it support AV1?
Yes. Two NVENC and four NVDEC engines support AV1, H.264 and H.265 encode and decode, plus JPEG decoders — suited to live transcoding, conferencing and video AI pipelines.
How does L4 reduce GPU costs?
At 72 W it delivers high performance per watt, so each server handles more requests or streams. That means fewer servers and a lower monthly GPU bill than oversized GPUs or CPU-only inference.
Is it suitable for edge and low-latency deployments?
Yes. Its low-profile, low-power design suits edge servers, and hosting in Indian data centres keeps latency low for users in India.
Can I run it with Docker and Kubernetes?
Yes — it's container-ready and works with the NVIDIA GPU Operator on Kubernetes for autoscaled inference.
What does the L4 cost in India?
From ₹36,700 per month for one L4 with 8 vCPU and 32 GB RAM, excluding GST. 3-, 6- and 12-month terms save 5%, 7.5% and 10%.
