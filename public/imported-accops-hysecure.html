    Overview

    Replace the network VPN with access to exactly one app at a time.

    A traditional VPN drops a remote laptop onto your network and trusts it from then on. Accops HySecure works the other way round. Accops describes it as a zero-trust private access gateway built on a software-defined perimeter: every request is checked against identity, device, time and location, and each approved app gets its own isolated, encrypted tunnel. Everything else on the network stays invisible.

    XcellHost deploys and runs it for you. We host the gateway in our Mumbai cloud or install it on your hypervisor, publish your web, client-server, RDP and SSH apps, write the access policies with your team and watch the gateway 24×7. Pair it with Accops HyID for MFA and single sign-on, and with HyWorks if you also want virtual desktops.

    How it works

    Every request passes three checks — then gets one tunnel.

    A typical hosted layout. Blue packets are approved sessions on their own tunnel; the orange one tries to reach a server it is not allowed and is dropped at the gateway.

    Prove the user

    Password against your directory, then a second factor — or passwordless with a FIDO key or QR scan.

    Prove the device

    Posture and device identity are read before login. A failing device is blocked or held in a minimum quarantine profile.

    Check the context

    Time window, location, IP and group decide which apps this session may see — re-checked after login.

    Open one tunnel

    An encrypted tunnel to each permitted app. No route to anything else, and every session is logged.

    Deployment options

    Run the gateway where it suits you — we operate it either way.

    Accops supports inbound gateways and Reverse Connect designs where app connectors dial out, so you open no inbound ports. Reverse Connect is a Gold and Platinum add-on, per Accops.

    Accops lists HySecure form factors for VMware ESXi, XenServer, Hyper-V, Nutanix AHV, Proxmox and Red Hat OpenShift, public clouds (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud), Kubernetes and bare-metal appliances. We confirm the right form factor for your edition.
