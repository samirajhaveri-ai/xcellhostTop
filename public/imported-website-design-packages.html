06
Tech stack

The right platform for the job, not the trend.

We recommend one in the scoping call and tell you why.

Used for Starter, Business and Professional

WordPress with a lean theme

The most widely used open-source CMS. Your team edits pages in a visual editor; we keep plugins to the few that earn their place, because every plugin is something to update.

  • Easy editing, large talent pool, no licence fee for the core
  • Runs on shared, VPS or Webuzo* hosting
  • Needs regular updates — the main reason an AMC exists
Used for E-commerce

WooCommerce on WordPress

An open-source store plugin: catalogue, variants, coupons, GST-inclusive pricing, order emails and payment-gateway plugins from Indian payment aggregators. Your content pages and store share one admin.

  • You own the store and the data; no per-sale platform fee
  • Gateway, shipping and invoice plugins are widely available
  • Large catalogues need more server — we size hosting with you
Used for Custom

Headless CMS or a custom application

A separate front end (for example a React-based framework) reading from a headless CMS, or a custom app for portals, bookings and dashboards. Chosen per project after scoping.

  • Fast pages and complete freedom in design and logic
  • Clean integration with CRM, ERP and internal APIs
  • Higher build cost; content edits may need a developer

Built-in compliance basics

Forms, payments and access — done properly from day one.

General information to help you plan, not legal advice. Your own counsel should confirm what applies to your business.

DPDP-ready forms

Every enquiry form gets a short notice (what you collect, why, how to withdraw consent, whom to contact) and an unticked consent box, and collects only the fields you need. The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025; most duties of businesses apply from 13 May 2027.

DPDP Act 2023 · Rules 2025

Analytics and cookies

Analytics and marketing tags can load only after the visitor agrees, through a consent banner, and the privacy notice lists them. Form entries are not sent to analytics.

Consent before tracking

Accessibility basics

We design to the main WCAG 2.2 Level AA checks: text contrast, keyboard navigation with a visible focus, alt text on images, labelled form fields with clear errors, and touch targets that are large enough. A full audit is a separate service.

WCAG 2.2 · W3C Recommendation, Oct 2023

Payment gateways

Online payments go through a payment aggregator authorised by the RBI, under its Payment Aggregator Directions of 15 September 2025. XcellHost integrates the gateway you choose. We are not a payment aggregator, we never handle your money, and your site never stores card numbers.

RBI-regulated PA · you contract with it
