Executive summary

Enter the UK through MSP partners, with UK hosting

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$70.9B

UK cloud plus cyber security demand in 2025, summed from two Mordor Intelligence estimates32. That is about . Treat it as an upper bound.

  • Cloud: US$31.6B–56.08B in 2025, from two analysts with different scopes43.
  • Cyber security: US$14.77B of demand (Mordor) and £14.7B of revenue earned by 2,603 UK cyber firms (DSIT)21.
  • AI: £33.6B of AI-related revenue across 10,488 AI companies, DSIT's experimental estimate8.
  • IT services overall: US$112.5B in 2025, with managed security among the fastest-growing lines5.
Why now
4 shifts

Four changes in 2026 open projects at the same time: new data rules, a cyber bill that names MSPs, tougher Cyber Essentials and a trade deal. See the trigger calendar.

  • Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: most data protection changes in force since 5 Feb 2026. Complaints procedures required from 19 Jun 202620.
  • Cyber Security and Resilience Bill passed the Commons on 16 Jun 2026. It brings managed service providers into scope, including those based abroad2423.
  • Cyber Essentials v3.3 since 27 Apr 2026: no MFA on a cloud service means an automatic fail29.
  • Threat level: 204 nationally significant incidents in the year to Aug 2025, up from 89. The JLR attack cost the UK an estimated £1.9B1112.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

A UK DC partner plus UK MSP and SI resellers first. Open a UK private limited company (Ltd) in the first six months.

  • Host UK workloads in a UK partner DC in London or Manchester. XcellHost's Frankfurt and Düsseldorf partner DCs are the EU option.
  • The CSR Bill requires non-UK managed service providers in scope to nominate a UK representative23. A UK Ltd also gives GBP invoicing and a G-Cloud route.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC, with a UK transfer mechanism for any personal data accessed from India.
