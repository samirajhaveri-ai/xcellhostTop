      Typical cover heads

      What a personal cyber policy may include. Actual cover is per policy wording.

      IRDAI's 2021 guidance on cyber insurance products for individuals lists these covers for insurers to build on. Each product chooses its own covers, limits and conditions.

      Conditions worth knowing
      • Insurers generally need a police complaint for a claim; IRDAI's guidance lets them accept an e-complaint on the cybercrime portal for small claims up to ₹5,000.
      • The model wording asks you to block a lost or misused card within 24 hours of finding out.
      • Money your bank, wallet or shopping site must refund is not paid again by the insurer.
      • You need a registered mobile number and email for bank alerts and OTPs.
      Often excluded
      • Physical theft with no cyber element, such as a stolen wallet
      • Intentional acts or gross negligence by the insured
      • Incidents that began before the policy started
      • Business losses, lost profits and illegal activity

        If you have personal cyber cover, notify the insurer as the policy requires, with your complaint numbers.

          FAQ

          Online fraud and personal cover, answered plainly.

          No. XcellHost is not an insurer or an IRDAI-registered intermediary. We offer a paid safety check and help after an incident. If you want a personal cyber policy, buy it from an insurer or an IRDAI-registered intermediary*, and read the policy wording first.

          IRDAI's 2021 guidance lists theft of funds, identity theft, social media, cyber stalking or bullying, malware and data restoration, phishing, unauthorised online transactions, email spoofing, media liability, cyber extortion and privacy breach covers. Each insurer picks its own covers, limits and conditions; some add online shopping fraud or family members.

          Call 1930, then your bank, then file on cybercrime.gov.in. Quick reports through the national helpline allow the receiving banks to be asked to hold the money. Keep every complaint number for the bank and, if you have one, your insurer.

          For transactions up to 31 December 2026, RBI's 2017 rules give zero liability when the fault lies with the bank, or with a third party and you report within three working days of the bank's alert; reports in four to seven working days have capped liability. If you shared your OTP or PIN, you bear the loss until you report it.

          RBI's amendment directions of 24 June 2026 apply to transactions from 1 January 2027. Zero liability for third-party breaches needs a report within five calendar days. Individuals who report to the bank and to 1930 or the cybercrime portal within five calendar days can get one-time compensation of 85% of a loss up to ₹50,000, capped at ₹25,000, paid within five days of the claim. Banks must resolve complaints within 45 days for domestic cases.

          Usually a police complaint is required. IRDAI's guidance allows insurers to accept an e-complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal for claims up to ₹5,000. Your policy wording sets the exact documents and time limits.

          Only if you meant to pay that person. Approving a collect request with your PIN sends money out. You never need a PIN or OTP to receive money, so any request that says otherwise is a scam.

          Callers pose as police, customs or a central agency, claim you are under investigation and keep you on a video call until you transfer money. No agency arrests people over a video call. Hang up, call a family member and report it on cybercrime.gov.in or 1930.
