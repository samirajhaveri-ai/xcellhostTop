    About Red Hat

    Community code in, supported platforms out — that is the Red Hat model.

    Summarised from redhat.com. Product names and features are Red Hat's and can change; we confirm them on your quotation.

    Red Hat builds enterprise products from open-source community projects and sells them as subscriptions — per Red Hat, a subscription covers tested releases, security errata, a certified hardware and software ecosystem, and Red Hat support. Its core platforms, per Red Hat, are Red Hat Enterprise Linux, Red Hat OpenShift and Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform, with Red Hat AI built on top.

    Red Hat has been part of IBM since 2019 and still sells across every cloud: OpenShift runs on-premises and as managed services on AWS, Azure and IBM Cloud. For India, XcellHost sources the subscriptions, builds and migrates the platform, and takes the first support call.
