Overview

An ISMS your auditor can follow — and your team can actually run.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the international standard for an information security management system (ISMS). Clauses 4 to 10 set out the management system — context, leadership, risk-based planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement. Annex A lists 93 reference controls in four themes. You assess your risks, decide which controls apply, record that in a Statement of Applicability and prove it works through internal audit and management review.

XcellHost prepares you for certification as a fixed-fee project, typically 3–6 months for an SME. Certification is issued by an accredited certification body (NABCB- or IAF-accredited) after its Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits — we coordinate them and can recommend partners. We run ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 and 20000-1 in our own 24×7 operations in Mumbai, so the advice comes from people who live with the audits every year.

Annex A gets the attention, but auditors start with the clauses. Any gap here is a nonconformity, whatever your firewall looks like.

Annex A · what changed in 2022

114 controls in 14 domains became 93 controls in 4 themes.

The 2022 revision merged 24 old controls, updated 58 and added 11 new ones. The transition window closed in October 2025 — every valid certificate today is to the 2022 version.

Certification is a cycle, not a one-off audit.

An accredited certification body (CB) audits you in two stages, issues a certificate valid for three years and comes back every year to check it still works.
