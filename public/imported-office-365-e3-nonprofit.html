    Overview

    Office for every desk — without paying for device tools twice.

    Office 365 E3 is Microsoft's enterprise productivity plan: the desktop Office apps, Exchange Online Plan 2 mail, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive, plus Microsoft Purview tools for labelling, data loss prevention, retention and eDiscovery. It leaves out the device and identity layer — Intune, Microsoft Entra ID P1 and Windows Enterprise — that comes with Microsoft 365 E3.

    That makes it a good fit for NGOs that already manage laptops and phones with another MDM, or don't need central device control at all. For verified nonprofits XcellHost lists it at ₹480 per user a month* instead of ₹1,910 — about 75% off — with no user cap, so it works for programme teams of 50 or 5,000.
