    Responsible AI & India compliance

    One rule everyone remembers: check the data colour before you paste.

    Module 7 goes deep, but the data traffic light is taught in week one and used in every lab. The course explains the rules; it is not legal advice, and your organisation's own policy always comes first.

    DPDP Act 2023 · Rules 2025Personal data in prompts

    The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with phased commencement; most obligations apply from May 2027. Breaches go to the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours.

    Taught in M0, M7 · applied in every lab
    CERT-In · 28 Apr 2022Incidents and logs

    Organisations must report specified cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours. Learners practise spotting and escalating AI-enabled phishing and data leaks fast.

    Taught in M7 · Lab 8
    MeitY · 5 Nov 2025India AI Governance Guidelines

    Principles-based guidance under the IndiaAI Mission that relies on existing laws. The course uses its themes of accountability, transparency and human oversight.

    Taught in M1, M7
    EU AI Act · Art. 4AI literacy for exporters

    Since 2 February 2025, providers and deployers of AI systems in the EU must take measures for staff AI literacy. Completion records from this course can support that evidence.

    Relevant if you serve EU clients

    Want an AI policy and governance for the whole organisation? See AI Adoption Framework and ISO 42001 consulting. Regulatory dates checked October 2026.
