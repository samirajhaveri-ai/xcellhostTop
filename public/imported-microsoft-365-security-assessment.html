Overview

Microsoft secures the platform. The settings are yours.

Most Microsoft 365 incidents do not need an exploit. They use what the tenant already allows: a mailbox that still accepts basic sign-ins, an admin without strong MFA, a rule that quietly forwards invoices to an outside address, a folder shared with "anyone with the link" three years ago. Each one is a setting, and each one can be checked.

XcellSecure Microsoft 365 Security Assessment is a fixed-scope review of one tenant by XcellHost's security team. We read your configuration with read-only roles, test it against the CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark and CISA's SCuBA baselines, weigh each gap against the licences you already pay for, and hand back a short list of what to fix first. Quick wins can be fixed by us under change control, and the result is retested. Priced in INR with a GST invoice.

    How it works

    Two benchmarks, your licences, one ranked list.

    We do not hand you a 300-row spreadsheet. Every check is mapped to a recognised benchmark, then filtered by what your licences allow and what actually reduces risk.

    CIS Benchmark

    CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7.0.0

    Consensus-built configuration guidance from the Center for Internet Security. Version 7.0.0 was published in 2026 and added recommendations moved over from CIS's Azure benchmark. Recommendations are split into profile levels, so a basic level and a stricter level can be applied separately.

    Source: cisecurity.org, CIS Benchmarks update, June 2026.
    CISA SCuBA

    Secure Cloud Business Applications baselines

    Secure configuration baselines from the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency for Entra ID, Defender, Exchange Online, Power BI, Power Platform, SharePoint and OneDrive, and Teams, with an open-source checker, ScubaGear. They became mandatory for US federal agencies under Binding Operational Directive 25-01 in December 2024. In India they are a strong reference, not a legal requirement.

    Source: cisa.gov, SCuBA project and BOD 25-01.

    Use cases

    When a tenant review pays for itself.

    TriggerAfter a phishing scare

    A mailbox was compromised or nearly was. Find every other door the same attacker could use, not just the one they tried.

    TriggerAudit or questionnaire due

    ISO 27001, SOC 2, an RBI or SEBI inspection, or a customer security questionnaire asks how Microsoft 365 is configured.

    TriggerNew IT partner or admin

    You inherited a tenant from a previous vendor. Get an independent baseline before you take responsibility for it.

    TriggerPaying for E3 or E5

    Business Premium, E3 and E5 include security features many tenants never switch on. Find out what you already own.

    TriggerMerger or new entity

    Two tenants, two sets of admins and sharing rules. Assess both before you connect or migrate them.

    TriggerCyber insurance renewal

    Insurers ask about MFA, admin accounts, backups and email security. Answer from evidence, not memory.
