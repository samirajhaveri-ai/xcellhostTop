What “dedicated” means

The GPUs are reserved for you — nobody else's traffic runs on them.

Serverless inference shares a pool of GPUs between many customers and bills per token. Dedicated inference gives one customer a fixed set of GPUs, running one model configuration, behind one private endpoint.

Dedicated Inference is the XcellHost Inference Cloud option for teams that need predictable capacity: the GPUs, their memory and the serving engine on them are yours for the reservation period. Load an open model from our catalog, or your own fine-tuned weights, and call it through an OpenAI-compatible API — so code written for hosted APIs moves across with a base-URL change.

Everything runs in XcellHost's Mumbai cloud, so prompts, outputs and weights stay in India — useful when your DPDP Act 2023 obligations, customer contracts or sector regulators ask where personal data is processed. XcellHost sizes the GPUs with you, deploys and monitors the endpoint, and bills in rupees on a GST invoice.

Deployment flow
How it works

From weights to a private endpoint — in five drawn steps.

Scroll through the drawing. Each step is something XcellHost engineers do with you during onboarding; most single-model endpoints go live once GPUs are allocated and the model is validated.

01Bring your model
  1. 01 · ModelBring your model — or pick from the catalog

    Choose an open model such as Llama 3.3 70B Instruct or Qwen2.5 72B Instruct, or hand over your own fine-tuned weights (Hugging Face format, safetensors). We check the licence terms with you before deployment.

  2. 02 · GPUPick the GPU class and precision

    FP16, FP8 or 4-bit weights change how much memory the model needs. The fit calculator below shows the maths; we confirm it with a load test on the real card.

  3. 03 · ReplicasSet replicas and autoscale bounds

    One replica is one full copy of the model. Set a minimum that is always on and a maximum the autoscaler may add when queues grow — capacity never goes past the bound you approved.

  4. 04 · EndpointGo live on a private endpoint

    An OpenAI-compatible HTTPS endpoint, reachable only from your allow-listed IPs or over private peering to your network. API keys are issued per application.

  5. 05 · OperateObserve, tune and upgrade

    GPU memory, queue depth and tokens per second are monitored by the XcellHost NOC. We tune batch limits, roll out new model versions side by side and resize when your traffic changes.
