Who wins when

Choose by fit, not by hype.

Both are good clouds — for different jobs. Here’s an honest read on when each one is the right call.

Why Indian businesses choose CloudBaba

  • India-first infrastructure
  • Local data residency by default
  • Lower latency for Indian users
