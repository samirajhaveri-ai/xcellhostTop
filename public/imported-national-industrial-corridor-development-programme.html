What NICDP is

New industrial cities, planned on the freight network and built ahead of demand.

The official framing, the institutions that run it and how a corridor city is financed.

The National Industrial Corridor Development Programme builds greenfield industrial cities along India's main transport arteries. The Western Dedicated Freight Corridor is the backbone of the Delhi–Mumbai corridor and the Eastern DFC of the Amritsar–Kolkata corridor; national highways, ports and airports serve the others. The cities are planned before industry arrives, with developed plots, trunk utilities and connections in place, so a manufacturer can lease land and start building straight away. The government calls this plug-and-play, and the townships also follow a walk-to-work layout with housing near the factories.

The programme comprises 32 projects under 11 corridors, to be developed in four phases (PIB, 4 Aug 2021). Twenty industrial nodes have been approved so far, twelve of them in the last five years. Four are complete: Dholera in Gujarat, Shendra-Bidkin in Maharashtra, and the integrated industrial townships at Greater Noida (UP) and Vikram Udyogpuri (MP). The other 16 are at different stages of implementation (Lok Sabha reply, 4 Aug 2026).

Cabinet decision · 28 August 2024

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved 12 new industrial nodes at an estimated ₹28,602 crore across 10 states and 6 corridors. They are to be greenfield smart cities "built ahead of demand" on plug-and-play and walk-to-work lines, aligned with PM GatiShakti, with an estimated 10 lakh direct and up to 30 lakh indirect jobs. PIB's February 2026 explainer puts their investment potential at ₹1,52,757 crore over about 25,975 acres.

NICDCNational Industrial Corridor Development Corporation

Incorporated in January 2008 as the Delhi Mumbai Industrial Corridor Development Corporation (DMICDC), later renamed NICDC. It plans, develops and coordinates the programme.

NICDITNational Industrial Corridor Development & Implementation Trust

Re-designated on 7 December 2016 from the DMIC Project Implementation Trust Fund. DPIIT has released ₹16,172.95 crore to it, and it has passed ₹14,569.97 crore on to project SPVs. Budget 2026-27 provides ₹3,000 crore.

State SPVsOne per node, Centre + State

The state contributes land. The Centre contributes equity and, where needed, debt. EPC contractors build the internal trunk infrastructure. Construction typically takes 36–48 months from EPC award.

MonitoringApex Monitoring Authority · DPIIT

Progress is tracked through PRAGATI and the PM GatiShakti portal, where external connections such as bulk water, power, telecom and roads have been mapped.

ICT & smart city features

A corridor city runs on six digital layers. Pick one to see what it needs.

The official documents describe ICT-enabled utilities, smart city planning and technology-enabled administration. In practice that means the systems below. The XcellHost mapping is our own.

Layer 01 · Security & resilience

Everything above it depends on this layer

A city operations centre, its SCADA links and its citizen portals are attractive targets. CERT-In's April 2022 directions require cyber incidents to be reported within six hours and logs to be kept for 180 days in India. OT networks need segmentation along IEC 62443 zones and conduits, and the ICCC needs a second site it can fail over to.

  • 24×7 SOC across IT and OT telemetry
  • OT asset inventory, segmentation and remote-access control
  • Managed DR with tested RTO/RPO and drills
  • CERT-In 6-hour reporting runbook and 180-day log store

Layer 02 · Utilities & OT

Water, power, effluent and gas, run by SCADA

Corridor cities run their own water treatment plants, CETPs, sewage plants and substations. Dholera's 400/220 kV substation is SCADA-enabled. Each plant has PLCs, RTUs, smart meters and sensors whose data must reach the operations centre without exposing the control network.

  • Device identity and certificates for meters and sensors
  • Edge nodes near plants for low-latency control data
  • One-way or brokered paths from OT into IT
  • OT backup of PLC programs and HMI images

PM GatiShakti National Master Plan

A GIS platform used to plan infrastructure across ministries. DPIIT reports 57 ministries and departments onboarded and about 1,700 data layers (731 ministry and 969 state) as of December 2025. It is now open to the private sector.

ULIP · Unified Logistics Interface Platform

Logistics data from government systems, exposed as APIs. As of July 2026 PIB counts 46 integrated systems across 12 ministries and departments, 142 APIs, more than 2,000 data fields, more than 260 applications and over 450 crore API transactions.

Logistics Data Bank

Tracks EXIM containers across India using only the container number. DPIIT says it covers 18 ports (31 terminals) and 5,800 railway stations through FOIS. LDB and ULIP are run by NICDC Logistics Data Services Ltd (NLDSL).

For tenants, these platforms mean API integrations with ERP, WMS and TMS systems. XcellHost hosts those integrations on Logistics Cloud and Manufacturing Cloud.
