    Overview

    Google Workspace is easy to buy. Running it well is the work.

    Google Workspace gives your team Gmail on your own domain, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Chat and Calendar, with one admin console to control users, devices and security. The licence is the easy part. Someone still has to create accounts on day one, remove access on the last day, keep mail authenticated so it lands in inboxes, stop files being shared to personal accounts, and answer "I can't log in" at 9 pm.

    With Managed Google Workspace, XcellHost does that work for a fixed fee per user per month. We move your mail in from Zoho, Microsoft 365, Exchange or any IMAP server, harden the tenant, and then run it from our Mumbai NOC — tickets, policies, user lifecycle, monitoring and a monthly report. You keep ownership of the tenant and your super-admin account.

    • Per-user fee — Essential ₹129 or Professional ₹249 per user / month*, on top of the Google Workspace licence.
    • Licences on quote — Business Starter, Standard, Plus or Enterprise, supplied and billed in INR by XcellHost.
    • Your tenant — XcellHost works through delegated admin roles; you can take the keys back at any time.
