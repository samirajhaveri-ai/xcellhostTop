      FAQ

      EC-Council certifications in India, answered.

      CEH v13, which EC-Council also calls “CEH AI”. It has 20 modules and 221 labs. The knowledge exam (312-50) has 125 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours. The optional CEH (Practical) has 20 challenges in 6 hours. Passing both earns CEH Master. EC-Council says new versions come every 12–18 months.

      It depends on the certification. CEH, CCSE, ECDE and the AI certifications need either official EC-Council training or two years in information security plus a US$100 application fee. CCISO needs five years in its domains. CPENT, WAHS, CCT, CSCU and the Essentials series have no eligibility criteria.

      Many EC-Council exams, including CEH, CND, CHFI, ECIH, CCT and CCISO, use a cut score set per exam form, between 60% and 85%. Others state a fixed 70%, including CSA, CTIA, CCSE, ECDE, CASE, EDRP, ECES, ECSS, CSCU and the Essentials. Scoring 90% or more on CPENT also earns LPT (Master).

      Three years. In that time you earn 120 ECE (continuing education) credits per certification and pay an annual membership fee — US$80 if you hold an ECE certificate, or US$20 if you hold only non-ECE ones such as CSCU or the Essentials. CCISO has a US$100 Continuing Education fee.

      EC-Council lists eight ANAB ISO/IEC 17024 certifications: CEH, CEH (Practical), CPENT, CCISO, CND, CHFI, ECIH and CCT. Its DoD 8140 list includes CEH, CEH Practical, CCISO, CND and CHFI, and maps ECIH to DCWF role 531.

      Certified Offensive AI Security Professional (C|OASP), Certified Responsible AI Governance & Ethics (C|RAGE) and Certified AI Program Manager (C|AIPM), all with beta exams in November 2026. There is also Artificial Intelligence Essentials (AI|E), with 75 questions in 2 hours and no eligibility criteria.

      ECSA is no longer on EC-Council's programme list, and CPENT is the current pen-testing programme. The standalone LPT exam has been replaced by LPT (Master) through CPENT. EISM was retired on 1 December 2023, and its holders moved to Associate CCISO. CEH v12 has been superseded by v13.

      On quote, in INR with GST. EC-Council's own site lists CEH from US$1,699 on-demand and US$2,499 live online, and says training partners set their own prices. We quote the course, voucher and any lab time together.
