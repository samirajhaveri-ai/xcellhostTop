    Overview

    Cloud is easy to start. Running it well every day is the hard part.

    Most Indian businesses now run more than one cloud: a hyperscaler for new apps, a private cloud for ERP or regulated data, and a few SaaS and edge pieces around them. Each has its own console, alarms, patch cycle and bill. Someone has to watch all of it at 3 a.m., approve changes, keep costs in check and prove to auditors that controls work. That is cloud operations — and it rarely fits inside a small in-house team.

    XcellHost Managed Cloud Operations puts every estate on one operations desk. Our Mumbai NOC & SOC monitor events, handle incidents and changes under ITIL 4 practices, run patching and backup checks, keep landing-zone guardrails in place and review spend every month. You keep ownership of accounts, subscriptions and data; we run them to agreed service levels and report in plain language.
