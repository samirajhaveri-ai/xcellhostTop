Blockchain access that keeps up when your users do.

Reliability & security
Built so one bad node never becomes your outage.

    

      
Load balancing & failover
The gateway health-checks every node on block height and response time and drops a lagging node out of rotation automatically. Reads and writes can be split.

      
Monitoring & alerting
Block lag, peer count, disk, error rates and latency watched by our 24×7 NOC, with alerts to you by email, Teams or WhatsApp.

      
Rate limits you control
Per-key requests-per-second limits and method allow-lists, so one runaway script cannot starve your production app.

      
API key security
Keys rotated on schedule, scoped per app, with IP and origin restrictions. For signing keys and secrets, custody in our Cloud HSM.

      
DDoS & edge protection
Public endpoints sit behind network DDoS filtering, with WAAP available for dApp front ends and APIs.

      
Private by default
Dedicated nodes are allow-listed to your IPs; add Private Connect for a private link with no public listener at all.

How it works
One request, five hops, a few milliseconds.
What happens between your app calling the endpoint and the answer coming back.

    

      
Your dAppSDK · bot · backend

      

      
Gatewaykey · rate limit · allow-list

      

      
Load balancerhealth · lag · read/write split

      

      
Node clusterfull · archive · validator

      

      
Monitoring24×7 NOC · alerts to you

    

    

      
01Authenticate
The gateway checks your API key, origin and IP, then applies your per-key rate limit and method rules.

      
02Route
The balancer sends the call to the healthiest in-sync node — heavy archive or trace calls to archive builds.

      
03Answer
The node executes the call and the result streams back over HTTPS or the open WebSocket.

      
04Watch
Latency, errors and block lag are recorded; a lagging node leaves rotation and our NOC is paged.

India compliance
Infrastructure that helps you meet Indian obligations.
We run the servers and keep the evidence. Regulatory decisions stay with you and your advisers.

    

      
PMLAFIU-IND registration
A Finance Ministry notification of 7 March 2023 made VDA service providers — exchange, transfer, safekeeping and issuer-related services — reporting entities under the PMLA. They register with FIU-IND, run KYC, report suspicious transactions and keep records for five years. Our logs and India-hosted storage support that record-keeping.

      
6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions
CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs to be kept for 180 days within India, and VDA exchange and custodian wallet providers to keep KYC and transaction records for five years. Gateway and node logs can be retained in Mumbai to match.

      
DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025
Wallet-linked and KYC data is personal data. The Act requires reasonable security safeguards and breach intimation to the Data Protection Board and affected people; the Rules were notified in November 2025 and phase in. India-hosted nodes and logs keep that data in the country.
