Yes. XcellHost webinars and online clinics are free to attend; register and we'll send the joining link and a recording afterwards.
Can XcellHost Speak At Our Event?
Yes — our cloud, cybersecurity and AI specialists speak at conferences, association meetings and customer forums. Use 'Invite us to your event' with the date and audience.
Can I Add An Event To My Calendar?
Yes. Every event has an 'Add to calendar' button that downloads an .ics file for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
Do You Run Private Workshops For Our Team?
Yes — DPDP Act readiness, ransomware tabletop exercises, Microsoft 365 security and cloud-cost reviews can be run on-site or online for your organisation.
