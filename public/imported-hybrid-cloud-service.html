    Overview

    Most estates are already hybrid — few are run as one.

    Hybrid cloud is not a product you install. It is a design and an operating model that makes separate environments behave like one.

    Some workloads belong in your server room — a plant-floor system, a licence tied to hardware, an app your branches reach over the LAN. Some belong in a private cloud, where regulated data stays on dedicated capacity in India. Others belong in public cloud, where a campaign or month-end peak needs capacity for a week and not a year. Treat them as islands and you get three networks, three sets of logins and three bills nobody can reconcile.

    XcellHost joins them into one estate. We connect your sites to our Tier IV data centre in Mumbai over IPsec or MPLS, extend one IP address plan and one identity directory across all of it, place each workload where it runs best, and run the whole estate from our 24×7 NOC and SOC. You get one ticket queue, one INR invoice with GST, and a clear record of where every piece of data lives.

    Works with VMware Hyper-V Proxmox OpenStack Kubernetes · OpenShift AWS Azure Google Cloud AD · Entra ID

    If one of these sounds like you, a hybrid design usually beats a big-bang migration.

    Security & India compliance

    Know where every record lives — and prove it to auditors.

    A hybrid design helps you meet Indian rules by keeping regulated data on controlled capacity in India while everything else runs where it is cheapest. Compliance stays your responsibility; we supply the controls and the evidence.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Map where personal data sits in each environment, restrict who can reach it and keep breach-response steps ready. We help you meet your duties as data fiduciary.

    CERT-In directions (2022)

    Logs from every environment kept for 180 days in India and incident handling built to support reporting within 6 hours.

    RBI, SEBI & IRDAI

    Where your regulator requires data in India or controls on outsourced IT, regulated systems are placed on dedicated capacity in Mumbai with documented access.
