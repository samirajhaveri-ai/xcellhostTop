    Overview · what is co-location?

    You own the hardware. We run the building around it.

    Co-location means your own servers, storage and network gear live in a professional data centre instead of a cupboard in your office. You keep the hardware, the licences and the root passwords. The facility provides the things that are expensive to build and hard to run well: secure space, clean redundant power, precision cooling, fire protection, physical security and a choice of network carriers.

    XcellHost hosts your equipment in its Tier IV data centre in Mumbai, watched around the clock by the same NOC and SOC that run our cloud. Take a single 1U slot for one server, or a quarter, half or full rack. Add metered power, cross-connects and remote hands as you need them. Pair it with XcellHost cloud, backup and DR on the same invoice, billed in INR with GST.

    Security & India compliance

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    Six checks between the street and your server, and the paperwork your auditor asks for.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Your personal data stays on your hardware, in Mumbai. Access logs, CCTV and our ISO 27001 controls help you show "reasonable security safeguards", and we support breach intimation with facility evidence.

    CERT-In directions

    The April 2022 directions ask for incident reporting within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for 180 days in India. Our NOC alerts you fast and keeps facility access and alarm logs to help you meet both.

    RBI · SEBI · IRDAI

    Payment-system data must be stored in India under RBI's localisation circular, and SEBI and IRDAI expect documented security controls. Hosting in Mumbai, with audit-ready access records, helps you meet them.
