Overview

A management system for AI — so every model has an owner, a risk rating and a record.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the first international standard you can be certified against for an AI management system (AIMS). It follows the same clause 4–10 structure as ISO 27001 — context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement — and adds what AI needs: AI risk assessment, AI system impact assessment and an Annex A of 38 controls covering AI policy, roles, resources, the AI lifecycle, data, transparency, responsible use and third parties. Annex B explains how to implement them; Annexes C and D list typical AI objectives, risk sources and sector uses.

It applies whether you build models, fine-tune them, or simply plug a hosted LLM into your products. XcellHost prepares you as a fixed-fee project — typically 4–6 months to a Stage 2-ready AIMS — and the certificate is issued by an accredited certification body after its Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits. We coordinate the CB and can recommend partners. We already run ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 and 20000-1 in our own 24×7 operations in Mumbai, so the AIMS we build sits on controls we know how to audit.
