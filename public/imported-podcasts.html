About the show

No slides, no sales pitch — just how it's done.

Each episode takes one problem Indian IT teams face this year and talks it through with someone who has solved it. What worked, what broke, what it cost, and what they'd do differently.

Every episode includes
  • 35–45 minutes — one topic, covered properly, the length of a commute
  • Chapters with timestamps so you can jump to the part you need
  • Three key takeaways you can take into Monday's meeting
  • A full transcript — searchable, quotable, easy to share with your team
  • India context — DPDP Act, CERT-In rules, INR budgets and local data residency
