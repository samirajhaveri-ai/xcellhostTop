    Overview

    When a breach is live, hours matter and evidence is fragile. Every reboot, re-image or well-meant clean-up can wipe the traces that tell you how the attacker got in and what they touched. XcellHost's DFIR team contains the incident first, then investigates the root cause and preserves court-admissible evidence — forensic images with hash verification and a signed chain of custody — with the discipline that legal, insurance and regulatory follow-ups demand. You get one accountable team from the first call to the final report: containment, investigation, a plain-language root-cause report, a remediation plan, a free re-test to prove findings are closed, and summaries shaped for CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and DPDP notifications. On retainer, response times are pre-agreed; for an emergency without a retainer, we mobilise the same day.

    India regulatory reporting

    The clock starts at detection. We help you meet it.

    Several Indian regulators expect cyber incidents to be reported within hours. We gather the facts, build the timeline and draft the reports — so your team can file on time.

    6:00:00CERT-In · sample
    6 h

    CERT-In

    Reportable cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them (CERT-In Directions, April 2022), with ICT logs kept for 180 days.

    6 h

    RBI

    Regulated entities report cyber incidents to RBI within 6 hours of detection under RBI's IT governance directions.

    6 h

    SEBI CSCRF

    SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework expects regulated entities to report incidents promptly, aligned to the 6-hour CERT-In window.

    6 h

    IRDAI

    Insurers report cyber incidents to CERT-In and IRDAI within 6 hours under IRDAI's information and cyber security guidelines.

    DPDP

    DPDP Act & Rules, 2025

    Personal-data breaches are intimated to the Data Protection Board and affected data principals as the DPDP Rules, 2025 require.
