    Overview · Microsoft 365 E5

    E3 at the core. Five rings of protection around it.

    Per Microsoft, Microsoft 365 E5 contains everything in E3 — Office apps, Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Intune, Windows Enterprise — and adds the full Defender XDR family (Office 365 Plan 2, Endpoint Plan 2, Identity, Cloud Apps), Microsoft Entra ID P2, Purview's advanced compliance tools, Power BI Pro and Teams Phone Standard.

    For a bank, NBFC, hospital, law firm or ministry that means the controls auditors ask for are already licensed: endpoint detection and response, privileged access that expires, eDiscovery with legal hold, Insider Risk signals and a year of premium audit data. XcellHost deploys each layer in order, tunes the alerts and can watch the whole estate from our 24×7 SOC.

      India compliance

      Six hours to report. E5 helps you know in minutes.

      E5's controls help you meet Indian rules; your policies and processes complete the picture.

      • CERT-In directions (2022) — report incidents within 6 hours, keep ICT logs 180 days within India. Defender XDR incidents and Audit (Premium) give the evidence; stream to Sentinel for long retention.
      • DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025 — intimate personal-data breaches to the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours. Purview shows what data was touched.
      • RBI, SEBI CSCRF & IRDAI — privileged-access control, SOC monitoring and audit trails are recurring themes. PIM, EDR and Audit (Premium) map to them — see the mapper.
      • Teams Phone in India — PSTN calling runs through Operator Connect or Direct Routing with an Indian carrier, configured to respect DoT toll-bypass rules.
