Guide · Microsoft licensing · India

The Cloud Solution Provider programme — CSP — is how most Indian organisations now buy Microsoft 365, Copilot, Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Windows 365 and Azure. Since September 2024 every licence-based CSP purchase runs on Microsoft's New Commerce Experience (NCE), which brought firm commitments: you choose a term, you get seven days to change your mind, and after that you pay for the term you picked. This guide explains those rules in plain language, with the official Microsoft source for each one, and shows where XcellHost fits.

General guidance. Microsoft terms change — confirm before purchase. Facts were checked against Microsoft Learn (Partner Center), Microsoft Licensing and microsoft.com on 31 October 2026. Prices are XcellHost's indicative INR figures from our product pages, excl. 18% GST. This is not legal or tax advice.

#01What CSP is — and why it matters to you

CSP is a Microsoft programme that lets authorised partners sell Microsoft cloud subscriptions, attach their own services, and bill the customer directly. Microsoft provides the service; the partner owns the commercial relationship — the quote, the invoice, renewals, seat changes and first-line support.

For a buyer, three things follow from that:

  • Your partner is your counterparty for billing. You pay the partner, in INR, on the partner's terms. Microsoft bills the partner. Partner Center's own documentation says the partner stays responsible for paying Microsoft even if the customer stops paying — which is why partners enforce the commitment rules carefully.
  • Your tenant stays yours. Subscriptions are provisioned into your Microsoft Entra tenant. The partner gets admin access only if you approve it, through granular delegated admin privileges (GDAP), and you can remove it.
  • The rules are Microsoft's. Terms, cancellation windows and renewal behaviour are set by Microsoft for every CSP partner. A partner can add services and payment terms around them, but cannot override Microsoft's commitment rules.
India note

Buying through an Indian partner means a GST tax invoice in rupees from an Indian company, local support hours and a contract under Indian law. Buying on microsoft.com with a card means a different seller and invoice. See Billing & GST in India.

#04NCE terms and billing plans

Every NCE licence subscription has two settings that people often confuse: the term (how long you commit) and the billing frequency (how often you are invoiced). Microsoft lists three term lengths — monthly, annual and triennial (three-year) — and says plainly that not every term exists for every product: "Terms available depend on the product and SKU combination."

TermBilling plans offeredWhat you commit toBest for
MonthlyMonthly (upfront)One month at a time; renews monthlySeasonal staff, pilots, projects with an end date
AnnualAnnual (upfront) or monthly12 months of the seats you buyA stable core of users — usually the bulk of a licence estate
TriennialTriennial (upfront), annual or monthly36 months; price fixed for the termLong-lived, predictable seats where price certainty matters most

Changing term or billing mid-way

  • Longer only. You can move a subscription to a longer term mid-term; you can't move to a shorter term mid-term.
  • Billing frequency moves with the term. Microsoft allows billing frequency changes together with a term change. Since 1 April 2025, Partner Center no longer supports immediate billing-frequency-only changes.
  • Triennial is fixed. Triennial subscriptions can't be changed mid-term.
  • Anything eligible at renewal. At renewal the partner can switch term or billing for the next term — for example an annual-upfront subscription can renew as annual paid monthly.
Tip

Mixing is normal. A common pattern is an annual subscription for your permanent headcount and a separate monthly subscription of the same product for contractors and interns. Both can sit in the same tenant.

Source: Partner Center — manage term duration and billing frequency (updated 12 Feb 2026).

#05How NCE pricing works

Price protection for the term

In new commerce, Microsoft attaches the current price-list price to a subscription when it is created, and "price changes during the term don't affect billing plan charges for an existing subscription." Licences you add later to the same subscription are charged at the same price, and their end date follows the subscription. Changes such as a new term or an upgrade apply the then-current price.

That is the main reason to choose a longer term: Microsoft notes that some customers "need three-year terms to preserve the pricing for the lifetime of the term."

The monthly-term premium

Monthly-term subscriptions cost more than annual ones. On microsoft.com/en-in, the monthly-commitment price of each Microsoft 365 Business plan is 20% higher than its paid-yearly price (checked 31 Oct 2026; Microsoft labels the yearly option "Save up to 16%"). Microsoft's own extended-service-term pricing implies the same gap in CSP: EST is billed at the monthly-term rate plus 3%, "or 23% if no monthly plan exists". Treat 20% as the typical premium and confirm the exact figure on the CSP price list for each SKU — the cost comparison tool lets you change it.

Watch out

Partners widely reported that, from April 2025, an annual term billed monthly costs about 5% more than the same annual term paid upfront. We could not find this on a Microsoft Learn page, so treat it as confirm with XcellHost when you compare quotes.

When prices change

  • Monthly price lists. Licence-based price lists update on the first of each month (midnight UTC), with changes previewed 30 days ahead. Azure plan and Marketplace prices refresh daily.
  • Annual local-currency updates. From fiscal year 2027, Microsoft moves to one local-currency price update for Commercial Cloud each January. The next one is effective 1 January 2027, with guidance issued every November.
  • Promotions are separate. Price list files don't include promotional pricing; promotions apply to the partner price for the term of the purchase (see section 8).
India note

Partner Center prices are set per customer market. Microsoft's pricing page notes India is one of a few markets that may carry geo-specific discounts on a small number of SKUs. Your INR quote from a partner is the figure that counts — XcellHost's product pages say quoted INR prices are held for 30 days.

#06The 7-day (168-hour) window

This is the rule that surprises most first-time NCE buyers. Microsoft's wording: partners "can cancel their subscription with a prorated refund within the first seven days (168 hours) of any term". After that, "cancellation is no longer available" and the partner is billed for the full term "even if the customer stops paying for or using the subscription".

How seat reductions work

You can add licences to a subscription at any time. You can reduce them only within seven days of when they were added. Microsoft applies this to licences added "at the initial purchase, on renewal, or at midterm", and refunds the full amount minus the days used, calculated daily.

Worked example

A Pune company buys 40 Microsoft 365 Business Premium seats on an annual term on 3 March at 11:00. On 6 March it learns 4 joiners deferred. Because it's within 168 hours, the partner reduces the subscription to 36 seats and the company is refunded for 4 seats minus 3 days of use. On 15 March it adds 5 seats for new hires; those 5 can be reduced until 22 March. If a user leaves in August, the seat stays paid until the term ends — reassign it to the next joiner instead.

Watch out

The window reopens only when a subscription renews into a new term. Trials that convert to paid get the standard 7-day window from conversion. Use the countdown simulator right below to see your exact deadline.

#07Seats, auto-renew and the extended service term

Adding seats and co-terming

Adding licences into an existing subscription keeps the same price and the same end date, so every seat renews together. Microsoft describes this as the two benefits of adding to one subscription: "the price is the same for the partner, and the end date of all the individual licenses is controlled by the end date of the subscription." New subscriptions created during a partial upgrade also take the original subscription's end date.

Auto-renew is on by default

Active subscriptions are set to renew automatically when the term ends. For new commerce subscriptions, the partner can turn auto-renew off or schedule changes — a different quantity, term, billing frequency or an upgrade — to take effect at renewal. After renewal you get a fresh 168-hour window.

No more free grace period — the extended service term (EST)

Until 2026, turning auto-renew off gave a short period of free service after the term ended. Microsoft ended that on 4 May 2026: "Customers who don't renew must either cancel at end of term without service or enter a paid extended service term (EST) to continue service."

#08Upgrades, trials and promotions

Upgrades: up, not down

  • "Upgrade means going from one paid subscription to another paid subscription on a higher tier. (Downgrades aren't supported.)" — for example Business Standard to Business Premium, or E3 to E5, where Microsoft lists the path.
  • Upgrades can be mid-term or scheduled for the end of the term. A full upgrade moves all licences and keeps the subscription ID; a partial upgrade moves some licences into a new subscription that keeps the original end date.
  • A same-term upgrade keeps the original end date; going from a monthly E3 to an annual E5 starts a new term.
  • No cancellation window applies to an upgrade. Double-check quantities before submitting.
Tip

If you might want to step down at renewal (say from E5 to E3 for some users), keep those users on a separate subscription so the quantity can be changed at that subscription's renewal without touching the others.

Trials

  • New commerce trials last one month and include 25 licences; quantity can't change during the trial.
  • Trials renew into paid subscriptions automatically unless auto-renew is turned off. If no values are set, the trial becomes an annual term, paid monthly, with 25 licences.
  • One trial per offer per customer; no trials for government, education or add-on offers.
  • Microsoft added "Copilot in 30" in CSP from 1 August 2026: a 25-user, 30-day Microsoft 365 Copilot Business trial for organisations with fewer than 300 employees.

Promotions

  • Nearly all NCE promotions apply automatically to eligible purchases and discount the partner price for the term of that purchase.
  • A renewal keeps the promotional price only if the renewal date falls inside the promotion's date range. First-purchase promotions always renew at standard price.
  • Eligibility depends on term, quantity and purchase history; cancelling a promoted subscription can remove eligibility for that offer later.
  • Examples current at the time of writing (October 2026 announcements): 30% off the partner price for customers buying at least 300 new Microsoft 365 E3 licences with an annual commitment (27 Oct 2026 – 30 Jun 2027); a 25% bundle on Business Basic + Copilot Business, and 15% on standalone Copilot Business for 1–300 seats, through 31 Dec 2026. Eligibility rules apply — ask XcellHost to check.

Sources: Partner Center — create and manage subscriptions (upgrades); free trials; new commerce promotions (updated 15 Jun 2026); July and October 2026 announcements.

#11Azure through CSP

Azure in CSP is sold as the Azure plan: pay-as-you-go access to Azure services "at the published prices", under the Microsoft Customer Agreement. One Azure plan can hold many Azure subscriptions, so new projects don't need new orders. You pay for what you consume each month.

Way to pay for AzureCommitmentBillingUse it when
Azure plan (pay-as-you-go)NoneMonthly, on actual usage; usage prices can change during a monthNew, variable or short-lived workloads
Azure reservations1 or 3 years for a specific resource typeUpfront, per Partner Center — ask about monthly billing for your reservationSteady VMs and databases that run all month
Azure savings plan for compute1 or 3 years of hourly spendOne-time or monthlySteady compute spend that may move between VM types or regions
  • Savings plans can reduce compute costs "by up to 65% from pay-as-you-go prices" per Microsoft; the discount rate depends on the term, not the amount. They need an Azure plan, apply at single or shared scope, and auto-renew is on by default.
  • Reservations can be cancelled or exchanged "for adjusted refund" per Partner Center — check the current reservation refund limits before relying on that.
  • Partner earned credit (PEC) rewards CSP partners who provide 24×7 operations management of an Azure plan. Savings-plan discounts are applied instead of PEC on covered usage.
  • From 2 November 2026, Microsoft is introducing time-bound Azure consumption promotions in CSP for selected products and customer segments; they don't reduce PEC.
Watch out — Azure overspend

Consumption has no ceiling by default. Set budgets and alerts per subscription, tag resources by owner, shut down dev/test out of hours, and review reservations quarterly. XcellHost's Managed Azure plans include monthly cost reports and quarterly cost reviews at the Professional level.

#14GDAP, Partner Admin Link and the Microsoft Customer Agreement

GDAP — granular delegated admin privileges

GDAP is how a CSP partner gets admin access to your tenant. Microsoft describes it as "least-privileged access following the Zero Trust cybersecurity protocol", granular and time-bound, and "Customers must explicitly grant the least-privileged access to their partners."

GDAP factPer Microsoft
Maximum relationship lengthTwo years
Auto-extendOptional; extends by six months (180 days) at a time until disabled or terminated
Global AdministratorCan't auto-extend; Microsoft advises requesting it only time-bound, for known issues
Pending requestsExpire after 90 days if you don't approve
Security groupsUp to 100 per customer for role assignments
If it expiresThe partner loses access; your subscriptions don't change
DAP (the old model)Since 25 Sep 2023 Microsoft no longer grants DAP for new customers (it grants default GDAP instead). Microsoft says GDAP is replacing DAP and recommends moving all customers to GDAP.
Azure managementPartners managing Azure work through a separate security group with Azure RBAC, not Global Admin
Tip

Review GDAP like any other privileged access: once a year, check which roles each partner holds, remove leftover DAP relationships from old resellers, and keep Global Administrator out of standing partner access.

Partner Admin Link (PAL)

PAL lets Microsoft recognise the partner who manages your Azure plan subscriptions. The partner links its partner ID to the user or service principal you have already given access to. In Microsoft's words, it "doesn't alter any permissions (Azure role) or provide extra Azure service data to the partner or to Microsoft." Enterprise Agreement subscriptions use a different mechanism, Digital Partner of Record.

The Microsoft Customer Agreement (MCA)

  • "Before you can place an order on a customer's behalf, the customer must accept and sign the Microsoft Customer Agreement."
  • Acceptance is recorded either by the customer directly in the Microsoft 365 admin center, or by partner attestation (name, email and acceptance date of the person who accepted). Indirect resellers can't attest — their indirect provider does.
  • Since 7 October 2025, partners whose MCA attestations pre-date 1 April 2023 and were not re-attested are blocked from new purchases, quantity changes, term changes and upgrades for those customers until it is fixed.
  • After attestation, your primary contact receives an automated email notice.
India note

Make sure the person who accepts the MCA is authorised to sign for your company, and keep the confirmation email with your procurement file. It is your contract with Microsoft for the services; your commercial terms with XcellHost sit alongside it.

#16Switching CSP partners and moving from an EA

Moving NCE subscriptions to a new partner

You don't have to wait for renewal to change partner. Microsoft supports partner-to-partner transfers of new commerce licence subscriptions mid-term:

  1. You accept the new partner's relationship request (and approve GDAP for them).
  2. The new (target) partner creates a transfer request to your current (source) partner.
  3. The source partner reviews and submits it. Transfers take up to 72 hours; requests not acted on expire after 30 days.
  • The transferred subscription is created "at the price of the original subscription" with a new subscription ID. User counts and renewal settings are preserved, and applied promotions carry over.
  • "There's no new cancellation window because transferred subscriptions aren't considered new subscription terms."
  • Up to 25 licence-based line items per billing plan per transfer. Azure subscriptions, reservations and savings plans under an Azure plan have their own transfer process.
Tip

Before switching, export your subscription list (product, quantity, term, billing, end date, auto-renew) from the admin center so you can check every line arrived intact.

Enterprise Agreement to CSP

Microsoft's Enterprise Agreement is a three-year agreement "designed for organizations with at least 500 users and/or devices" (250 for public sector), with an annual true-up. Some organisations below or near that size move to CSP for monthly flexibility and partner-managed billing.

  • In July 2026 Microsoft widened access to its EA to CSP for Azure transition tool, which moves Azure from an EA to CSP "without service disruption". It is available to partners with a Cloud & AI Solutions Partner designation and CSP direct bill authorisation, or a Frontier Distributor designation.
  • For licence-based products, the move is usually timed to the EA anniversary or end, with new CSP subscriptions starting as EA ones end.
  • Reports from licensing advisers say Microsoft removed volume price levels for online services from November 2025, narrowing the EA price advantage — we could not confirm this on a Microsoft page, so confirm with XcellHost for your products.

Sources: Partner Center — transfer new commerce licence-based subscriptions (updated 8 May 2026); July 2026 announcements; Microsoft Licensing — Enterprise Agreement guidance and FAQ.

#18Mistakes and gotchas to avoid

Buying annual for temporary users

Annual seats can't be reduced after 7 days. Put contractors and seasonal staff on a separate monthly subscription.

Missing the 168-hour window

Order mistakes — wrong SKU, wrong quantity, wrong term — must be fixed within 7 days. Check every order confirmation the day it arrives.

Letting auto-renew decide

Renewal locks in another term. Review quantity, term and SKU 30 days before each end date.

Turning auto-renew off and walking away

Since 4 May 2026 that moves eligible subscriptions into a paid extended service term at the monthly rate + 3%.

Unassigned licences

Paid seats sitting unassigned after leavers. Reassign before you buy more; run a monthly assigned-vs-active report.

Upgrading in a hurry

Mid-term upgrades have no cancellation window and you can't downgrade. Confirm the count before submitting.

Azure without budgets

Pay-as-you-go has no cap. Budgets, alerts, tags and reservations for steady workloads stop bill shock.

Standing Global Admin for partners

Grant least-privilege GDAP roles; keep Global Administrator time-bound; remove old DAP links.

Unclear MCA acceptance

Outdated attestations can block purchases and seat changes. Confirm who accepted and when.

Assuming promo prices last

A promotion renews at promo price only inside its date range; first-purchase promos always renew at list.

#20Glossary

CSP
Cloud Solution Provider — Microsoft's programme for partners to sell, bill and support Microsoft cloud subscriptions.
NCE
New Commerce Experience — the purchase platform for CSP licence subscriptions; all licence-based CSP purchases since 1 Sep 2024 (except some public-sector legacy offers).
Direct bill partner
A CSP partner that buys from Microsoft directly and bills its customers.
Indirect provider
A CSP distributor that buys from Microsoft and supplies indirect resellers.
Indirect reseller
A CSP partner that buys through an indirect provider and serves customers.
Term
The commitment length of a subscription: monthly (P1M), annual (P1Y) or triennial (P3Y).
Billing frequency
How often a term is invoiced — upfront, annually or monthly.
Cancellation window
The first 168 hours (7 days) of a licence term, when cancellation with prorated refund is allowed.
Co-terming
Aligning end dates so licences renew together — automatic when you add seats to an existing subscription.
EST
Extended service term — a paid month-to-month extension after a non-renewed subscription ends; monthly rate + 3% (or + 23% where no monthly plan exists).
Upgrade
Moving licences to a higher-tier SKU, fully or partially; no downgrades; no cancellation window.
Promotion
A time-bound discount on the partner price for eligible purchases, applied for the term.
Azure plan
The CSP offer for pay-as-you-go Azure under the Microsoft Customer Agreement.
Reservation
A one- or three-year commitment to a specific Azure resource type for a lower rate.
Savings plan
A one- or three-year hourly-spend commitment on Azure compute, flexible across VM types and regions.
PEC
Partner earned credit — a credit for CSP partners who provide 24×7 operations management for an Azure plan.
GDAP
Granular delegated admin privileges — least-privilege, time-bound partner access to your tenant, approved by you.
DAP
Delegated admin privileges — the older, broad partner access model that GDAP is replacing.
PAL
Partner Admin Link — links a partner ID to existing Azure credentials for recognition; grants no extra access.
DPOR
Digital Partner of Record — the partner-recognition mechanism for Enterprise Agreement subscriptions.
MCA
Microsoft Customer Agreement — the customer contract with Microsoft that CSP purchases require.
MCA-E
The Microsoft Customer Agreement as used by enterprises buying from a Microsoft sales team.
EA
Enterprise Agreement — Microsoft's three-year volume agreement for 500+ users/devices (250 public sector).
True-up
The EA's annual reconciliation of products, users and devices added during the year.
MOSA / MOSP
Microsoft Online Subscription Agreement / Program — the older direct online agreement, replaced by the MCA for many direct purchases.
SPLA
Services Provider License Agreement — how hosting providers license Microsoft software monthly on servers they run for you.
OIDAR
Online information database access and retrieval — the Indian GST category for certain online services supplied from outside India.
ITC
Input tax credit — GST paid on business purchases that a registered business can generally set off, subject to GST law.
