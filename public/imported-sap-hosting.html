    Overview

    SAP off your server room, not out of your control.

    Most Indian SAP estates still sit on a rack in the office or a co-location cage: hardware ageing, one Basis person on call, backups nobody has restored in a year. SAP hosting moves those systems into XcellHost's Mumbai cloud and puts a 24×7 team behind them.

    We host the SAP products you already license — SAP ECC 6.0, SAP S/4HANA, SAP BW or BW/4HANA, SAP Business One and SAP Solution Manager — as full landscapes with development, quality and production systems. Our Basis engineers handle the system work: monitoring, transports, kernel and database patches, backups, restore tests, user and role housekeeping and DR drills. Your functional team and partners keep working exactly as before, over SAP GUI, Fiori or the B1 client.

    Licences stay yours (bring your own licence, BYOL). If you later choose RISE with SAP or a hyperscaler, we help with that move too — see AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.

    Architecture

    Two tiers, two sites, backups on both.

    A typical production landscape on XcellHost's cloud. Each design is fitted to your product, sizing and recovery targets.

    Request pathSynchronous replication (HA)Asynchronous replication / backup copy (DR)
    App tier

    Web dispatcher, ASCS/ERS and application servers on separate VMs, so a single host fault does not stop production.

    DB tier

    HANA on SAP-certified* infrastructure, or SQL Server / AnyDB where your release supports it, with a synchronous replica for HA.

    DR to a second site*

    Asynchronous replication with recovery targets agreed per system; failover rehearsed in a yearly drill.

    Backups

    Database-aware backups to an immutable vault, with a copy at the second site and restore tests on record.
