    Overview

    You can't improve what you can't measure — so measure every machine.

    Most Indian IT teams know roughly how protected their laptops and servers are. Few can show it. #CyberFit Score is Acronis' security posture assessment: per Acronis, the protection agent checks each Windows workload for six basic controls, gives it a score out of 850 and lists recommended fixes. Acronis bases those recommendations on recognised good practice such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.

    The score shows in the Cyber Protect console next to every device, and Acronis also offers a free standalone version for single Windows machines. XcellHost turns the number into results: we scan your fleet, rank the gaps, fix them through our NOC and send management a monthly score and trend — billed in rupees with GST.

    Why it matters in India

    Show regulators and boards a number, not a promise.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Incidents must be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for 180 days in India. A fleet with anti-malware, backup and firewall on every machine — watched by a 24×7 NOC — helps you detect and report in time.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches. Disk encryption and backup scores per device are simple, dated evidence of those safeguards.

    RBI & SEBI frameworks

    Banks, NBFCs and SEBI-regulated entities are expected to secure endpoints and test recovery. A monthly score with trend gives auditors and boards a baseline to track.

            It is Acronis' security posture assessment for machines. Per Acronis, the protection agent checks six controls, gives the machine a score out of 850 and recommends fixes for any gaps. Acronis introduced it in Acronis Cyber Cloud 9.0 Update 1 in 2020.

            Per Acronis documentation, 0–579 is Poor, 580–669 Fair, 670–739 Good, 740–799 Very good and 800–850 Excellent.

            Anti-malware (275 points), backup (175), firewall (175, with 100 for the public profile only or 75 for the private profile only), full disk encryption (125, or 75 if only some disks are encrypted), VPN (75) and network security — outgoing NTLM denied — (25). These add up to 850, per Acronis documentation.
