Overview

Governance, risk and compliance as a service — run by people who run it for themselves.

Managed GRC gives you a working security and compliance function for a fixed monthly fee. A named virtual CISO owns the roadmap; our analysts keep the policy library, risk register, vendor reviews and control tests moving on a calendar; and evidence is collected as work happens, not scrambled together a week before the auditor arrives. One control set is mapped across ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, the DPDP Act, CERT-In directions and RBI / SEBI / IRDAI expectations, so each piece of evidence counts more than once.

Why outsource it: a full-time CISO plus a compliance analyst is a large fixed cost, and most mid-size Indian companies need that expertise a few days a month, not every day. XcellHost has run these standards in its own operations for years — we hold ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, 20000-1 and SOC 2 Type II — and our 24×7 NOC & SOC in Mumbai already produces much of the evidence auditors ask for. We prepare you; certificates and reports are issued by accredited certification bodies, licensed CPA firms or QSAs, whom we coordinate.

      Why Indian companies are acting now

      Four rulebooks with deadlines — tracked on one calendar.

      These are the obligations we see driving most Indian GRC programmes today. We map them to one control set so the same evidence answers all of them.

      DPDP Act 2023₹250 crmaximum penalty for failing to protect personal data

      Notice and consent, reasonable security safeguards, breach intimation to the Data Protection Board and affected people, retention and erasure, grievance redressal. Significant Data Fiduciaries also need an India-based DPO, impact assessments and independent audits. The DPDP Rules, notified in November 2025, phase most duties in over 18 months.

      • Personal-data inventory & notices
      • Breach runbook & drills
      • DPO support with the DPDP add-on
      CERT-In 20226 hto report a notifiable cyber incident · logs kept 180 days in India

      CERT-In's April 2022 directions require reporting of specified incidents within six hours, ICT logs retained for 180 days within India, clocks synchronised to NIC or NPL time servers, and a named point of contact.

      • 6-hour reporting playbook
      • Log-retention & NTP checks
      • Point-of-contact register
      RBI · SEBI · IRDAI3sector regulators, one board-approved control set

      RBI's Master Direction on IT governance, risk, controls and assurance (in force from April 2024), SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF, 2024) and IRDAI's 2023 information and cyber security guidelines all expect a board-approved policy, an accountable CISO, periodic audits, VAPT and BCP/DR testing.

      • Board & IT committee reporting
      • Audit & VAPT calendar
      • Regulator-ready evidence index
      ISO 27001:202293Annex A controls · 2013 certificates expired after 31 Oct 2025

      The transition window has closed: every valid ISO 27001 certificate must now be on the 2022 edition, with its reshaped Annex A and new controls such as threat intelligence, cloud services security, configuration management, data masking and DLP.

      • Gap check against the 2022 edition
      • Statement of Applicability refresh
      • Surveillance audit support

      Summaries for planning, not legal advice. Confirm how each rule applies to you with your legal and compliance team.
