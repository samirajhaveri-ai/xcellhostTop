Overview

From an idea to a working AI app. No hand-off in between.

Most AI ideas stall between a promising chat demo and something staff or customers can actually use. The gap is plumbing: finding the right documents, calling the CRM, stopping the bot from saying the wrong thing, putting it on WhatsApp and watching what it costs. XcellAI Apps Builder puts that plumbing into blocks you drag onto a canvas.

Describe what you need, or start from a template. The builder lays out a flow — trigger, retrieval, model, tools, guardrails, reply — that you refine visually and test in a live preview. When it works, publish it as a web widget, WhatsApp number, internal assistant or API. Workspaces are hosted in XcellHost's Mumbai data centre and/or partner data centres*, with engineers on call 24×7.

    How it works

    Four steps. A real app at the end.

    The same path whether you are building an HR assistant for 50 staff or a WhatsApp agent for a lakh customers.

    1. 1 · Describe

      Type the job, paste an example conversation or pick a template. The builder proposes blocks, a system prompt, the data it needs and ten test questions.

      Iterate with follow-up requests
    2. 2 · Refine

      Edit on the canvas and in the live preview. Connect documents, add tools and set guardrails. Every change is versioned and can be undone.

      Builders, reviewers and owners in one space
    3. 3 · Test & publish

      Run the test set, compare models on answer quality and cost, get a reviewer's approval, then publish to web, WhatsApp, Teams-style internal chat or API.

      Custom domain, HTTPS and rate limits by default
    4. 4 · Run & own

      Conversations, costs, failures and thumbs-down are tracked. Fix a prompt, re-run the evals and redeploy — or export the flow and take it elsewhere.

      Monitored 24×7 by XcellHost

    India compliance

    AI apps touch personal data. Build them to help you meet Indian rules.

    The builder does not make you compliant on its own. It gives you the controls and evidence your DPO and IT team will ask for.

    DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

    Collect only what the app needs, record consent and purpose, mask personal data in prompts and logs, and keep a timeline ready — a breach must be intimated to the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours as the Rules phase in.

    6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions

    CERT-In's April 2022 directions ask for reportable incidents within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for a rolling 180 days within India. App, access and admin logs can be retained in Indian data centres.

    INData residency

    Documents, embeddings, conversations and logs stay in XcellHost's Mumbai DC and/or partner DCs*. Use open models on Indian GPUs when prompts must not leave the country.

    WAWhatsApp opt-in & templates

    Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform needs customer opt-in and approved templates for business-started messages. We set up the number, templates and opt-out handling with you.

    Regulatory and platform summaries checked October 2026 for orientation only — not legal advice. Your organisation remains responsible for its own compliance; confirm current requirements with CERT-In, the Data Protection Board and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform policies.
