    Product identity

    Xcell + one word, written as one.

    XcellHost names its service families and products with the Xcell prefix followed by a capitalised word: XcellCloud, XcellSecure, XcellTrust. On product pages, the family sits in front of the product name.

    Rules
    1. One word, CamelCase. XcellSecure — not Xcell Secure, Xcell-Secure or XCELLSECURE.
    2. Capital X, lower-case "cell". Never Excel, XCell or xcell.
    3. Acronym marks keep their spaces. Xcell AI SIEM, Xcell AI MDR, Xcell AI XDR.
    4. Page title lockup. Family, a divider, then the product: XcellSecure|Microsoft Entra ID
    5. Partner products keep their owners' names. Write "Microsoft 365", not "XcellMicrosoft".
