    Overview

    A backup is a promise. We keep checking it.

    Managed Resilience Operations is XcellHost's monthly service that runs the people-and-process side of backup, disaster recovery and business continuity — on the platforms you already have or the ones we supply.

    Most recovery failures are found on the worst day: a job that had been failing quietly for weeks, a copy encrypted along with production, a DR site nobody had booted since go-live. XcellHost Managed Resilience Operations closes those gaps. Our Mumbai NOC watches every backup and replication job around the clock, fixes failures the same night, keeps an immutable copy out of reach of attackers and proves recoverability with scheduled restore tests and DR drills.

    You agree recovery targets per system — how much data you can lose (RPO) and how long you can be down (RTO). We build the protection to match, write the runbooks, rehearse them with your team and hand you a monthly service report with job success, restore-test results and drill timings. When something real happens, our SOC and NOC work the incident together, from detection to a clean restore.

      • DNS & VPN re-pointed
      • Planned failback

      A P3 request: we locate the right version and restore it to the original or an alternate location, then confirm with the requester and log the cause.
