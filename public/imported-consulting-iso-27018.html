Overview

Your customers trust you with personal data. ISO 27018 shows them how you keep it.

ISO/IEC 27018:2019 is the code of practice for protecting personally identifiable information (PII) in public clouds, written for organisations that act as a PII processor — the SaaS, platform or hosting business that stores and processes personal data on a customer's behalf. It extends the ISO/IEC 27002 controls with privacy guidance and adds a set of PII-specific controls built on the ISO/IEC 29100 privacy principles: process only on the customer's instructions, no advertising use without express consent, disclose sub-processors, notify breaches, return or erase data at exit and document where data lives.

Like 27017, it is certified through an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS: an accredited certification body audits the 27018 controls inside your ISMS scope. In India it lines up closely with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where your customers are data fiduciaries and you are their data processor. XcellHost maps the two, implements the controls, prepares the evidence and coordinates the audit — and we run ISO 27001, 27017 and 27018 in our own cloud operations from Mumbai.

Who's who

Same chain, two vocabularies. 27018 is written for the processor.

ISO talks about PII principals, controllers and processors; the DPDP Act calls them data principals, data fiduciaries and data processors. If your customers decide why personal data is processed and you run the service that processes it, 27018 is your standard.

How 27018 certification works

A code of practice, certified through your ISO 27001.

27018 controls go into your ISMS, the certification body audits them with everything else, and the certificate scope says so. ISO 27701 is the optional next step if you also want a certified privacy management system.

  1. 1Have — or build — a 27001 ISMS

    27018 needs the management system underneath: scope, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, internal audit and management review.

  2. 2Bring personal data into scope

    Privacy risks join the risk register, the 27018 controls go into the SoA, and your PII inventory, processor terms and sub-processor list become evidence.

  3. 3The certification body audits it

    Stage 1 reviews documents, Stage 2 tests what you do. Already on 27001? The 27018 extension is often added at a surveillance or special audit.

  4. 4Certificate, then a three-year cycle

    The certificate references 27018 in its scope. Annual surveillance audits follow, with recertification in year three.

XcellHost is not a certification body and does not issue certificates. Certification is granted by an accredited certification body (accredited by NABCB or another IAF member). We prepare you, coordinate the audits and can recommend certification body partners.

      None of these frameworks says "get ISO 27018". All of them expect personal data that is purpose-bound, secured, located and deletable — which is what 27018 builds into a cloud service.

      DPDP Act

      Fiduciaries & processors

      The fiduciary stays responsible for its processors and may engage one only under a valid contract. Expect DPDP clauses in every enterprise deal.

      DPDP Rules

      Phased in from 2025

      The Rules were notified in November 2025, with most obligations phasing in over 18 months — including a detailed breach report to the Board within 72 hours.

      CERT-In

      April 2022 directions

      Report notifiable cyber incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for 180 days in India — your breach runbook has to fit both clocks.

      RBI

      Outsourcing & payments

      Regulated entities must assess IT and cloud providers, and payment system data must be stored only in India. Data location matters.

      SEBI · IRDAI

      Sector cyber frameworks

      SEBI's CSCRF and IRDAI's information security guidelines expect third-party risk management and data protection from service providers.

      ISO 27018 questions, answered.

      ISO/IEC 27018:2019 is a code of practice for protecting personally identifiable information (PII) in public clouds, for organisations acting as PII processors. It adds privacy guidance to ISO/IEC 27002 controls and a set of PII-specific controls based on the ISO/IEC 29100 privacy principles.

      Any cloud, SaaS, hosting or managed-service business that stores or processes personal data on behalf of its customers. It is most valuable when enterprise or overseas customers ask how you protect their users' data, or when DPDP clauses start appearing in contracts.

      No. Like ISO 27017, it is certified through an ISO 27001 ISMS. The certification body audits your ISMS with the 27018 controls in scope and issues an ISO 27001 certificate whose scope references 27018.

      Not by itself. It covers much of what a data processor is asked to do — processing on instructions, security, breach notice, sub-processors, erasure and data location. Fiduciary duties such as notice, consent and consent managers sit outside it. The mapping explorer on this page shows the split; our DPDP programme covers the rest.

      27018 is a set of controls for cloud PII processors. ISO/IEC 27701 is a privacy information management system (PIMS) extension to 27001 for both controllers and processors. Many providers do 27018 first, then add 27701 when they also want a certified privacy management system.

      Indicatively, about 10 weeks for a 27018 extension on an existing 27001, around 20 weeks for 27001 and 27018 together, and about 12 weeks for a stand-alone DPDP programme. Your team's availability and the certification body's calendar are the usual variables.

      27018 does not require a particular country — it requires you to document where PII is stored and processed and tell customers. The DPDP Act allows transfers abroad except to countries the government restricts, while some sector rules (such as RBI's payment-data storage rules) do require Indian storage.
