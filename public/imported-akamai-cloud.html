Egress · pooled transfer

Each plan adds to one shared pool. You pay only for what goes over it.

From Akamai's network transfer documentation, read 18 Nov 2026: inbound traffic is free. Traffic inside a VPC or VLAN, and over private IPv4 or IPv6 within the same data centre, is free. Outbound internet traffic is metered.

One global pool

Most core regions share an account-wide pool. Each Linode's allowance, from 0 to 20 TB a month depending on plan, is added to it, prorated by the hours it runs.

Some regions pool separately

Newer sites such as Jakarta and São Paulo keep a region-specific pool and charge different overage rates.

No allowance

G8 Dedicated, GPU and Accelerated plans include no transfer, so all their egress is billed. NodeBalancers use transfer but add none to the pool.

Object Storage adds 1 TB

Each Object Storage subscription adds 1 TB a month to the global pool. Its own egress is always metered.

Migration from hyperscalers

Move one wave at a time, and keep the old estate until the new one proves itself.

Most migrations to Akamai Cloud are lift-and-shift for VMs and re-deploys for containers. Services that only exist on one hyperscaler need a decision before they move. We find those first.

  1. 01Inventory

    We list VMs, disks, buckets, databases, load balancers, DNS and egress volumes. We also flag every proprietary service you use.

  2. 02Map

    Each item gets an Akamai equivalent: Compute, LKE, Block and Object Storage, NodeBalancers, VPC and Cloud Firewall. Or it stays where it is, or gets replaced.

  3. 03Land

    We build the landing zone in Mumbai or Chennai: VPCs, firewalls, access, logging and backups, managed as Terraform code.

  4. 04Copy

    We copy images and data, sync buckets with S3-compatible tools and replicate databases. We check every copy against its checksums.

  5. 05Cut over

    We lower DNS TTLs and switch during an agreed window. The source stays read-only so you can roll back.

  6. 06Run

    Our NOC takes over monitoring, patching and backups, with a monthly report on cost and transfer pool use.

Moves easily

Linux and Windows VMs, containers on Kubernetes, S3-compatible object data, MySQL and PostgreSQL, Nginx/HAProxy-style load balancing, DNS zones.

Needs a decision

Proprietary serverless functions, managed queues and analytics, vendor-specific AI services, and anything with a hard dependency on a hyperscaler's identity system.

Often stays hybrid

Microsoft 365 and identity, and workloads you keep in our hybrid cloud or on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
