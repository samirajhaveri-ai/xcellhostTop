Overview

Full business authentication, without the long wait.

DigiCert Extended Validation (EV) SSL carries the highest level of identity checking available for a website certificate. Before issuing, DigiCert confirms that your organisation legally exists, operates from a real address, is actively trading, and that the person ordering is authorised to do so. Your legal name, registration number and jurisdiction then sit inside the certificate.

Browsers no longer paint a green address bar, but the verified details are one click away in the certificate viewer and the DigiCert seal — and DigiCert notes that anti-phishing and reputation tools use EV data. XcellHost prepares your MCA, GST and authorisation documents so vetting moves in days, installs the certificate and handles the yearly re-validation.

    EV certificates in India

    Where money and personal data move, verified identity earns its keep.

    No Indian regulation mandates EV by name, but the frameworks below expect strong protection on customer channels — and EV puts your vetted legal identity on the pages that matter most.

    RBI-regulated entities

    Banks, NBFCs and payment aggregators work under RBI's IT governance and cyber-security directions. EV on login and payment pages makes the identity check visible to customers and auditors.

    SEBI's CSCRF

    Brokers, AMCs and other regulated entities must protect data in transit and inventory critical assets — certificates and their expiry included.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards. Strong encryption with verified identity on sign-up and KYC pages is a sensible one.

    CERT-In & MeitY

    CERT-In tracks phishing that imitates Indian brands; MeitY's GIGW 3.0 expects government sites on HTTPS. EV makes a look-alike site easier to tell apart.

    Documents that usually clear EV in IndiaMCA master data (CIN / LLPIN)GST registration (GSTIN)EV authorisation signed by an officerBank letter if under 3 years oldPhone number in a public directory
