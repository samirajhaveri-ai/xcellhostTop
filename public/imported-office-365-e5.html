    Overview

    The top Office 365 plan. Security and compliance, without the device bundle.

    Office 365 E5 is Microsoft's most complete Office 365 licence. It suits organisations that already manage Windows and devices another way but need serious mail security, legal-grade eDiscovery, analytics and voice.

    Per Microsoft, Office 365 E5 includes everything in Office 365 E3 — the Office desktop apps, Exchange Online Plan 2 mailboxes with an archive, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive — and adds Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 2, the Microsoft Purview premium compliance tools (eDiscovery Premium, Audit Premium, Insider Risk Management, Communication Compliance, Customer Key and Customer Lockbox), Power BI Pro, and Teams Phone Standard with Audio Conferencing.

    What it leaves out is the Microsoft 365 device layer: Intune, Microsoft Entra ID P1/P2, Windows Enterprise and Defender for Endpoint. If you already run devices with another MDM, or you'll add Entra and Intune separately, Office 365 E5 is usually ₹1,770 per user per month less than Microsoft 365 E5 at the list prices on this page. XcellHost licenses it through CSP, bills in rupees with GST and sets it up properly.

    Who it's for

    Built for regulated work. Not for everyone in the building.

    BFSI, healthcare, legal and government teams get the most from E5. Most tenants mix it with E3 or frontline plans — you can.

    A good fit

    • Banks, NBFCs, brokers and insurers who need mail threat protection, communication surveillance and audit evidence for RBI, SEBI and IRDAI reviews.
    • Hospitals and diagnostics chains handling patient data under the DPDP Act, wanting DLP, sensitivity labels and Insider Risk on clinical records.
    • Law firms and in-house legal that run holds, searches and review sets in eDiscovery Premium instead of exporting PSTs.
    • Government bodies and PSUs needing a year of audit records and customer-managed encryption keys.
    • Organisations with their own device management (another MDM, Group Policy or Configuration Manager) who don't want to pay for Intune twice.

    Probably not

    • Under 300 users with no compliance pressure — Microsoft 365 Business Premium may cover you for less.
    • You want Intune, Entra ID P2 and Defender for Endpoint — Microsoft 365 E5 bundles them and usually costs less than adding them one by one.
    • Deskless staff on shared phones or tablets — Office 365 F3 or Microsoft 365 F1/F3 is the right licence.
    • Email-only users who never open desktop Office — Office 365 E1 at ₹830 is enough.

    India compliance

    Evidence that stands up to an audit. DPDP, CERT-In, RBI and SEBI.

    No licence makes you compliant on its own. Office 365 E5 gives you the controls and the records; XcellHost helps you configure them to match Indian rules.

    DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025

    Sensitivity labels, DLP and Insider Risk help you find and guard personal data. If a breach happens, Audit Premium and eDiscovery help you reconstruct it for the Data Protection Board intimation and the detailed report the Rules expect.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    CERT-In expects logs kept for 180 days within India and incidents reported within 6 hours. Per Microsoft, Audit Premium keeps Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive and Entra audit records for E5 users for one year by default; other activities stay 180 days unless you set a policy.

    RBI, SEBI CSCRF & IRDAI

    Communication Compliance, retention policies and eDiscovery support the mail, chat and record-keeping evidence regulators ask for. We map controls to your framework; your auditor decides.

    Data in India
