    Overview

    The cloud, moved closer — without losing the cloud.

    Edge cloud means running part of your application on small, managed compute nodes close to where people and machines are — a rack in your plant, a box in the back office of a store, or a server in a metro data centre — instead of sending every request to a central region. Round trips get shorter, raw data is processed where it is created, and a site keeps working when its link to the internet doesn't.

    XcellHost supplies, installs and runs those nodes as one fleet. Each node runs VMs or containers you deploy from a single console; it connects back to XcellHost's Mumbai cloud over an encrypted link for control, backup, logs and the workloads that belong in the core. Our NOC patches and watches every node 24×7, and you get one INR invoice for the lot.

    How it works

    Decide at the edge. Control, keep and report from Mumbai.

    Live traffic stays local; control, backups, logs and heavy analytics travel over one encrypted link to the core.

    01Devices talk to the node

    Cameras, terminals and machines reach the edge node over the site LAN — typically a millisecond or two away.

    02The node decides locally

    Apps answer, models infer and data is filtered on site. The node queues anything bound for the core.

    03Only what matters goes up

    Events, summaries, logs and backups cross the encrypted link — not the raw video or every sensor tick.

    04Mumbai controls the fleet

    Releases and policies flow down from the control plane; our NOC and SOC watch every node 24×7.

    Security & India compliance

    Data processed where it's created — and evidence kept where auditors expect it.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Faces in CCTV, patient images and customer data can be processed on site and discarded, with only events sent onward — data minimisation by design. Helps you meet your duties as a data fiduciary; your own notices and consents still apply.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Node and application logs are shipped to Mumbai and retained for 180 days in India, with clocks synced to NTP. Our SOC helps you report incidents within the 6-hour window.

    RBI, SEBI & IRDAI localisation

    For regulated entities, payment and policy data stays on Indian soil — at the site, in partner data centres* in India, or in Mumbai. Helps you meet localisation and outsourcing expectations.

    MeitY-empanelled provider

    XcellHost is MeitY empanelled and certified to ISO 9001, 27001, 27017, 27018 and 20000-1, with SOC 2 Type I & II reports for the core.
