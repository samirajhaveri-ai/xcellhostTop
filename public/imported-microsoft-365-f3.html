    Overview

    Built for people who never sit at a desk.

    Microsoft 365 F3 is Microsoft's full frontline plan. Per Microsoft, it bundles Office 365 F3, Enterprise Mobility + Security E3 and Windows 11 Enterprise E3 — so a nurse, a field engineer or a store supervisor gets Teams, editable Word, Excel and PowerPoint on the web and on phones and tablets, a 2 GB Exchange Kiosk mailbox and 2 GB of OneDrive, on a device your IT team can manage.

    It costs a fraction of an E-plan because desktop Office, big mailboxes and archives are left out. Most Indian businesses give F3 to shift and field staff and keep E or Business plans for desk-based teams in the same tenant.     Who it's for

    Right for the shop floor. Wrong for the back office.

    F-plans are meant for frontline workers — Microsoft describes them as people on the go, often on mobile or shared devices, working directly with customers or the public.

    Choose F3 for

    Field service engineersNurses & ward staffHospitality supervisorsStore & warehouse leadsFactory line leadsSales & delivery crews
    • They edit forms, checklists and reports on a phone or tablet
    • They need a real mailbox, but a small one
    • IT must manage or share the device

    Pick something else if

    • They live in desktop Excel or Outlook → Microsoft 365 E3 or Office 365 E3
    • Mail goes beyond 2 GB → Office 365 E1 (50 GB)
    • Read-only, chat & shifts only → Microsoft 365 F1 (₹230)
    • Communication-only crews → Teams Essentials for Frontline (₹165)
    • Devices already managed elsewhere → Office 365 F3 (₹350)
    Management

    One tablet per counter. A clean session for every shift.

    Intune P1 in F3 lets IT enrol Android and iOS devices in shared-device mode: a worker signs in with their own Entra ID, works, signs out — and app data is cleared for the next person. Conditional Access only lets compliant devices reach Teams, mail and files.

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    Frontline data, kept accountable in India.

    Store, ward and field data still falls under Indian law. F3 gives you the controls; XcellHost helps you configure them.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Customer and patient data captured on phones is personal data. Sensitivity labels, Conditional Access and remote wipe help you meet reasonable-security duties under the Act and the DPDP Rules 2025.

    CERT-In directions

    Report incidents within 6 hours and keep logs for 180 days. Purview Audit (Standard) retains audit records for 180 days by default, per Microsoft.

    RBI · SEBI · IRDAI

    Branch and agent staff in BFSI need MFA, device control and audit trails — map F3 controls to your regulator's IT framework with us.

    Microsoft India regions

    Microsoft runs cloud regions in Pune, Chennai, Mumbai and — since August 2026 — Hyderabad. Core Microsoft 365 data residency in India is available; we confirm it per service.
