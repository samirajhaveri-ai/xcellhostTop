The opportunity
US$5.2B

Cloud, cyber security and online education in Indonesia, 2025, summed from three analyst estimates235. That is about . Treat it as an upper bound.

  • Cloud: US$2.46B in 2025, growing 14.3% a year to US$5.5B by 20312.

  • AI pull: 80% of Indonesian users use AI tools daily, and AI app revenue grew 127% from H1 2024 to H1 20251.
Why now
10 weeks

From today to 16 January 2027, when the PDP Law's implementing regulation applies. Other dated triggers follow it. See the trigger calendar.

  • GR 33/2026 signed 16 Jul 2026: 72-hour breach notice, DPO and DPIA duties, and fines up to 2% of annual revenue2425.
  • Banks and payments: OJK's IT governance rules took effect 1 Mar 2026, and BI demands a 1-hour incident notice3334.
  • AI: drafts of a Presidential Regulation on an AI roadmap and AI ethics were completed on 27 Jul 2026, and are expected by end-20261011.
  • Threat level: ransomware hit the temporary National Data Centre on 20 Jun 2024, with a US$8M demand and 282 public services disrupted44.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

A Jakarta DC partner plus Indonesian SI/MSP resellers first. Open a PT PMA (foreign-owned company) between month 6 and 12.

  • Host personal and regulated data in a Jakarta or Batam partner DC. Banks must keep their data centre and DR centre in Indonesia unless OJK approves otherwise31.
  • A PT PMA now needs IDR 2.5 billion of paid-up capital (≈ US$138k) and an investment plan above IDR 10 billion per business line and location37.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC, only 1.5 hours behind Jakarta. Keep personal data out of Mumbai until transfer tools exist25.
