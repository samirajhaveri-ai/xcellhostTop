    Why it matters in India

    Decide where data lives — and prove it to auditors.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Knowing exactly which cluster, site and backup location holds personal data makes your records of processing and breach response simpler. Cyber Frame lets production and backup locations differ by design.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Incidents must be reported within 6 hours and ICT system logs kept for 180 days within India. Keeping workloads and their logs on an India-based cluster helps you meet this.

    RBI & SEBI

    RBI requires payment-system data to be stored only in India, and SEBI's CSCRF expects tested backup and recovery. A local cluster with scheduled DR drills helps regulated entities meet both.

    Predictable rupee cost

    Monthly billing in INR with GST, instead of dollar-priced hypervisor renewals and separate backup and security tools.
