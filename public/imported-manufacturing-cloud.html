What Manufacturing Cloud is

One platform from the shop floor to the ERP and the annual report.

Indian plants run on a stack that grew one project at a time: an ERP server in the head office, an MES at each plant, CAD workstations with licences tied to one desk, PLC data that never leaves the line, and a flat network where an infected office PC can reach a SCADA screen. Meanwhile listed companies owe BRSR disclosures, PLI applicants must evidence production and sales, and every body corporate must report cyber incidents to CERT-In within six hours.

XcellCloud Manufacturing Cloud brings it together: SAP and other ERP in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, MES and historians on cloud or edge nodes at the plant, PLM and CAD on cloud desktops, IoT Cloud for machines and meters, an OT assessment to segment the line from the office, and DR for the systems that keep dispatch moving.
