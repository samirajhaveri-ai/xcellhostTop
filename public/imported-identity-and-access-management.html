    Overview

    Twelve passwords and a shared spreadsheet are not access control. One managed identity is.

    Most Indian businesses run on a mix of Microsoft 365, a CRM, an HRMS, Tally or an ERP, and a handful of SaaS tools. Each one has its own login, its own admin and its own list of who still has access. Identity & access management (IAM) replaces that patchwork with one identity per person in Microsoft Entra ID, connected to every app through single sign-on and automatic provisioning.

    XcellHost designs the directory, connects your apps with SAML, OpenID Connect and SCIM, writes the Conditional Access policies that decide who gets in from where, and links it all to your HR process so access starts on day one and ends the hour someone leaves. Then we run it: app onboarding, policy changes, access requests and monthly reports, 24×7 from Mumbai.

    Why it matters

    Most breaches start with a valid login. Control the login, shrink the risk.

    Stolen or reused passwords, accounts left active after people leave, and staff with more access than their job needs are the most common ways into a business. IAM closes each of those doors — and leaves an audit trail when regulators ask.

    Identity risks we close
    • Reused & weak passwordsOne strong sign-in with MFA replaces a password per app.
    • Orphan accountsLeavers are disabled at the source and removed from connected apps.
    • Access creepMovers lose the old role's apps when they gain the new ones.
    • Risky sign-insUnfamiliar devices and locations are challenged or blocked by policy.
    • No audit trailEvery sign-in and access change is logged in one place.
    India: what regulators expect
    0CERT-In incident reporting window (April 2022 directions)
    0CERT-In log retention — sign-in & audit logs included
    DPDPAct 2023 — reasonable security safeguards, incl. access control to personal data
    RBI · SEBI · IRDAIMFA, least privilege & periodic access reviews in sector frameworks (e.g. SEBI CSCRF)

    IAM helps you meet these expectations — it does not make you compliant on its own. We document policies and log retention for your auditors.
