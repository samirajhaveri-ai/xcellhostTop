Our night = their day

Almost everyone is online and mobile, but much of the mid-market still runs on-premise servers, local backups and email with no DMARC. Large enterprises use global vendors; small and mid-sized firms, credit unions, professional practices and state agencies are under-served by providers who can deliver security and cloud as a managed service.

That gap is our opening. XcellHost doesn't need to build a data centre to start: backup, disaster recovery, Microsoft 365, email security, endpoint protection and a 24×7 SOC can be delivered remotely from Mumbai, with a local partner for sales, on-site work and invoicing in TT dollars.

Why now

Six forces are opening the market at the same time.

Central Bank cyber reporting

Since February 2025 banks, insurers, payment providers and e-money issuers must notify the Central Bank of material cyber incidents within 24 hours and file a full report within 72 hours — on top of its 2023 cybersecurity best-practice guideline and annual self-assessments.

Data protection catching up

The Data Protection Act 2011 is only partly in force, and the government has said work on data laws is urgent. Firms that prepare now — data mapping, access control, encryption, breach response — avoid a scramble later.

Government going digital

A Ministry of Public Administration and Artificial Intelligence, a national digital transformation strategy and a 2020 cloud computing policy are pushing agencies towards cloud services.

AI data-centre ambitions

In 2026 the government signed MoUs for AI-ready data centres of about 450 MW combined, subject to due diligence. More local capacity would make hybrid and in-country hosting practical.

India–T&T ties

A large Indo-Trinidadian community, OCI cards extended to the sixth generation of the diaspora, and July 2025 agreements that put T&T forward as the first Caribbean adopter of UPI — Indian technology has goodwill here.

Energy and industry

Oil, gas, LNG and petrochemical plants run operational technology that is increasingly connected — and increasingly targeted. They need backup, segmentation and monitoring that works across sites.

      Risks

      What could slow us down and how we plan around it.

      Foreign-exchange availability

      US dollars can be slow to obtain in T&T.

      MitigationPartners invoice in TT dollars; annual prepay discounts; pricing reviewed each quarter.

      Data residency expectations

      Some clients will want data kept in-country.

      MitigationPartner-hosted option for in-country data; clear documentation of where data sits.

      Trust in a new brand

      Buyers favour known local or global names.

      MitigationLocal partners, reference customers, ISO certifications and free assessments.

      Latency from India

      Interactive apps hosted in India may feel slow.

      MitigationLead with services that don't depend on latency: backup, SOC, email, Microsoft 365.

      Changing regulation

      Data protection and cybercrime rules may change.

      MitigationLocal counsel review; offers designed to map to Central Bank and DPA requirements.

      Partner concentration

      One weak partner can stall the market.

      MitigationTwo to three partners per segment; quarterly scorecards.
