    What Agent 365 is

    Agents are multiplying. Agent 365 keeps count.

    Microsoft describes Agent 365 as the control plane for AI agents: one place to observe, govern and secure them, whichever tool they were built with.

    Every team can now make an agent. Sales builds one in Copilot Studio, finance switches on a partner agent, a developer deploys one from Microsoft Foundry, and someone connects an agent running on another cloud. Within months nobody can say how many agents exist, who owns them, or what data they can touch.

    Microsoft Agent 365 answers those questions. Per Microsoft, it keeps a registry of agents in your tenant, gives agents their own identity through Microsoft Entra Agent ID, shows how agents connect to people and data on an Agent Map, and applies Entra access controls, Defender threat protection and Purview data protection to them. It governs agents; it does not build, host or run them.

    Microsoft announced Agent 365 at Ignite in November 2025 as an early-access Frontier preview. It became generally available for commercial customers on 1 May 2026, licensed per user. XcellHost, a Microsoft Gold Partner, supplies the licence with a GST invoice in rupees, sets it up in your tenant and can run agent governance for you from our 24×7 NOC and SOC in Mumbai.

    Why it matters in India

    Indian rules ask who did what. Agents count too.

    An agent that reads customer data or acts on a user's behalf falls under the same Indian obligations as a person doing the job. Agent 365 records and controls help you meet them. They do not make you compliant on their own, and XcellHost does not give legal advice.

    DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025

    Purpose limits, security safeguards and breach intimation apply to personal data an agent processes. Purview DLP and audit for agents help you limit and prove what agents saw.

    CERT-In directions, April 2022

    Report notifiable incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for 180 days. Defender alerts and agent audit logs feed that evidence trail.

    RBI FREE-AI report, August 2025

    For banks and NBFCs the committee recommends board-approved AI policy, an AI inventory and ongoing monitoring. The registry and Agent Map give you that inventory.

    SEBI AI/ML consultation, June 2025

    SEBI's proposed guidelines put responsibility for AI outputs and data security on the regulated entity. Named agent owners and access reviews support that accountability.
