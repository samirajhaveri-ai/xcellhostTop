    Overview

    SAP runs the money, stock and people of many Indian businesses — and much of it still sits on ageing servers in an office or a co-location rack. Hardware refreshes, HANA memory upgrades, backups that nobody tests and a Basis team stretched thin all add risk. With SAP mainstream maintenance for ECC 6.0 ending at the end of 2027, the move to S/4HANA is now a board-level date. XcellHost moves your SAP estate to Microsoft Azure in India — S/4HANA, ECC, BW/4HANA or SAP Business One — on SAP-certified virtual machines, with SAP HANA replicated across availability zones and a disaster-recovery copy in a second India region. Then we run it: 24×7 SAP Basis from our Mumbai NOC/SOC, Azure consumption on one rupee invoice with GST, and a team that has run business-critical systems since 1999.
