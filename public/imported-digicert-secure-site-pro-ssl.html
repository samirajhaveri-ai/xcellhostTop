Overview

More than a padlock — a security suite built around your certificate.

Secure Site Pro SSL is DigiCert's top-tier organisation-validated (OV) certificate. DigiCert confirms your domain and your registered business, then puts your company name inside the certificate so any visitor who checks can see who runs the site. It chains to DigiCert's own roots and works in practically every browser and device.

What sets Pro apart is what comes with it: a co-signature with a post-quantum algorithm, a daily malware scan, a severity-ranked vulnerability assessment, Certificate Transparency monitoring, the DigiCert Secured and Smart Seals and the CertCentral console. Website security in India

Regulators expect encryption. Pro adds the evidence.

No Indian rule names a certificate brand. But each major framework expects data in transit to be protected and websites to be watched — which is exactly what Pro's daily checks document.

DPDP Act, 2023

Anyone processing personal data must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a breach. Encrypting every form and login — and scanning for malware — are basic, provable safeguards.

CERT-In directions

Incidents such as website compromise or defacement must be reported within six hours. A daily malware scan helps you spot trouble before customers or CERT-In do.

RBI & SEBI entities

RBI's IT directions and SEBI's CSCRF expect strong encryption on customer channels, asset inventories and periodic vulnerability checks. Pro's assessment is a useful early warning between VAPT cycles.

MeitY & government sites

The Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) expect sites to be served over HTTPS. An OV certificate also shows citizens the verified department or PSU behind a portal.
