Executive summary

Enter Sweden through partners and the NIS2 compliance wave

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$2.84B

Cyber security (Mordor, 2025) plus generative AI (Statista, 2025) in Sweden13. That is about . We found no reliable Swedish cloud market-size figure, so cloud is left out. Sweden is a mature, competitive market where AI use runs well above the EU average.

  • Cyber security: US$1.53–1.86B in 2025, depending on the source, growing about 8.8–8.9% a year12.
  • Generative AI: US$982M in 2025, projected at US$6.49B by 20313. 35% of Swedish firms used AI in 2025, against 20% across the EU5.
  • ICT market: CAD 39.3B in 2024, 4.7% of GDP and more than 40% of the Nordic ICT market4.
  • Data centres: colocation was worth US$340M in 2025 across 39 facilities, with 17 more planned7.
Why now
11 deadlines

Eleven dated compliance, identity and technology deadlines fall between December 2026 and December 2027. See the trigger calendar.

  • The Cybersecurity Act has applied since 15 Jan 2026. It sets 24 h / 72 h incident reporting and requires management training1920.
  • DORA has applied since 17 Jan 2025. Finansinspektionen collects the register of ICT contracts every year by 28 February29.
  • The government's cloud policy of 28 May 2026 takes a risk-based view of providers outside the EU and expects exit clauses24.
  • Applications for the state e-ID, Sverige-id, open on 1 Dec 2026. EU wallets follow on 24 Dec 20263835.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

Start with a Nordic DC partner and Swedish SI and MSP resellers. Open a Swedish aktiebolag (AB) between month 6 and 12.

  • Host regulated workloads in a Swedish partner DC or in XcellHost's EU partner DCs, so the hosting itself is not a third-country transfer.
  • An AB needs SEK 25,000 of share capital. At least half the board and the managing director must live in the EEA43.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC. India has no EU adequacy decision, so this runs under SCCs with a transfer assessment3132.

    HighMature, well-supplied market

    Hyperscaler regions, Nordic DC groups and Swedish cloud providers are established, and buyers prefer local-language support2.

    HighSovereignty and third-country scrutiny

    The cloud policy, eSam guidance and memo Ds 2025:29 make buyers weigh supplier origin and foreign access2426.

    Mitigation: EU hosting by default, EU-held keys, SCCs plus TIA for support access, and exit plans in every contract.
    MediumProtective security limits

    Security-sensitive activities need assessments and possible consultation with the authority, which can stop a procurement27.

    Mitigation: stay out of security-classified scopes, and qualify every public or defence-adjacent deal early.
    MediumAuthority changes

    MCF tasks moved to FRA in July 2026, and AI Act authorities are still proposed2328.

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with Swedish counsel, and refreshes to this page.
    MediumEEA residency for the AB board

    Half the board and the MD must live in the EEA unless Bolagsverket grants a dispensation43.

    Mitigation: recruit the country lead and a non-executive director in Sweden before incorporation.
    MediumSupply-chain incidents

    The Aug 2025 attack on Miljödata disrupted more than 200 municipalities17; supplier risk is now board-level.

    Mitigation: publish our own security evidence, run incident drills with partners, and offer supplier assurance.

    Changes in SEK, EUR, INR and USD rates, plus partner credit risk.
