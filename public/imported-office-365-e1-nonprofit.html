    Overview

    The licence every NGO desk starts on — browser Office, mail and Teams for ₹0.

    Office 365 E1 is Microsoft's web-first enterprise plan. Per Microsoft, it gives each user the web and mobile versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote and Outlook, a business mailbox on your own domain, Teams, SharePoint team sites and 1 TB of OneDrive storage. There are no desktop Office apps — everything runs in the browser or on a phone.

    On XcellHost's nonprofit track, E1 is the free tier for your first 300 verified users*: the people who mostly read and write email, join meetings and edit shared files — admin teams, volunteers and field offices. Staff who need desktop Office, managed laptops or advanced security move to Microsoft 365 E3 Nonprofit or E5 Nonprofit in the same tenant.

    Eligibility & TechSoup India verification

    Microsoft decides who qualifies. XcellHost prepares the paperwork with you and sets up the tenant the day you are validated.

    1. 1
      Check you qualify

      Per Microsoft: a nonprofit or NGO with recognised legal status in India, operating not-for-profit with a mission that benefits the community. Public libraries and museums may also qualify.

    2. 2
      Gather documents

      Typically your registration certificate (trust, society or Section 8 company), PAN, 12A / 80G and NGO Darpan ID. Microsoft may ask for others.

    3. 3
      Register & validate

      An employee or strategic volunteer registers on Microsoft for Nonprofits — per Microsoft, an IT provider can't register for you. TechSoup India registration helps with documents; per TechSoup, Microsoft has run the validation itself since August 2025.

      Claim seats, stay eligible

      Once validated, XcellHost links your tenant and assigns seats. Microsoft asks grantees to re-attest to its donor rules regularly.

    India compliance

    Beneficiary data, donor records, kept the Indian way.

    E1 gives you the basics; the controls you need depend on what data you hold. We help you decide — this is guidance, not legal advice.

    DPDP Act 2023

    NGOs that collect beneficiary or donor data are data fiduciaries. Children's data needs verifiable parental consent. E1 gives MFA, audit logs and controlled sharing; labels and DLP need E3.

    CERT-In directions (2022)

    Covered entities report cyber incidents within 6 hours and keep logs for 180 days. We help you check what applies and how long Microsoft 365 keeps your audit logs at your licence level.

    FCRA & 12A / 80G records

    Grant files, utilisation certificates and donor receipts belong in SharePoint libraries with versioning and controlled access — not personal drives.

    Data residency
