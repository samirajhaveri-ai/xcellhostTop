Industry challenges

Hospitals run on software now. Downtime is a clinical risk.

Six problems we hear from hospital IT heads, lab owners and clinic groups — and the part of HealthCare Cloud that answers each one.

01

Imaging volumes keep growing

Every new CT or MRI adds terabytes a year. On-site PACS storage fills, and buying another array becomes an annual event.

Elastic PACS archive in India
02

Ransomware targets hospitals

An encrypted HIS stops registration, billing and pharmacy at once. Backups on the same network are often encrypted too.

Immutable, air-gapped backups
03

ABDM integration

ABHA linking, facility and professional registries, consent-based sharing — each needs a hosted service that stays up and keeps logs.

Sandbox and production environments
04

Who opened that record?

Shared logins and vendor remote access leave no trail. The DPDP Act expects reasonable safeguards; CERT-In expects logs for 180 days.

PAM, MFA and SIEM on every access
05

Medical devices on flat networks

Modalities, monitors and lab analysers often run old operating systems that cannot be patched. One flat VLAN puts all of them at risk.

Segmented device zones, watched 24×7
06

Small IT teams

Most hospitals and labs run IT with a handful of people. Nights, weekends and audit season stretch them thin.

24×7 NOC + SOC from Mumbai

    Regulatory mapping

    Requirement, how we help, and the evidence you keep.

    Each row names the instrument and date as published, checked October 2026. HealthCare Cloud helps you meet these requirements; your organisation and your software vendors stay responsible for their own compliance and certifications.

    ABDMHealth IDs and registriesNational Health Authority — ABHA (14-digit health account), Health Facility Registry, Healthcare Professionals Registry
    We host the HIS or HMIS service that captures and verifies ABHA and links your facility and doctors to the registries, on uptime-monitored infrastructure in India.
    Hosting architecture · uptime reports · API access logs
    ABDMSandbox, certification, then production keysNHA HIP / HIU guidelines — integrate with the ABDM Sandbox (milestones M1 ABHA, M2 HIP, M3 HIU), certification through empanelled agencies, production access after verification
    Separate sandbox, test and production environments with their own credentials and network rules, so certification evidence and live traffic never mix. Certification itself is done by your software vendor with the empanelled agency.
    Environment register · change log · credential vault records
    ABDMConsent-based sharing, high availabilitySame guidelines — consent artefacts, revocable consent, HIP service "must offer high uptime"; FHIR R4; records kept as the Health Data Management Policy and Information Security Policy require
    99.95% uptime SLA, DR in India*, immutable backups and logs of every consent-linked API call in SIEM. Data migration support if you change provider — the guidelines expect it.
    SLA report · DR drill report · export test record
    MoHFWTeleconsultation recordsTelemedicine Practice Guidelines — 25 Mar 2020 (Board of Governors, MCI; Appendix 5 to the 2002 Regulations): keep logs of each interaction and the records used
    Hosting for your teleconsult platform with call logs, chat transcripts and documents stored in India under a retention policy you set. Access by registered practitioners only, with MFA.
    Retention policy · storage location attestation · access logs
    NABHDigital Health Standards1st edition for hospitals released 17 Sep 2023; Silver, Gold and Platinum levels; HIS / EMR certification for vendors; cybersecurity is an assessed area
    Infrastructure evidence for the IT and cyber chapters: backup and restore tests, DR drills, access control, patching and SOC reports, ready for your assessors.
    Restore-test records · patch reports · SOC monthly reports
    CDSCOSoftware that is a medical deviceMedical Devices Rules 2017; device definition includes software (S.O. 648(E), 11 Feb 2020); licensing for Class A/B from 1 Oct 2022 and Class C/D from 1 Oct 2023
    For SaMD makers: controlled hosting with change records, environment documentation and logs that support your quality system. Classification and licensing stay with the manufacturer.
    Change tickets · environment specs · access and audit logs
    DPDPReasonable security safeguardsDPDP Act 2023; DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025 with 18-month phased compliance; breach intimation without delay
    Encryption, role-based access, logging and a breach runbook that drafts the Board and patient notices. DPO support through vDPO.
    Safeguards register · breach runbook · notice templates
    DPDPChildren's health dataDPDP Rules 2025 — verifiable parental consent, with exemptions for essential services such as healthcare
    Paediatric records hosted under the same controls; we help your DPO document which processing relies on the healthcare exemption.
    Processing register entries · consent flows
    CERT-In6-hour reporting, 180-day logsDirections under section 70B — 28 Apr 2022
    SOC triage inside the 6-hour window, logs kept in India for at least 180 days, NTP-synced clocks across HIS, PACS and network devices.
    Incident report drafts · log retention evidence
    PCI DSSv4.0.1 for patient paymentsJune 2024; future-dated requirements effective 31 Mar 2025
    Billing and payment pages kept out of card scope where possible; segmented zone and readiness help via PCI DSS consulting where card data is handled.
    Segmentation diagram · scope note
    DoTVisitor Wi-Fi loginsDoT Wi-Fi directions (23 Feb 2009) — users authenticated, e.g. by SMS login, records kept one year
    Visitor Wi-Fi with OTP login and one-year records through Managed Wi-Fi, kept apart from clinical networks.
    Login records · VLAN design
    MCADaily backup of books in IndiaCompanies (Accounts) Rules, rule 3(5) — since 5 Aug 2022
    Daily backups of hospital accounting and billing on servers in India; see daily backup of books.
    Backup job logs · server-location sheet
