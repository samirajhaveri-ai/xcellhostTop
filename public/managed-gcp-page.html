    Overview

    Google Cloud gives you the building blocks. We keep them running.

    Managed Google Cloud is XcellHost's operations service for companies that already run on Google Cloud — or are about to. We take the day-to-day work off your team: watching alerts at 3 a.m., applying patches in agreed windows, testing restores, tightening IAM and cutting the bill.

    Google Cloud is organised as an organisation of folders and projects, with services such as Compute Engine, Google Kubernetes Engine, Cloud Run, Cloud SQL and BigQuery inside them. That flexibility is the point — and it is also why estates drift: projects pile up without labels, VMs stay oversized, logs get kept in the wrong place and nobody owns the on-call rota.

    XcellHost brings a written runbook, a 24×7 NOC/SOC in Mumbai and a monthly review built around Google's Well-Architected Framework: operational excellence, security, reliability, cost, performance and sustainability. You keep your organisation and your data. We bring the people, process and reporting — and one INR invoice with GST.

    Security & compliance · India

    Built to help you meet DPDP, CERT-In and sector rules.

    Google secures the cloud; you and XcellHost secure what runs in it. We set up and keep checking the controls Indian auditors ask about.

    6 hCERT-In directions (April 2022): report specified cyber incidents within six hours of noticing them.
    180 daysCERT-In: keep ICT system logs for a rolling 180 days, within India.
    DPDP 2023Digital Personal Data Protection Act: reasonable security safeguards and breach intimation to the Board and affected people.
    Summaries for orientation only — not legal advice. XcellHost helps you meet these; accountability stays with your organisation.
    Data in India

    Resource-location organisation policy restricting projects to Mumbai and Delhi where the service allows.

    Log retention

    Audit and system logs routed to a locked bucket in India, sized for 180-day (or longer) retention.

    Least-privilege IAM

    Group-based roles, no owner keys on laptops, quarterly access reviews.

    Encryption keys

    Customer-managed keys in Cloud KMS for sensitive datasets, secrets in Secret Manager.

    Edge protection

    Cloud Armor WAF and DDoS policies on public load balancers.

    Posture & findings

    Security Command Center findings triaged by our SOC; integrates with Managed Sentinel if you run a SIEM.

    Regulated? For RBI-, SEBI- or IRDAI-regulated entities we support outsourcing due-diligence, audit-access clauses, exit plans and evidence packs that help you meet your regulator's cloud and outsourcing expectations.
