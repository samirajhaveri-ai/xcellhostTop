Overview

Every subdomain, one certificate — from the CA most enterprises already trust.

The DigiCert Wildcard SSL certificate secures one registered domain and every first-level subdomain under it — shop., mail., app., pay. — at organisation-validation (OV) level. When your team launches a new subdomain next month, it is already covered: no new order, no second validation, no extra invoice. It chains to DigiCert's roots — trusted by more than 99% of browsers and devices — and carries a US$1M warranty.     Wildcards in India

    More subdomains, more exposure — regulators expect all of it encrypted.

    No Indian rule names a certificate brand. Every framework does expect data in transit to be protected and certificates to be tracked — a wildcard makes that one asset instead of many.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Anyone handling personal data must take reasonable security safeguards. Every login, form and API on every subdomain should be encrypted — the wildcard covers new ones from day one.

    CERT-In directions

    Cyber incidents, including website compromise, must be reported within six hours. Expired or missing certificates on forgotten subdomains are a common way in.

    RBI & SEBI entities

    RBI's IT directions and SEBI's CSCRF expect strong encryption on customer channels and an inventory of critical assets — certificates and expiry dates included.

    MeitY & public sector

    Government web guidelines expect sites served over HTTPS. XcellHost is MeitY-empanelled and installs certificates for public-sector hosting.
