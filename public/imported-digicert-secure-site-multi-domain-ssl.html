      Multi-domain TLS in India

      Every domain you run is in scope. One certificate keeps them consistent.

      No Indian rule names a certificate brand, but each framework expects data in transit to be protected — on every domain, not just the main one.

      DPDP Act, 2023

      Anyone processing personal data must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent breaches. Forgotten campaign or regional domains collecting leads count too.

      CERT-In directions

      Incidents such as website compromise must be reported within six hours. A single inventory of domains and certificates makes those incidents easier to spot and scope.

      RBI & SEBI

      RBI's IT governance directions and SEBI's cyber-resilience framework expect encrypted customer channels and managed certificates. OV puts your verified entity on every login domain.

      MeitY & government sites

      Ministries, PSUs and their vendors follow MeitY guidance such as GIGW, which expects portals to be served securely over HTTPS — often across several domains.
