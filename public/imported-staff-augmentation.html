Labour codes

Four codes replace 29 central laws

The Code on Wages 2019, Industrial Relations Code 2020, Code on Social Security 2020 and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code 2020 took effect on 21 November 2025, per the Ministry of Labour & Employment. Appointment letters for every worker are now mandatory.

Code on Wages

One definition of wages, paid on time

A single definition of "wages" applies across the codes; if excluded allowances exceed 50% of total pay, the excess counts as wages. For a monthly wage period, wages are due before the seventh day of the following month. Minimum wages apply to all employees.

Social Security

Fixed-term staff and gratuity

Fixed-term employees get the same benefits as permanent staff in proportion to service, including gratuity after one year rather than five. This matters for contract-to-hire and long engagements.

OSH Code · Ch. XI

Contract labour, now inside the OSH Code

The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act 1970 has been folded into the OSH Code. Its contract-labour chapter applies at 50 or more contract workers (previously 20), allows a single licence covering several states, bars contract labour in an establishment's core activities with listed exceptions, and makes the principal employer pay wages if a contractor defaults.

GST

18% on manpower supply, forward charge

Staffing services fall under SAC heading 9985 (for example 998513 contract staffing, 998514 temporary staffing) at 18% GST under CBIC Notification 11/2017-CT (Rate). Tax is charged on the full invoice value, including the salary component. Reverse charge applies to security personnel from non-corporate suppliers, not to IT staffing from a company like XcellHost.

DPDP

Your data, handled as a processor

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and its Rules notified in November 2025 (phased in), engineers process personal data only on your instructions. Our contract sets out access, logging, breach reporting to you and deletion. See vDPO for your own DPDP programme.
