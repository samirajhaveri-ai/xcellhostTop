    Overview

    SOC 2 is how US buyers decide whether to trust your platform.

    SOC 2 is an attestation report on the controls at a service organisation, built on the AICPA's Trust Services Criteria. Security — the nine common criteria CC1 to CC9 — is always in scope. Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality and Privacy are added when your customers need them. A Type I report checks that controls are designed well on one date; a Type II report tests that they actually worked over an observation window of 3 to 12 months.

    XcellHost gets you ready as a fixed-fee project — typically 3–6 months of readiness, then the observation window. The report itself can only be issued by an independent, licensed CPA firm; we coordinate the audit and can recommend partners. We hold SOC 2 Type I and Type II for our own 24×7 cloud and SOC operations in Mumbai, so you work with people who sit through the same fieldwork every year.

    The Trust Services Criteria

    One mandatory category, four optional ones — 61 criteria in all.

    Security is the common criteria every SOC 2 report covers. Add the other four only when a customer, contract or product promise calls for them — each one adds controls, evidence and audit time.

    Bridge letters

    Your Type II report ends on a date; buyers' reviews don't. A bridge (gap) letter is a statement from your management — not the CPA firm — that nothing material changed between the period end and today. We draft it with you each quarter until the next report lands.
