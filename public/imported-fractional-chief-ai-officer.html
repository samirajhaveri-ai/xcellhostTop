Overview

Pilots are easy. Owning what comes next is the hard part.

Most companies now have a few AI pilots that worked — a support bot, an invoice-reading agent, a sales copilot. Then progress stalls. Nobody owns the roadmap, new ideas arrive every week, vendors pitch overlapping tools, risk teams start asking questions and the board wants to know what it's all worth. A Chief AI Officer solves that, but a full-time hire is expensive, slow to find and often more than you need today.

XcellHost's Fractional Chief AI Officer is a senior AI leader who joins your existing planning meetings for 4, 8 or 12 days a month. They own the AI roadmap, evaluate vendors, set up governance that satisfies RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and the DPDP Act, run the portfolio of live AI systems — scaling, rebuilding or retiring them — and report value to leadership. Time flexes with activity, and the role works alongside your CIO and CISO rather than replacing them.

Why it matters in India

Regulators, boards and customers are all asking.

RBI FREE-AI framework

RBI's framework for responsible AI in banks and NBFCs expects board-approved AI policy, risk tiers and human oversight.

SEBI on AI/ML

SEBI expects market intermediaries using AI to govern models, test them and stay accountable for their outputs.

DPDP Act & Rules

AI that touches personal data needs a lawful basis, purpose limits, safeguards and breach readiness.

India AI governance guidelines

MeitY's guidelines favour principle-based, accountable AI adoption — easier with one owner.

CERT-In reporting

AI systems are part of your attack surface; incidents still fall under the 6-hour reporting rule.
