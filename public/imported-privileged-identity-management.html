    Overview

    Admin rights should be borrowed, not owned.

    In most Microsoft 365 and Azure tenants, a handful of people hold Global Administrator or other powerful roles permanently — every hour of every day, on every device they sign in from. Most of that time, nobody is using the privilege. If one of those accounts is phished, the attacker inherits it.

    Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management (PIM) changes the default. People become eligible for a role instead of holding it. When they need it, they activate it for a set window — after MFA, a business justification and, where you choose, an approval — and PIM removes it when the window ends.

    XcellHost designs the role model with you, converts standing assignments without locking anyone out, sets activation rules per role, protects your emergency (break-glass) accounts and runs the first access review. Licences come from us too: PIM needs Microsoft Entra ID P2 (₹755 per user per month) or Entra ID Governance, billed in INR with a GST invoice.

    Why it matters

    Attackers go for the admin account. PIM makes it empty most of the day.

    Many serious breaches start with a stolen sign-in, then look for an account with standing privilege. Taking that privilege away by default shrinks what one compromised password can do.

    Identity is the front door

    Phished passwords, token theft and reused credentials are a common way in. An account that is only eligible for a role cannot use it without passing MFA and your activation rules again.

    CERT-In 6 hours · 180-day logs

    CERT-In's April 2022 directions require reporting cyber incidents within 6 hours and keeping ICT logs for 180 days in India. PIM's activation history shows who held which role, when and why; XcellHost streams it to Log Analytics or Sentinel so it outlives Entra's built-in 30-day audit retention.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal-data breaches. Limiting who can reach mailboxes, files and tenant settings — and for how long — is one of the clearest safeguards to show.

    RBI · SEBI · IRDAI

    Regulated entities are expected to apply least privilege, control and review privileged access and use MFA for administrators. PIM helps you meet those expectations with time-bound access and review evidence; your auditor decides what is sufficient.

    Capabilities

    Everything PIM controls, set up the way your team works.

    These are Microsoft Entra PIM features. XcellHost decides the right setting for each role with you and documents why.

    Just-in-time activation

    Eligible users switch a role on only when a task needs it.

    Time-bound assignments

    Eligibility itself can carry a start and end date — ideal for contractors and projects.

    Approval to activate

    Named approvers for sensitive roles; Microsoft recommends at least two.

    MFA or authentication context

    Require MFA on activation, or a Conditional Access authentication context such as phishing-resistant sign-in.

    Justification & ticket

    A reason on every activation, plus an optional ticket number for your ITSM trail.

    Notifications & alerts

    Emails when roles are activated or assigned, and alerts for risky patterns such as too many Global Admins.

    Access reviews

    Periodic reviews confirm each person still needs a role; unneeded ones are removed.

    Audit history

    Downloadable history of assignments and activations for internal and external audit.

    Last-admin protection

    PIM stops you removing the last active Global Administrator or Privileged Role Administrator.
