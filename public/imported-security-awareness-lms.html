Overview

Awareness people finish, not awareness they skip.

The usual model is one long session a year: a slide deck, a sign-in sheet, and most of it forgotten by the next quarter. Attackers do not wait a year. They send a fake invoice on a Tuesday and a "CEO" WhatsApp on a Friday.

XcellSecure Security Awareness LMS replaces that with short, frequent, interesting lessons — animated videos, comic-strip stories, micro-lessons and quick quizzes — delivered in the language people think in and matched to what their job exposes them to. It is part of a people-security programme: train, test with a phishing simulation, and teach again where people slip.

The LMS helps you meet the awareness parts of these frameworks with tracked training and exportable evidence. We are not a regulator or law firm; confirm what applies to your entity with your compliance team.

DPDP s.8(5)DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

Data fiduciaries must protect personal data with reasonable security safeguards (section 8(5); Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules 2025). Staff who handle personal data well are part of those safeguards — the LMS records who was trained, on what, and when.

6 hCERT-In directions (April 2022)

Specified cyber incidents must be reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of being noticed. That only works if staff recognise and report incidents fast. Our "reporting an incident" module teaches who to tell, how and how quickly.

RBIBanks & NBFCs

RBI's Cyber Security Framework for banks (June 2016) lists user, employee and management awareness among its baseline controls, and its 2023 IT governance Master Direction expects ongoing training. The LMS gives you the programme and the record.

SEBI CSCRFSecurities market entities

SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (August 2024) includes awareness and training requirements for regulated entities. Role-based paths and completion reports support them; check the requirements for your category.

MeitY · DPDPConsent & notice basics

Short modules explain consent, notices and data-principal rights in plain words for HR, sales and support staff. Pair with our vDPO service for the privacy programme itself.

RecordsEvidence in India

Completion, quiz and certificate records export as PDF and CSV for auditors and regulators. Data residency options are confirmed in the quotation.*

A.6.3ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Annex A control 6.3, "Information security awareness, education and training", asks that personnel get appropriate awareness and regular updates on policy. See our ISO 27001 readiness service.

12.6PCI DSS v4.0.1

Requirement 12.6 covers a formal awareness programme, training at hire and at least every 12 months, and (12.6.3.1) phishing and social-engineering topics. See our PCI DSS service.

SP 800-50r1NIST learning programmes

NIST SP 800-50 Rev. 1 (September 2024) describes how to build a cybersecurity and privacy learning programme. Our plan → deliver → measure loop follows the same structure.

CIS 14CIS Controls v8

Control 14, "Security Awareness and Skills Training", covers a programme, role-specific training and recognising social engineering. Role paths map to its safeguards.

SCORM · xAPIe-learning standards

SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 and xAPI packages from your own HR or compliance teams can run and report alongside the library.

NeutralCertificates

LMS certificates record course completion. They are not professional certifications, and we are not affiliated with any certification body.
