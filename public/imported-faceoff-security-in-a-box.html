Why now, in India

A face on a video call is no longer proof. Neither is a familiar voice.

Face-swaps, cloned voices and fake officers on video are everyday fraud tools. Indian authorities have issued advisories, amended the IT Rules and tightened video KYC checks. These are the rules and warnings the box is built to help with.

Digital arrest

Fake officers, live on video

MHA has warned that fraudsters pose as police, CBI, Narcotics, RBI or ED officials, keep victims on Skype or other video calls, and use studios styled as police stations with uniforms to look genuine1.

3,962 Skype IDs and 83,668 WhatsApp accounts used for digital arrests blocked by I4C; 7.81 lakh SIM cards blocked till 28 Feb 20252.

IT Rules

Synthetic content rules, in force

The IT Rules amendment on synthetically generated information was notified on 10 Feb 2026 and applies from 20 Feb 2026. AI-generated content must be labelled with traceable metadata, and unlawful content must come down within 3 hours of a government or court order3.

CERT-In

Report in 6 hours. Verify by call-back.

CERT-In's directions of 28 Apr 2022 require specified incidents to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them4. Advisory CIAD-2024-0060 (27 Nov 2024, severity High) asks for call-back verification on sensitive transactions, detection tools and forensics capability5.

RBI V-CIP

Video KYC must prove a live person

RBI's KYC Directions, 2025 ask V-CIP for face liveness and spoof detection, face matching, live GPS geo-tagging, real-time (not pre-recorded) interaction and V-CIP data stored in India6.

01 · Deepfake media

SyntheticMediaGuard

Deepfake image & video detection.

Checks photos, recorded clips and live video for face-swaps, re-enacted faces and fully generated people before they are trusted by a KYC officer, an editor or an investigator.

How it works, in general

  1. Frames are sampled and the face region is isolated.
  2. Models look for blend seams, texture and lighting that do not fit the scene, and lips that do not match speech.
  3. Signals are combined into a verdict; flagged media opens a case with the evidence kept on the box.

Use cases

  • Video KYC and V-CIP sessions
  • Viral clips before a newsroom runs them
  • Digital evidence triage for cyber cells
  • Remote job interviews and board calls

Confirm with FaceOff* supported formats and resolutions · live stream vs file · results on your own sample set · streams per box

02 · Behaviour

BehaviorID

Behavioural identity verification.

Checks that the person using a session behaves like the real account holder, so a stolen password, OTP or remote-access session is not enough to take over an account.

How it works, in general

  1. The system learns a person's usual rhythm: typing, swiping, pointer movement and navigation.
  2. Each new session is compared continuously, not just at login.
  3. A mismatch triggers step-up verification or blocks a risky action.

Use cases

  • Net-banking and app account takeover
  • Mule accounts run by one operator
  • Admin and treasury consoles
  • Call-centre agent desktops

Confirm with FaceOff* web, Android and iOS collection · learning period · how behavioural data is minimised. Behaviour that identifies a person is personal data under the DPDP Act, so plan notice and purpose up front.

03 · Voice

Voice Forensics

Cloned & synthetic voice detection.

Flags calls and voice notes made with cloned or generated voices, such as a "CFO" approving a payment or a "relative" asking for urgent money.

How it works, in general

  1. Audio is analysed for traces that synthesis tends to leave: odd spectral patterns, too-steady pitch, missing breaths, channel seams.
  2. Where a person has consented, the call can be compared with their enrolled voice.
  3. The result routes the call: carry on, call back on a known number, or stop.

Use cases

  • Contact-centre caller checks
  • CEO and vendor payment fraud
  • Helplines and grievance desks
  • Voice evidence for investigations

Confirm with FaceOff* Indian languages and accents · telephony and SIP integration · minimum audio length · real-time vs recorded

04 · Identity

Zero-Trust Identity

Continuous verification, every access.

Treats every access request as untrusted until checked, instead of trusting a session for hours after one login.

How it works, in general

  1. Each request is checked against who the user is, the device, behaviour and context such as time and location.
  2. Low-risk requests pass quietly; risky ones ask for more proof.
  3. Every decision is logged for audit and incident response.

Use cases

  • Privileged and admin users
  • Remote staff and vendors
  • Control rooms and sensitive registries
  • Approvals for high-value transactions

Confirm with FaceOff* identity provider and SSO connectors · MFA methods · policy model. See also XcellHost AI Identity Security.

05 · Threat intelligence

TrustShield OSINT

Open-source threat intelligence.

Watches public sources for fake profiles of your leaders, lookalike domains, fake customer-care numbers, leaked data and deepfakes that use your name.

How it works, in general

  1. Public sources are collected: social media, websites, domain registrations, app stores and open forums.
  2. Findings are matched to your brands, people and assets, and ranked by risk.
  3. Each finding becomes a case with evidence and a suggested next step.

Use cases

  • Executive and brand impersonation
  • Fake helpline numbers and apps
  • Police and agency intelligence
  • Early warning for the SOC

Note. Collecting open sources needs controlled outbound access; analysis stays on the box*. Confirm with FaceOff* source coverage · languages · takedown support.

06 · Video

Video Intelligence

Real-time video analytics.

Turns camera feeds into alerts and searchable events, processed on the appliance rather than sent to an outside cloud.

How it works, in general

  1. Camera streams are read on your network.
  2. Models detect people, objects and events you define, such as entry to a restricted zone.
  3. Alerts go to your control room; footage and metadata stay on site.

Use cases

  • Campuses and data centres
  • Bank branches and ATMs
  • Public spaces and events
  • Critical infrastructure sites

Confirm with FaceOff* streams per box · camera protocols · retention settings. CCTV images of people are personal data; signage and retention rules apply.

07 · On-box AI

Home-Grown SLM

In-house small language model that runs fully on the box. No data leaves your network.

Gives analysts and officers a plain-language assistant without sending case data, faces or call recordings to a public AI service.

How it works, in general

  1. An analyst asks a question: why was this flagged, what links these cases, draft the incident note.
  2. The small language model answers on the appliance, using case data that is already there.
  3. Answers and prompts are logged on site for review.

Use cases

  • Air-gapped and sovereign SOCs
  • Police and government case desks
  • BFSI fraud and risk teams
  • First drafts of CERT-In reports

Confirm with FaceOff* languages · model updates without internet · hardware sizing.
