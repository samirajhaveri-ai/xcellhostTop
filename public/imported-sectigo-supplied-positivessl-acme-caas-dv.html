Overview

Budget DV certificates that never lapse.

PositiveSSL ACME Certificate-as-a-Service is Sectigo’s low-cost PositiveSSL DV certificate delivered through the ACME protocol. Your ACME client binds to the subscription once, then validates, fetches, installs and renews certificates on schedule — by HTTP file (http-01) or DNS record (dns-01).

A standard subscription costs ₹ 2,175.00 a year (list ₹ 6,090.00) and covers domain + www; a wildcard main domain is ₹ 14,790.00. Add domains up to 101. It carries a US$50,000 warranty and a dynamic seal — the right fit for blogs, small business sites, staging and internal hosts. Need a higher warranty? See Sectigo ACME CaaS.

Works withcertbot · acme.shwin-acme on IIScert-manager · DockerCaddy · LiteSpeedAWS · Azure · GCP · DigitalOcean

Lifetimes are shrinking. Public certificates are limited to 200 days from 15 Mar 2026, 100 days from 15 Mar 2027 and 47 days from 15 Mar 2029 (CA/Browser Forum SC-081). With ACME, each of those renewals happens without anyone logging in. More in the 47-day SSL guide.
