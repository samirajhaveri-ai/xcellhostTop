    Overview

    Phishing simulation, run as a service — so you get habits, not a one-off test.

    A phishing simulation sends staff a harmless email that looks like the kind of message attackers use. Nothing is stolen and nothing runs. What you learn is who opens it, who clicks, and — most importantly — who reports it. People who slip get a short, relevant lesson straight away; people who report it get a thank-you.

    XcellHost runs the whole programme for you. We plan a 12-month calendar, pick realistic but generic scenarios on test domains, schedule the sends, assign training, deal with the "is this real?" questions, and give you a monthly report with click, report and training numbers by department. Where you hold Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 2 or Microsoft 365 E5, we use its built-in Attack simulation training; otherwise we run a third-party platform — confirmed at scoping.

    What's included

    Six kinds of simulation, one awareness programme.

    We rotate techniques so staff learn the pattern, not one template. Every scenario is generic, uses a fake test domain and is approved by you before it goes out.

    01

    Credential-harvest simulation

    A link to a practice sign-in page. We record only that the form was submitted — the password itself is never stored.

    Lesson: check the address bar before you sign in
    02

    Attachment simulation

    A harmless file that tells us it was opened. It runs nothing on the device.

    Lesson: unexpected attachments get a second look
    03

    Link simulation

    A link to a practice page, or a link inside an attachment, to teach hovering and checking the real destination.

    Lesson: the text of a link is not its destination
    04

    QR-code simulation

    A QR code in an email or on a printed notice, so staff learn that scanning is clicking — on a phone, away from desktop protection.

    Lesson: treat a QR code like any other link
    05

    OAuth consent simulation

    A practice "this app wants access to your mailbox" prompt, to show how consent can hand over data without a password.

    Lesson: read what an app is asking for
    06

    SMS & voice awareness

    Awareness modules and, on Enterprise, agreed SMS or call exercises on lures such as "urgent payment" or "OTP verification".

    Lesson: never share an OTP, verify on a known number

    Security & compliance in India

    Awareness training regulators expect — with the evidence to show it.

    A managed programme helps you meet awareness and incident-handling expectations. It does not, on its own, make you compliant.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Simulation data is personal data

    We collect the minimum (opened, clicked, reported, trained), never passwords, keep results for an agreed period and use them for training only. We help you add the programme to your employee privacy notice.

    CERT-In

    6-hour reporting, 180-day logs

    When a staff report turns out to be a real incident, the SOC escalation path supports CERT-In's 6-hour reporting window, and reported-mail logs are retained for at least 180 days.

    RBI · SEBI · IRDAI

    Regulated entities

    Banks, NBFCs, brokers and insurers are expected to run regular cyber-awareness programmes for staff. Monthly reports and completion records give your auditors evidence of yours.

    ISO 27001

    Awareness control evidence

    Training completion and campaign records map to the awareness, education and training control in your ISMS — useful at surveillance audits.

    Why the report rate matters 1 report

    One early report from a staff member can be enough for a SOC to find and pull the same message from every other mailbox. That is why we measure reporting, not just clicks.

    • Reporting is rewarded, never punished
    • Results by department, not public shaming
    • No real passwords captured, ever

    Regulatory references are summaries for orientation, not legal advice. Check the current text of the DPDP Act and rules, CERT-In directions and your sector regulator's circulars with your compliance team.
