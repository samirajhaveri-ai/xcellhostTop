Why now
10 deadlines

Ten dated compliance and technology deadlines fall between December 2026 and December 2027. Each one starts a project. See the trigger calendar.

  • KSC amendment (NIS2) in force since 3 Apr 2026. Entities register by 3 Oct 2026 and must comply by 3 Apr 202725.

    Pricing & tax approach

    • EUR price book for partners. PLN billing by the partner, or by our sp. z o.o. later. Review FX quarterly.
    • VAT: 23% standard, with reduced rates of 8%, 5% and 0%. The place-of-supply rules decide whether Polish VAT applies43. Confirm reverse-charge treatment with an adviser.
    • Withholding tax: 20% on intangible services paid to non-residents, which can be exempt under a treaty's business-profits clause with formalities. The India treaty treats technical services as royalties, at 15%45.
    • CIT: 19%, or 9% for small taxpayers and in the first tax year44. KSeF e-invoicing applies once the entity issues Polish invoices42.

    • Content: a KSC registration and gap checklist, a DORA ICT-provider evidence pack and an AI Act inventory template.
    • LinkedIn and partner co-marketing, plus outreach through India–Poland business networks.
