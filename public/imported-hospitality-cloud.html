What Hospitality Cloud is

The systems behind the welcome, run for you.

A hotel or restaurant runs on a handful of systems that cannot stop: the PMS at the front desk, the POS in the outlets, the booking engine and channel manager, guest Wi-Fi and the cameras. Too often they sit on a server in the back office with no backup, one internet line and a shared admin password.

XcellCloud Hospitality Cloud moves them into our Mumbai Tier IV data centre and runs the network around them: managed guest Wi-Fi with OTP login, SD-WAN between properties, a PCI-ready payment segment with PCI DSS support, WAAP and CDN for your booking site, and a 24×7 SOC.

Industry challenges

Six moments in a guest's stay where IT lets you down.

Follow one guest from booking to the next visit. Each stop is a problem hospitality teams tell us about — and the fix.

01 · Book

Overbookings

Website, travel sites and phone bookings update rooms at different speeds, and two guests get the same room.

Central reservations hosted with one inventory
02 · Arrive

Paper and photocopies

ID copies in drawers, foreign-guest Form C done late, consent never asked.

Consent and Form C reminders in the hosted PMS
03 · Stay

Wi-Fi on a shared password

One password on a card, no login records, guests on the same network as the PMS.

OTP portal, isolated guest network
04 · Pay

Card data in the wrong places

Card numbers typed into the PMS or kept on paper widen PCI scope and break card-on-file rules.

Tokenised payments, small PCI segment
05 · Night

Nobody watching at 2 a.m.

A POS admin login from a new device or a ransomware alert waits until the morning shift.

24×7 SOC and CCTV alerts
06 · Return

Guest history lost

A crashed server or a property-level system means no history, no preferences, no loyalty.

Central guest profile with backup and DR
