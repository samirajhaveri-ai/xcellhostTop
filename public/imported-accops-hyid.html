    Overview

    One identity check in front of every app — not one per app.

    Accops HyID is the identity and access management product in Accops' Digital Workspace suite. Accops describes it as adaptive MFA and SSO for on-prem and cloud apps: it adds a second factor — OTP, push, hardware token, FIDO key, fingerprint, face or QR code — to the apps and logons you already run, and lets users reach them through one sign-in.

    XcellHost designs the policies, connects HyID to your directory, enrols your users and keeps it running. We host it in our Mumbai cloud or install it on your servers, wire it to your VPN, VDI, Windows logons and SAML or OAuth apps, and watch it 24×7. You get one INR invoice and one support number in Mumbai.

    How it works

    HyID sits between the sign-in and the app — whatever protocol the app speaks.

    A typical layout. Blue packets are normal sign-ins; the orange one comes from an unusual context and is asked for a FIDO key before it continues.

    01Check the password

    Against Active Directory, LDAP, Azure AD, a local database or a federated IdP such as Okta.

    02Read the context

    Role, device posture, location and time decide whether to sign in silently, ask for a factor, or block.

    03Ask for a factor

    Push, OTP, token, FIDO key, fingerprint, face or QR — per user group, per app.

    04Open the app and log it

    SSO over SAML, OAuth or RADIUS; every decision lands in the audit log and your SIEM.
