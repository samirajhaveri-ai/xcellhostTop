Overview

Email carries your most sensitive work. S/MIME proves who sent it.

Invoices, bank details, salary files, contracts and KYC documents still move by email — and ordinary email has no built-in proof of who wrote it or whether it was changed on the way. An email signing (S/MIME) certificate fixes both. It attaches a digital signature, issued against an identity a certificate authority has checked, so the recipient's mail app can show that the message really came from you and that not a word was altered after you hit Send.

The same certificate also encrypts: once two people have exchanged signed mail, they can send messages and attachments that only the intended mailbox can open — not the network, not a compromised relay, not someone reading a forwarded copy. XcellHost supplies Sectigo and DigiCert S/MIME certificates for individuals, teams and whole companies, helps with the identity checks, installs them on Outlook, Apple Mail, Gmail and phones, and bills in rupees with GST.

What S/MIME is

Two keys, two jobs: your key signs, theirs encrypts.

S/MIME (Secure/Multipurpose Internet Mail Extensions) is the open standard built into every major mail app for signing and encrypting email with an X.509 certificate.

Priya · senderuses her private key
message + signaturePriya's private key
Rohan · recipientchecks with Priya's public key

Priya's mail app signs with her private key, which never leaves her device. Rohan's app checks it with the public key inside her certificate — proving it was Priya and that nothing changed.

RSA 2048 or 3072-bit keys

Generated on the user's device or in your management console; 3072-bit for longer-lived business keys.

SHA-256 signatures

Modern hashing that every current version of Outlook, Apple Mail and Gmail verifies.

1, 2 or 3-year plans

Each certificate is valid for up to 825 days under the S/MIME rules; longer plans are reissued for you within the term.

Key escrow & backup

For business fleets, encryption keys can be escrowed so old encrypted mail stays readable when a laptop is lost or someone leaves.

Fleet deployment

Bulk issuance and auto-enrolment through Sectigo Certificate Manager or DigiCert's management platform for hundreds of mailboxes.
