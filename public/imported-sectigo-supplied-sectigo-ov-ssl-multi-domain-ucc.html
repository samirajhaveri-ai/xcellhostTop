    Overview

    One verified certificate for every name you run — websites, mail and group companies.

    Sectigo OV SSL Multi-Domain/UCC secures several different fully qualified domain names with one certificate. The base price covers a primary domain plus 2 additional names (SANs); you add more at a fixed per-year rate, up to 250 names in total. "UCC" — Unified Communications Certificate — is the name Microsoft environments use for exactly this: one certificate carrying mail.yourbusiness.in, autodiscover.yourbusiness.in and your other Exchange and Skype/Teams-era hostnames.

      Validation process

      What Sectigo checks before it puts your name on the certificate.

      OV vetting confirms the organisation behind the domains. Have these ready and most orders issue in 1–3 days.

      Step 1

      Order and CSR

      Place the order and send a CSR (we can generate it). The organisation name and address you enter must match your official records.

      Step 2

      Organisation check

      Sectigo confirms your organisation exists and is active, using a government registry or a trusted business directory.

      Step 3

      Callback verification

      Sectigo verifies a telephone number for your organisation from a trusted directory and calls it to confirm the order.

      Step 4

      Domain control and issue

      Each name is checked by DNS record, approver email or HTTP file. Sectigo issues once all checks pass — typically 1–3 days.
