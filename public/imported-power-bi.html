    Quick answer

    What is Power BI — and who needs which licence?

    What it is

    Microsoft Power BI is a business-intelligence service: you connect data sources, shape them into one semantic model, build interactive reports in Power BI Desktop or the browser, and share them through the Power BI service, Teams, SharePoint or an app.

    Who pays

    Anyone who publishes or shares needs Power BI Pro (₹1,165 per user per month, paid yearly, Microsoft list price). Premium Per User (₹1,995) adds bigger models and 48 refreshes a day. On Microsoft Fabric capacity of F64 or larger, viewers with a free licence can read shared reports.

    What XcellHost does

    We sell the licences on an INR GST invoice, size Pro vs PPU vs Fabric for you, connect Tally, Excel, SQL and ERP data through the gateway, build the model and dashboards, set up row-level security — and support it after go-live.

    Copilot in Power BI

    Ask the report a question — in plain English.

    Copilot in Power BI uses generative AI over your semantic model. Microsoft lists these capabilities today (some are in preview):

    • Ask questions about your dataSummaries and answers about the open report, from the Copilot pane — generally available.
    • Create and edit report pagesAuthors describe the page they want; Copilot drafts the visuals.
    • Narrative summariesA narrative visual that explains what changed, and summaries you can add to subscriptions.
    • Write DAXDraft DAX queries and add descriptions to measures in the model.
    • Standalone Copilot (preview)Find reports and answer questions across content you can access.
    What it needs: paid Fabric capacity (F2 or higher) or Power BI Premium (P1 or higher). A Pro or PPU licence alone is not enough, and trial capacity is not supported. For tenants outside the US/EU data boundary, an admin setting must allow processing outside your geography; some Copilot experiences are not offered in every Azure region — we confirm availability for your capacity region.
