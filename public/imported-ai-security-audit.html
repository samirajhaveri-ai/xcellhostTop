    §1 · Overview

    An AI audit tells you what's proven — not what's promised.

    Most companies now run AI somewhere: a support chatbot, a copilot for staff, a RAG assistant on internal documents, an agent that updates the CRM, a credit or fraud model. Policies often say the right things. An audit checks whether the controls actually operate — who approved the model, whether an impact assessment exists, how prompts and outputs are logged, who reviews third-party AI, and what happens when something goes wrong.

    XcellAI AI Security Audit is an independent, evidence-based review of your AI governance and security controls against ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, with security checks drawn from the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and MITRE ATLAS, and an India layer for the DPDP Act, RBI FREE-AI and SEBI's AI/ML proposals. You get findings graded by severity, an AI risk register and a remediation plan your teams can act on.

    XcellHost is not a certification body.

    We audit and prepare you; certification to ISO/IEC 42001 is granted only by an accredited certification body, which must stay independent of the consultants who helped you. We prepare you for that audit and can coordinate with the certifier you choose — the certificate itself comes from them.

        ISO/IEC 42001:2023

        The certifiable AI management system standard. Annex A lists 38 controls under 9 objectives; you justify inclusions in a Statement of Applicability. Risk guidance sits in ISO/IEC 23894.

        NIST AI RMF 1.0 + AI 600-1

        Voluntary US framework (January 2023) built on Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. The Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024) adds 12 GenAI risk areas.

        EU AI Act — for exporters

        In force since August 2024. The 2026 Digital Omnibus moved stand-alone high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and product-embedded ones to 2 August 2028. Matters if you serve EU customers.

        India

        DPDP Act 2023 with the DPDP Rules notified 13 November 2025 (most duties from May 2027), MeitY's India AI Governance Guidelines (November 2025), RBI's FREE-AI framework (August 2025), SEBI's AI/ML consultation (June 2025) and CERT-In's AIBOM guidelines (July 2025).

        Indicative mapping for audit planning, not legal advice. Clause and article references are summarised; we confirm applicability for your sector, role (provider or deployer) and geography during scoping.
