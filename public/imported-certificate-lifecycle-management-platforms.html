    Overview

    A certificate you can't see is an outage on a timer.

    CLM platforms replace the spreadsheet with a live system of record — and the calendar reminder with automation.

    Most organisations hold far more certificates than they think: public TLS on websites and APIs, private certificates on VPNs, Wi-Fi and laptops, Microsoft CA templates nobody documented, and certificates a vendor installed years ago. A certificate lifecycle management (CLM) platform finds them all, puts each one in an inventory with an issuer, an expiry date and an owner, and checks it against your policy — key size, algorithm, lifetime, approved CAs.

    Then it does the work: requesting, approving, issuing, installing, renewing and revoking certificates through ACME, SCEP, EST, agents and connectors. XcellHost deploys the two platforms it sells and supports — DigiCert Trust Lifecycle Manager and Sectigo Certificate Manager — sets up discovery and policies, rolls out automation to your servers, and can run the whole thing as a managed service from our Mumbai NOC, billed in rupees with GST.

    Why now · CA/Browser Forum SC-081v3

    398 → 200 → 100 → 47 days. Renewals multiply eightfold.

    The ballot passed on 11 April 2025. Every publicly trusted TLS certificate follows the same schedule — and domain-validation reuse shrinks with it.

    Until 14 Mar 2026398 days1×renewal / yr
    From 15 Mar 2026200 days · now2×renewals / yr
    From 15 Mar 2027100 days4×renewals / yr
    From 15 Mar 202947 days8×renewals / yr
    Renewals a year, per certificate1×398-day lifetime
    • Domain-validation reuse 398 → 200 days (2026) → 100 days (2027) → 10 days (2029)
    • Organisation validation can be reused for at most 398 days from 2026
    • Let's Encrypt is moving its default to 64 days (Feb 2027) and 45 days (Feb 2028)

    Sources: CA/Browser Forum ballot SC-081v3 (cabforum.org, 11 April 2025); ISRG / Let's Encrypt, “From 90 to 45”. Renewals counted as whole cycles per year at the maximum lifetime; most teams renew earlier than that. See the full 47-day SSL guide.

    How it works

    Sources in, policy in the middle, renewals out to every endpoint.

    One CLM core sits between where certificates are found and where they are installed. Everything it does is logged.

    01

    Discover

    Scheduled scans of IP ranges and ports, certificate-transparency log searches for your domains, cloud-account imports and CA imports build the first inventory — from any issuer.

    02

    Govern

    Each certificate gets an owner, a business service and a policy. The engine flags weak keys, SHA-1, unapproved CAs, wildcard sprawl and lifetimes above your limit.

    03

    Automate

    Renewals run through ACME (RFC 8555) with ACME Renewal Information (RFC 9773), SCEP/EST for devices, agents for servers and connectors for load balancers and clouds.

    Security & compliance

    Keys stay where they belong. Evidence is always current.

    CLM helps you meet the encryption, inventory and logging expectations of Indian regulators. It is one control in your programme, not compliance by itself.

    Private-key handling

    Keys are generated on the server, by the agent or in your HSM / key vault. XcellHost engineers never email or store private keys; access uses role-based permissions and approvals.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Specified incidents must be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for 180 days. A certificate inventory with lifecycle logs shortens incident scoping.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Data fiduciaries must use reasonable security safeguards such as encryption. Certificates that never lapse and keys that meet policy are part of showing that.

    RBI · SEBI CSCRF · IRDAI

    Sector frameworks expect an IT asset inventory, cryptographic controls and periodic audits. Certificate hygiene reports give auditors certificate-level evidence.

    What auditors ask for
    0%of certificates with a named owner
    0CERT-In log retention in India
    0CERT-In incident reporting window

    Targets to design for, not guarantees. Summaries for planning, not legal advice — confirm with your compliance team.

    Hundreds of certificates, an audit every year

    Internet banking, payment APIs, partner VPNs and internal portals across several CAs. CLM gives the inventory and evidence RBI and SEBI auditors expect, and stops weekend expiries on customer-facing channels.

    • Certificate hygiene report per audit cycle
    • Mutual-TLS certificates for partner APIs tracked
    • Approvals and segregation of duties

    Many domains, constant releases

    Customer sub-domains, CDNs and Kubernetes ingress create certificates daily. ACME and cert-manager renew them in the pipeline; CLM keeps the overview and catches anything outside it.

    • cert-manager and ingress automation
    • Wildcard sprawl reduced to per-service certs
    • CT-log watch for names you didn't request

    Plants, devices and a Microsoft CA

    Machine and device certificates from an internal CA sit next to public TLS for ERP and supplier portals. SCEP/EST and MDM integrations keep device identities current without visits to every site.

    • Device certificates via SCEP / EST
    • AD CS templates discovered and managed
    • Multi-site estate in one inventory

    Tenders, citizen portals and Indian CAs

    Public portals need certificates that never lapse, and some tenders specify an Indian CA such as eMudhra. A CA-agnostic inventory tracks them alongside DigiCert and Sectigo certificates.

    • Indian CA certificates in the same inventory
    • Reports for CERT-In empanelled auditors
    • Renewals scheduled outside service hours
