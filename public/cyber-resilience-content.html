Frequently asked questions

How do you recover from ransomware without reinfecting yourself?
You need backups the attacker cannot reach, a way to tell which snapshots are clean, and an orchestrated restore. XcellHost delivers Druva's cyber resilience in India: air-gapped immutable backups, threat hunting over backup metadata to find indicators of compromise, quarantine of infected data, recommended clean restore points and mass restore, run 24×7 by our SOC.
How do you know which restore point is clean?
Snapshots are scanned and their health tracked. Recovery Intelligence surfaces anomalies and recommends the last known-good point, and threat hunting searches backup metadata for indicators of compromise.
Can you restore at scale, quickly?
Yes — orchestrated mass restore rebuilds endpoints, servers, SaaS data and identity in a planned sequence rather than one ticket at a time.
Is there a recovery guarantee?
Druva publishes a Data Resiliency Guarantee of up to $10 million covering risks including cyber attacks. Terms apply and XcellHost will share the current programme details with your quote.
How are backups kept safe from the attacker?
They are written to an air-gapped, immutable store in a separate security domain, with zero-trust access. Compromised admin credentials in your estate cannot delete or alter them.
What does Safe Mode do?
It restricts platform access during an incident: scheduled backups pause, and deletions or policy changes by administrators are blocked until the lock is cleared.
Does this help with CERT-In reporting?
Yes. We assemble the timeline, affected-systems list and recovery evidence so your team can report within the six-hour window.
How is it priced?
Per workload or per user, plus the XcellHost managed service, billed in INR with GST. Share your estate and we'll size it.
