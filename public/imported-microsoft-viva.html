Overview

An engaged, productive workforce — with the signals to prove it.

Viva is Microsoft's employee experience platform. It puts communication, feedback, analytics and learning in one place, inside the tools your people already open every morning.

Microsoft Viva is a set of modules that sit inside Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365. Connections gives everyone one home for news, tools and tasks; Engage and Amplify carry conversations and campaigns; Glint, Pulse and Insights turn feedback and work patterns into actions; Learning brings courses into the flow of work. Microsoft adds AI on top, so summaries, suggestions and the Copilot Dashboard come from the same platform.

For Indian organisations with people spread across head offices, branches, homes and shop floors, the hard part is not buying licences — it is getting a plant supervisor, a sales rep in Indore and a developer in Pune to use the same front door. XcellHost licenses Viva through Microsoft's CSP programme on an INR GST invoice, builds the dashboard and intranet, designs your first survey and runs adoption with your HR and comms teams.

The modules

Seven modules, three jobs — pick the ones your people need.

Microsoft groups Viva into communications & communities, workplace analytics & feedback, and learning & knowledge. Each card shows what it does in practice.

Viva Connections

A branded home in Teams with a dashboard of cards (shifts, leave, approvals, IT help), a news feed and quick links to your intranet — the same front door on desktop and the Teams mobile app.

  • Dashboard cards for everyday tasks
  • Targeted news feed by role or site
  • Links to your SharePoint home
Viva Engage

Communities, storylines and leadership channels where people can ask, share and recognise each other — across departments, cities and shifts. Leaders can host Q&A and town halls in the open.

  • Interest & site communities
  • Leadership corner & AMAs
  • Praise and recognition
Viva Amplify

For corporate communicators: plan a campaign once, draft for each audience, publish to Teams, Outlook, SharePoint and Engage together, then see reach and engagement in one report.

  • Campaign planning & approvals
  • One draft, many channels
  • Reach & engagement reporting
Viva Insights

Workplace analytics from how work happens — meetings, after-hours load, focus time — with personal insights for each employee and de-identified, aggregated views for managers and leaders.

  • Focus plan & quiet time
  • Team & org work patterns
  • Copilot Dashboard
Viva Glint

Organisation-wide engagement surveys built on research-backed questions, with dashboards for every manager and recommended actions so results lead to change, not a slide deck.

  • Engagement & lifecycle surveys
  • Manager dashboards
  • Recommended actions
Viva Pulse

Quick team surveys any manager can send in Teams — a few questions, results in days and suggested resources to discuss with the team.

  • Templates for common topics
  • Runs inside Teams
  • Suggested next steps
Viva Learning

Brings learning from your own library, Microsoft Learn, LinkedIn Learning and other providers into Teams and Microsoft 365. Managers assign courses, people discover and share them where they work, and progress is tracked in one place.

  • Search content from many providers
  • Assign & track required training
  • Share a course in a Teams chat
  • Recommendations by role

Privacy by design

Personal insights belong to the person — leaders see patterns, never people.

Microsoft designs Viva so an individual's work-pattern insights are visible only to that individual. Managers and leaders see aggregated, de-identified data above minimum group sizes your admins control.

Youyour own meetings, focus time, after-hours loadonly you
Your managerteam averages, above a minimum group sizeaggregated
Leaders & HRorg trends, de-identified, with privacy settingsde-identified
Individual data stays individual

Personal insights in Viva Insights are shown only to the employee — not to their manager, HR or IT.

Minimum group sizes

Team and org views are aggregated and hidden for groups smaller than the threshold your admins set.
