§ 01 Overview

A policy says what should happen. An audit checks that it does.

Most organisations have an information security policy, a firewall, backups and some form of access review. Far fewer can show — with dated evidence — that each of those controls actually works today, across every site, cloud account and vendor that touches their data.

XcellHost Cyber Security Audit is an independent evaluation of your security controls and practices. We plan the scope with you, review documents, interview owners, test a sample of each control, and record exactly what we saw. You get findings ranked by severity, a corrective and preventive action (CAPA) plan with owners and dates, and a retest that closes the loop.

§ 02 Why it matters in India

Regulators, customers and insurers now ask the same question: when were you last audited?

Four pressures are making a periodic, evidence-based cyber security audit a normal part of running a business in India.

01

CERT-In expects regular audits

CERT-In's cyber security audit guidelines (2025) set out how audits should be scoped, performed and reported, and expect organisations to audit at least once a year and after major changes.

02

DPDP Act 2023 safeguards

Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal-data breaches. An audit is practical evidence that those safeguards are designed and tested.

03

Sector regulators

RBI, SEBI (CSCRF) and IRDAI expect regulated entities and their technology providers to have periodic audits and to close findings within agreed timelines.

04

Customers & insurers

Enterprise buyers send security questionnaires, and cyber insurers ask for proof of controls. A recent audit report answers most of them in one go.

Found in an audita ranked list, an owner and a date — fixed on your schedulevsFound in an incidenta 6-hour CERT-In clock, a breach notice and a regulator asking for your last audit

§ 04 Methodology

Six phases, one audit trail — every step leaves a workpaper.

The approach follows recognised audit practice: plan on risk, test on a sample, record what you saw, and report it so the right person can act.

  1. P1Plan & scope

    Objectives, frameworks, sites, systems and critical assets; risk-based sampling plan; NDA and audit authorisation letter signed.

  2. P2Document review

    Policies, procedures, risk register, past audit reports and the statement of applicability read against the frameworks.

  3. P3Interviews & walkthroughs

    Control owners explain how each process runs; we observe it on screen or on site.

  4. P4Testing & sampling

    Samples drawn from real records — tickets, access lists, logs, backups, configurations — and tested for design and operating effectiveness.

  5. P5Findings & report

    Each exception rated by severity, linked to evidence, with management responses recorded before the final report.

  6. P6CAPA & retest

    Corrective and preventive actions agreed with owners and dates; closed items re-verified in a retest.

§ 07 Use cases by industry

Same method, different pressure points.

Banks, NBFCs, brokers & insurers

Annual audits are expected by RBI, SEBI CSCRF and IRDAI, and auditors want evidence that findings from last year were closed.

  • Privileged access & maker-checker
  • SOC monitoring and log retention
  • Outsourcing and vendor oversight
  • Closure of previous audit findings

Hospitals, labs & health-tech

Patient data is personal data under the DPDP Act, often spread across HIS, PACS, lab systems and vendors.

  • Access to patient records
  • Backups of clinical systems
  • Shared and legacy workstations
  • Third-party device and software vendors

Plants, MSMEs & exporters

ERP and plant networks meet; customers abroad send security questionnaires. Plant-floor depth sits in our OT assessment.

  • IT / OT boundary and remote access
  • ERP and Tally servers
  • Ransomware-ready backups
  • Customer questionnaire evidence

SaaS, IT & ITeS companies

Enterprise customers ask for ISO 27001 or SOC 2 evidence before signing. An audit shows where you stand first.

  • Cloud tenant configuration
  • Change and release control
  • Joiner-mover-leaver process
  • Readiness for certification

PSU vendors & government suppliers

Tenders often ask for a recent audit by a CERT-In-empanelled auditor. We align the audit and can arrange one on request.

  • CERT-In-aligned scope
  • MeitY cloud guidance
  • Tender-ready audit letter
  • Application and network review

Retail, D2C & marketplaces

Customer data, payment flows and many SaaS tools. Audits focus on what an outsider can reach.

  • Web and API exposure
  • Payment-flow controls (PCI DSS context)
  • Customer data & consent
  • Marketing and logistics vendors
