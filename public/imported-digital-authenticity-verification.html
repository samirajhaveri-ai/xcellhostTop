Why now, in India

A video call is no longer proof that a person is real.

Face-swaps, cloned voices and fake officers on video are now common fraud tools. Indian regulators have responded with advisories, amended rules and stricter KYC checks.

Digital arrest

Fake officers, live on video

MHA has warned that fraudsters pose as police, CBI, Narcotics, RBI or ED officials, keep victims on Skype or other video calls, and use studios styled as police stations with uniforms to look genuine1.

3,962 Skype IDs and 83,668 WhatsApp accounts used for digital arrests blocked by I4C; 7.81 lakh SIM cards blocked till 28 Feb 20252.

Deepfakes

MeitY advisories, 2023

On 7 Nov 2023 MeitY told significant social media intermediaries to identify misinformation and deepfakes and remove reported content within 36 hours3. A second advisory on 26 Dec 2023 asked all intermediaries to tell users clearly what content is prohibited under the IT Rules4.

IT Rules

Synthetic content rules, in force

The IT Rules amendment on synthetically generated information was notified on 10 Feb 2026 and applies from 20 Feb 2026. AI-generated content must be labelled with traceable metadata, and unlawful content must come down within 3 hours of a government or court order5.

CERT-In

Deepfake countermeasures

CERT-In advisory CIAD-2024-0060 (27 Nov 2024, severity High) asks organisations to add verification protocols with call-backs for sensitive transactions, deploy detection tools and build forensics capability6.

    Privacy by design

    Faces and voices are personal data. We treat them that way.

    Under the DPDP Act, 2023 a face or voice that identifies someone is personal data. XcellSecure is built to collect less, keep nothing and show its work.

    No media retention

    Stateless APIs analyse in memory. Only the score, reason codes and a hash are returned.

    Consent first

    Every call carries a consent reference and a stated purpose, ready for your consent manager.

    Purpose limitation

    A KYC score is tagged for KYC. Policies block reuse of results for other purposes*.

    Stays in India

    On-prem by default; private-cloud option in XcellHost’s Mumbai data centre.

    Explainable result

    Reason codes let a reviewer tell a customer why a check failed and offer another route.

    No exclusion

    Grey scores route to a human, not an auto-reject, in line with RBI’s rule that liveness must not exclude persons with special needs.

    FAQ

    Digital authenticity verification, answered.

    It checks whether a face, voice, video or ID in front of you is real, live and belongs to the person claiming it. XcellSecure combines deepfake detection, liveness, voice-clone checks and document matching, and returns one trust score from 1 to 10 with reasons.

    Most detectors answer real or fake. The Trust Factor Engine weighs signals from 8 AI models across vision, audio and physiology and gives a graded score from 1 to 10. That lets you approve clear sessions, ask for more proof on grey ones and block clear fakes, instead of forcing a yes or no.

    The models span three families: vision (face liveness, texture and generation artifacts, lip-sync), audio (voice cloning and replay) and physiology (pulse signal from skin colour changes, micro-expressions and blink patterns). The exact line-up per module is confirmed during scoping.

    No. XcellSecure APIs are stateless and run on premise. Media is analysed in memory and dropped; only the score, reason codes and a hash are returned for your audit log. No video data is stored or processed in the cloud.

    RBI's KYC directions ask V-CIP applications for face liveness and spoof detection, face matching, live GPS geo-tagging and India-located storage of V-CIP data. XcellSecure supplies the liveness, spoof and face-match signals, and its on-prem APIs keep data in your Indian set-up. Geo-tagging and recording stay with your V-CIP platform.

    Faces and voices that identify someone are personal data. The design helps you meet the Act's duties with no media retention, a consent reference and purpose on every call, and reason codes you can explain. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025 and the core duties apply from 13 May 2027.
