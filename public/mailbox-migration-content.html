What is mailbox migration?

Mailbox migration moves everything in your users' mailboxes — emails, folders, contacts, calendars, tasks, rules, permissions, shared mailboxes and archives — from one email platform to another. XcellHost migrates from Google Workspace, Exchange, IMAP, Zimbra, Lotus Notes or Microsoft 365 into Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and other platforms, running in parallel with delta sync so users keep sending and receiving mail until cutover.
