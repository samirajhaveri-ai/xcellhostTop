    Overview

    When a plant PC dies, the line stops. Recovery should take minutes.

    Plant floors run on Windows and Linux PCs that nobody wants to touch: HMIs at the line, SCADA and historian servers in the control room, engineering stations that hold the only copy of a project. Many are years past vendor support, sit on networks with no internet, and have no IT person nearby. When a drive fails or ransomware spreads, a rebuild can take a shift or longer — and every hour of lost production costs money.

    Acronis Cyber Protect for OT takes full image backups of those systems while they run, keeps copies where you choose — local disk, NAS, tape or cloud — and lets a local operator restore a failed machine with One-Click Recovery, or move it to new hardware with Universal Restore. The same agent adds anti-ransomware and anti-malware protection. XcellHost sizes it, deploys it plant by plant, trains your operators and supports it from our 24×7 NOC & SOC in Mumbai.

    How it works

    Four steps from a fragile PC to a recoverable one.

    Agent-based, image-level backup — the way Acronis describes it for OT. Watch one HMI go through the cycle.

    01Agent, no downtime

    Install the agent on a live OT system — no reboot and no production stop, per Acronis.

    02Image backup, daily

    Full, incremental or differential images on a schedule from several times a day to monthly — or triggered by a reboot or a failing drive.

    033-2-1, air-gapped

    Three copies on two media types, one off-site: local disk, NAS/SAN, tape or XcellHost's India cloud — immutable and encrypted.

    04One-click restore

    Local staff restore in minutes with One-Click Recovery, or rebuild onto new hardware with Universal Restore.

        It is Acronis' backup, recovery and cyber protection product for operational technology: the Windows and Linux systems that run plants, such as HMIs, SCADA servers, historians and engineering workstations. It takes image backups without stopping production, restores failed systems quickly — including by non-IT staff — and adds anti-ransomware and anti-malware protection in the same agent. XcellHost supplies, deploys and supports it in India.

        Per Acronis, Windows and Linux systems at Purdue Levels 2, 3 and 3.5: HMI, SCADA and jump servers; historians and I/O servers; patch servers and historian mirrors in the IT/OT DMZ. Many manufacturers also use it for Level 4 and 5 IT systems such as ERP. PLCs, drives and sensors at Levels 0–1 have no general-purpose OS for an agent, so we protect their engineering workstations and project files instead.

        No downtime or reboot is needed to install the agent or run backups on live systems, per Acronis. We still deploy inside maintenance windows agreed with your production team, and test on one machine of each type first, because plant change control matters more than vendor claims.

        Acronis typically recommends image backups at least daily. Schedules can run several times a day down to monthly, and backups can also start on events such as a reboot or a drive bad-block warning. We usually set daily images for HMIs and SCADA servers, more frequent backups for historians, and an extra backup after every engineering change.

If the system fails, an operator selects it and the machine restores itself from its last backup, without an IT engineer on site. It's designed for remote, unstaffed or night-shift sites. XcellHost trains your shift staff and runs drills so they're confident using it.

        Yes. Acronis Universal Restore lets you restore a system image to dissimilar hardware — for example after a motherboard or drive failure on an old PC whose model is no longer sold — and move systems between physical and virtual machines.
