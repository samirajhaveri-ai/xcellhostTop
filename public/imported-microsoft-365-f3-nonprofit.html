    Overview

    Frontline Microsoft 365 for NGOs — priced for field teams.

    Microsoft 365 F3 is Microsoft's frontline plan: people who work on the move, often on a phone or a shared tablet, rather than at a desk. Per Microsoft, F3 users get full Office for the web and mobile apps (on devices with integrated screens smaller than 10.9 inches), a 2 GB mailbox in Outlook on the web, 2 GB of OneDrive, Teams with frontline tools like Shifts and Walkie-Talkie, and device and identity management through Intune and Microsoft Entra ID P1.

    For verified nonprofits the same plan is listed at ₹230 per user a month* against ₹630 — about 63% less. That makes it the natural licence for ASHA-style community health workers, field coordinators, outreach staff and volunteers who run surveys and shifts. XcellHost checks your eligibility, sets up the tenant and shared devices, and keeps the licences billed in INR with GST.

    Who it's for

    Right for the field, wrong for the desk.

    Pick F3 Nonprofit if…

    • Staff work in villages, wards, camps or centres — mostly on a phone
    • Several workers share one tablet across shifts
    • You run rosters and need a time clock that works on mobile
    • Work is forms, checklists, photos and short documents
    • Every rupee per head matters for the programme budget

    Choose another plan if…

    • People work on laptops all day and need desktop Excel or Outlook → Office 365 E3 NP or M365 E3 NP
    • Mailboxes go beyond 2 GB or need an archive
    • An office team under 300 needs full device security → Business Premium NP
    • Tablets are 11″ or larger — mobile app rights need screens under 10.9″ (web apps still work)

    01

    Who qualifies

    Per Microsoft, a not-for-profit or NGO with recognised legal status in India and a community-benefit mission (poverty relief, education, health, social welfare, environment, culture or human rights). Public libraries and museums can qualify too.

    02

    Who doesn't

    Government bodies, schools, colleges and universities, hospitals outside Microsoft's listed categories, political, labour and trade associations, financial institutions and individuals.

    03

    How verification works

    A paid employee or authorised volunteer registers on Microsoft's nonprofit programme — a partner can't register for you. Keep your registration certificate, 12A/80G and PAN handy; if you already hold a TechSoup India validation, share it with us. Microsoft decides every application and asks grantees to re-attest periodically.

    04

    Who can use the licences

    Discounts apply to staff and volunteers of the organisation; beneficiaries, members and donors can't be licensed at nonprofit rates, and licences can't be shared or resold.

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    Beneficiary data stays accountable.

    Field programmes collect health, household and identity data. F3 gives you the controls; XcellHost helps you set them up. These features help you meet your obligations — they don't make you compliant on their own.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Consent-based processing of beneficiary personal data, breach intimation to the Data Protection Board and affected people. MFA, device wipe and access logs support that.

    CERT-In directions

    Report cyber incidents within 6 hours and keep logs for 180 days. Microsoft's standard audit retention plus our log export cover the trail.

    FCRA & donor reporting

    Programme records, approvals and reports kept in SharePoint with retention — easier to show funders and auditors what happened, when.
