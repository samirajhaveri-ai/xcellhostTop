    Overview

    SSL for every hosted site, without a support ticket per renewal.

    A shared or reseller hosting server can carry hundreds of domains, and each one needs a valid certificate. Done by hand, every certificate means a CSR, a validation email or DNS record, a copy-paste into the panel and a calendar reminder. Certificate lifetimes are now being cut in steps — to 200 days from 15 March 2026 and 47 days from 15 March 2029 — so that manual work multiplies on every server you run.

    AutoInstall SSL by XcellHost moves that work into the control panel. Installed once by your admin, the plugin lists the domains on the server, creates keys and CSRs, completes file- or DNS-based validation, installs the certificate and verifies it. It checks every certificate daily for expiry or revocation, renews 28 days before expiry and emails you if anything fails. Resellers can sell it under their own brand. XcellHost supplies the certificates, sets up the plugin and supports it from Mumbai.

    Why now

    Shorter certificates mean more renewals. On a hosting server, that adds up fast.

    CA/Browser Forum ballot SC-081v3, passed on 11 April 2025, sets three dated cuts for publicly trusted TLS certificates and for how long a domain check may be reused.

    1. Until 14 Mar 2026398 daysDCV reuse 398 days×1
    2. From 15 Mar 2026200 daysDCV reuse 200 days×2
    3. From 15 Mar 2027100 daysDCV reuse 100 days×4
    4. From 15 Mar 202947 daysDCV reuse 10 days×8
    Renewal jobs per year · one server, 120 domains · illustrative

    Renewals per domain per year rounded to 1, 2, 4 and 8 to allow renewal before expiry. Source: cabforum.org · Ballot SC-081v3. Organisation validation reuse stays capped at 398 days.

    How it works

    Panel plugin, certificate authority, your server — joined up and watched daily.

    01 · RequestKey & CSR on your server

    The plugin generates the private key and CSR on the hosting server and sends the CSR with your account token.

    02 · ValidateDCV placed for you

    The validation file or DNS value is written automatically; the CA checks it and issues the certificate.

    03 · Install & watchInstalled, verified, renewed

    The certificate is installed and verified on the site, checked daily, and renewed 28 days before expiry.
