01Files grow, staff don't

Notings, letters, replies and reports take most of a section's day. AI gives a first draft; the dealing hand and officer check it against the file.

02Unclear what is allowed

Some ministries restrict public AI apps on office devices. Staff need a simple rule: classify the data, then pick the approved tool.

03Decisions must be explainable

Citizens can ask for records and reasons under the RTI Act. An AI-assisted decision still needs a human author and a reasoned note.

Hands-on labs

Seven labs that move like a file. Sample data only.

Every lab uses sample or unclassified material. Nothing from a live file is needed, and nothing classified is ever used.

    Summarise a 40-page sample file into a four-para noting, with page references you verify.

  2. Lab 2RTI reply on the clock
