How it works

Two certificates, one device, a lifetime of proof.

Most fleets use a long-life birth certificate to prove where a device came from, then a shorter operational certificate for everyday connections.

01 · At the factory

Birth certificate

The device generates a key pair in its secure element; the manufacturing CA signs a certificate binding the serial number to that key.

  • 10–20 year validity to match product life
  • Batch issuance at line speed
  • Every unit logged for audit
02 · First boot

Onboarding & operational certificate

The device proves its birth identity over mutual TLS, then enrols for an operational certificate scoped to your cloud and tenant.

  • EST, SCEP, ACME or CMPv2 enrolment
  • Registered with AWS, Azure or your broker
  • 1–5 year validity, policy-driven
03 · In the field

Renew, re-issue, revoke

Certificates renew before they expire, re-issue after an ownership change and get revoked the moment a device is lost, cloned or retired.

  • Unattended renewal over EST
  • CRL & OCSP status for brokers
  • One dashboard for every certificate

Benefits

The simplest way to secure a fleet is managed PKI.

Six reasons device makers and fleet operators move from shared secrets to per-device certificates.

01

Stronger than passwords or tokens

A private key on the device can't be phished, guessed or reused, and both ends authenticate each other.

02

Stop cloning, spoofing & piracy

Every unit carries an identity nobody can copy, so counterfeit or rogue hardware can't join your network or your service.

03

Sign & encrypt every message

Two-way signing and encryption guard against data theft, man-in-the-middle attacks, device takeover and tampered readings.

04

End-to-end certificate management

One identity from manufacture to decommissioning, with every issue, renewal and revocation recorded.

05

Save time, deploy faster

No in-house CA to build: infrastructure, staff, key ceremonies, policies and backups are covered for one predictable yearly cost.

06

Plugs into your systems

Ready-made REST API, EST, CMPv2 and SCEP integrations mean less home-grown crypto code — and fewer subtle key-generation bugs.

Why home-grown crypto is risky: research presented at an IEEE conference in 2019 analysed RSA certificates collected on the internet and found 1 in 172 shared a key factor with another — mostly on IoT devices with weak random-number generation. Proven, vendor-tested enrolment avoids that class of bug.
