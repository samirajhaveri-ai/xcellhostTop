01The short version#

Druva sells backup and recovery as a service. Druva runs the software and the storage, so you don't buy a backup server, disks or tape. You pay in one of two ways. SaaS apps and laptops are licensed per user. Servers, VMs, databases and cloud workloads are licensed per TB of backup data stored each month, measured after deduplication and compression. Each workload family comes in up to three editions: Business, Enterprise and Elite.

Per user for SaaS and endpoints

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Power BI, Entra ID, Active Directory, Okta and laptops are all licensed per user. One endpoint licence covers a user with several devices.

Per TB-month for servers and cloud

Data centre workloads (VMware, Hyper-V, Nutanix, Proxmox, NAS, SQL, Oracle) and AWS/Azure workloads are licensed per "TB/month after deduplication". That is the backup data Druva stores, not the size of your servers.

Credits are prepaid TB-months

One Druva credit is 1 TB of deduplicated, compressed backup data stored for one month. You must use at least 80% of your credits within the term, and up to 20% can roll over. Backups stop at 110% use.

Editions decide the features

Business covers core backup and recovery. Enterprise adds long-term retention tiering, multiple regions, Data Lock and local TurboTier. Elite adds DR as a service for servers and legal hold for SaaS.

Your data can stay in India

Druva lists AWS Asia Pacific (Mumbai) and Azure Central India (Pune) as storage regions for enterprise workloads. Druva has offered inSync and Phoenix from AWS Mumbai since January 2018.

XcellHost bills in rupees

Our Druva page lists ₹249 per Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace user, ₹399 per laptop, ₹6,999 per TB for servers and VMs, and ₹5,999 per TB for AWS and Azure, a month. All are indicative and exclude 18% GST.

Tip

Count users and terabytes separately before you compare quotes. Make a list of SaaS seats (by app), the people who use laptops, and the total data on your servers and cloud workloads. Tools 1, 2 and 5 in this guide use exactly those numbers.

        08Credits and TB-months#

        For servers, VMs, databases and cloud workloads on a direct contract, Druva sells credits. One credit is 1 TB of deduplicated, compressed backup data stored in Druva's cloud for one month. A server with 10 TB of data that produces 8 TB of backup data uses 8 credits a month.

        • Daily burn: credits used per day = deduplicated TB stored × 12 ÷ 365. 1 TB stored for a day uses about 0.033 credits. The balance updates daily at 12:15 AM UTC.
        • One shared pool: all credits go into one pool. They are not assigned to individual servers.
        • Term: typically 12 or 24 months, set in the contract. Example: 10 TB for 12 months ≈ 120 credits; 50 TB for 24 months ≈ 1,200 credits.
        • 80/20 rule: use at least 80% of purchased credits during the term. Up to 20% of unused credits can roll over at renewal, subject to your contract. Anything above 20% expires.
        • Overage: alerts at 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%. At 110% of available credits, backups are suspended until you buy a top-up or renew. Restores stay available under your retention policy.
        • Expiry: at term end, backups are suspended whatever the balance. Unused evaluation credits do not carry over to a commercial account.

        Druva's worked example of the early-deletion fee: deleting 14 TB after 7 months in the cold tier costs 0.35 × 5 × 14 = 24.5 credits. Once LTR is turned on for a partner-provisioned customer, it can only be turned off by Druva Support.

        Tip

        Move data to LTR only if you expect to keep it for more than a year. Otherwise the early-deletion fee can cancel out the saving. On XcellHost's Druva page, longer retention on TB workloads adds 10% (3 years), 25% (7 years) or 35% (10 years) to the per-TB price.

        11The Data Resiliency Guarantee#

        Since August 2022 Druva has offered qualifying customers up to US$10 million of coverage across five risk types: cyber, human, application, operational and environmental. It comes with service levels, not insurance.

        Service levels (per Druva)

        • 100% confidentiality of backup data
        • 100% immutability: backups not deleted, encrypted or changed
        • 99% reliability of backup services
        • 99.999% durability
        • Up to 99.5% availability

        Who qualifies (per Druva's FAQ)

        • You must buy the Security Posture & Observability licence
        • Annual spend minimum, contract-length requirement and a Druva Health Check (figures not published)

        17Migration, true-ups and renewals#

        Most buyers come to Druva from an on-premises backup server, a Microsoft 365 backup tool or nothing at all. The licensing questions are the same each time: what to count, when the count is checked, and what happens when you grow.

        Moving from on-prem backup

        • Keep the old system until Druva holds the retention you need. You may pay for both for a while.
        • Old backup chains are not converted into Druva credits. Plan which history to keep on the old system, or restore and re-protect it.
        • Size credits on source data, dedup rates, change rate and retention, the inputs Druva uses.
        • Tape and long-term archives are a separate decision. Druva's LTR and archive tiers can replace some of them.

        Growth and true-ups

        • Per user: once you pass your allocation plus 5% or 100 (whichever is lower), new activations fail. Order more before you hire.
        • Credits: top-ups are bought through your account executive and added to the pool. Backups stop at 110%.
        • Renewal: use at least 80% of credits; at most 20% rolls over.
        • Through XcellHost: monthly bills follow your usage; confirm how increases and reductions are handled.

        18Mistakes to avoid#

        1. Counting laptops instead of people. Druva endpoint licences are per user. Check which unit your quote uses.
        2. Comparing "per TB" prices that measure different TBs. Front-end TB, stored TB and a provider's billing TB are not the same.
        3. Buying Enterprise for SaaS when legal needs legal hold. Legal hold, federated search and defensible deletion are Elite on M365, Google and endpoints.
        4. Expecting DRaaS on Business or Enterprise. It is Elite, or a per-VM add-on on Enterprise.
        5. Letting credits expire unused. Below 80% use, you lose everything above the 20% rollover.
        6. Ignoring the 110% stop. A data surge (an untruncated SQL log, a CBT reset) can burn credits fast and halt backups.
        7. Deleting LTR data early. The early-deletion fee applies in the first 12 months in the cold tier.
        8. Assuming Hyderabad. AWS Mumbai and Azure Central India are listed; Hyderabad is not.
        9. Expecting the US$10M guarantee through an MSP. MSP customers are not eligible.
        10. Paying twice for MDDR. Druva includes it; ask what an extra MDDR line adds.

          FAQ

          Druva licensing, answered.

          Per user for SaaS apps (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Power BI), identity (Entra ID, Active Directory, Okta) and endpoints. Per TB per month of backup data after deduplication for data centre and public cloud workloads. Direct contracts for TB workloads are often sold as prepaid credits.

          One credit is 1 TB of deduplicated, compressed backup data stored in Druva's cloud for one month. Credits are used daily at stored TB × 12 ÷ 365, from one shared pool, over a term that is typically 12 or 24 months.

          You must use at least 80% of purchased credits during the term. Up to 20% of unused credits can roll over at renewal, subject to your contract. Anything above that expires. Unused evaluation credits do not carry over to a commercial account.

          Druva sends alerts at 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%. At 110% of available credits, backups are suspended until you buy a top-up or renew. Existing backups stay available for restore under your retention policy.

          Druva's published unit is TB per month after deduplication, which is the backup data stored, not the size of your servers. XcellHost's Druva page prices per TB of protected (front-end) data. Ask every quote which TB it uses.

          Druva licenses endpoints per user. The licence is used when a user's first device is activated, and one licence covers a user with several devices. XcellHost's Druva page currently prices laptops per device at ₹399 a month, so confirm the unit on your quote.

          Druva gives free shared and resource mailbox licences equal to 50% of your Microsoft 365 user licences. 100 user licences cover 50 shared or resource mailboxes. Beyond that you buy more.

          For data centre workloads, Business covers core backup, deduplication, recovery, DruAI and MDDR. Enterprise adds long-term retention tiering, multiple regions, Data Lock and TurboTier. Elite also includes DRaaS. For M365, Google and endpoints, only Enterprise and Elite are listed, and Elite adds federated search, legal hold and defensible deletion.

          One per-user licence for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, Power Platform, Entra ID, Active Directory and Okta, with pooled storage. Druva's page mentions both unlimited pooled storage and 300 GB per user, so confirm the allowance on your order. Price on quote.
