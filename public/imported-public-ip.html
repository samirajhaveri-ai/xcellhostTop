F · bring your own IP

Your prefix, our network. Five checks, then we announce it.

Keep the addresses your customers, partners and allow-lists already know. We announce them only after the paperwork and routing records prove you hold them.

  1. 1Hold the space

    Provider-independent space from IRINN — run by NIXI as India's National Internet Registry — or directly from APNIC. IRINN's minimum delegation is a /24 for IPv4 and a /48 for IPv6, on demonstrated need.

  2. 2Sign the LOA

    A Letter of Authority on your letterhead naming the prefixes and allowing XcellHost to announce them. We give you the template.

  3. 3Create route objects

    route / route6 objects in the registry database, so upstream filters accept the announcement.

  4. 4Publish ROAs

    An RPKI ROA for each prefix with our origin ASN (or yours) and the right max-length. IRINN registers ROAs on request from your registered email.

  5. 5We announce

    We validate the ROA, set filters and max-prefix, announce the prefix and route it to your VPC, firewall or dedicated servers.

ROA max-length check

A ROA that is shorter than what you announce makes the more-specific routes RPKI-invalid, and networks that enforce origin validation drop them. Try it:

H · reverse DNS

PTR records that match. Mail servers check.

Receiving mail servers look up the PTR for your sending address and expect it to point back to a name that resolves to the same address (forward-confirmed reverse DNS). Set it once, per address.

  • Use a real hostname, not a generic one like static-25.isp.example
  • Forward and reverse must agree
  • Pair with SPF, DKIM and DMARC

    

    
For a /24 or larger we can delegate the in-addr.arpa zone to your name servers or Managed DNS. Smaller blocks use RFC 2317 classless delegation or PTRs set by us.

  







  

    
I · reputation & abuse
A clean address is an asset. We keep it that way.
Addresses carry history. One compromised server sending spam can get a whole range listed. Our abuse desk works the problem from the first report.

    

      
Before you get it
Recycled addresses are checked against major public blocklists before they are assigned. Listed addresses are held back until clean.

      
While you hold it
Reputation watch checks every address daily and alerts you and our NOC on a new listing.

      
When abuse is reported
Reports to our abuse contact open a ticket. We notify you, help find the cause, and ask for a fix within an agreed window before any null-route.

      
Getting delisted
Once the cause is fixed we file delisting requests with the evidence and track them to closure.

    

    
CERT-In 6-hour rule. Under CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022, service providers and body corporates in India must report specified cyber incidents within 6 hours of noticing them. A compromised server found through an abuse report can be one; our desk helps you decide and file. See Virtual Firewalls for outbound filtering that stops it at the edge.
