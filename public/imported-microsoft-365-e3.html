      Overview · Microsoft 365 E3

      Four products in one licence — so nothing falls between them.

      Per Microsoft, Microsoft 365 E3 combines the Office 365 E3 productivity suite with Windows 11 Enterprise, Microsoft Intune for device management, Microsoft Entra ID P1 for identity and Conditional Access, and Defender for Endpoint Plan 1. One per-user licence covers the apps, mail, files, meetings, the device and the sign-in — the parts that usually come from four different vendors.

      For an Indian enterprise that means one GST invoice for productivity and device control: laptops enrolled from the box with Autopilot, a policy that blocks sign-ins from unmanaged phones, files labelled Confidential that can't be mailed outside, and audit logs kept for 180 days by default. XcellHost licenses it through Microsoft's CSP programme, migrates your mail and files, builds the identity and Intune baseline and runs the helpdesk from Mumbai.

      What's included · per Microsoft

      Six areas, checked against Microsoft's service descriptions.

      Pick an area. Names follow Microsoft's current branding — Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), Viva Engage (formerly Yammer), Microsoft Purview.

      Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise

      • Desktop Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote — plus Access and Publisher on Windows PCs.
      • Install on up to 5 PCs or Macs, 5 tablets and 5 phones per licensed user, per Microsoft.
      • Office for the web and mobile with full editing and real-time co-authoring.
      • Forms, Lists, Planner, To Do, Bookings, Loop, Clipchamp and Stream.
      • Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat included for signed-in users; the full Microsoft 365 Copilot is a paid add-on.

      Exchange Online (Plan 2)

      Primary mailbox100 GB
      Archive · auto-expandingup to 1.5 TB
      • 100 GB primary mailbox per user, with an auto-expanding archive of up to 1.5 TB.
      • Litigation Hold and retention policies to keep mail for legal or regulatory periods.
      • Exchange Online Protection anti-spam and anti-malware filtering on every message.
      • Shared calendars, resource booking and Bookings pages for customers.

      Teams, SharePoint & OneDrive

      • Teams chat, channels, meetings and webinars — in the "with Teams" licence, or via Teams Enterprise alongside the "(no Teams)" licence.
      • OneDrive: 1 TB per user, raised to 5 TB for plans of five or more users, with more on request per Microsoft.
      • SharePoint team sites and intranet, Viva Engage communities and Viva Connections.
      • External sharing controls per site, with expiry links and guest access reviews.

      Microsoft Entra ID P1

      • Conditional Access — allow, block or require MFA by user, device, app and location.
      • MFA and passwordless sign-in with Microsoft Authenticator, passkeys or FIDO2 keys.
      • Hybrid identity with Entra Connect from your on-premises Active Directory, plus self-service password reset with writeback.
      • Single sign-on to SaaS apps such as Zoho, Salesforce or your HRMS. Risk-based policies and PIM need Entra ID P2 (in E5).

      Intune & Windows 11 Enterprise

      • Microsoft Intune Plan 1 — enrol, configure, patch and wipe Windows, macOS, iOS and Android devices.
      • Windows Autopilot — ship a laptop straight to an employee and it builds itself on first sign-in.
      • Windows 11 Enterprise E3 as an upgrade right over a licensed Windows 10/11 Pro device — it doesn't replace the base Pro licence.
      • App protection policies on personal phones: keep company data in Outlook and Teams, block copy-paste out.

      Defender & Purview baseline

      • Defender for Endpoint Plan 1 — next-gen antivirus, attack surface reduction and device-based controls.
      • Purview Information Protection — manual sensitivity labels (Confidential, Internal) with encryption.
      • Data loss prevention for Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams.
      • Audit (Standard) and eDiscovery (Standard) — audit records kept 180 days by default per Microsoft.
      • Not in E3: EDR with automated investigation, Defender for Office 365 P2, Defender for Identity, Insider Risk, eDiscovery Premium — those come with E5.

      Licensing rules worth knowing

      Per user, not per device

      One licence covers one named person on all of their (up to 15) devices.

      With Teams or "(no Teams)"

      Per Microsoft, suites with Teams are again sold worldwide since 1 Nov 2025; the no-Teams version plus Teams Enterprise is also available. We quote the cheaper fit.

      Annual commitment

      Seats can be added any time; reductions are made at renewal, with a short cancellation window after purchase under Microsoft's terms.

      Mix in one tenant

      E3 can sit beside E5, Office 365 and frontline F1/F3 licences — give each person the plan their job needs.

      Who it's for · and who it isn't

      Right for the desk-based majority. Not for everyone in the building.

      A good fit

      • Enterprises of 1,000–10,000 users standardising on one Microsoft stack.
      • Companies issuing Windows laptops that want them built, patched and locked down by Intune.
      • Firms leaving on-premises Exchange or Google Workspace and their MDM tool together.
      • Groups with several entities that want one tenant, one identity and one policy set.

      Look elsewhere if

      • You're regulated BFSI, healthcare or legal needing EDR, PIM and eDiscovery Premium — E5 fits better.
      • Staff work on the shop floor without their own PC — F1 or F3 cost far less.
      • Devices are already managed by another MDM and Windows is Pro — Office 365 E3 may be enough.
      • You have under 300 users — Business Premium is often the better value.

      Sensitivity labels, DLP and access controls protect personal data; Audit (Standard) records who opened what, for breach investigation and intimation to the Data Protection Board.

      Incidents must be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs kept for 180 days. E3's audit log holds 180 days by default per Microsoft; we can stream logs to Sentinel for longer retention.

      RBI, SEBI & IRDAI

      Regulated entities usually need EDR, privileged access control and eDiscovery — we generally recommend E5 for those users and E3 for the rest.
