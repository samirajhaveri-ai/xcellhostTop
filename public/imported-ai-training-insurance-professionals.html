    Why this course

    Your teams already paste policy text into chatbots. Teach them to do it safely, and well.

    Insurance work is document-heavy and rule-bound, which makes it a good fit for generative AI and an easy place to make costly mistakes. This course turns ad-hoc experiments into repeatable, reviewed workflows.

    01

    Wordings are long and dense

    Policy wordings, endorsements and circulars run to dozens of pages. AI can summarise them in minutes, but only if people check the clause it cites.

    Taught in M3 · Lab 1
    02

    Claims queues never shrink

    Intake notes, document checklists and first-contact replies take hours. AI can draft them, so handlers spend their time on judgement.

    Taught in M5 · Lab 3
    03

    AI must never decide alone

    Claim rejections, loadings and fraud findings affect policyholders' rights. The course builds a human decision into every workflow, by design.

    Taught in M6 · M8
    04

    Personal and health data is sensitive

    Proposal forms and claim files hold health, financial and KYC data. Staff learn what can go into which tool and how to redact it first.

    Responsible AI & India compliance

    What IRDAI and the DPDP Act mean for an insurance team using AI.

    A neutral summary of the rules the course covers, as of October 2026. It is training content, not legal advice: your compliance team and the official texts decide how they apply to you.

    IRDAI · cyber security

    Information and Cyber Security Guidelines

    IRDAI's 2023 guidelines for regulated entities were revised on 6 April 2026. Cyber incidents must be reported to CERT-In within six hours of detection, and regulated entities must take technical and organisational measures to comply with the DPDP Act.

    In class: approved tools only, and what to do if someone pastes policyholder data into a public chatbot.
    IRDAI · AI governance

    Working Group on AI Governance

    IRDAI set up a working group on AI governance in the insurance sector on 17 June 2026, with three months to make recommendations. Its terms of reference include ethical, transparent and explainable AI in claims and fraud prevention, an AI audit framework, and skill gaps. At the time of writing, no AI-specific IRDAI rules had been issued; check irdai.gov.in for updates.

    In class: an AI-use inventory and audit trail your team can keep from day one.
    IRDAI · fraud

    Insurance Fraud Monitoring Framework Guidelines, 2025

    These apply from 1 April 2026. Insurers need a board-approved anti-fraud policy with red-flag indicators, a fraud monitoring unit and committee, and must take part in the Insurance Information Bureau's fraud-monitoring technology framework.

    In class: AI surfaces indicators; the fraud monitoring unit investigates and decides.
    MeitY · privacy

    DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

    The Rules were notified in November 2025 and phase in over 18 months; most obligations, including notice, consent and breach intimation, apply from May 2027. Proposal forms and claim files hold the personal and health data these rules protect.

    In class: purpose limitation, minimisation and redaction before any prompt.
