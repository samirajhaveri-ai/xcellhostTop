Overview

Visualise the work — so everyone reads the same picture.

Visio is Microsoft's diagramming app. It turns a process, a network, a building or a team into a clear drawing that stays connected to the data behind it.

Microsoft Visio gives you professional templates and thousands of shapes for flowcharts, org charts, network and cloud diagrams, floor plans and engineering drawings. Shapes snap, connectors route themselves around obstacles, and diagrams can be linked to Excel, SQL Server or SharePoint so colours and numbers update when the data does. Teams and the browser let several people edit the same file at once.

Every business and enterprise Microsoft 365 plan already includes basic Visio in the browser. Visio Plan 1 and Plan 2 add the full shape library, data linking and — in Plan 2 — the desktop app. XcellHost buys it for you through the Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider programme, invoices in rupees with GST, deploys template packs, trains your teams and can map your processes in BPMN for you.

From whiteboard to live dashboard — scroll through six steps.

The same 24 pieces move from scattered sticky notes to a template, a flowchart, a data-linked diagram, a shared Teams file and a Power BI report.

  1. 01The ideaCapture

    A process lives in sticky notes, emails and people's heads. Visio starts where that mess is — a blank page or a whiteboard photo.

    Brainstorm · mind map
  2. 02TemplatesPick a starting point

    Choose from templates for flowcharts, BPMN, org charts, networks, floor plans and timelines. Plan 2 adds 250,000+ shapes and partner solutions.

    Templates · stencils
  3. 03DrawingDrop & auto-connect

    Drag shapes onto the page; connectors snap to connection points and re-route when you move things. Containers, layers and grids keep large diagrams tidy.

  4. 04DataLink the numbers

    Link shapes to Excel, SQL Server or SharePoint lists. Data graphics turn values into colours, icons and bars — and refresh when the source changes.

  6. 06PublishReport & present
