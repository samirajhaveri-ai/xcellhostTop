Overview

One report your clients' auditors rely on — instead of auditing you one by one.

A SOC 1 report covers controls at a service organisation that matter to its clients' internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). If you run payroll, process claims, service loans, reconcile accounts or host billing for other companies, their external auditors need comfort that your part of the chain works. A SOC 1 gives it to them once, in a standard form. It is performed under SSAE 18 (AICPA, US) or ISAE 3402 (IAASB, international); ICAI's SAE 3402 is the Indian equivalent. A Type I covers design at a point in time; a Type II also tests operating effectiveness over a period, usually 6–12 months.

You define the control objectives, describe the system and run the controls. An independent CPA firm examines them and issues the opinion — XcellHost never does. Our job is everything before and around that: scoping, control design, the system description, IT general controls, complementary user entity controls (CUECs), evidence, mock testing and coordinating the auditor. We hold SOC 2 Type I & II and ISO 27001 for our own 24×7 operations in Mumbai, so we know what an attestation season feels like from the inside.
