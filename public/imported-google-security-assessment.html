Overview

Google runs the service. You decide who gets in and what gets out.

Google Workspace is secure by design, but many of its protections are switches an admin has to turn on, and many of its conveniences are open by default. 2-Step Verification that is allowed but not enforced. A browser extension or AI app with permission to read every email. A Drive folder of payroll files shared with "anyone with the link". These are the gaps attackers and auditors find.

XcellSecure Google Security Assessment is XcellHost's fixed-scope review of one Workspace tenant. We use a read-only admin role to work through the settings that matter, test them against the CIS Google Workspace Foundations Benchmark and CISA's SCuBA Google Workspace baselines, and give you a ranked list of fixes for your edition. Running workloads on Google Cloud too? Add a project review against the CIS Google Cloud benchmark. Priced in INR with a GST invoice.

How it works

Three benchmarks, one ranked list for your edition.

CIS
Google Workspace Foundations Benchmark v1.4.0

Consensus guidance from the Center for Internet Security for the admin console, from directory and apps to security and reporting. Version 1.4.0 updated its CIS Controls v7 and v8 mappings.

Source: cisecurity.org, CIS Benchmarks update, September 2026.
CISA
SCuBA Google Workspace baselines

US CISA's secure configuration baselines for Gmail, Drive and Docs, Meet, Calendar, Chat, Groups for Business, Sites, Classroom and common controls, with an open-source checker called ScubaGoggles. A reference standard here, not an Indian legal requirement.

Source: cisa.gov, SCuBA project.
CIS
Google Cloud Platform Foundations Benchmark v5.0.0

Used only if you add Google Cloud projects: IAM, logging, networking, storage and organisation policies.

Source: cisecurity.org, CIS Benchmarks update, June 2026.

    Likelihood × impact for your business gives High, Medium or Low. Settings that differ by OU are called out, because exceptions are where gaps hide.

  6. 6

    Agreed quick wins applied in a change window — pilot OU first — then retested and shown before and after.
