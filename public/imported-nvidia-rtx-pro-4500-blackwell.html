Overview

A Blackwell GPU in one PCIe slot.

NVIDIA makes the RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell in two editions. We host the one built for servers.

The RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition is a single-slot, full-height, full-length PCIe card with 32 GB of GDDR7 memory and a 165 W power limit. It has no fan of its own: the server's fans push air through its heatsink, which is why it can sit densely in standard rack servers.

It carries the same Blackwell building blocks as larger cards — fifth-generation Tensor Cores with FP4, fourth-generation RT cores, and NVENC and NVDEC video engines — at a size and power draw that suit inference, RAG, video analytics, rendering and virtual workstations. For Indian teams, that means a modern GPU you can run in Mumbai without paying for HBM you don't need.

Source: NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition and Workstation Edition product pages, nvidia.com, retrieved 12 Nov 2026.
