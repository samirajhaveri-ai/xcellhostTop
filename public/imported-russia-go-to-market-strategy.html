Sanctions & export-control compliance · status at 23 Nov 2026

Three regimes, checked against primary sources

A neutral summary of what the EU, the US and the UK prohibit, with dates. XcellHost is an Indian company. These regimes bind EU, US and UK persons and companies, US-origin items, and any staff, subsidiaries, partners or banks with those links. They also carry a secondary-sanctions risk for companies elsewhere. Confirm every point with sanctions counsel.

EU
Regulation (EU) No 833/2014, Article 5n21 packages adopted to 23 Jul 2026733
  • ProhibitedIT consultancy: added by the 8th package and in effect from 6 Oct 20223. The Commission's FAQ of 22 Jan 2026 lists it at Art. 5n(1)(e) and says it includes software maintenance4.
  • ProhibitedEnterprise-management and industrial design / manufacturing software (Annex XXXIX): added by the 12th package of 18 Dec 2023; the wind-down ended on 20 Mar 202434. The ban reaches this software when it is delivered as SaaS4.
  • NarrowerCloud services are generally not IT consultancy, so that ban does not cover them. The software ban still applies to covered software delivered through the cloud4.
  • ProhibitedAI services, high-performance and quantum computing, including GPU-as-a-Service: 19th package, adopted 23 Oct 2025 and applying from 25 Nov 202545.
  • ProhibitedManaged cyber security services to the Russian Government and entities established in Russia: 20th package, adopted 23 Apr 20266.
  • NoteThe 21st package (in force 24 Jul 2026) made no reported change to IT services. It added Annex IV entities, including intermediaries in India7.

Article numbering: an earlier commentary places IT consultancy at Art. 5n(2) and the software ban at 5n(2b)3. The Commission's 2026 FAQ uses 5n(1)(e) and 5n(3)4. We cite the FAQ; check against the Official Journal text.

US
OFAC (EO 14071) · BIS (EAR)Treasury and Commerce, June 202412
  • ProhibitedOFAC determination of 12 Jun 2024, "Prohibition on Certain Information Technology and Software Services". It bars IT consultancy and design services, and IT support or cloud-based services for enterprise-management and design and manufacturing software, to any person in Russia. Effective 12 Sep 20241.
  • RestrictedBIS export controls on certain EAR99 software for enterprise resource planning and other business functions to Russia and Belarus. Effective 16 Sep 20242.
  • ReachThese apply to supplies from the US or by US persons. Export controls also follow US-origin software when it is re-exported2.
  • Secondary riskUS Russia-related sanctions allow sanctions on non-US persons, including foreign financial institutions, under EO 14024 as amended by EO 14114 (Dec 2023)10. Exact scope: not re-verified against the order text.
UK
Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019OFSI / Department for Business and Trade guidance89
  • ProhibitedAccounting, business and management consultancy, PR (reg. 54C), from 21 Jul 20228.
  • ProhibitedIT consultancy and design, plus advertising, architecture, auditing and engineering, from 16 Dec 2022. UK guidance excludes services such as data storage and VPNs from the IT consultancy definition8.
  • ProhibitedConstruction services from 20 May 2026; legal advisory (reg. 54D) from 30 Jun 20238.
  • RestrictedSectoral software: transfers by download, SaaS or app access are covered (Chapter 4N, Schedule 3IA, inserted in 2025). Guidance published 24 Apr 20259.
Secondary riskFirms outside the West are listed too. The EU's 19th package added 17 third-country entities to Annex IV, 3 of them in India. It also listed third-country banks and payment providers in Annex XLV5. The 21st package added more Annex IV intermediaries, including some from India7. Dealing with a listed party can cost an Indian provider its Western vendor, banking and customer relationships.
India's positionStated neutrally. India's Ministry of External Affairs said on 18 Jul 2025 that "India does not subscribe to any unilateral sanction measures"11. India is bound by UN Security Council measures; we did not re-verify a primary MEA statement saying it follows UN sanctions only. Indian law does not make EU, US or UK rules binding on XcellHost, but XcellHost's partners, banks and many customers are bound by them.

Executive summary

A conditional, compliance-first plan

The case on one screen: what is realistic, what changed recently, the entry model and what we aim to reach in 18 months if counsel clears it.

The opportunity
Narrow

Russia is a large digital market: 136 million internet users29. Little of it is open to XcellHost. Western partner products are unavailable, personal data must be stored in Russia, and critical infrastructure must use Russian software.

  • 144M people, 94.4% online, 225M mobile connections (157%)29.
  • India–Russia trade: US$68.7B in FY2024–25 and US$59.9B in FY2025–26, mostly Indian imports of oil and petroleum products30.
  • GDP grew about 1% in 2025 (Rosstat, via The Bell)31.
  • Our addressable segment is mainly Indian companies in the corridor, served from India for their Indian operations.
What changed recently
6 changes

Six legal and tax changes between May 2025 and April 2026. See the calendar.

  • 1 Jul 2025: Federal Law 23-FZ prohibits using databases outside Russia to record and store Russian citizens' personal data17.
  • 30 May 2025: leak fines rise; a repeat leak costs 1–3% of annual revenue, up to ₽500M19.
  • 1 Sep 2025: significant CII must use software from the Russian software register21.
  • 25 Nov 2025 / 23 Apr 2026: EU bans on AI, HPC and managed cyber security services56. 1 Jan 2026: VAT rises to 22%23.
Recommended entry model
Screen first

No Russia-facing sales until a written legal opinion. Start India-side: security, GRC and third-party-risk services for Indian firms that trade with Russia.

  • Months 0–6: sanctions policy, screening, vendor-licence matrix, bank review and counsel opinions.
  • India-side: Indian customers, Indian data, Mumbai Tier IV, and only partner products permitted for that customer.
  • Russia-facing, if cleared: XcellHost-delivered assessments and Indian-vendor products only. Mumbai is never the primary store of Russian citizens' personal data17.

    Questions partners will ask

    Short answers based on the sources above. Not legal or tax advice.

    Not as the primary store of Russian citizens' personal data. Since 1 July 2025, Federal Law 152-FZ, as amended by 23-FZ, does not permit using databases outside Russia to record, systematise, store, update or retrieve that data, with narrow exceptions. The Mumbai Tier IV therefore serves Indian customers' Indian data only in this plan.

    Not by Indian law. India's Ministry of External Affairs said in July 2025 that India does not subscribe to unilateral sanction measures.
