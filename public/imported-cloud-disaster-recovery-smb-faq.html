Cloud DR for SMBs, answered.

A backup is a copy of your data. Disaster recovery is a ready-to-run copy of your systems in another location, plus the runbook and drills to switch to it. With backup alone you still need new hardware and a restore; with DR your servers start at the DR site in minutes to hours, depending on the tier.

RPO (recovery point objective) is how much data you can afford to lose, measured in time — for example, the last 15 minutes. RTO (recovery time objective) is how long you can afford to be down. Our DR plan picker turns both into a tier and a monthly estimate.

Replicas and immutable copies are kept in XcellHost's own Tier IV data centre in Mumbai, or at a partner DR site in India if you need a second city*. No copies leave India unless you ask in writing.

You or we declare a disaster under the runbook. We start your servers at the DR site in the agreed order from the chosen recovery point, switch DNS or VPN, and your staff reconnect from office, home or a branch. The whole sequence is rehearsed in drills first.

Once the office is repaired, changes made at the DR site are synced back while you keep working. A short planned cut-over moves you home, and replication resumes in the normal direction.
