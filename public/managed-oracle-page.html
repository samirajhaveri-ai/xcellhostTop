    Overview

    Oracle built the cloud around its database. We run it around your business.

    Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is where many Indian companies already running Oracle Database, E-Business Suite or JD Edwards end up — the licences carry over, the database services are native, and both OCI regions in India are live. What it does not come with is a team that watches it at 3 a.m.

    XcellHost is that team. We assess what you run, design the tenancy, move the workloads and then operate them from our Mumbai NOC/SOC: monitoring, incidents, patching, backups, Data Guard, security posture and cost. You keep control of your OCI tenancy and your data; OCI usage is billed at actuals on one INR invoice with GST, and our management fee is fixed up front.
