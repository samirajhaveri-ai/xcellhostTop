    Overview

    Email is still where most intrusions begin. Vortex SEG checks it at the door.

    Vortex SEG is a secure email gateway that sits in front of your mail platform — as the MX gateway, through an API integration or as an SMTP relay. It inspects inbound, outbound and internal mail for phishing, impersonation, malware and sensitive data, then delivers, quarantines, encrypts or archives each message according to your policy.

    You keep Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Zoho Mail, Exchange or Zimbra exactly as they are — there is no mailbox migration. XcellHost licenses it for your own team, or deploys, tunes and operates it for you with 24×7 monitoring from Mumbai.

    Features

    Nine controls in the suite — none of them an add-on.

    Hover or tap a card for what it does in practice.

    Anti-Phishing

    Scores links, landing pages, sender reputation and language for lures, credential harvesting and urgent-payment scams.

    Rewrites links for time-of-click checks

    Anti-Spoofing

    Flags lookalike domains, display-name fraud and executive impersonation — the core of business email compromise.

    Warns users with an external-sender banner

    Anti-Malware

    Multiple anti-virus engines plus a dynamic sandbox that opens unknown attachments in isolation before delivery.

    Unsafe files stripped, message still delivered

    Email DLP

    Policies for personal and financial data — Aadhaar, PAN, account numbers, client files — on every outbound message.

    Block, quarantine for review, or encrypt

    OCR Attachment Scanning

    Reads text inside images, screenshots and scanned PDFs, so a photographed document is checked like typed text.

    Catches the "I'll just scan it" leak

    SPF, DKIM & DMARC

    Verifies inbound authentication and helps you publish and enforce your own records, so nobody can send as your domain.
