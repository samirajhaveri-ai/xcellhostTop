Overview

The control panel your team already knows, on resources you don't share.

cPanel & WHM is the most widely used web hosting control panel. WHM lets you create and manage hosting accounts; cPanel gives each account point-and-click tools for websites, email, databases, DNS, SSL and backups. On a VPS, it runs on resources that belong only to you.

XcellHost cPanel VPS comes with cPanel & WHM installed on AlmaLinux, a cPanel licence tier that matches the number of accounts you host, NVMe storage and a dedicated IPv4. It's ideal for agencies, developers and businesses with many sites — and for hosting resellers who want their own brand. Billing is in rupees with GST.
