What is the NVIDIA B300?

NVIDIA B300 is the Blackwell Ultra data-centre GPU. Each B300 carries 288 GB of HBM3e at up to 8 TB/s, fifth-generation Tensor Cores with an enhanced FP4 Transformer Engine, and NVLink at 1.8 TB/s per GPU. It delivers about 1.5× the FP4 compute of B200 and twice the attention-layer throughput, and ships in 8-GPU HGX B300 servers — 2.3 TB of GPU memory in one system. NVIDIA B300 FAQs

What is the NVIDIA B300?
B300 is NVIDIA's Blackwell Ultra data-centre GPU, with 288 GB of HBM3e at up to 8 TB/s, fifth-generation Tensor Cores with an enhanced FP4 engine and NVLink at 1.8 TB/s.
How is B300 different from B200?
B300 raises memory from 192 GB to 288 GB HBM3e, delivers about 1.5× the FP4 compute and doubles attention-layer throughput, while keeping 8 TB/s bandwidth and 1.8 TB/s NVLink.
How much does B300 cost in India?

Can I rent a single B300 or do I need all eight?
Both. Choose 1×, 2×, 4× or 8× B300, or a multi-node cluster.
Is B300 better for training or inference?
Both. The memory and NVLink suit large-model training, and the FP4 engine and attention speed-up suit high-volume inference.
Do I need a long-term contract?
No. Monthly reserved terms are available; 6- and 12-month terms lower the hourly rate.
Which software is supported?
PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, CUDA, cuDNN, TensorRT, TensorRT-LLM, NeMo, Triton, vLLM and NIM, on Ubuntu with Kubernetes or Slurm.
Is the data hosted in India?
Yes. XcellHost GPU capacity is hosted in Indian data centres.

No. Prices are in INR and exclude 18% GST, which is added at billing.
