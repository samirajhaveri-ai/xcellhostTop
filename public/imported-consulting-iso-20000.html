Overview

A service management system auditors can test — built around how your team already works.

ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 is the international standard for an IT service management system (SMS). It sets auditable requirements for how you plan, agree, deliver, support and improve IT services: a defined scope and service portfolio, SLAs with customers and contracts with suppliers, capacity and budgets, controlled change and release, incident, request and problem handling, and assurance of availability, continuity and information security. Certification is issued by an accredited certification body after a two-stage audit.

XcellHost gets you ready for that audit. We score your current practice clause by clause, design the SMS around the language your teams already use — usually ITIL 4 and an ITSM tool you already own — write the documents, coach process owners, run the internal audit and stand with you at Stage 1 and Stage 2. We run our own 24×7 NOC under ISO 20000-1, so the templates come from daily use, not a binder.

    Who it's for in India

    When customers, tenders or regulators ask how you run IT services.

    ISO 20000-1 is rarely a legal requirement on its own. It is how you prove, in one certificate, the controls that RFPs and regulators keep asking about.

    IT services & MSPs

    Managed-services RFPs often list ISO 20000-1 next to ISO 27001. It shows you run services to agreed levels, not just projects.

    Data centres & cloud

    Government, PSU and enterprise tenders for hosting and cloud commonly ask for certified service management operations.

    Banks, NBFCs & fintech

    RBI's 2023 Master Direction on IT governance expects documented change, incident, capacity and continuity management.

    Capital markets

    SEBI's CSCRF asks regulated entities for asset inventories, change and patch control and tested incident response.

    GCCs, BPO & SaaS

    Global parents and clients audit your SLAs. One internationally recognised certificate answers many questionnaires.

    Government suppliers

    Tender eligibility lists often include ISO 20000-1 for IT support, facility management and helpdesk contracts.

    CERT-In and DPDP run through your incident process. CERT-In's 2022 directions expect notifiable cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours and logs kept for 180 days; the DPDP Act requires personal-data breaches to be reported to the Data Protection Board and affected people. We build those paths into your incident and major-incident procedures. Guidance only, not legal advice.

    It is the international standard with requirements for a service management system. Part 1, published in 2018, is the certifiable part. It covers context and scope, leadership, planning, support, the service management processes in clause 8, performance evaluation and improvement. An organisation that meets it can be certified by an accredited certification body.

    ITIL 4 is good-practice guidance on how to run services; individuals can be certified in it, organisations cannot. ISO 20000-1 is a set of auditable requirements for the organisation. They fit together: we keep your ITIL practices and add the scope, policy, records and reviews an auditor needs to see.

    No. Certification is issued by an accredited certification body after a Stage 1 and a Stage 2 audit. XcellHost prepares you, runs the internal audit, coordinates the audits and helps you close findings. We can recommend accredited certification partners; you contract and pay them directly.

Stage 2 tests implementation: auditors sample tickets, changes, SLAs, supplier reviews, internal audit and management review records.
