    Quick answer

    What is Microsoft Power Apps? Low-code apps on your business data.

    Microsoft Power Apps is Microsoft's low-code platform for building business apps. You draw screens (canvas apps) or generate them from a data model (model-driven apps), connect them to Dataverse, SharePoint, Excel, SQL or hundreds of other services, and publish to phones, browsers and Microsoft Teams — without a full software project.

    For most Indian businesses the useful version is narrow: replace one paper form or shared Excel sheet with an app, with approvals and an audit trail. XcellHost sells the licences in INR, builds the first app with your team, sets up governance so apps don't sprawl, and supports them afterwards.

    • Licence per user — Power Apps Premium is ₹1,665 per user / month (Microsoft India list, paid yearly; checked 17 Oct 2026).
    • Or pay as you go — billed through Azure per active user per app each month.
    • Microsoft 365 includes basics — standard connectors and Microsoft 365 data only; premium needs a Power Apps licence.

    What you can build

    Three ways to build, one data model underneath.

    Pick the app style by who uses it and where. All three can share the same Dataverse tables, security roles and audit history.

    Canvas apps

    You design every screen, pixel by pixel, in Power Fx. Best for phones and tablets: inspections, site visits, stock counts, attendance with photo and location.

    Phone · tablet · browser

    Model-driven apps

    Start from Dataverse tables and relationships; forms, views, dashboards and business process flows are generated. Best for back-office work: cases, vendors, assets, onboarding.

    Desktop-first · role-based

    Apps in Teams & agents

    Pin an app as a Teams tab so people use it where they already chat, and add Copilot or agent experiences inside model-driven apps where Microsoft supports them.
