What a vCISO does

Someone who owns security — and answers for it.

Most Indian mid-size firms have firewalls, antivirus and an IT team, but nobody who owns security as a business risk. Policies are copied, risks live in someone's head, auditors and customers send questionnaires nobody can answer, and the board hears about security only after an incident.

A virtual CISO fills that seat part-time. Your XcellConsult vCISO sets the security strategy, keeps a living risk register, writes and enforces the policy suite, leads audit readiness and incident drills, and reports to management and the board in plain language — with our 24×7 SOC in Mumbai behind them.

Senior security leadership, as a role — not a resume. We assign an experienced security lead and a deputy for cover; you see their profiles with the proposal. No named individuals or certifications are promised on this page.

      India compliance

      Indian rules, read by someone who owns them.

      Your vCISO keeps these obligations on the security calendar and prepares the evidence. Where a regulator requires your own CISO, we support that person — we do not replace them.

      6 hCERT-In incident reporting

      CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 (under section 70B(6) of the IT Act) require specified cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them, and a designated point of contact. Your vCISO writes the runbook and rehearses it.

      180 dCERT-In log retention

      The same directions require ICT system logs to be kept for a rolling 180 days, with a copy maintained or accessible within Indian jurisdiction. We check what is logged, where it is kept and whether it can be produced on request.

      RBIRBI IT governance directions

      RBI's Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (7 Nov 2023, effective 1 Apr 2024) asks regulated entities for a senior CISO who reports to the executive overseeing risk, not to the head of IT, and reviews cyber risk with the board at least quarterly. A vCISO supports that office with risk reviews, policies and board packs.

      SEBISEBI CSCRF

      SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (August 2024) requires every regulated entity to have a CISO. SEBI's FAQs of 11 June 2025 allow a remote or group-level CISO but not a part-time one. For SEBI entities our vCISO works as advisor and "office of the CISO" to your designated CISO.

      IRDAIIRDAI cyber security guidelines

      IRDAI's Information and Cyber Security Guidelines (24 April 2023) expect a governance structure for information security and incident reporting to CERT-In within 6 hours, with a copy to IRDAI. We build the structure, calendar and evidence.

      DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

      The Act requires reasonable security safeguards and intimation of personal-data breaches to the Data Protection Board and affected people, with the Rules notified in November 2025 and phased in. The vCISO owns the security side; our vDPO covers the privacy programme.

      Regulatory summaries checked October 2026 from CERT-In, RBI, SEBI and IRDAI publications, for orientation only — not legal advice. Your organisation and its board remain responsible for compliance; confirm current requirements with the regulator.
