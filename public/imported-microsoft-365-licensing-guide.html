01 · Foundations

How Microsoft 365 licensing works

Microsoft 365 is licensed per user, per month, bought as a subscription for a fixed term, and assigned to people inside one tenant — your organisation's Microsoft 365 directory. Understand those four words — user, subscription, term, tenant — and most licensing questions answer themselves.

  • A subscription is a quantity of licences for one product. Buying "50 × Business Premium" creates one subscription with 50 seats. You assign a seat to a user; that user gets every service in the plan.
  • A licence follows the person, not the device. One licensed user can install the Microsoft 365 desktop apps on up to five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five phones — that's Microsoft's own wording for Apps for business.
  • Plans are bundles of service plans. "Microsoft 365 E3" is really Exchange Online Plan 2 + SharePoint + OneDrive + Microsoft 365 Apps + Intune + Entra ID P1 + Windows Enterprise and more. Admins can switch individual service plans off per user.
  • You can mix plans in one tenant. Microsoft says Enterprise, Business and standalone plans can be combined in a single account — for example E3 for office staff and F3 for the shop floor.
  • Terms and billing are separate choices. A one-year term can be billed monthly or yearly. Under Microsoft's new commerce rules, what you commit to is what you pay for until renewal (section 12).
Tip

Licences are cheapest to fix before you buy. Most over-spend we see comes from buying one plan for everyone. A mixed tenant — premium plans for people who need them, frontline plans for those who don't — is normal and fully supported.

03 · Small & mid-size

Watch out

Hitting 301 users does not mean moving everyone to E3. Keep Business Premium for up to 300 people and license the rest with Enterprise or Frontline plans — or plan a full move when growth makes the 300 line permanent. Section 18 covers the upgrade path.

05 · Teams

Teams or no Teams? The unbundling story, settled

For two years "do we still get Teams with E3?" was the most common question we heard. Here is the verified sequence and where things stand on 31 October 2026.

  • Today, India included: Microsoft's licensing news says Teams-inclusive Enterprise suites are available "worldwide again" to all customers, new and existing, from 1 November 2025. No-Teams versions remain, at a lower price, for organisations that use another meeting platform.
  • No-Teams + Teams Enterprise costs more than the with-Teams suite after Microsoft raised the standalone Teams Enterprise price in November 2025 — so the two-SKU route only makes sense if you genuinely don't need Teams.
  • Business and Frontline plans were always sold with Teams outside the EEA; no-Teams Business versions also exist.
  • E7 is also available with and without Teams, per Microsoft's launch post.
Watch out

Some Microsoft Learn pages still describe the 2024 rule ("Teams-inclusive suites no longer sold to new customers"). The November 2025 licensing announcement is newer and is what partners transact against — ask us to confirm the exact SKU before you order.

Four AI licences, often confused. In order of cost:

Microsoft states Copilot Chat is "available at no additional cost for all Microsoft Entra account users with an eligible Microsoft 365 subscription". Web-grounded chat with enterprise data protection; the July 2026 update added Copilot Chat enhancements and analytics to every suite.

The SMB add-on for Business plans, also sold as "Business Standard/Premium with Copilot" bundles (₹1,955 / ₹2,660 on our page). Microsoft has announced that usage-based billing for select advanced AI features becomes default-on for new Copilot Business licences from 1 December 2026. Standalone add-on: price on quote.

3Microsoft 365 Copilot — enterprise add-on

Grounded in your mail, files, chats and meetings through Microsoft Graph; needs a qualifying Microsoft 365 base licence. Indicative ₹2,649* per user per month on our E7 page; final price on quote. Agents built in Copilot Studio need an Azure subscription or prepaid capacity.

4Agent 365 and Microsoft 365 E7

Agent 365 is Microsoft's "control plane for AI agents" — inventory, access and governance — generally available since 1 May 2026. E7, the Frontier Suite, combines E5, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Agent 365 and the Microsoft Entra Suite, generally available 1 May 2026. See Microsoft 365 E7 (indicative ₹8,699*) and Agent 365 (licence on quote).

Tip

Pilot before you commit. Copilot is a per-user annual commitment; start with 10–20 people whose work is document- and meeting-heavy, measure, then scale. Microsoft's Copilot pricing page shows monthly billing about 20% above the yearly price — and only a 7-day refund window once paid.

08 · Sector plans

Education, nonprofit and government

Education: A1, A3, A5

Office 365 A1 is free and web-only. Microsoft 365 A3 adds the desktop apps and device management; A5 adds advanced security, compliance and analytics. Microsoft retired the transitional Office 365 A1 Plus (with desktop apps) — unassigned A1 Plus users were moved to A1 on 1 August 2024 and remaining licences expire. Institutions needing desktop apps now license A3/A5 or the desktop-apps add-on. Prices on quote; eligibility is verified by Microsoft.

Nonprofit: the 2025 grant change

Microsoft discontinued both the free Office 365 E1 grant and the Microsoft 365 Business Premium grant. Its nonprofit updates page (section dated 1 July 2025) says existing grant licences expire on the next renewal date and won't renew. Nonprofits still get up to 300 granted Microsoft 365 Business Basic licences and discounts of up to 75% on many offers, including Business Premium, Business Standard and E1. Our nonprofit prices: Business Premium ₹480, E3 ₹700, E5 ₹2,420, F3 ₹230, Office 365 E3 ₹480.

Watch out

Grant licences are not converted automatically. Microsoft asks nonprofits to move users manually in the admin centre before the grant subscription ends, to avoid data loss. Book the move before your renewal date.

Government

Microsoft 365 Government (GCC, GCC High, DoD) is for US federal, state, local and tribal entities and certain US-regulated contractors. It is not an option for Indian ministries, PSUs or state bodies, which buy commercial plans. For sovereign-hosting requirements, see India specifics.

11 · Rules

Licensing rules that trip people up

Shared mailboxes

A shared mailbox needs no licence of its own up to 50 GB — but every person who opens it must hold an Exchange Online licence. It needs a licence if you want more than 50 GB (Exchange Online Plan 2 takes it to 100 GB), an auto-expanding archive, or a litigation hold (Plan 2, or Plan 1 + Exchange Online Archiving).

Resource accounts and meeting rooms

Room and equipment mailboxes don't need a licence below 50 GB. Teams auto attendants and call queues use free resource-account licences. Meeting-room devices are different: Microsoft says per-user suite licences are not authorised on shared meeting devices — each Teams Rooms system needs Teams Rooms Basic (free, up to 25) or Teams Rooms Pro.

Guests and external users

Guests (B2B collaboration) do not consume your Microsoft 365 seats. Premium Entra features for guests are billed on a monthly active user (MAU) model through an Azure subscription linked to your tenant. Microsoft states the first 50,000 MAU are free on the External ID basic tier, and reserves the right to enforce that limit for B2B with 12 months' notice.

Archives are per person

Microsoft prohibits using one user's archive mailbox to store mail from multiple users. "Journal everything into the admin's archive" is a licensing breach — license the archive per mailbox.

Mixing plans and the 300-seat line

Business, Enterprise, Frontline and standalone plans can all sit in one tenant. The only family cap is 300 Business seats in total. Many 400-person firms run 280 Business Premium + 120 F3 — fully compliant.

Frontline eligibility

F-plans are intended for frontline workers whose main job isn't at a desk. Giving F3 to office staff to save money creates compliance and productivity problems — no desktop apps, a 2 GB mailbox and 2 GB OneDrive.

Where the rules are written

Microsoft publishes the authoritative detail in three places: the service descriptions on Microsoft Learn (what each plan contains and its limits), the Product Terms (use rights) and Microsoft Licensing guidance pages (suites and prerequisites). We cite the exact pages in Sources.

12 · Terms

Terms, billing and cancellation under new commerce

Partners like XcellHost sell Microsoft 365 through Microsoft's new commerce platform. It supports monthly, annual and three-year terms for many subscriptions, with monthly or upfront billing. The rules that cost money:

  • 7-day window. Microsoft's cancellation policy gives a prorated refund within seven calendar days of purchase for licence-based subscriptions. After that, "the cancellation window doesn't reopen until the subscription itself renews into a new term."
  • Seats go up any time, come down only at renewal. Reductions happen inside the 7-day window or at renewal. Plan renewal reviews 30–60 days ahead.
  • Monthly terms cost more. Microsoft's own price pages show monthly-commitment prices about 20% above the annual price — for example Copilot Business at $21.00 a year-term versus $25.20 month-to-month. Paying an annual term in monthly instalments may also carry a small uplift; ask for both quotes.
  • Prices are fixed for the term you commit to. Microsoft's July 2026 price rise applied at renewal or new purchase, so annual and three-year terms protect you from mid-term increases. Microsoft introduced three-year Enterprise SKUs in June 2025.
  • Upgrades mid-term are allowed — for example Business Premium to E3, or E3 to E5 — including partial upgrades of some seats. Downgrades wait for renewal.
  • Trials last one month, can be cancelled during the trial and convert to paid otherwise; a cancelled trial can't be restarted.
Tip

Use monthly terms for seasonal or uncertain headcount (contract staff, projects) and annual terms for your stable core. The Microsoft CSP Licensing Guide goes deeper on terms, co-terming, promotions and transfers.

14 · India

India specifics: INR, GST, data residency, DPDP and Teams Phone

INR billing with GST

Buying through an Indian partner gives you an INR tax invoice with 18% GST, a local contract and local support. GST-registered buyers can normally take input tax credit on that GST. Every price on this page excludes GST; the calculator adds it.

Where your data lives

Microsoft has four Azure regions in India — Central India (Pune), South India (Chennai), West India (Mumbai) and India South Central (Hyderabad). For Microsoft 365, India is covered by the Product Terms data-residency commitment: if your tenant is provisioned in India, Microsoft commits to store at rest in India Exchange mailbox content, SharePoint and OneDrive files, Teams chats and stored Copilot interactions.

India is also a Local Region Geography for the Advanced Data Residency (ADR) add-on, which extends commitments to more services such as parts of Purview. ADR must cover 100% of the paid licences in the tenant. Multi-Geo is the option for global firms that need some users' data in India and others abroad.

India note

Data location is set by the tenant's default geography at sign-up. A tenant created with a non-India address stays outside the India commitment unless migrated. Check the Data Location card in the Microsoft 365 admin centre before you assume.

DPDP Act and Rules

The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 14 November 2025 with an 18-month period for phased compliance. The highest penalty — up to ₹250 crore — applies to failing to keep reasonable security safeguards. Licensing matters here: Conditional Access (Entra ID P1), device compliance (Intune), DLP and sensitivity labels (Business Premium/E3), and insider-risk and audit tools (E5 or the Purview Suite) are the controls most DPDP programmes rely on. This is general information, not legal advice.

Teams Phone in India

PSTN calling in India runs on Operator Connect for India or Direct Routing, built to Indian toll-bypass rules. Microsoft requires an India-specific Teams Phone licence from a licensed Indian telecom operator — even if the user already has Teams Phone through E5. Wireline numbers need Location-Based Routing.

18 · Migration

Migration paths

Google Workspace → Microsoft 365
  1. Map Google editions to plans: Business Starter → Business Basic; Business Standard/Plus → Business Standard or Premium.
  2. Buy licences for the move; keep Google active until cut-over.
  3. Migrate Gmail, Calendar, Contacts and Drive in batches; switch MX last.
  4. Rebuild shared drives as SharePoint sites and Teams; retrain on Outlook and OneDrive.
  5. Cancel Google seats after the verification window.
Exchange on-premises → Exchange Online
  1. Choose cut-over (small) or hybrid (staged, larger) migration.
  2. License every migrated mailbox; shared and room mailboxes under 50 GB need none.
  3. Sync identities with Entra Connect; plan MFA and Conditional Access before go-live.
  4. Move mailboxes; repoint MX, Autodiscover and SMTP relays.
  5. Decommission or keep one management server per Microsoft's hybrid guidance.
Business → Enterprise upgrade
  1. Trigger: approaching 300 Business seats, or need for E5 security, Teams Phone or Windows Enterprise.
  2. Use a mid-term upgrade (Business Premium → E3/E5) — no data migration, same tenant.
  3. Align the new subscription's renewal date with your others.
  4. Re-check Intune and Defender policies — Defender for Business and Defender for Endpoint differ.
  5. Keep Business seats for staff who don't need Enterprise features.

19 · Avoid

Common mistakes to avoid

  1. One plan for everyone. Mix Business, Enterprise and Frontline plans by role.
  2. Buying monthly "to be flexible" for the whole tenant. You pay roughly 20% more; keep monthly for genuinely variable seats.
  3. Missing the 7-day window. Typo a quantity, notice on day 8, pay for a year.
  4. Forgetting auto-renew. Review quantities 60 days before renewal — that's your only chance to reduce.
  5. Licensing shared mailboxes unnecessarily — or not licensing them when they exceed 50 GB or need a hold.
  6. Giving F1 users email. F1 has no mailbox rights; buy F3.
  7. Assuming E3 includes Teams Phone or Power BI. Both are E5 inclusions.
  8. Treating Copilot Chat as Microsoft 365 Copilot. Only the paid licence works across your mail, files and meetings.
  9. Buying E5 for "security" and never switching it on. Deploy Defender, Entra P2 and Purview, or don't pay for them.
  10. Ignoring the 300 cap when growing. Plan the Enterprise move before seat 301.
  11. Room devices on user licences. Teams Rooms need Rooms Basic or Pro.
  12. Wrong tenant geography. A tenant created outside India is outside the India data-residency commitment.

    Glossary

    Tenant
    Your organisation's Microsoft 365 / Entra ID directory; holds users, domains and subscriptions.
    SKU
    A purchasable product, e.g. "Microsoft 365 E3 (no Teams)".
    Service plan
    A component inside a SKU (e.g. Exchange Online Plan 2) that admins can enable or disable per user.
    NCE / new commerce
    Microsoft's partner purchasing platform with monthly, annual and three-year terms and a 7-day cancellation window.
    CSP
    Cloud Solution Provider — Microsoft's programme for partners who sell and bill Microsoft cloud services.
    Term vs billing
    Term is the commitment length; billing frequency is how often you are invoiced.
    True-up / right-size
    Matching licence quantities and plans to real use, normally at renewal.
    Frontline worker
    Staff whose primary work isn't at a desk; the audience for F1/F3.
    Copilot Chat
    Web-grounded AI chat at no extra cost for eligible Microsoft 365 users.
    Microsoft 365 Copilot
    Paid add-on that works across your work data via Microsoft Graph.
    Agent 365
    Microsoft's governance and control plane for AI agents.
    E7 (Frontier Suite)
    E5 + Microsoft 365 Copilot + Agent 365 + Entra Suite in one licence.
    Defender Suite / Purview Suite
    The renamed E5 Security / E5 Compliance add-ons.
    MAU
    Monthly active users — how External ID bills guests above the free tier.
    ADR
    Advanced Data Residency — add-on extending in-country storage commitments.
    Operator Connect for India
    Teams Phone PSTN connectivity through a licensed Indian operator.
    GST input tax credit
    Recovery of GST paid on business purchases against output GST, subject to eligibility.

      FAQ

      Microsoft 365 licensing, answered.

      300. Microsoft's service description says Business plans are designed for organisations with up to 300 users and that the 300 limit applies to provisioned licences across the whole Business family — Basic, Standard, Premium and Apps for business combined. You can add Enterprise or Frontline plans beyond that in the same tenant.

      Yes. Microsoft states that Enterprise, Business and standalone plans can be combined in a single account. A common pattern is Business Premium for up to 300 office staff plus F3 for frontline workers, or E3 for some users and E5 for others.

      Microsoft describes the desktop apps as installable on up to five desktop computers, five tablets and five mobile devices per licensed user. Business Basic, Office 365 E1 and the F plans don't include the desktop apps at all.

      Yes, again. From 1 November 2025 Microsoft made Enterprise suites with Teams available worldwide to new and existing customers, and kept cheaper no-Teams versions for those who use another meeting platform. Between April 2024 and October 2025 new customers outside existing agreements had to buy a no-Teams suite plus Teams Enterprise.

      Copilot Chat is included at no extra cost for Entra users with an eligible Microsoft 365 subscription. Microsoft 365 Copilot — which works across your mail, files, chats and meetings — is a paid add-on (or part of E7 and the Business "with Copilot" bundles) and needs a qualifying base plan.

      Not up to 50 GB. A shared mailbox needs a licence to grow beyond 50 GB (Exchange Online Plan 2 gives 100 GB), to use an auto-expanding archive, or to be placed on litigation hold. Every user who accesses it must have an Exchange Online licence.

      Under Microsoft's new commerce policy, licence-based subscriptions can be cancelled with a prorated refund within seven calendar days of purchase. After that, quantities can be increased but not reduced until the subscription renews into a new term.

      Microsoft's own price pages show monthly-commitment prices about 20% above the annual price — for example Microsoft 365 Apps for business at 10.00 a month on an annual plan versus 12.00 month-to-month in the listed currency. Monthly terms can be cancelled at the end of any month, which suits variable headcount.

      If your tenant was provisioned with India as its default geography, Microsoft's Product Terms commit to storing core customer data at rest in India — Exchange mailboxes, SharePoint and OneDrive files, Teams chats and stored Copilot interactions. The Advanced Data Residency add-on extends this to more services and must cover all paid licences.

      Yes. XcellHost invoices in INR with 18% GST. All prices in this guide exclude GST. GST-registered businesses can normally claim the GST as input tax credit — confirm with your tax adviser.
