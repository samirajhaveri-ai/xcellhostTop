    Overview

    A small box on the desk. The desktop, data and updates stay central.

    Accops HyDesk is Accops' range of thin clients, zero clients and thin PCs. Each device is a compact endpoint that connects to a virtual desktop — Accops HyWorks, Microsoft RDS, VMware Horizon or Citrix Workspace, per Accops — so the operating system, apps and files live in the data centre, not under the desk.

    XcellHost supplies the right HyDesk model for each role, enrols every device with the HyWorks controller, sets kiosk and USB policies, and pushes upgrades in agreed windows. Pair it with HyWorks desktops hosted in our Mumbai cloud, or point the devices at the VDI you already run on your own servers.

    Key capabilities

    Everything a PC did at the desk — without the PC to look after.

    Tap a numbered point on the device. Each capability is listed by Accops for HyDesk; some depend on model and edition.

    1. 1
      Central upgrades & patching

      Installations, upgrades and OS patching run from a central console — no local IT visit. In HyWorks, an upgrade can be pushed to one device or to every device of a model.

    2. 2
      Multi-monitor & touch

      Multi-monitor support (Hy1000: dual display), plus single- and multi-touch on Linux-based devices.

    3. 3
      Kiosk mode

      Kiosk-based access boots into the session and hides the underlying OS — ideal for counters and shared desks.

    4. 4
      Approved USB redirection

      Printers, scanners, biometric readers and engineering devices redirected into the session; only approved peripherals.

    5. 5
      Hybrid & offline work

      Hybrid models run local apps too, and offline mode lets users keep working locally during an outage, per Accops.

    6. 6
      Monitor, assist & audit

      See device activity, sessions, idle time and power; HyAssist remote control across NAT; audit logs of sessions, USB use, firmware changes and power events.

    How it works

    Two lanes from every desk: the session, and the management.

    Blue packets are screen and input traffic to the desktop. Orange packets are policies and upgrades from the controller. Sample layout.

    01Power on

    The device boots Accops embedded Linux (or Windows IoT on Hy6000) and finds the HyWorks controller.

    02Receive settings

    The controller sends its device configuration — display, kiosk mode, USB rules — and the device applies it.

    03Sign in & work

    The user signs in and lands on a shared or personal desktop or a published app. Only pixels reach the desk.

    04Stay current

    Upgrades and patches go out centrally; XcellHost watches health and idle power-down from Mumbai.

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    A device with nothing on it is a device with nothing to leak.

    HyDesk keeps data off the endpoint. XcellHost adds India-resident logs and the reporting trail regulators ask for.

    No local data store

    Apps and files stay in the data centre, per Accops; a restart returns a thin client to a clean state.

    Locked-down local OS

    Kiosk-mode zero clients and a locked-down OS limit what a user — or malware — can change at the desk.

    Only approved USB

    Redirect approved readers and printers; block unknown storage drives by policy.
