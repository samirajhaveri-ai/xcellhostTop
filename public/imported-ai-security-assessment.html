    Overview

    AI arrived faster than the controls around it. An assessment shows you the gap.

    In most Indian companies AI didn't arrive as one project. A support chatbot here, a sales team using a public AI writer there, an AI feature switched on inside the CRM, a data-science team's credit model, a pilot agent that can raise tickets. Each one touches data, and few were reviewed by security. XcellAI AI Security Assessment gives you one picture: what AI you run, where data flows, which risks matter, and what to fix first.

    It is a scoped, non-destructive engagement led by XcellHost's security team, using the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025), the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, MITRE ATLAS and the NIST AI RMF. You get a risk register, a remediation plan and a report written for both engineers and leadership. When you need deep adversarial testing, that's our AI Red Teaming service.

      Frameworks mapping

      One finding, every framework your auditors and regulators use.

      Each risk in your register is cross-referenced, so the same evidence serves security, compliance and the board. Pick a risk theme to see how it maps.

      OWASPTop 10 for LLM Applications 2025

      LLM01 Prompt Injection to LLM10 Unbounded Consumption — the baseline for testing GenAI apps. The Agentic Top 10 (December 2025) adds risks for autonomous agents.

      MITRE ATLASAdversary tactics for AI

      An ATT&CK-style knowledge base of real attack techniques against AI and ML systems. We use it to threat-model each data flow.

      NISTAI RMF 1.0 + AI 600-1

      Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — and the Generative AI Profile (July 2024) with 12 GenAI risks such as confabulation, data privacy and value-chain integration.

      ISO/IEC42001:2023 & 23894:2023

      The AI management system standard (38 Annex A controls) and AI risk-management guidance. Findings are tagged to Annex A so a later ISO 42001 programme can reuse them.

      India & export rules · the assessment helps you prepare for
      DPDP Act 2023DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13 Nov 2025; security safeguards, breach intimation and consent duties phase in through Nov 2026 – May 2027.
      MeitYIndia AI Governance Guidelines (5 Nov 2025): seven principles including trust, accountability and safety.
      RBI FREE-AICommittee framework (13 Aug 2025): 7 sutras and 26 recommendations for AI in regulated finance.
      SEBIJune 2025 consultation on responsible AI/ML use by market participants — governance, testing and disclosure.
      CERT-InMarch 2025 advisory on safe use of AI tools, plus existing incident-reporting directions.
      EU AI ActFor exporters: transparency duties from 2 Aug 2026; stand-alone high-risk rules from 2 Dec 2027 after the 2026 Digital Omnibus.

      We're a security partner, not a law firm. The report shows which controls support each obligation; legal interpretation stays with your counsel and compliance team.
