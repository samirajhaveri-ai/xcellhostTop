    Overview

    AWS gives you the building blocks. Someone still has to run them.

    Managed AWS by XcellHost · for Indian businesses on Amazon Web Services

    Amazon Web Services looks after the physical data centres, hardware and the services themselves. Everything you build on top — instance sizes, networks, identities, patches, backups, alarms, budgets — is yours to configure and keep healthy. That split is AWS's shared responsibility model, and it is where most teams run short of hours.

    XcellHost takes on your side of that line. We onboard your accounts, put guardrails and monitoring in place, answer alarms 24×7 from our Mumbai NOC & SOC, patch on schedule, test restores, review security findings and tune spend every month. You keep ownership of your accounts and data; we keep them running.
