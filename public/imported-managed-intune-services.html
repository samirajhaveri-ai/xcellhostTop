    Overview

    You probably already own Intune. We make it do its job.

    Microsoft Intune is Microsoft's cloud service for managing devices and apps on Windows, macOS, iOS/iPadOS, Android and Linux. It enrols devices — through Windows Autopilot, Apple Automated Device Enrollment, Android Enterprise or the Company Portal — pushes settings and apps, checks each device against your compliance rules, and passes that result to Microsoft Entra Conditional Access, which decides whether the device may open email and files. Intune Plan 1 comes inside Microsoft 365 Business Premium, E3, E5, F1, F3 and EMS E3/E5.

    Owning the licence and running the service are different jobs. XcellHost's Managed Intune Services designs your policies, enrols the fleet, then operates it every day: compliance drift, failed app installs, update rings, BitLocker recovery, lost phones, and every joiner, mover and leaver — with an L1/L2 helpdesk, a 24×7 NOC and a monthly report. You pay per device per month, in rupees, on a GST invoice.

    Device controls and evidence that help you meet DPDP, CERT-In, RBI and SEBI expectations.
