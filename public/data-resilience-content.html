Data resilience means protecting every workload — SaaS apps, endpoints, data-centre servers and cloud-native services — with immutable backups and tested recovery. Native SaaS retention is short and coarse, and it sits inside the same account an attacker takes over. XcellHost delivers Druva's managed platform in India with air-gapped copies and 24×7 support.

Frequently asked questions

What does data resilience cover, and why not just use native SaaS retention?
Data resilience means protecting every workload — SaaS apps, endpoints, data-centre servers and cloud-native services — with immutable backups and tested recovery. Native SaaS retention is short and coarse, and it sits inside the same account an attacker takes over. XcellHost delivers Druva's managed platform in India with air-gapped copies and 24×7 support.
How are hybrid and data-centre workloads protected?
Agentless, direct-to-cloud backup for VMware, Hyper-V and Nutanix AHV, plus SQL Server and Oracle databases — with no backup hardware on site.
How are laptops and remote staff covered?
Automated backup for Windows, macOS and Linux endpoints with self-service restore, remote wipe for lost devices and OS-migration support.
Where is my data stored?
In the Druva region you license. XcellHost confirms the region in writing and helps align retention with your DPDP Act obligations.
Which SaaS applications are protected?
Microsoft 365 (Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams), Google Workspace, Salesforce and Microsoft Entra ID, with air-gapped, unified protection.
Which cloud-native services are supported?
Amazon EC2, EBS, RDS and S3, plus Azure VMs and SQL, with cross-account, cross-region and cross-cloud recovery options.
Is Microsoft 365 native retention not enough?
It is short, coarse and lives in the same tenant an attacker would compromise. Independent, immutable backup gives longer retention and item-level recovery.
How is it priced?
Per user for SaaS and endpoints, per workload for servers and cloud, plus the XcellHost managed service — one INR invoice with GST.
