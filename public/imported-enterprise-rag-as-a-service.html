Overview · Enterprise RAG

Ground every AI answer in your enterprise knowledge — without widening who can see it.

A language model on its own answers from what it absorbed in training — it has never read your credit policy, your SOPs or last week's board circular. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) fixes that at answer time: the system finds the most relevant passages in your approved content and gives them to the model with the question, so the reply is built from your material and cites it. In an enterprise, that retrieval also has to honour who is asking.

XcellAI Enterprise RAG as a Service runs on Sarv's Knowledge & RAG platform — knowledge bases, hybrid retrieval, retrieval testing and agent connectors. XcellHost adds the enterprise layer around it: identity and permissions, connectors, governance, evaluation, private hosting in India and a 99.95% SLA. Per Sarv, the platform can pair retrieval with fine-tuning for domain assistants, as in its Kar Saathi deployment.

Plan your retrieval workflow

Six decisions that decide whether answers can be trusted.

Sarv's knowledge-base controls cover ingestion, chunk curation, retrieval testing and agent connectors. We walk through each with your owners, security and compliance teams before anything goes live.

01

Source preparation

Choose the websites, documents or Markdown that are approved to answer from. Check extracted text, headings and tables before they become chunks.

We checkScanned PDFs, broken tables, duplicate versions, content that must never be indexed.
02

Knowledge retrieval

Try hybrid, text-only and vector search side by side. Compare which chunks come back, their rank, relevance and whether they are active.

We checkExact codes and names (text wins) versus paraphrased questions (vector wins).
03

Knowledge curation

Edit weak chunks, write FAQs for real questions, switch on only the source subset you trust, and keep an owner and version review for each.

We checkEvery source has a named owner and a review date.
04

Index & workload review

Confirm embedding coverage and which embedding model was used — separately from "job finished". Test response time and cost at production-like volumes.

We checkZero un-embedded chunks; latency and cost at your expected query rate.
05

Answer evaluation

Write down the evidence each common, ambiguous and unanswerable question should use. Judge retrieval first, then the wording of the answer.

We checkA signed-off golden set with expected sources.
06

Deployment requirements

Decide which users and agents may read which source, where data is hosted, and how edits and deletions in a source reach the index.

We checkA deleted or restricted document stops being cited within the agreed window.

    RAG, fine-tuning, or both?

    One changes what the model can look up. The other changes how it behaves.

    Tap what matters for your project — the recommendation moves.

    RAG

    Retrieval at answer time

    Supplies current information from approved sources with each question. Update or restrict a document and the next answer changes — no retraining.

    • Citations and audit trail
    • Access enforced per user
    • Deletions honoured on refresh
    Both

    A domain assistant

    Fine-tune for the vocabulary and style of a field, retrieve for the facts that change. Per Sarv, its Kar Saathi deployment combines the two.

    • Domain fluency
    • Current, cited facts
    • More effort to evaluate
    Fine-tuning

    Change model behaviour

    Trains a model on examples so it follows a format, tone or task. It does not keep facts current, cannot enforce per-user access, and cannot show where an answer came from.

    • Consistent format and tone
    • Shorter prompts
    • Retrain when behaviour must change

    Identity & access

    Answers that respect the permissions you already have.

    If a person cannot open a document in the source system, the assistant should not quote it to them. We design for that from day one.

    Single sign-on*

    SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect against your identity provider, including Microsoft Entra ID — run with our Managed Identity Service. MFA and conditional access stay where they are.

    Role-based access*

    Admins, knowledge owners, reviewers and end users get separate rights. Knowledge bases are scoped to groups, and every admin change is logged.

    Document-level permission inheritance*

    Access labels come from the source system's ACLs at sync time and are applied as a filter during retrieval, so restricted chunks never reach the prompt.

      Governance & security

      Controls your CISO and DPO will ask about first.

      Each control is agreed in writing, tested in the pilot and evidenced in the monthly report.

      Audit logs*

      Who asked what, which sources were retrieved, which model answered and what was shown — exportable to your SIEM.

      PII redaction & DLP*

      Personal identifiers masked in prompts and logs before the model sees them. Pair with Scrutiny DLP across endpoints and email.

      Retention

      Separate retention for sources, chunks, prompts and logs — for example, 180 days for logs in India.

      Guardrails*

      Topic policies, prompt-injection checks, refusal for out-of-scope questions and a hand-off to a person.

      Model choice

      Hosted models or private open models on GPU in India — chosen per use case. See OpenAI and Gemini managed services.

      No training on your data*

      Your content and prompts are not used to train models; we confirm the terms of every model provider in the contract.

      Encryption with customer-managed keys*

      TLS in transit and encryption at rest, with keys you control through Cloud HSM (BYOK). Revoke the key and the data becomes unreadable.

      Compliance & India data residency

      Your documents become an AI's evidence. Govern them like evidence.

      Chunks, embeddings, prompts and logs can all carry personal data. The way we set up enterprise RAG helps you meet the rules below — compliance itself stays with your organisation.

      DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

      Index only data with a lawful purpose, honour erasure by removing and re-indexing, and keep access per source. As the Rules phase in, a breach is intimated to the Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours.

      6 hCERT-In directions (April 2022)

      Reportable incidents go to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them, and ICT logs are kept for a rolling 180 days within India. Audit and access logs are planned for that.

      BFSIRBI, SEBI & IRDAI

      Outsourced technology must be governed, auditable and exit-ready — RBI's IT outsourcing directions, SEBI's CSCRF and IRDAI's guidelines. You get source lists, access records, test results and an exit plan.

      42001ISO/IEC 42001

      The AI management system standard. Enterprise RAG evidence — risk register, evaluation, monitoring — feeds your programme; our ISO 42001 consulting helps you build it.
