Quick answer

What is the NVIDIA B300?

The NVIDIA B300 is the Blackwell Ultra GPU. Per NVIDIA, each one carries up to 288 GB of HBM3e at 8 TB/s, delivers 15 PFLOPS of dense NVFP4 compute (1.5× Blackwell) and runs attention layers 2× faster than Blackwell. GPUs talk over fifth-generation NVLink at 1.8 TB/s each. Eight B300s make an HGX B300 system with 2.1 TB of GPU memory, and 72 of them, with 36 Grace CPUs, make the liquid-cooled GB300 NVL72 rack.

Meet the NVIDIA B300

Blackwell Ultra, built for what comes after big models.

Some AI jobs need more than raw compute. They need room: room for model weights, for long prompts, and for the key-value cache that grows with every reasoning step. Earlier Blackwell GPUs start to run short of that room on the largest mixture-of-experts and dense transformer models. B300 adds it.

Per NVIDIA, Blackwell Ultra raises memory to 288 GB of HBM3e per GPU, 50% more than Blackwell and 3.6× an H100. Dense NVFP4 compute rises 1.5× to 15 PFLOPS, and new attention acceleration doubles the speed of the layers reasoning models spend most of their time in.
