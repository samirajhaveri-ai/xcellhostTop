What is the NVIDIA B300?

NVIDIA B300 is the Blackwell Ultra data-centre GPU. Each B300 carries 288 GB of HBM3e at up to 8 TB/s, fifth-generation Tensor Cores with an enhanced FP4 Transformer Engine, and NVLink at 1.8 TB/s per GPU. It delivers about 1.5× the FP4 compute of B200 and twice the attention-layer throughput, and ships in 8-GPU HGX B300 servers — 2.3 TB of GPU memory in one system.
