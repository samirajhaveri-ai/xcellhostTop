Overview

Security needs an owner at the top table. A vCISO gives you one from next month.

Customers send security questionnaires, auditors ask for evidence, regulators expect incident reports within hours and boards want to know how exposed the business really is. Answering all of that is the job of a Chief Information Security Officer — but most Indian mid-size businesses can't justify, or quickly find, a full-time one.

A virtual CISO owns the same scope on retainer: strategy, risk, policy, compliance, vendor risk, incident readiness and board reporting. XcellHost's vCISO is a named senior leader backed by our NOC, SOC, backup and cloud teams, so the roadmap doesn't stop at a slide deck — we can implement the controls too. Plans are month-to-month and billed in rupees.
