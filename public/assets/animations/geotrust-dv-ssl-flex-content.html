Overview

One low-cost DV certificate for every name and every subdomain you run.

GeoTrust DV SSL (FLEX) is a domain-validated TLS certificate from GeoTrust, a DigiCert brand, built to be flexible. You prove you control each domain — by email, DNS record or a file on the server — and the certificate is issued in seconds. Visitors get the padlock, an encrypted connection and the assurance that they are on the right domain.

FLEX means you can combine standard domains and wildcards in any mix — up to 250 entries on one certificate, managed and renewed together. The main domain can itself be a wildcard, you can start with a single name and add more later. XcellHost plans the cheapest mix with you, completes the DNS checks, installs the certificate and reissues it every time browser rules require it.

Main domain & additional domains

Every entry is standard or wildcard. You decide which, one by one.

The first entry becomes the certificate's common name; every other entry is a Subject Alternative Name (SAN). Either kind can be a single name or a wildcard.

    DV in India

    Encrypt every subdomain. Know where DV is enough.

    Indian frameworks don't name certificate brands, but all of them expect data in transit to be protected. DV gives every name full-strength encryption; regulated, customer-facing pages usually add a verified identity.

    DPDP Act, 2023

    Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards against personal-data breaches. HTTPS on every form, login and API — including forgotten subdomains — is a basic one.

    CERT-In directions

    Cyber incidents must be reported within six hours. Expired or missing certificates are a common, avoidable incident — managed reissues keep them off the list.

    RBI & SEBI entities

    Banks, NBFCs, payment firms and brokers must protect data in transit. DV suits internal and marketing hosts; customer-facing banking pages normally use OV or EV.

    MeitY & public services

    Government guidelines require HTTPS across public services. One FLEX certificate can cover a portal, its wildcard subdomains and partner domains.

    CERT-In reporting window
    6hours

    Reportable incidents must reach CERT-In within six hours of being noticed. An outage caused by a lapsed certificate is easier to prevent than to report — every reissue on this page is ours to run.

    Source: CERT-In directions, April 2022
