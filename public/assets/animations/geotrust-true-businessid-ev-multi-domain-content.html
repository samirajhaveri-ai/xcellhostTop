    Overview

    Many websites, one verified company. One certificate.

    GeoTrust True BusinessID with EV Multi-Domain is an Extended Validation SSL certificate that lists several domain names as Subject Alternative Names (SANs). GeoTrust vets your company once, and every domain on the certificate carries the same verified legal name, registration number and country. Five domains are included; you can add up to 245 more, to 250 in total.

    It suits groups with several brands, country sites (.in, .com, .ae), or separate login, payment and investor portals. You get one vetting, one renewal date and one GST invoice instead of a pile of single certificates. XcellHost plans the SAN list with you, prepares the MCA, GST and authorisation documents, installs on every server and handles reissues.

      GeoTrust EV in India

      Groups with many sites have many doors to protect.

      No Indian rule asks for EV by name. But regulated groups run several customer-facing domains, and these frameworks expect each one to be protected and tracked.

      RBI-regulated entities

      NBFCs and payment aggregators often run separate apply, login and pay domains. EV on all of them, from one vetting, makes your identity consistent and checkable.

      SEBI's CSCRF

      Brokers and AMCs must protect data in transit and keep an inventory of critical assets. One multi-domain certificate is one entry with one expiry date to watch.

      DPDP Act, 2023

      Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards on every channel that collects personal data, not just the main website.

      CERT-In & MeitY

      CERT-In regularly warns about phishing that imitates Indian brands. A verified name on each of your sites makes look-alikes easier to tell apart.
