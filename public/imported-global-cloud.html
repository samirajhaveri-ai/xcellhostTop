    Overview · Global Cloud

    A server in Mumbai is far from London. Put the workload where the users are.

    Every request from Singapore, Dubai or London to a Mumbai server crosses thousands of kilometres of sea cable — and your overseas customers feel it on every page load, API call and checkout. XcellCloud Global Cloud places cloud virtual machines in partner data centres* near those users, while your Indian workloads and data stay in Mumbai.

    You get one console and one API for every region, a private network that joins them, cross-region backup and failover, and a single team on call. XcellHost designs the footprint with you, migrates the workloads and runs them 24×7 from our Mumbai NOC & SOC — billed in rupees on one GST invoice.

      Security & India compliance

      SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

      Decide which data leaves India — and keep the rest in Mumbai.

      Global Cloud is designed so the residency boundary is a choice you make per workload, not an accident. It helps you meet Indian rules; it doesn't make you compliant on its own.

      Residency lanessample design
      6hours · CERT-In incident reporting
      180days · log retention in India
      1home region for regulated data · Mumbai
      DPDP Act, 2023

      Personal data may go abroad except to countries the Government restricts. We record where each dataset lives, apply reasonable security safeguards and help you notify breaches to the Data Protection Board and affected people.

      CERT-In directions (2022)

      Cyber incidents reported within 6 hours; ICT system logs kept for 180 days within Indian jurisdiction. Logs from every region are shipped to Mumbai and our SOC handles reporting support.

      RBI · SEBI · IRDAI

      Payment-system data, and data held by SEBI- and IRDAI-regulated entities, generally has to stay in India. Those workloads stay on BOM-01; only non-regulated tiers go abroad — confirm scope with your compliance team.

      MeitY & XcellHost controls

      XcellHost is MeitY empanelled and certified to ISO 9001, 27001, 27017, 27018 and 20000-1 with SOC 2 Type I & II. Partner data centres* carry their own certifications — shared with you per region.
