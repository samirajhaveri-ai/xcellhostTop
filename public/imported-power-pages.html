    QWhat is Microsoft Power Pages?

    Power Pages is Microsoft's low-code platform for secure, business-facing websites. Pages, forms and lists are built in a visual design studio and read and write Microsoft Dataverse — the same data layer used by Power Apps, Power Automate and Dynamics 365. Visitors can browse anonymously or sign in, and what each person sees is controlled by web roles and table permissions.

    Use it when a plain brochure site is not enough: dealers who place orders, vendors who upload GST and bank documents, customers who raise warranty claims, candidates who apply and track status. Microsoft licenses it by capacity per website per month — packs of 100 authenticated users or 500 anonymous users, or pay-as-you-go through Azure.

    XcellHost, a Microsoft Gold Partner in Mumbai, does the whole job: sizes and supplies the capacity on an INR invoice with GST, designs and builds the portal, connects it to your ERP, Tally, Zoho or SQL data, sets up sign-in, web roles and the firewall, and supports it after launch from our 24×7 team.

      Microsoft Entra External ID for customers, dealers and vendors; Entra ID for staff; other OpenID Connect, SAML or social providers where needed.

      Studio scan for issues such as cross-site scripting and insecure libraries, with fix guidance.
