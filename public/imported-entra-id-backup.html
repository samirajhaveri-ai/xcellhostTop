    The value

    "Microsoft has it covered" — only the platform.

    Knowing how Entra ID is backed up before an incident is what separates a short recovery from hours of detective work.

    The platform

    Microsoft runs the service

    Microsoft keeps Entra ID available and secures the infrastructure. Its shared responsibility model leaves accounts, access management and your data with you — and it plans for deletions and misconfigurations to happen.

    The clock

    30 days, then gone

    Deleted users, Microsoft 365 groups, app registrations and service principals sit in a recycle bin for 30 days. After that — or after a hard delete — they cannot be recovered, along with their memberships and permissions.

    The data

    Identity data is yours

    Who belongs to which group, which policy blocks what, which app holds which permission: that configuration state is your responsibility, not Microsoft's.

    The gap

    Native is not a backup

    Built-in tools keep a short history inside the same tenant. A real backup adds long retention, a copy outside the tenant and granular restore of objects the native tools skip.

    Sources: Microsoft Learn — Shared responsibility in the cloud, Soft deletion in Microsoft Entra, Recover from deletions. Checked October 2026.

    Why it matters in India

    Regulators ask how you recover access. Show them a tested restore.

    Indian rules increasingly expect backups, logs and recovery you can prove. An Entra ID backup held in India helps you meet them; it does not by itself make you compliant.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Report incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for 180 days in India. Backup job logs and change history give you an identity audit trail to hand over.

    DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

    Reasonable security safeguards for personal data, with backups for continued processing listed among them. User records restored from an India-held copy support that duty.

    RBI · SEBI CSCRF · IRDAI

    Regulated entities are expected to plan for recovery of critical systems, test backups and control privileged access. Restore drills and role-change history provide the evidence.

    XcellHost maps these controls to your own obligations during onboarding. Check sector rules with your compliance team.
