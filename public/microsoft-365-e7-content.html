    Overview

    Microsoft 365 E7 — which Microsoft also calls the Frontier Suite — puts four products under one licence: Microsoft 365 E5 (Office apps, Teams, Exchange, SharePoint, Defender, Purview, Intune and Entra ID P2), Microsoft 365 Copilot, the full Microsoft Entra Suite (Private Access, Internet Access, ID Governance and Verified ID) and Agent 365, Microsoft's control plane for registering, securing and watching the AI agents your teams build or buy. Per Microsoft's announcement, it became generally available on 1 May 2026 at a US list price of US$99 per user a month — about 15% below the four products bought one by one. It is the right fit when you need all four; if you only need E5 and Copilot, buying those two is cheaper, and our calculator shows that honestly. XcellHost licenses E7 through Microsoft's CSP programme, bills you in rupees with GST, and handles the rollout — identity, data readiness, the Copilot pilot and agent governance — with support from our 24×7 NOC/SOC in Mumbai.
