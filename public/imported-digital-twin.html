What it is

A model that changes when the real thing changes. That is the twin.

A digital twin is a virtual model of a physical asset, process or place that is kept in sync with it by live data from sensors and control systems. A 3D drawing shows what a pump looks like. A twin shows what the pump is doing right now, how it got there, and what it is likely to do next.

  • Synced, not static. Values arrive from PLCs, meters and IoT sensors every few seconds, so the model is never a stale snapshot.
  • Connected. The twin knows the motor drives the conveyor that feeds the packer, so one fault can be traced to the assets it affects.
  • Testable. You can change a set-point, a speed or a schedule on the twin and see the effect before anyone touches the plant.
How a twin differs
CAD or BIM modelGeometry and specs. Accurate on the day it was drawn.
SCADA screenLive values for operators, tag by tag, with little context or history.
Offline simulationAnswers what-if questions, but with assumed inputs, not today's data.
Digital twinGeometry, live values, relationships and history in one model, with simulation run on real data.

Industry 4.0 to 5.0

Twins are the data backbone for smart factories, and the people-centred Industry 5.0 work of cobots and operator assistance. See our Industry 5.0 programme and Manufacturing Cloud.

Smart Cities Mission

Launched on 25 June 2015 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs for 100 cities, the mission set up Integrated Command and Control Centres in every one of them. It formally closed on 31 March 2025; the command centres and their data remain, and are a natural base for city twins.

OT security · IEC 62443

We design twin connectivity to the IEC 62443 series: zones, conduits and least-privilege access between OT and IT. BIS has adopted parts of the series as identical Indian Standards, for example IS/IEC 62443-4-1:2018 for secure product development. See OT protection and OT assessment.

DPDP for people data

Hospital, building and city twins often touch personal data: badge swipes, CCTV, occupancy, wearables. That falls under the DPDP Act, 2023. We minimise it at the edge and map what remains with our DPDP compliance platform.

CERT-In reporting

CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require reportable cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours of noticing them. Our 24×7 SOC watches twin gateways and data paths and runs that clock with you.

Data stays in India

By default the twin platform runs in XcellHost's Tier IV data centre in Mumbai. You can also keep it at your plant edge or in partner data centres*, with rupee billing and GST invoices.
