Executive summary

Enter Mauritius through Mauritian partners, data on the island

The case on one screen: what the market is worth, why the timing works, how to enter and what we aim to reach in 18 months.

The opportunity
US$770M

Value added by Mauritius's ICT sector in 2025: Rs 36.3 billion, up 6.9%, from Statistics Mauritius1. That is about . It measures what the local sector produces, exports included, so treat it as a ceiling and not as customer spending.

  • Cloud and data centres: a 5 MW market in 2025, forecast to reach 11 MW by 2030, a 17.08% CAGR4. Ten facilities operate across five locations5.
  • Finance: financial services were 13.4% of GDP in 2025, with more than 20,000 global business entities7, 19 banks and 23 insurers8.
  • Cyber security: 5,754 incidents were reported on MAUCORS+ from 1 Jan to 5 Nov 2026, about 19 a day9. Mauritius ranks first in Africa on the ITU's 2024 index32.
  • AI: the National AI Strategy 2025–2029 was launched on 10 Apr 202617. Budget 2026–27 funds practical AI skills for 25,000 people18.
Why now
1 Jan 2027

Every data controller must have an in-house Data Protection Officer, 46 days from now1213. Critical-infrastructure owners must comply with new rules by 1 June 202711. See the trigger calendar.

  • DPO Regulations 2026: the officer must come from the controller's own staff and hold a recognised certification. Controllers notify the Data Protection Office within 14 days of the designation12.
  • Critical information infrastructure: regulations in operation since 1 Jun 2026 cover finance, public service, ICT and broadcasting, energy and water, and transport. Owners have 12 months to comply11.
  • GDPR alignment: the Data Protection Commissioner says Mauritius is pursuing an EU adequacy assessment in 202615. The government also plans to revise the 2021 cyber law and set up a National Cyber Resiliency Agency16.
  • Tax on digital supply: VAT has applied to foreign suppliers' digital services since 1 Jan 202622. Budget 2026–27 brings non-resident suppliers of software and remote maintenance into income tax18.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

Mauritian system integrators, MSPs and telecom operators first, with hosting in a partner data centre on the island. Open a Mauritian company between month 9 and 15.

  • Host customer data in Mauritius, in a partner's Tier III or Tier IV facility. The site is not contracted yet; it is a phase 1 task. The delivery model follows Global Cloud.
  • Lead with the India corridor: trade was US$887 million in FY2024–25, 11 Indian public-sector companies operate in Mauritius27, and UPI and RuPay have worked there since 12 Feb 202429.
  • Run tooling and the second line from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC. Access from Mumbai is a transfer abroad under the Data Protection Act, so it needs documented safeguards or consent14.
