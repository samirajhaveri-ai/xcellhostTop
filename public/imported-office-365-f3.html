Overview

Frontline licence, Office 365 track. Devices managed your way.

Per Microsoft, Office 365 F3 gives frontline workers Office for the web and the Office mobile apps, an Exchange Online Kiosk mailbox of 2 GB used through Outlook on the web, Microsoft Teams with frontline apps such as Shifts, Walkie Talkie, Tasks and Approvals, SharePoint, 2 GB of OneDrive and Viva Engage (formerly Yammer) core features. Unlike Microsoft 365 F1, F3 users can create and edit documents, not just read them.

What it leaves out is the device and identity layer that Microsoft 365 F3 (₹630) adds — Intune, Microsoft Entra ID P1, Defender for Endpoint and Windows Enterprise. If your shared phones and tablets are already managed by another MDM, Office 365 F3 saves ₹280 per user per month. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

Frontline phones carry customer data. Plan for DPDP and CERT-In.

Office 365 F3 keeps mail and files in Microsoft's cloud instead of on the device. These are the Indian rules we design around — no licence makes you compliant by itself.

DPDP Act 2023

Customer and employee personal data on shared phones: keep it in OneDrive and SharePoint, not the camera roll, and remove access the day someone leaves.

CERT-In 180-day logs

Per Microsoft, Audit (Standard) keeps audit records for 180 days for non-E5 users — matching CERT-In's 180-day log expectation.

BFSI field staff

For bank and NBFC field agents, pair F3 with Entra ID P1 Conditional Access and an MDM so customer data stays on managed devices.

Data in India

Microsoft offers India data residency for core Microsoft 365 data at rest for Indian tenants — confirm per service. India regions: Pune, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad.
