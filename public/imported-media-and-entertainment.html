          Regulatory mapping

          Requirement, how we help, and the evidence you keep.

          Each card names the instrument and date as published, checked October 2026. M&E Cloud helps you meet these requirements and protect your content; you stay responsible for your own compliance and for any legal action.

          MIB

          Unauthorised exhibition of film copies

          Cinematograph (Amendment) Act 2023 — new section 6AB bars using an infringing copy to exhibit a film to the public for profit; 3 months to 3 years' imprisonment and a fine of ₹3 lakh up to 5% of audited gross production cost
          How we helpMasters, dailies and screeners on access-controlled storage in India, every download logged, forensic-watermark workflows so a leak can be traced to its source.
          EvidenceAccess and download logs · watermark session map
          MIB

          Takedown through nodal officers

          Section 7(1B)(ii) of the 2023 Act; MIB nodal officers (PIB, 3 Nov 2023) can direct intermediaries to remove pirated content within 48 hours, on application by the copyright owner or an authorised person
          How we helpLeak evidence packs — URLs, timestamps, watermark matches — ready for your legal team to file with the nodal officer. We support, you apply.
          EvidenceEvidence pack template · incident timeline
          Copyright

          Rights in films, music and recordings

          Copyright Act 1957 — the owner's exclusive rights, enforced by the rights holder
          How we helpRights metadata kept with each asset in your MAM, licence windows enforced by DRM policies, takedown evidence on request.
          EvidenceMAM rights fields · DRM policy export
          MeitY · MIB

          OTT code of ethics and classification

          IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021 — notified 25 Feb 2021; publishers of online curated content self-classify as U, U/A 7+, U/A 13+, U/A 16+ or A
          How we helpHosting for your catalogue and rating metadata services; staging environments to check rating displays before release.
          EvidenceRelease records · catalogue change log
          MeitY · MIB

          Parental locks and age verification

          Same Rules — parental locks for U/A 13+ and above; reliable age verification for A-rated content
          How we helpProfile, PIN and entitlement services hosted with uptime monitoring; logs of lock changes kept for audit.
          EvidenceService uptime reports · lock-change logs
          MeitY · MIB

          Three-tier grievance redressal

          Same Rules — publisher, self-regulatory body, then MIB oversight mechanism; applies to OTT and digital news publishers
          How we helpHosting for your grievance desk with timestamped tickets and retention, so response times can be shown at each level.
          EvidenceTicket logs · retention policy
          DPDP

          Subscriber data safeguards

          DPDP Act 2023; DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025 with 18-month phased compliance; breach intimation without delay
          How we helpEncryption, access control and logging for subscriber, viewing and payment-reference data; breach runbook; DPO support through vDPO.
          EvidenceSafeguards register · breach runbook
          DPDP

          Kids' profiles

          DPDP Rules 2025 — verifiable parental consent before processing a child's personal data
          How we helpConsent records for kids' profiles stored with audit trails; profile services kept separate from ad and analytics pipelines.
          EvidenceConsent logs · data-flow map
          CERT-In

          6-hour reporting, 180-day logs

          Directions under section 70B — 28 Apr 2022
          How we helpSOC triage inside the 6-hour window; logs from origin, CDN, workstations and storage kept in India for 180 days; CDN log streaming at ₹999* a month.
          EvidenceIncident drafts · retention evidence
          PCI · RBI

          Subscription payments

          PCI DSS v4.0.1 (future-dated requirements from 31 Mar 2025); RBI card-on-file tokenisation in effect from 1 Oct 2022
          How we helpSubscriptions handled through your payment aggregator's tokens, billing kept out of card scope; readiness via PCI DSS consulting where needed.
          EvidenceData-flow map · scope note
          MCA

          Daily backup of books in India

          Companies (Accounts) Rules, rule 3(5) — since 5 Aug 2022
          How we helpDaily backups of finance and royalty systems on servers in India; see daily backup of books.
          EvidenceBackup job logs · server-location sheet
