What BFSI Cloud is

A cloud built for the questions your regulator will ask.

Banks, NBFCs, insurers and market intermediaries can use cloud, but they stay accountable for it. The RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing of IT Services expects contracts with audit and inspection rights, named sub-contractors, tested business continuity, data storage in India where rules require it, and a clear exit. SEBI's cloud framework and IRDAI's cyber security guidelines expect much the same of a cloud provider.

XcellCloud BFSI Cloud is our answer to that list. Workloads run on shared or dedicated capacity in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, DR runs at a partner data centre in India*, and every environment comes with segmented networks, Cloud HSM, a 24×7 SOC, WAF and DDoS protection, privileged access control, immutable backups — and the paperwork your board, auditors and regulator will read.

Applicability differs. RBI's IT outsourcing directions cover material IT outsourcing by commercial banks (including SFBs and payments banks), Tier 3 and 4 UCBs, NBFCs in the middle, upper and top layers, credit information companies and AIFIs; RRBs are excluded in the covering letter. RBI has also said it is setting up a cloud facility for the financial sector, initially run by IFTAS (8 Dec 2023). Use the readiness checker for your entity type.
