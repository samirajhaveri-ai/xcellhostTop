    Overview

    One panel to run a server, resell hosting and install apps — with the app installer built in.

    Webuzo is a multi-user Linux hosting control panel from Softaculous. The server owner works in the Admin panel, resellers carve out their own packages, and each customer gets an End-user panel for domains, email, databases, DNS, files and one-click apps. It runs on a fresh AlmaLinux, Ubuntu, Rocky Linux or CloudLinux server — your own, or one from XcellHost.

    XcellHost sells the licence in rupees with a GST invoice, binds it to your server's IP, installs and hardens the panel, and moves your existing sites across. After go-live you call one team, 24×7, in English or Hindi.
