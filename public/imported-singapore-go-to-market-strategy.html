The opportunity

Singapore cloud computing market in 2024, the base year of two published reports34. That is about .

  • E-learning: US$4.4B in 2025, growing 12.1% a year to 203414.
  • Singapore drew US$1.31B of private AI funding, the most in Southeast Asia1.
Why now
6 changes

Six regulatory moves between October 2025 and October 2026, including two MAS consultations on TRM and third-party risk4547. Each one starts a compliance project for buyers.

  • Digital Infrastructure Bill passed on 7 Oct 2026. It will license data centres of 3 MW and above, data centres of 10 MW and above, and cloud providers with S$100M+ in Singapore revenue. Fines reach S$1M or 10% of Singapore turnover2829.
  • MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines published on 7 Oct 2026, effective 7 Oct 202748.
  • Cybersecurity (Amendment) Act: key provisions in force since 31 Oct 2025, covering third-party and overseas critical systems23.
  • Health Information Bill passed on 12 Jan 2026. MOH intends it to take effect in early 202736.
Recommended entry model
Partner-led

Start with a Singapore DC partner and local SI/MSP resellers. Set up a Singapore Pte Ltd between months 6 and 12 to hold licences and sign contracts with regulated customers.

  • Host customer data in a Singapore partner data centre. The partner holds any Digital Infrastructure Act licence28.
  • Managed SOC monitoring and penetration testing are licensable services under the Cybersecurity Act. Deliver them through a licensed partner until we hold our own licence25.
  • Run tooling, playbooks and second-line support from the Mumbai 24×7 NOC & SOC. Any personal data processed in India must meet the PDPA transfer-limitation obligation34.

    Pricing & tax approach

    • SGD price book for Singapore customers and a USD book for partners. Review FX quarterly.
    • GST: 9% on supplies made in Singapore. Under the overseas vendor registration regime, an overseas supplier registers if B2C sales to Singapore exceed S$100,000 a year and global turnover is at least S$1M. Some business customers self-account through reverse charge50.
    • Withholding tax: royalties 10%, and 10% under the India treaty. Technical and management fees for services rendered in Singapore are taxed at the prevailing corporate rate51.
    • Rates are as summarised by PwC, reviewed 2 Jul 2026. Get local tax advice for each contract structure.

    Risks & mitigations

    HighCompetition

    Hyperscalers, global colocation operators and local telcos are all established in Singapore.

    Mitigation: lead with managed security, digital trust and AI skills at India cost, not with raw compute.
    HighLicensing

    Managed SOC monitoring and pen testing need a CSA licence. The DI Act adds licensing for DCs and large cloud providers.

    Mitigation: use licensed partners first, then hold our own licences through the Pte Ltd.
    MediumRegulated buyers

    MAS outsourcing and TPRM rules mean long due diligence and audit rights.

    Mitigation: a ready due-diligence pack, ISO and SOC 2 evidence, and an exit plan.
    MediumChanging rules

    DI Act subsidiary legislation, the TRM Notice amendments and the TPRM Guidelines are still pending.

    Mitigation: a quarterly legal review with local counsel, and updates to this page.
    MediumDC capacity & power

    New capacity is allocated through calls, and sustainability rules apply.

    Mitigation: resell partner capacity, and run GPU and DR in Mumbai where the data rules allow.
    MediumCross-border data

    Under the PDPA, data sent to Mumbai needs comparable protection.

    Mitigation: Singapore-first hosting, transfer clauses, and Mumbai access limited to tooling and support.
    LowerTalent cost

    Local hiring in Singapore costs more than in India.

    Mitigation: a small local team, with delivery from Mumbai.
    LowerFX & collections

    Exposure to movements in SGD, INR and USD.

    Mitigation: SGD/USD price books, prepaid or quarterly billing, and partner credit limits.
