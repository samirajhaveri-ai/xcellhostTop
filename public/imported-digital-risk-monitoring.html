    Overview

    Attackers don't need your network to use your name.

    A domain one letter away from yours. A "customer care" page on social media with your logo and a fake helpline. A copy of your app on an unofficial store. A staff email and password sitting in a public dump. None of this touches your servers — so firewalls, EDR and SIEM never see it.

    XcellHost Digital Risk Monitoring watches the open web, social platforms, app stores, code and paste sites and selected restricted forums for your brands, domains, executives and staff accounts. Analysts verify each hit, rate it, and either hand you a clear action or run the takedown on your behalf — under a letter of authority you sign.

    Why it matters in India

    Impersonation is how most fraud reaches your customers.

    UPI, app-first banking and WhatsApp support made Indian customers fast to transact — and fast to trust a page that looks like yours.

    01

    Fake helplines

    Search results and social posts carrying a "customer care" number that is not yours. Callers are walked through payments or remote-access apps.

    02

    Lookalike sites

    KYC-update, refund and job-offer pages on domains close to yours, often live for only days. Speed of detection decides the damage.

    03

    Rogue apps

    Repackaged or fake apps using your name, shared as APK links on messaging groups or listed on unofficial stores.

    04

    Reused staff passwords

    A personal-site breach exposes a work email and the same password. Without MFA, that's a way in.

    The cost isn't only the fraud. Customers blame the brand they thought they were dealing with. Regulators expect you to have looked. And under CERT-In's 2022 directions, phishing, spoofing and fake mobile apps are reportable incident types with a 6-hour clock.

    Methodology

    Discover, monitor, verify, act, report — every month.

    Monitoring without verification floods you with noise. Verification without action leaves the fake page live. Each phase has an owner.

    1. P1Discover

      Build the asset register: brands, logos, domains, apps, official handles, executives and staff email domains.

    2. P2Monitor

      Continuous collection across domain registrations, web, social, app stores, code and paste sites and selected forums.

    3. P3Verify & rate

      An analyst removes false positives, captures evidence and rates each hit by likely customer or business impact.

    4. P4Act

      Takedown requests to hosts, registrars, platforms and stores; credential resets via your IT; SOC alert if needed.

    5. P5Report

      Monthly exposure score, takedown log, open items and what changed — for IT, risk and the board.

    Letter of authority

    Takedowns are filed only for brands and assets you own, under a signed authorisation.

    Masked by default

    Leaked credentials are shown partially masked and shared only with named contacts. We never test them.

    Observe, don't engage

    Restricted-forum monitoring is passive. No purchases, no contact with sellers, no access to stolen data.

    Evidence trail

    Screenshots, timestamps and request references kept for each case — useful for counsel and regulators.

    CERT-In directionsApril 2022

    Phishing, spoofing, identity theft, data leaks and fake mobile apps are among the reportable incident types, with a 6-hour reporting window and 180-day log retention in India.

    Early detection, evidence capture and a time-stamped case log to support a 6-hour report.
    DPDP Act 2023& DPDP Rules

    Reasonable security safeguards for personal data and intimation of breaches to the Data Protection Board and affected individuals.

    Leaked-credential and data-leak alerts that can start your breach assessment sooner.
    RBIbanks, NBFCs, payment firms

    RBI's cyber-security controls for banks include anti-phishing and anti-rogue-app services to identify and take down fake sites and apps.

    Managed brand, domain and app monitoring with a takedown log for supervisory review.
    SEBI CSCRFregulated entities

    Threat-intelligence and continuous-monitoring expectations across cyber resilience functions.

    External-threat intelligence on your brand feeding your SOC and periodic reviews.
    IRDAIinformation & cyber security guidelines

    Insurers are expected to monitor threats and manage incidents, including those reaching policyholders.

    Policyholder-facing impersonation monitored and reported monthly.
    MeitY & national reportingcybercrime.gov.in · 1930

    Victims of online fraud are directed to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and the 1930 helpline.

    Ready guidance for your support team when customers report a fake page or call.

    Regulatory summaries are indicative and simplified. For .in lookalikes, dispute routes such as INDRP (and UDRP for generic top-level domains) are pursued with your counsel.
