Twelve Topics, Each With A Brief And The Questions We Will Ask.

None of these is recorded yet. Filter by track, pick an episode to read its brief, then open it in the episode page template below.

MeitY notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules on 13 November 2025. Consent-manager registration applies from 13 November 2026; notice and consent, fiduciary duties, children's data and data-principal rights from 13 May 2027. What should a mid-size Indian company have finished by each date?

  • Which obligations start on which date?
  • What does a consent notice people can actually read look like?
  • Who inside the company owns breach intimation?
  • How do processor and cloud contracts need to change?
DPDP Compliance PlatformVirtual DPOMicrosoft Purview DLP*

Most small firms find out how good their backups are on the worst day of the year. A practical hour on prevention, copies an attacker cannot delete, restore drills and the first calls to make.

  • What should be offline or immutable, and what can stay online?
  • How often should a full restore actually be tested?
  • What happens in the first hour of an attack?
  • What do cyber insurers ask for before they quote?
Ransomware ProtectionCloud DR for SMBsCyber InsuranceDFIR

"Sovereign" gets used loosely. We separate where data sits, who can reach it, which laws apply and when an India-hosted private or government cloud is the right answer.

  • Data residency or sovereignty — what is the difference?
  • Which sectors are told to keep data in India?
  • Can global and India-hosted clouds work together?
  • What should a contract say about access and exit?
Government CloudPrivate CloudHybrid CloudData Centres

NIST published its first post-quantum standards — FIPS 203, 204 and 205 — in August 2024. For most companies the work starts with knowing where cryptography is used today.

  • What is "harvest now, decrypt later" and who should worry?
  • What goes into a cryptographic inventory?
  • Which systems should move first?
  • Where do certificates and HSMs fit?
PQC ReadinessCryptographic PostureCloud HSM

Agents that read mail, raise tickets and call APIs need identities, permissions and audit trails, like any employee. Where they help, where they fail and how to govern them.

  • Which tasks are agents ready for today?
  • How narrow should an agent's permissions be?
  • How do you log and review what an agent did?
  • What changes when agents touch personal data?
AI WorkersAI Security AssessmentAI Identity SecurityMicrosoft Agent 365*

Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker and ONDC changed how services are built in India. What the next wave of digital public infrastructure means for businesses that plug into it.

  • Which public rails matter most for a private business?
  • What does building on them demand for security and uptime?
  • Where do states, cities and industrial corridors fit?
Viksit Bharat & DPIDPI RoadmapGovernment Cloud

CA firms run client books across offices and filing seasons. Hosting, access, backup and the Companies (Accounts) Rules requirement — since August 2022 — for a daily backup of electronic books on servers in India.

  • What changes when Tally* moves to a hosted desktop?
  • How should multi-user access and client separation work?
  • What does the daily India backup rule ask for?
  • How do you plan for filing-season peaks?
Cloud for CAsDaily Backup of BooksSMB Cloud DesktopAI Training for CAs

CERT-In's Directions of 28 April 2022 require specified incidents to be reported within six hours of noticing them, and logs to be kept for 180 days in India. We walk the clock from first alert to filed report.

  • Which incidents are reportable?
  • Who decides, and who files?
  • Which logs must exist, and for how long?
  • How do SOC and forensics teams share the six hours?
Security OperationsDFIRNext-Gen SIEMCERT-In AI Auditing

Inference, video and IoT work better near the user. When edge beats a central region, how to run many small sites, and what that costs to operate.

  • Which workloads actually gain from edge?
  • How do you secure and patch many small sites?
  • How do edge and central cloud share data?
Edge CloudEdge NetworkCDN CloudIoT Cloud

Tenants grow faster than their rules. Identity, labels, DLP, retention, backup — and a named owner for each layer after go-live.

  • Where do most tenants drift first?
  • Which policies cut the most risk for the least effort?
  • What does backup of Microsoft 365 data involve?
  • Who owns governance once the project ends?
M365 GovernanceM365 Security AssessmentEntra ID BackupManaged Microsoft 365

Most breaches start with a login. Phishing-resistant MFA, removing standing admin rights and checking every request — in the order that works for a real IT team.

  • Which MFA methods resist phishing?
  • How do you remove shared and standing admin accounts?
  • What does "zero trust" mean on a Monday morning?
Identity & AccessMFAPrivileged Access

Training and inference have different economics. How to size GPU capacity, keep data in India and avoid paying for idle cards.

  • When does renting beat reserving or buying?
  • How do you size for inference rather than training?
  • What does keeping model data in India involve?
GPU ServersAI FactoryServerless InferenceDedicated AI Servers
