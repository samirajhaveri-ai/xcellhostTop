Per CA/Browser Forum · Ballot SC-081v3Public TLS certificates get shorter lives

Maximum validity of publicly trusted TLS certificates steps down from 398 days, in three stages, to 47 days by 2029.

  1. 200 days15 Mar 2026
  2. 100 days15 Mar 2027
  3. 47 days15 Mar 2029

The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025. Consent-manager rules follow after 12 months; notice, consent, security safeguards, breach intimation and data-principal rights after 18 months.
