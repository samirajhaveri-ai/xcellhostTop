Overview

Card data is the riskiest data you hold — so hold as little of it as you can.

PCI DSS v4.0.1 is the security standard the card brands require from every business that stores, processes or transmits cardholder data — or can affect its security. It is maintained by the PCI Security Standards Council and enforced through your acquiring bank. Its 12 requirements in 6 goals cover networks, stored data, vulnerability management, access, monitoring and policy. Depending on your merchant or service-provider level, you prove compliance each year with a Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) or a Report on Compliance written by a Qualified Security Assessor, plus quarterly external scans by an Approved Scanning Vendor.

XcellHost does the work in between: scoping, gap assessment, remediation, SAQ completion and evidence, and we coordinate the QSA and ASV partners who sign off — we are not a QSA or ASV ourselves. The biggest saving usually comes first: tokenisation, P2PE and segmentation can take most of your systems out of scope. Under RBI's card-on-file rules most Indian merchants should not be storing card numbers at all, and we can run PCI-ready infrastructure for what's left in our Tier IV data centre.

Twelve requirements. Here's what each one asks of you.

Every SAQ and every Report on Compliance is a slice of these twelve. Click a requirement to see what it covers, what changed in v4 and the evidence an assessor asks for.

What changed in v4

The future-dated requirements stopped being optional on 31 March 2025.

Version 4.0 added 64 new requirements. Thirteen applied at once; the other 51 were best practice until 31 March 2025 and are now assessed like everything else. v4.0.1 clarified wording without adding new ones.
