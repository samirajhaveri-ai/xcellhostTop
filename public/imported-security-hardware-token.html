    Overview

    Passwords and SMS codes can be handed over. A hardware key cannot.

    Most account takeovers still start with someone typing a password, or reading out a one-time code, on a page that only looks real. A FIDO2 security key removes that step: it holds a private key that never leaves the device, it only answers the exact website it was registered with, and it needs a physical touch — or a fingerprint — to do it.

    Not every system speaks FIDO2 yet. Older VPNs and business apps take OTP hardware tokens, and Indian statutory filings need a Class 3 DSC on a certified PKI USB token. XcellHost supplies all three, enrols them into Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft 365, your VPN and signing tools, and keeps spares, PIN resets and replacements running.

    Quick answer · updated October 2026

    What is a security hardware token?

    A small physical device — USB-A, USB-C, NFC or a key-fob with a screen — that proves who you are when you sign in or sign a file. FIDO2 keys sign a one-time challenge for the specific website, so phishing pages get nothing usable. OTP tokens show a time-based code for systems that only accept codes. PKI tokens store a certificate such as a DSC and do the signing on the device. Microsoft makes passkeys (FIDO2) available in every Entra ID edition, including Free.

    Why it matters in India

    Stolen sign-ins are the easy way in. Regulators now expect stronger ones.

    Breaches that start with a phished password or an approved fake push look the same in every sector. Hardware keys close that route and leave a clean audit trail.

    CERT-In directions (April 2022)

    Report specified incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for 180 days. Key-based sign-ins give a clear who-and-when record for every admin action.

    DPDP Act 2023

    Reasonable security safeguards for personal data. Phishing-resistant access for staff who reach customer data helps you meet that duty.

    RBI, SEBI & IRDAI frameworks

    Banking, securities and insurance cyber-security frameworks expect multi-factor authentication, especially for privileged and remote access. Keys help you meet those expectations.

    NIST SP 800-63B-4 (2025)

    The US digital-identity guideline used as a global reference: AAL2 must offer a phishing-resistant option, and AAL3 requires one with a non-exportable key — what a hardware FIDO2 key provides.

    CCA · DSC crypto tokens move to FIPS 140-3

    DSCs already on FIPS 140-2 tokens stay valid until they expire, but renewals and fresh downloads need a FIPS 140-3 token. GSTN also asks users of new tokens to install emSigner v3.3. Source: CCA advisory on migration from FIPS 140-2 to FIPS 140-3; GSTN advisory, 19 Sept 2026.

    How it works

    The website asks a question. Only the right key, on the right site, can answer.

    01 · Register

    One key pair per site

    At enrolment the key creates a new key pair for that service. The service keeps only the public half.

    • Private key never exported
    • Tied to the site's address
    • Enrolled after a verified sign-in
    02 · Sign in

    Touch, PIN or fingerprint

    The site sends a random challenge. The key signs it only after a touch plus PIN or fingerprint.

    • No code to type or read out
    • Look-alike sites get no answer
    • Nothing reusable is sent
