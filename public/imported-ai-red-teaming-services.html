    Overview

    A model is not the product. The whole stack around it is what gets attacked.

    A GenAI feature is a chain: a system prompt, a model, a retrieval index full of your documents, tools the model can call, and the people and apps that read its answers. A weakness can sit at any link — and most of them do not show up in a normal web application test.

    XcellHost AI Red Teaming is an authorised, time-boxed engagement in which our security engineers try to make your AI system do what it must not: leak data, ignore its instructions, act outside its permissions or produce output your downstream systems trust blindly. Everything happens inside a signed scope and rules of engagement, on an environment you choose, and ends with a fix plan and a re-test.

    Methodology

    Scoped, numbered, signed off — red teaming with a paper trail.

    Every engagement follows the same seven steps. Nothing is tested before step 01 is signed, and nothing leaves the engagement without step 07.

    1. 01
      Scope & rules of engagement

      Systems, environments, test window, data handling, stop conditions and named contacts — signed by you and XcellHost before any testing.

      output · signed ROE
    2. 02
      Threat model

      We map how data, instructions and actions flow through the system and where trust boundaries sit — mapped to OWASP and MITRE ATLAS.

      output · attack-surface map
    3. 03
      Test design

      Test cases per in-scope category, written for your use case, languages and user roles, with agreed pass criteria.

      output · test plan
    4. 04
      Adversarial testing

      Manual, expert-led testing supported by automated evaluation runs, inside the agreed window and rate limits. Stop conditions are honoured immediately.

      output · evidence log
    5. 05
      Triage & severity

      Each finding is reproduced, rated on likelihood and business impact, and checked for false positives before it is reported.

      output · findings register
    6. 06
      Report & remediation

      Executive summary, technical findings, risk register and a prioritised fix plan — walked through with your team.

      output · report & plan
    7. 07
      Re-test & close

      We re-run the affected tests after your fixes and issue a closure note per finding.

      output · re-test letter
