    Overview · what it does

    The same gateway engine, built and run for a whole group.

    Email Security Enterprise is a secure email gateway for organisations that run many mail domains, several subsidiaries or brands, and separate IT teams. It sits in front of your mail platform — as the MX gateway, through an API integration or as an SMTP relay — and inspects inbound, outbound and internal mail for phishing, impersonation, malware and sensitive data. Each message is then delivered, quarantined, encrypted or archived according to policy.

    Under the hood it is the Vortex SEG platform by Cyberstanc. The enterprise edition adds what large estates need: one console across every domain and tenant, active-active clusters, role-based and delegated administration, long-term archival with eDiscovery, SIEM/SOAR integration and a change-managed rollout. XcellHost licenses it, deploys it and operates it from our 24×7 SOC in Mumbai, billed in INR with GST.

    Large tenants usually start with the API for internal mail and post-delivery clean-up, then move MX to the cluster domain by domain. Journaling feeds the archive. Microsoft's built-in protection stays on as a second layer.

      Why it matters in India

      Email is where regulators look first — evidence is what they ask for.

      DPDP Act, 2023Requires reasonable security safeguards for personal data. Outbound DLP, OCR and encrypted delivery help you show that Aadhaar, PAN and customer data are not mailed out casually.
      CERT-In directions, 2022Cyber incidents reported within 6 hours; ICT logs kept for 180 days within India. Gateway logs and SOC case notes are kept in India and ready to hand over.
      RBI-regulated entitiesBanks, NBFCs and payment firms face cyber-security and IT-governance expectations from RBI. Phishing controls, archival and audit trails help you meet them.
      SEBI-regulated entitiesSEBI's cyber-security and cyber-resilience framework expects logging, monitoring and incident response. SIEM integration and immutable logs feed that programme.
