      How a knowledge assistant works

      Retrieval first, then the model answers.

      Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lets a GPT model answer from your documents instead of guessing. Watch a single question travel through the pipeline we build and run.

      “What is the notice period for a branch manager?”
      1. IngestSharePoint, PDFs, Drive, wikis, tickets — synced on a schedule.
      2. Chunk & embedSplit into passages, turned into vectors with an embeddings model.
      3. RetrieveHybrid keyword + vector search finds the top passages, filtered by who is asking.
      4. GenerateThe model answers only from those passages, with a system prompt we version.
      5. Cite & logEach answer links its sources; the trace is logged for evaluation.

      • Azure OpenAI resources can sit in India regions such as South India; model and deployment-type availability varies by region — we confirm per model.
