    Overview

    Your SAP Business One stays the same. The server room goes away.

    SAP Business One runs finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, production and service for thousands of small and midsize companies. On the SAP HANA version it adds in-memory analytics, fast search and the browser-based Web Client — but HANA also needs SAP-certified hardware, Linux skills and disciplined backups that most SME IT teams don't have in-house.

    SAP B1 HANA on Cloud is XcellHost's managed hosting for SAP Business One on HANA, in our Mumbai cloud. We run the HANA database, the SAP Business One server components, Remote Desktop access for the desktop client, the Web Client, backups, monitoring and security. Your SAP Business One partner keeps handling functional support and licences; your branches, CA and sales team simply log in.

If you need to stay on SQL Server for an add-on, ask us about SQL hosting.
