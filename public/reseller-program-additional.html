Rules of engagement

Do we compete with you? Here it is in writing.

We sell direct and through partners. The line between the two is a published rule — not a conversation you win each time.

Register in the portal

Submit the customer, workload and value. You get an accept, a decline with the reason, or a conflict notice within two business days — never silence.

If we’re already there

If the account is already ours or another partner’s, we say so immediately and tell you what’s still open. We don’t shadow a deal you’ve invested in.

Direct team stands down

On a registered opportunity our direct sellers stop quoting, and inbound requests from that customer route back to you — for the full window.

Renewals stay yours

The partner who won the customer keeps full margin on every renewal and expansion while they hold the relationship — no rate decay.

Who owns what

You own the commercial relationship. Your customer owns their data and tenant. We own the platform and the SLA — and we don’t market to your customers.

If it goes wrong

Conflicts go to your partner manager, then the channel director, with a decision inside five business days. If we breach the rules, your margin is honoured anyway.
