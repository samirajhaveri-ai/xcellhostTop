    Responsible AI & professional guardrails

    AI changes the drafting. Not who answers for the work.

    A neutral summary of the rules the course works within. It is not legal or professional advice; ICAI pronouncements and your firm's policies take precedence. Checked October 2026.

    Five rules we never bend
    1. No identifiable client data in public AI tools.Anonymise extracts, or use tools the firm has approved under contract.
    2. Verify every section, rule and circular.AI invents and misnumbers provisions. Check the bare Act and the notified rules before you rely on any citation.
    3. UDIN and signing stay human.Certificates, audit reports and filings are signed by the CA after review. AI never generates a UDIN or signs.
    4. Document AI use in the working papers.Record what was asked, what was produced and what you checked, so another auditor can follow it.
    5. Professional judgement is not delegated.Materiality, opinions and advice are the CA's decisions.
    CA Act, 1949 · Second ScheduleConfidentiality of client information

    Clause (1) of Part I treats disclosing information acquired in a professional engagement, without the client's consent or a legal requirement, as professional misconduct. Pasting client data into an outside AI tool needs that test.

    ICAI Code of EthicsConfidentiality and professional competence

    The Code lists confidentiality and professional competence and due care among the fundamental principles. Both apply when staff use AI tools on engagements.

    UDIN · since 2019Unique Document Identification Number

    ICAI made UDIN mandatory for certificates from 1 February 2019, for GST and tax audit reports from 1 April 2019, and for all other attest functions from 1 July 2019. It is generated by the signing member.

    Income-tax Act, 2025 · 1 April 2026New Act, new section numbers

    The 2025 Act replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. Tax audit, for example, moved from section 44AB to section 63, with the report in Form 26 for tax years from 1 April 2026. AI trained on older text will cite the old numbers.

    SA 230 · SQC 1Audit documentation and quality control

    SA 230 sets what audit documentation must show. SQM 1 and SQM 2 were due on 1 April 2026, but the ICAI Council deferred them until further announcement, so SQC 1 continues to apply.

    DPDP Act 2023 · Rules 2025Digital Personal Data Protection

    Client and employee records contain personal data. The Rules were notified in November 2025, with most obligations applying from May 2027: notice, consent, security safeguards and breach reporting.

    GST · IMS & GSTR-2BInvoice Management System

    Since October 2024 recipients accept, reject or keep pending supplier invoices in IMS, and GSTR-2B is generated on the 14th from those actions. Module 2 reconciles against it.

    ICAI's own AI workAI Committee, AICA courses and CA GPT

    ICAI runs a Certificate Course on AI for Chartered Accountants (AICA, Levels 1 and 2) through its AI Committee, and launched CA GPT for members on 1 July 2024. This course is independent of ICAI and is not an ICAI programme.
