    The threat landscape

    Six ways a normal workday turns into an incident.

    Endpoints, cloud workloads and network systems are exposed every day. Here is what each threat costs a business — and how Scrutiny EDR answers it.

    Ransomware

    Files and systems locked until a ransom is paid — billing, accounts and operations can stop for days.

    EDR spots the behaviour, isolates the device and helps you recover.

    Phishing

    A convincing email tricks someone into opening an attachment or giving away a password.

    Risky activity after a click is flagged and stopped.

    Malware

    Harmful software slips in through downloads or shared drives and quietly damages or steals data.

    Malicious files are detected and quarantined.

    Exploits

    Gaps in unpatched software are used to get a foothold — often without anyone noticing.

    Unusual behaviour from trusted apps raises an alert.

    Unauthorised access

    Someone signs in with stolen or weak credentials and moves around as if they belong.

    Odd access patterns are flagged and the device contained.

    Insider misuse

    An employee or contractor misuses access — deliberately or by mistake — and sensitive systems are put at risk.

    Activity is recorded on the timeline for review.
