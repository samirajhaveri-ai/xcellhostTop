Overview

Privileged access is where most damage is done — so put it behind a vault, a clock and a camera.

Privileged accounts — domain admins, server root, database owners, firewall and switch admins, cloud root and break-glass accounts, and the logins your vendors use — can change or wipe anything. In many Indian companies they are shared, rarely changed and kept in a spreadsheet or someone's head. Privileged access management (PAM) takes those credentials out of people's hands, stores them in an encrypted vault and hands them out only for an approved task, for a limited time.

XcellHost runs PAM as a service from Mumbai: we discover your privileged accounts, vault and rotate them, broker admin and vendor sessions through a gateway that records every keystroke and screen, and send the activity to your SIEM or our 24×7 SOC. For Microsoft 365 and Azure admin roles we pair it with Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management, so on-prem and cloud privilege follow one policy.

Why it matters

Attackers rarely break in as admin. They log in as one.

Identity-led intrusions follow a familiar pattern: a stolen or reused password, then a hunt for an account with more rights. PAM removes the standing rights they are looking for and leaves a record if anyone tries.

CERT-In directions (April 2022)

Report notable cyber incidents within 6 hours and keep ICT logs for a rolling 180 days in India. PAM session logs and recordings make the "who did what, when" question answerable in minutes.

RBI & SEBI

RBI's IT governance Master Direction (2023) expects MFA for privileged users and oversight of elevated access; SEBI's CSCRF (2024) sets privileged access expectations for regulated entities. PAM helps you meet and evidence them.

DPDP Act 2023

Reasonable security safeguards for personal data start with who can reach the databases that hold it. PAM limits, times and logs that access.

Guidance only — PAM supports your controls and evidence; confirm obligations with your compliance advisers.
Capabilities

Eight controls, one privileged access layer.

Discovery

Scan AD, servers, databases and cloud for privileged, local-admin and service accounts — including ones nobody remembers.

Credential vault

Encrypted storage for passwords, SSH keys and secrets, with role-based access and dual control for the most sensitive ones.

Rotation

Change passwords and keys after each checkout or on a schedule, and keep dependent services in sync.

Just-in-time access

Request, approve with MFA, use for a fixed window, then lose the right automatically — no permanent admin rights.

Session management

Broker RDP, SSH, database and web-console sessions without revealing the password; record video and keystrokes.

Command control

Allow, warn or block specific commands in live sessions, and let the SOC watch or end a session in real time.

Vendor access

Give outsourced IT and OEM engineers browser-based, time-boxed access to named systems — no VPN, no shared passwords.

Audit & analytics

Searchable session history, approval trails, risky-activity alerts and reports, streamed to Microsoft Sentinel or your SIEM.
