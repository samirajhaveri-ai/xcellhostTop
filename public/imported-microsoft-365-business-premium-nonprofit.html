    Overview

    The whole Microsoft stack, sized for a smaller NGO.

    Microsoft 365 Business Premium is Microsoft's top plan for organisations of up to 300 users. It puts desktop Office, Exchange mail, Teams and SharePoint together with the security tools larger enterprises buy separately: Defender for Business for laptops and phones, Defender for Office 365 Plan 1 for phishing and malicious attachments, Intune for device management, Microsoft Entra ID P1 for Conditional Access, and Purview for sensitivity labels and DLP.

    For verified nonprofits XcellHost lists it at ₹480 per user a month*. It's the bridge tier: right for a head office and a few field offices under 300 people. Past that size — or if you want 100 GB mailboxes and Windows Enterprise — Microsoft 365 E3 Nonprofit at ₹700 is the better home.

    Eligibility

    Eligibility & TechSoup India verification.

    Nonprofit pricing needs Microsoft's approval of your organisation. Here's what qualifies, what changed in 2025 and how we help.

    Qualifies

    Per Microsoft: nonprofits and NGOs with recognised legal status in India and a community-benefit mission; public libraries and museums.

    Doesn't qualify

    Government bodies, schools, colleges and universities, political, labour and trade associations, financial institutions and individuals.

    Documents

    Registration certificate (Trust, Society or Section 8), 12A/80G if held, PAN and address. Share any existing TechSoup India validation. A paid employee or authorised volunteer registers; Microsoft decides.

    What changed · 1 July 2025
    Before10 free Business Premium licences (grant) + discount
    NowGrant ended at renewal — discount only, per Microsoft

    Per Microsoft, the Business Premium nonprofit grant was discontinued from 1 July 2025 and the free nonprofit grant is now up to 300 Microsoft 365 Business Basic licences. If you held granted licences, we'll move those users to paid Business Premium Nonprofit or Business Basic before renewal so nothing is lost.
