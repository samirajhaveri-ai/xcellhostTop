    Overview

    Buying an SSL certificate is the easy part. Generating the key and CSR, passing domain validation, installing the certificate with the right intermediate chain and checking that every browser trusts it is where most teams lose a day — or ship a broken padlock. The XcellHost SSL Installation Service hands that whole job to our engineers. You pick a time slot and arrange access; a named XcellHost engineer joins over a temporary login, a screen share or a call, installs and tests the certificate, and sends you a short report. It works for certificates bought from us or anywhere else, and it's billed in rupees with a GST invoice.

        Installation done right, in India

        Encryption is expected — a broken padlock is a finding

        Indian rules don't care which CA you chose. They care that data in transit is protected, certificates are tracked and incidents are reported fast.

        DPDP Act, 2023

        Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards. An expired or misconfigured certificate on a login or payment form undermines exactly that.

        CERT-In directions

        Cyber incidents must be reported within six hours. Weak protocols and broken chains are common scan findings we remove at install.
