Overview

Lift email engagement. Put your logo next to your name.

BIMI — Brand Indicators for Message Identification — lets a mailbox provider show your company's logo beside your messages in the inbox list and inside the mail. To use it, your domain must pass email authentication (SPF, DKIM and a DMARC policy at enforcement), and your logo must be backed by a Mark Certificate that proves you own it. Vendors report open and engagement lifts of up to 10% once a brand logo shows in the inbox.

DigiCert issues two kinds: the Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) for registered trademarks, which also earns a blue verified tick in Gmail, and the newer Common Mark Certificate (CMC) for logos you've used publicly for at least a year, without a trademark. XcellHost checks which one you qualify for, moves your DMARC to enforcement, converts your logo to SVG Tiny PS, collects the documents and publishes the DNS record — then bills it all in rupees with GST.

    Brand trust in India
    Impersonation is the threat. DMARC plus a logo is the answer.
    Fake order, bank and delivery emails using real Indian brand names are routine. BIMI rewards the businesses that lock their domain down.
    
    
    
      
    Brand impersonation
    Phishing that borrows a known brand's name and look is one of the most common lures CERT-In warns about. A verified logo — and its absence on look-alike domains — gives customers a visual cue.
    
      
    Gmail & Yahoo sender rules
    Since 2024, bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo must authenticate with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. BIMI builds on the same foundation and turns the work into something customers can see.
    
      
    DPDP Act, 2023
    Businesses handling personal data must take reasonable security safeguards. Stopping your domain from being spoofed protects the customers you email.
    
      
    DMARC adoption
    Many Indian domains publish DMARC at p=none, which only monitors. Moving to quarantine or reject is required for BIMI — and is the step that actually blocks spoofing.
