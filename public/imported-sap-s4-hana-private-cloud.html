    04Architecture

    App tier, HANA pair, DR copy — drawn the way we build it.

    A typical production design. Exact layout, RPO and RTO targets are agreed in your design document.

    1. Users & integrationsFiori, SAP GUI and interfaces arrive through VPN/MPLS and the Web Dispatcher.
    2. Application tierApp servers spread over separate hosts; ASCS/ERS clustered so the enqueue survives a host loss.
    3. HANA HA pairPrimary and secondary HANA with synchronous system replication; a cluster moves the virtual IP on failure.
    4. DR to a second site*Asynchronous replication to a second data-centre location; switchover rehearsed in drills.
    5. BackupsData and log backups kept apart from the database, with copies off-site and restore tests.

    Illustrative design. DR location, RPO/RTO and replication mode are confirmed per customer. *Second-site DR to be confirmed with XcellHost.
