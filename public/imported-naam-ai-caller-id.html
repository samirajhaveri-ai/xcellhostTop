Naam features

More than a name. Less than your contact list.

Each feature below is as documented by Naam on naam.ai or its Google Play listing, accessed 17 Nov 2026.

Caller identity, not just a name

Naam says it shows the caller's name, designation and location, even for unsaved numbers, with "information about profession and organisation as well", so a supplier or a bank's relationship manager is recognised before you answer.

Spam and fraud detection

Naam flags top spammers so the call screen "will flash red", detects fraud calls and spam messages, and shows a number's report history.

Blocking

Block telemarketers, spammers, scammers and fraudsters from the profile section or a block list, with real-time, AI-based spam reporting for calls and messages.

Intelligent dialer

Naam identifies the names of unknown numbers while you dial, so a wrong digit is caught before the call goes out.

Regional languages

naam.ai lists English, Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati and Telugu. The full current list: confirm with XcellHost*.

No contact sync

naam.ai states "Contact access Not Required" and that it is "Hosted on Indian servers". The Play listing says "No contact, location, or media syncing."

SMS sender ID and spam warnings

Set Naam as the default messaging app to see sender ID and spam warnings on SMS and MMS, as described on its Play listing.

Reverse lookup

Search a number that is not in your call log, for example a missed call written on a note or a number from an email signature.

Lead validation

Naam describes "lead validation to check a number is genuine before you follow up". Bulk or API checks for CRM lists: confirm with XcellHost*.

    SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

    Identify callers, call customers, and stay within the rules.

    Caller identification processes personal data, and outbound calls are regulated. A plain summary of what applies, checked on 17 November 2026. Not legal advice.

    India

    DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025

    Processing personal data of people in India needs a lawful ground, usually notice and consent, plus security, accuracy and erasure duties. The core obligations apply from 13 May 2027. Use number checks for a stated purpose and keep a record of it.

    India

    "Publicly available" is narrow

    Section 3(c)(ii) excludes personal data made or caused to be made public by the person it relates to, or by someone legally obliged to publish it. Data simply found online, or published by third parties, is not automatically outside the Act. Treat caller details as personal data.

    India · calls & SMS

    TRAI TCCCPR 2018, amended 2025

    The second amendment (TRAI release, 12 Feb 2025): promotional calls from the 140 series, service and transactional calls from 1600, no ordinary 10-digit numbers for telemarketing, complaints accepted within 7 days and acted on within 5, and no fresh consent request for 90 days after an opt-out. Respect DND preferences.

    India · caller name

    CNAP, the network caller name

    DoT has directed operators to show the subscriber's registered name on calls (Calling Name Presentation), on by default with an opt-out, rollout targeted for March 2026 (reported 29 Oct 2025). App-based caller ID such as Naam works alongside it.

    Naam · privacy

    What Naam says about your data

    naam.ai states "Contact access Not Required" and "Hosted on Indian servers"; the Play listing says "No contact, location, or media syncing". IndiaAI (11 Jun 2024) reports Naam does not collect phone contacts or SMS for caller identification. Read Naam's own privacy policy on naam.ai before rollout.

    Naam · support

    Feedback and data requests

    Naam's Play listing gives support@naam.ai for feedback. For staff data requests during a business rollout, XcellHost* coordinates with Naam. Whether a business or ad-free edition exists: confirm with XcellHost*.

    Everywhere

    Opt-out handling

    One opt-out list across calls, SMS, WhatsApp and email. Check it before every campaign. Suppressed numbers stay suppressed.

    Everywhere

    Data retention

    If staff copy caller details into your CRM, keep them only as long as the purpose needs, then delete or re-check.
