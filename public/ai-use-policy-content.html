How Does XcellHost Use AI, And Is My Data Safe?

XcellHost uses AI to detect threats faster, triage support tickets, automate routine operations and draft documents. Your data is never used to train public AI models, is processed only on vetted enterprise AI platforms, stays logically isolated per client, and every AI-assisted security action, infrastructure change or client deliverable is reviewed by a qualified XcellHost engineer before it takes effect.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
  • Faster threat detection. AI correlates alerts across your estate in seconds, so our SOC analysts act on real incidents sooner.
  • Quicker ticket resolution. AI triage routes and pre-diagnoses tickets, so engineers start with context, not a blank screen.
  • No loss of control. Automation proposes; our engineers decide. Nothing changes in your environment without human approval.
  • Audit-ready evidence. AI use in your services is logged and explainable for your DPDP, ISO and RBI/SEBI audits.

01Purpose And Scope

In short:This policy explains how XcellHost uses AI, the limits we set, and the rights you have as a client.

XcellHost Cloud Services Pvt. Ltd. ("XcellHost", "we") has delivered managed cloud, cybersecurity and digital-trust services to Indian businesses since 1999. Artificial intelligence now helps us protect client environments, run operations and serve customers faster.

This policy applies to every XcellHost employee, contractor and partner, and to every system that processes client data or delivers a client-facing service — including our cloud, security, backup, Microsoft 365, GPU and support services.

02Our AI Principles

In short:Seven principles guide every AI decision we make.

Human AccountabilityA named XcellHost professional is accountable for every AI-assisted outcome.

Privacy By DesignWe use the minimum data needed and never share one client's data with another.

Security FirstAI tools go through the same security review as any production system.

TransparencyWe tell you where AI is used in your services when you ask.

FairnessWe do not use AI to make discriminatory or consequential decisions about people.

AccuracyAI outputs are verified by qualified staff before they reach you.

ComplianceWe align AI use with Indian law and recognised standards.

03How We Use AI

In short:AI helps in four areas: security, operations, automation and internal productivity.

Cybersecurity And Threat DetectionReal-time log analysis, anomaly detection, threat-intelligence correlation, phishing and DMARC analysis, and faster incident investigation in our 24×7 SOC.

IT Operations And NOCTicket triage and categorisation, root-cause suggestions, capacity forecasting, and proactive alerts from monitoring data.

AutomationScripted remediation playbooks, configuration checks, backup verification, patch scheduling and report generation.

Communication And DocumentationDrafting and summarising reports, knowledge articles and proposals — always reviewed by a person before they reach a client.

04How We Protect Your Data

In short:Client data never goes into public AI tools, never trains third-party models, and never mixes with another client's data.
  • No client personal data, confidential data or credentials are entered into public or consumer AI tools.
  • We use only enterprise AI platforms that encrypt data in transit and at rest and contractually prohibit using customer data to train their models.
  • Data minimisation: an AI system receives only the data it needs for its specific task.
  • Client isolation: each client's data is logically segregated; AI outputs for one client are never built from another client's data.
  • Where a service is hosted in India, AI processing of that service's data stays in India unless you agree otherwise in writing.
  • Personal data is processed in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

06Approved Tools And Governance

In short:Only reviewed AI tools are allowed near client data, accessed through company accounts.
  • Every AI tool is assessed for security, privacy, data residency, certifications and contract terms before approval.
  • Access is through single sign-on with role-based permissions; personal AI accounts are not permitted for client work.
  • We keep an internal register of approved AI tools and the services they support.
  • Tools are re-assessed whenever a vendor changes its data-handling, training or security practices, and at least once a year.

07What We Will Never Do

In short:Some uses of AI are off-limits, whatever the business case.
  • Use your data to train public or third-party AI models.
  • Let AI make final decisions on security containment, production changes or client communications without a human.
  • Use AI for discriminatory decisions in hiring, credit, insurance or similar matters.
  • Generate deceptive content aimed at clients, regulators, partners or the public, including deepfakes.
  • Use AI to monitor individuals beyond what your contract and the law allow.
  • Enter credentials, keys or secrets into any AI tool.

08Your Rights As A Client

In short:You can ask, opt out and challenge — and we will respond.

Right To AskAsk your account manager which AI tools support your services and what data they see.

Right To Opt OutRequest a manual alternative where technically feasible. We will tell you any effect on timelines or pricing first.

Right To ChallengeQuestion any AI-assisted output; a senior engineer will review it and respond.

Right To DeletionOn contract exit, we delete your data from AI-assisted systems along with our other systems, as per your agreement.

09AI Incidents And Errors

In short:If AI gets something wrong, we fix it, tell you, and learn from it.

An AI incident is any case where an AI system produces a harmful, inaccurate or unauthorised result, or processes data it should not. Staff report these through our ISO 27001 incident process.

Where an AI incident is also a cybersecurity incident, we follow CERT-In's reporting directions, including the six-hour reporting window. Where personal data is affected, we notify you promptly so you can meet your obligations under the DPDP Act.

10Standards And Regulations

In short:We align AI use with Indian law and international standards.
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and CERT-In Directions, 2022
  • India AI Governance Guidelines issued by MeitY
  • ISO/IEC 27001 (our certified information-security management system) and ISO/IEC 20000-1
  • ISO/IEC 42001 for AI management systems (used as our reference framework)
  • Sector rules our clients follow, such as RBI, SEBI and IRDAI guidance, where they apply to a service

11Training And Accountability

In short:Everyone who uses AI at XcellHost is trained and accountable.

All staff complete responsible-AI and data-protection training when they join and every year after. Engineers who use AI in client environments receive additional training on verification and escalation.

Breaches of this policy are handled under our disciplinary process. Our leadership team owns this policy and reviews AI risk as part of our information-security management reviews.

12Review And Changes

In short:We review this policy every quarter and tell clients about material changes.

We review this policy quarterly, and sooner if the law, our services or AI technology change significantly. Material changes are announced to active clients by email and on this page, with a new effective date.

FAQAI Use Policy — Frequently Asked Questions

Does XcellHost use my data to train AI models?
No. Client data is never used to train public or third-party AI models, and our AI vendors are contractually barred from doing so.
Will AI make changes to my servers automatically?
Not without human control. AI can suggest changes, and only pre-approved low-risk playbooks run automatically. All other changes are reviewed and executed by an XcellHost engineer.
Is my data processed outside India?
For services hosted in India, AI processing of that data stays in India unless you agree otherwise in writing.
Can I opt out of AI in my services?
Yes, where technically feasible. Ask your account manager and we will offer a manual alternative, noting any impact on timelines or pricing.
Which laws does this policy follow?
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, the IT Act 2000, CERT-In Directions 2022 and MeitY's India AI Governance Guidelines, with ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 as reference standards.
How do I ask which AI tools are used for my account?
Email support@xcellhost.cloud or contact your account manager. We will tell you which AI tools support your services and what data they access.
