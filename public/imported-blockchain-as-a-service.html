Overview

One record that every member trusts — without one member owning it.

When a bank, an exporter, an insurer and a shipping line each keep their own copy of the same trade, they spend days reconciling. A permissioned blockchain gives known parties one shared, append-only record: every change is signed, checked against agreed rules and kept in the same order on every member's node.

Running that network is the hard part — certificate authorities, ordering nodes, peers, upgrades, new members, backups and 3 a.m. alerts. XcellCloud Blockchain as a Service takes it off your hands: we design, deploy and operate Hyperledger Fabric, Hyperledger Besu or Corda networks in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, with HSM-backed keys, an explorer and 24×7 monitoring. You keep control of the business rules and the members.

    How it works

    One transaction, five checks before it is final.

    The Hyperledger Fabric flow — Besu and Corda differ in detail, but every permissioned ledger signs, orders and validates before it writes.

    Client appsigned proposal
    Endorsing peerssimulate · sign
    Ordering serviceRaft / BFT · cut block
    Validatepolicy · versions
    Commitevery member's ledger
    01Propose & endorse

    The app sends a signed proposal; peers of the required members run the contract and sign the result.

    02Order

    The ordering service puts endorsed transactions in a fixed order and cuts them into blocks.

    03Validate

    Every peer checks the endorsement policy and that no data changed since simulation.

    04Commit & notify

    Valid transactions update the world state; events reach your apps and the explorer.

    PMLAFIU-IND registration

    A Finance Ministry notification of 7 March 2023 made VDA service providers — exchange, transfer, safekeeping and issuer-related services — reporting entities under the PMLA. They register with FIU-IND, run KYC, report suspicious transactions and keep records for five years. Most enterprise ledgers carry no VDAs; if yours does, our logs and India-hosted storage support that record-keeping.

    6 h · 180 dCERT-In directions

    CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 require cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours and ICT logs to be kept for 180 days within India. Node, CA and gateway logs can be retained in Mumbai to match, with an incident contact on our side.

    DPDPDPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025

    Personal data written to an immutable ledger cannot be erased later. We design for hashes and references on-chain with personal data off-chain, plus reasonable security safeguards and breach intimation support. The Rules were notified in November 2025 and phase in. See our vDPO service.
