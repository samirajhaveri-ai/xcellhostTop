Deployment

Three places a WAF can sit.

Most sites use the edge. Apps with strict data residency use a dedicated WAF in the VPC. Kubernetes teams can enforce policy at the ingress.

You change one DNS record to an edge hostname. TLS is issued or uploaded at the edge, and your firewall accepts only edge addresses so nobody can go around the WAF. DNS can stay with us on Managed DNS.

  • Live in hours for most sites
  • Floods absorbed before your link
  • Partner edge network*

The WAF runs as a single-tenant instance in your Virtual Network, between the virtual firewall and the Virtual Load Balancer. Decrypted traffic never leaves our Mumbai Tier IV data centre, which suits apps with strict data-residency rules.

  • Your TLS keys stay in your VPC
  • Fits existing subnets and routes
  • Logs to your SIEM in India

A WAF module on the ingress controller applies CRS and your own rules per route. Rule changes go through pull requests like any other code, and our SOC still watches the alerts.

Compliance

What regulators actually ask for.

A WAF helps you meet these. It does not make you compliant on its own.

PCI DSS v4.0.1 · 6.4.2

Automated protection for public web apps

Public-facing web applications must sit behind an automated technical solution that keeps detecting and preventing web-based attacks. The old option of a yearly manual review is gone. It became mandatory on 31 March 2025, with the other future-dated v4 requirements.

Source: Qualys blog, 27 Aug 2026, on PCI DSS v4.0.1. Not re-verified against the PCI SSC text.
PCI DSS · 6.4.3 & 11.6.1

Scripts on payment pages

Every script on a payment page must be inventoried, authorised and integrity-checked, and changes to the page or its headers must raise an alert. Our Enterprise tier adds client-side protection for this.

Source: Qualys blog, 27 Aug 2026.
CERT-In · 28 Apr 2022

6 hours to report, 180 days of logs

Website defacement or intrusion, attacks on applications and DDoS attacks are on CERT-In's reportable list, with a 6-hour deadline. Logs must be kept for 180 days within India. We store WAF logs in India and draft the first report with you.

Source: CERT-In Directions 20(3)/2022, via taxguru.in, read 19 Nov 2026.
DPDP Act 2023

Reasonable security safeguards

Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach. A tuned WAF in front of login, account and checkout pages is one of the controls you can show.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, s. 8(5). Not re-verified.

A WAF sits in front of a website or API and inspects each HTTP request: the URL, headers, cookies and body. It blocks requests that look like attacks, such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting or a known exploit. A network firewall works on IP addresses and ports and does not read the request.

The OWASP Core Rule Set is an open-source set of generic attack rules used by many WAF engines. Each matching rule adds to an anomaly score, and the request is blocked when the score reaches a threshold (5 by default). Paranoia levels 1 to 4 add stricter rules: more attacks caught, more false positives. Most production sites run PL1 or PL2.
