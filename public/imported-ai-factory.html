    Overview · what an AI factory is

    A GPU on its own does not run AI. The stack around it does.

    An AI factory is all of it under one operator: the GPUs, the fabric between them, storage that keeps them fed, schedulers, model tooling, endpoints and the people on call. Buy GPUs alone and you end up building the rest yourself.

    The XcellHost AI Factory is a managed, full-stack platform for training, fine-tuning and serving AI models. It runs in our Mumbai Tier IV data centre on NVIDIA L4, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and H200 GPUs, with high-speed networking, NVMe, parallel and S3-compatible storage, Kubernetes or Slurm scheduling, MLOps tooling, a model hub and private inference endpoints, all as one service.

    Most teams start with a pilot on a few GPUs and get stuck at production. Drivers, networking, job scheduling, storage throughput, model serving, monitoring and governance each turn into a project of their own. With XcellHost, one team owns every layer, data stays in India, and you pay a monthly invoice in rupees with GST. Every GPU configuration is priced on quote.
