Overview

Email was never built to prove who sent it. S/MIME fixes that.

An S/MIME certificate binds your email address to a cryptographic key issued by DigiCert. Mail apps use it to stamp each message with your signature and to lock its contents so only the intended recipient can open them.

Signing tells the recipient two things at a glance: the message really came from your mailbox, and nothing in it was changed on the way. A spoofed "please update our bank details" mail cannot carry your signature.

Encryption keeps the body and attachments unreadable from the moment you press send until the recipient opens them — on the wire and while the message sits on mail servers.

Ready in minutes. The individual certificate checks only that you control the mailbox. Organisations that need the company name in the certificate, plus client authentication and Office document signing, choose the business certificate.

Why it matters in India

Email carries personal and financial data. Regulators expect it to be protected.

DPDP Act, 2023

Data fiduciaries must take reasonable security safeguards for personal data. Encrypting it in email is a direct, demonstrable control.

CERT-In directions

Phishing and impersonation are reportable incidents. Signed mail reduces the chance a spoofed message succeeds.

RBI & SEBI entities

Regulated firms are expected to secure customer communication. S/MIME adds sender assurance and confidentiality.

Global customers

Exporters and IT/ITeS firms are often asked for encrypted email to satisfy GDPR and HIPAA clauses.
