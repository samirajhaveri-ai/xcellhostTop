    Quick answer

    What is Microsoft Power Automate — and what does XcellHost do with it?

    Power Automate is Microsoft's low-code automation service. A cloud flow starts on a trigger — an email, a form, a new row, a schedule — and moves data between apps through connectors. A desktop flow is a software bot that clicks and types in Windows or web apps that have no API. Approvals, AI document extraction and process mining sit on the same platform.

    XcellHost sells the licences (Premium per user, Process or Hosted Process per bot) with an Indian GST invoice, and does the work around them: picking which processes to automate, building and testing the flows, connecting Tally, SAP or Zoho, setting up DLP and environments, training your makers and watching the flows after go-live.

    Copilot in Power Automate

    Describe the job in a sentence. Review the flow Copilot drafts.

    Copilot is built into the Power Automate designer. It helps people who know the process — not just developers — get to a first working version, and it helps makers fix what breaks.

    • Cloud flows — describe what you need in plain language, over several turns, and Copilot builds and edits the flow.
    • Desktop flows — analyse bot activity, create flows from natural language (preview) and get help repairing automation errors.
    • Process Mining — guided data ingestion and process insights asked in natural language.
    • Automation centre — ask about past runs, work-queue performance and product features.

    Per Microsoft's documentation, India is a region where Copilot in Power Automate is switched on by default; admins can ask Microsoft support to turn it off at tenant level. Feature names and preview status change — XcellHost confirms what's live in your tenant.
