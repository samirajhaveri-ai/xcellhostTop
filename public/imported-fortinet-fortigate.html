      Inside FortiOS

      Security processors do the heavy lifting, so inspection stays switched on.

      FortiGate appliances use Fortinet-designed security processors (FortiASIC) alongside the main CPU to offload firewalling, VPN encryption and inspection work, per Fortinet. The practical effect: you can run IPS and SSL inspection without the box becoming the bottleneck — as long as it is sized correctly.

          FAQ

          FortiGate, answered.

          FortiOS is the operating system on every FortiGate, from the smallest desktop model to data-centre chassis. The same interface, policy model and features run across the range, so your team learns one system, and branches and head office share one policy design. It also connects the firewall to other Fortinet products — FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, FortiClient, FortiSwitch and FortiAP.

          FortiGuard is Fortinet's threat research and update service. A subscription keeps the FortiGate's IPS signatures, antivirus, sandbox, web-filter ratings and other intelligence up to date. Without it the appliance still routes and firewalls traffic, but it can no longer recognise new threats or filter websites by category.

          Per Fortinet: ATP covers network threats — IPS, application control, antivirus, advanced malware protection and cloud sandbox. UTP adds web, DNS and video filtering and botnet/C2 blocking. Enterprise Protection adds AI-based inline malware prevention, DLP, attack-surface security rating and IoT security. All include FortiCare Premium 24×7 support. UTP suits most offices; Enterprise suits regulated firms.

          Yes. SD-WAN is part of FortiOS on every FortiGate, so you can combine two or more internet links, steer applications by SLA and fail over automatically without a separate SD-WAN licence, per Fortinet. An optional SD-WAN bundle adds underlay and application monitoring services. Many Indian businesses use it to replace or back up costly MPLS links.

          FortiGates include Fortinet-designed security processors next to the main CPU. They offload tasks such as firewall session handling, VPN encryption and parts of content inspection. In practice this is why a small desktop FortiGate can keep IPS and SSL inspection on. Inspection still has limits, which is why we size against Fortinet's threat-protection and SSL-inspection figures, not the headline firewall number.

          It depends on internet bandwidth, how much traffic you inspect (SSL inspection is the heaviest), users, VPN users and branch tunnels. As a rough guide, small offices fit the 30G–60F, growing offices with faster links the 70G–90G, and campuses or hubs the 120G or 200G. Use the sizer above, then ask us for a free written sizing note.

          Yes. Every bundle includes Fortinet's FortiCare support for the device. On top of that, XcellHost's managed firewall service (from ₹2,999* per device per month) covers 24×7 monitoring, rule changes, FortiOS upgrades, renewal tracking, monthly reports and help during incidents.

          The firewall keeps passing traffic under its existing rules, but IPS, antivirus and web-filter updates stop and some services stop working, so protection falls behind quickly. We remind you well before expiry and quote renewals for 1, 3 or 5 years; longer terms usually cost less per year.

          Yes. We export your existing rules, NAT and VPN settings, translate and clean them up, review them with you and cut over in a planned window with a roll-back plan. Fortinet also offers a FortiConverter migration service, included in Enterprise Protection per Fortinet. Migration is priced on quote after a policy review.

          If the office can't work without internet or branch connectivity — a bank branch, a hospital, a plant — an HA pair removes the firewall as a single point of failure. It means two identical appliances, each with its own subscription. For small branches, a second internet link via SD-WAN is often the better first step.

          CERT-In's April 2022 directions expect ICT system logs to be kept for 180 days within India and incidents to be reported within 6 hours. A FortiGate's own disk is small, so we forward logs to FortiAnalyzer or a SIEM sized for 180 days, sync clocks via NTP and document who to call. That helps you meet the direction; the obligation stays with your organisation.

          To you. The appliance and its FortiGuard and FortiCare contracts are registered to your company on Fortinet's support portal, and you hold the admin credentials. If you later stop our managed service, you keep the device, the configuration and the subscription.
