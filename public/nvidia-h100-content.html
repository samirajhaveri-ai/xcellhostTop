What are the NVIDIA H100 Tensor Core GPU specifications?

  1. 1

    80 GB HBM memory

    80 GB of HBM3 at 3.35 TB/s feeds the Tensor Cores fast enough for large-batch training and high-throughput serving.

  2. 2

    Hopper Transformer Engine

    Fourth-generation Tensor Cores with FP8: 3,958 TFLOPS FP8 and 1,979 TFLOPS FP16 with sparsity.

  3. 3

    NVLink 900 GB/s

    Cards in a node talk to each other at NVLink speed, so multi-GPU training scales without the PCIe bottleneck.

  4. 4

    Multi-Instance GPU

    Split one GPU into up to seven isolated instances, each with its own memory and compute — ideal for serving many small models.

  5. 5

    SXM mezzanine board

    Mounted directly on the HGX baseboard for higher power limits and better cooling than a PCIe card.
