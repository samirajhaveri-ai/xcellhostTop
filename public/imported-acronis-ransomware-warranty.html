    Overview

    A backup is a promise. This one comes with a financial guarantee.

    Most businesses only find out whether their backups work on the worst day of the year. A recovery point that was never tested, sat on a share the attacker could reach, or was quietly deleted by a compromised admin account is not a recovery point at all. Ransomware crews know this, which is why backups are one of the first things they go after.

    The Acronis Anti-Ransomware Recovery Warranty puts money behind recovery. Per Acronis, when a qualifying recovery point cannot be restored after a covered event, eligible customers may be reimbursed for qualifying recovery costs, up to the lesser of US$1 million or the fees paid to Acronis for the applicable services in the previous 12 months. There is nothing extra to buy: it applies by default to recovery points that meet its prerequisites. XcellHost's job is to make sure yours do.

    How it works

    Back up, secure, lock, validate — and a failed recovery can be reimbursed.

    The sequence Acronis describes for a recovery point to be covered. Every step is a setting or a job that has to keep running — which is exactly where a managed service earns its keep.

    1. 01
      Back up to Acronis cloud

      Endpoint disk, volume or file backups stored in Acronis-hosted cloud storage.

    2. 02
      Secure the console

      Two-factor authentication switched on, so a stolen password alone cannot reach the backups.

    3. 03
      Lock with immutability

      Immutable storage in Compliance mode: deleted backups stay for the retention period and the setting cannot be weakened.

    4. 04
      Validate before you need it

      Checksum, screenshot or heartbeat validation proves the point can be restored.

    5. 05
      Recover — or be reimbursed

      Restore from the qualifying point. If that fails, Acronis takes on financial responsibility under its Warranty Policy.

    What's covered · what isn't

    Read the fine print before the incident, not after.

    Summarised from Acronis' warranty page and Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud release notes (checked October 2026). The binding terms are Acronis' Warranty Policy and your customer agreement; XcellHost confirms eligibility at quote.*

    Covered, per Acronis
    • Ransomware and other cyberattacks that make protected data inaccessible
    • Accidental deletion and operational mistakes
    • Corruption, failed backups and system failures — the event need not be ransomware
    • Qualifying recovery costs reimbursed if a qualifying point cannot be recovered, up to the lesser of US$1M or 12 months of applicable Acronis fees
    Not covered · limits
    • Not cyber insurance and does not replace it — no business-interruption, ransom or liability cover is described
    • Does not prevent incidents or data loss; it backs recovery
    • Backups kept elsewhere — a local NAS, your own S3 bucket or other non-Acronis-hosted storage — do not meet the storage prerequisite
    • Endpoints only: disk, volume and file archives. Other backup types are outside the stated scope
    Eligibility · all four
    1. Archive in Acronis-hosted cloud storage
    2. Two-factor authentication enabled
    3. Immutable storage, Compliance mode
    4. Validated by checksum, screenshot or heartbeat

    Per Acronis it is available to all Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud partners at no additional cost and enabled by default for everyone who meets these prerequisites. Acronis evaluates every recovery point against them.
