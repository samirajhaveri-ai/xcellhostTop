    EV in India

    Where identity matters most, regulators are watching too.

    No Indian rule names a certificate brand or demands EV. But the sectors that pick EV — banking, broking, insurance and government — are the ones whose frameworks expect strong encryption, verified identity and continuous watch.

    RBI-regulated entities

    Banks, NBFCs and payment players work under RBI's IT and cyber-security directions. EV on net-banking and payment pages makes the identity check visible, and Pro's scans support continuous monitoring.

    SEBI's CSCRF

    Brokers, depositories and AMCs must protect data in transit and track critical assets. CertCentral's inventory and expiry alerts help with certificate records.

    CERT-In directions

    Phishing and website compromise must be reported within six hours. CT monitoring flags rogue certificates for your domains; daily scans spot compromise early.

    DPDP Act & MeitY

    The DPDP Act requires reasonable security safeguards for personal data, and MeitY's GIGW expects government sites on HTTPS. EV shows citizens and customers the vetted entity behind a portal.
